No experience needed: every show is made by students, and most of them had never done it before
they joined.

## What you can do

- **Acting.** Audition for any show. You don't need a CV or headshots.
- **Directing and producing.** Pitch a show to the committee and make it happen.
- **Set and workshop.** Design and build sets in our workshop.
- **Lighting.** Rig, focus and programme the lights for a show.
- **Sound.** Design the sound for a show, mix live music and run the cues.
- **Costume and make-up.** Find, make and fit costumes, and do the make-up.
- **Front of house.** Welcome the audience, run the bar and learn the building.
- **Design and marketing.** Posters, trailers and social media for every show.

## How to start

1. **Join the theatre.** Membership is sold by the Students' Union: buy it from
   [the theatre's page on the SU shop](https://su.nottingham.ac.uk/shop?activity_id=42&category_id=2),
   then sign in here with the same email address. See [membership](/policies/membership) for what
   it costs and what it gives you.
2. **Come along.** Come to socials, auditions and crew calls. We post them every week.
3. **Make theatre.** Most people are on a show within a few weeks, on stage, backstage or front of
   house.
