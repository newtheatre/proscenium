Our own building has been closed since 2023, so for now we perform in one space. We don't publish
an equipment list here, because the kit changes from term to term: ask us for the current
specification instead.

## The Portland Studio

The Portland Studio is on University Park, opposite Mooch. It has 62 seats, two of which are usually
held back for front of house, and it has step-free access.

## Detailed specifications

For plans, lighting and sound equipment, power and get-in arrangements, write to
[technical@newtheatre.org.uk](mailto:technical@newtheatre.org.uk) and we will send you the current
specification. Our Technical Manager and Company Technical Directors look after the technical side of
the theatre; see [the committee](/about#the-committee).
