# Tickets and booking

This is starter text. Read it through and change anything that is not how your theatre works.

## Paying for your tickets

You can pay when you book, or reserve your tickets and pay at the box office when you arrive.
Reserved tickets are not yours until they are paid for.

## Reserved tickets that are not collected

Reserved tickets that have not been paid for are released :setting{name="box-office.reservations.release-before"} before the performance starts. This is after the house opens, when most people have arrived, so that anyone waiting at the box office can have the seats.

If your tickets are released, we keep your booking. If you then arrive, come to the box office with your booking reference: if seats are still free, we will seat you, and you pay then. If the performance has sold out, we cannot.

Tickets you have already paid for are never released.

## Cancelling

If you can no longer come, please let your reserved tickets go from the link in your booking email, so somebody else can have them.
