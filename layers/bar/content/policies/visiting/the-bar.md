- You must be :setting{name="bar.age-check.minimum-age"} or over to buy alcohol, and it is against the law to buy it for someone younger.
- If you look under :setting{name="bar.age-check.challenge-age"}, we will ask for proof of age: a passport, a photocard driving licence or a card with the PASS hologram. Without it we cannot serve you. Please do not take it personally.
- We will not serve anyone who appears drunk. Our staff's decision is final.
- Ask at the bar about allergens before you order. We cannot promise that anything is completely free of an allergen.
- If you open a tab, settle it before you leave.
