If you open a tab we hold the name you give, and your email address until your receipt has been sent. If we refuse a sale because of age, we record that a refusal happened, as licensing good practice expects. We do not record who you are.

::when{setting="bar.age-check.description" is="true"}
That record may include a short description of the person refused, so that staff on later shifts know. It never includes a name.
::
