---
title: How members behave
order: 80
revised: 2026-11-18
---

We expect members to treat one another, our volunteers and our audiences with respect, to look after the theatre's spaces and equipment, and to follow our policies and any reasonable instruction about safety.

We may suspend or end a membership for serious or repeated misconduct. Before we do, we will tell you what the concern is, give you a fair chance to respond, and tell you how to appeal. We may suspend at once where someone's safety is at risk.

Where we have a constitution or our own complaints and disciplinary procedures, they give the detail and take priority over this summary.
