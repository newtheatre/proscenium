You join through your account on this website. Each kind of membership says who can join it and what, if anything, it costs.

Some memberships are checked before they start, for example against a list from a students' union, or by a member of our team. We will tell you if we cannot confirm yours, and why.

::when{module="membership-fees"}
If your membership has a fee, it starts when the fee is paid. An application that has not been paid for after :setting{name="membership.applications.expire-after"} lapses, and you are welcome to apply again.
::
