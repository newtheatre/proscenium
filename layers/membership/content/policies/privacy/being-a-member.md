---
title: When you are a member
order: 80
revised: 2026-11-18
---

We hold which membership you have, when it starts and ends, and your membership number. Where membership is checked against a list from another organisation, such as a students' union, we keep the references on that list only in a scrambled form (a hash) that lets us check a match without being able to read them. We may keep the name on the list for :setting{name="membership.verification.names-keep-for"} so that staff can resolve a mismatch.
