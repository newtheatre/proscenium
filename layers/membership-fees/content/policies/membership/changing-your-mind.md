title: Changing your mind
order: 60
revised: 2026-11-18
law: gb
---

If you paid to join online, you can cancel within 14 days of joining and get your money back.

::when{has="core.organisation.contact-email"}
To cancel, contact :setting{name="core.organisation.contact-email"}.
::

If you used a member benefit in that time, such as a member-price ticket, we may keep back a fair amount for it.

After 14 days you can still leave whenever you like, but we do not refund fees for the time left.
