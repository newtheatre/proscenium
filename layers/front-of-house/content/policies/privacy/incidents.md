---
title: If there is an accident or incident
order: 100
revised: 2026-11-18
---

If you are involved in an accident, an injury or another incident at one of our venues, we record what happened, who was involved and what we did. This may include information about your health. We are required by health and safety law to keep these records, and we may need them to protect someone's life. We keep them for :setting{name="front-of-house.retention.incidents"}, or for :setting{name="front-of-house.retention.incidents-child"} where a child was involved.
