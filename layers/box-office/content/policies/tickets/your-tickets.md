---
title: Your tickets
order: 70
revised: 2026-11-18
---

We email your tickets as soon as you have paid, each with its own code. Show it on your phone or on paper. You can also find your tickets in My account, and the box office can look you up by your email address.

Each ticket lets one person in once. Keep your tickets to yourself: if a ticket is copied, only the first one scanned gets in.

::when{setting="box-office.tickets.transferable" is="true"}
You may give your tickets to someone else. A reduced-price ticket can only be used by someone who qualifies for it. Tickets must not be sold on for more than you paid, and we may cancel tickets that are.

#otherwise
Tickets are for the people you booked for. If you need to pass them to someone else, ask the box office.
::
