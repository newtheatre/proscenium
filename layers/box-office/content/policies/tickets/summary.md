- When you book, you make an agreement with us. These are its terms.
- The price you see is the price you pay. We add nothing at the end.
- If you reserve tickets without paying, pay for them at least :setting{name="box-office.reservations.release-before"} before the performance starts. After that we may give your places to someone else.
- If we cancel a performance, you get all your money back without having to ask.
- If you cannot come, tell us as early as you can. What we can do is set out below.
- Tell us about access needs when you book and we will do our best to meet them.
- Nothing here takes away rights the law gives you.
