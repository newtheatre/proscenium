revised: 2026-11-18

Every price we show is the full price for that ticket, including any fees and any tax. Nothing is added at checkout.

Some tickets are cheaper for particular groups, such as students or members. Book one only if you qualify. We may ask for proof at the door, and if you cannot show it we may ask you to pay the difference.

If we have made an obvious mistake in a price, we will contact you. You can then pay the right price or cancel for a full refund.
