---
title: If we cancel or change a performance
order: 90
revised: 2026-11-18
---

- **If we cancel**, we refund everything you paid within :setting{name="box-office.refunds.pay-within"}. You do not need to ask.
- **If we move it to another date or time**, your tickets are good for the new one. If you cannot or would rather not come, tell us before the new date and we will refund you in full.
- **If we make a major change**, such as moving to a different venue or presenting a substantially different show, we will tell you and offer a refund.
- **If we have to stop a performance before half-way**, we will offer you tickets for another date or a refund.

A change of cast is not usually a major change, unless we sold the performance on the strength of a named performer.

Refunds go back to the card or method you paid with. We cannot refund your other costs, such as travel or a hotel, unless the law says we must.
