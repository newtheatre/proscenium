---
title: If you reserve and do not pay in time
order: 60
revised: 2026-11-18
when: 'setting="box-office.checkout.payment-timing" not="pay-now"'
---

A reservation keeps places for you without payment. In return, we ask you to arrive in good time, because an empty reserved seat is one someone else could have had.

- **Pay for reserved tickets at least :setting{name="box-office.reservations.release-before"} before the performance starts.** A few performances set a different time. Your confirmation email gives the time for your booking.
- **After that time we release reservations that have not been paid for.** That means we may sell those places to people waiting at the door.
- **We keep your booking if your tickets were released.** If you then arrive, come to the box office with your booking reference. If seats are still free, we will seat you, and you pay then. What we can no longer do is promise you a place, because we may have sold it.
- **We never let in more people than the venue safely holds.** If the performance has filled up, we will not be able to admit you, and you will owe nothing.
- **We do not charge you for a reservation you did not use.** But if you know you cannot come, please let your tickets go from the link in your booking email so that someone else can have the seats.
- **Tickets you have already paid for are never released.**

If you often reserve and do not come, we may ask you to pay when you book in future.
