We must provide what you booked with reasonable care and skill, and as we described it. If we fall short, tell us. We will reply within :setting{name="core.complaints.reply-within"} and put it right where we can, which may mean a refund or a price reduction.

We do not limit our responsibility for death or injury caused by our carelessness, for fraud, or for anything else the law does not let us limit. Beyond that, we are responsible only for losses that were a foreseeable result of our breaking these terms.

These terms are governed by the law of :setting{name="core.organisation.law"}. You can bring a claim in the courts of the part of the UK where you live.
