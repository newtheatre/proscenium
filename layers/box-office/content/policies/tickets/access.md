We want everyone to be able to come. Tell us what you need when you book, or contact the box office, and we will do our best. Our [accessibility page](/policies/accessibility) says more.

We keep wheelchair spaces back from general sale until shortly before each performance, so please book early if you need one.

::when{setting="core.access-scheme.enabled" is="true"}
If you need someone with you in order to attend, join our :setting{name="core.access-scheme.name"}. Members of the scheme can book a free ticket for a companion.
::
