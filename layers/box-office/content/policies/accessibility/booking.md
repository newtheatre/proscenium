---
title: Booking when you have access needs
order: 45
revised: 2026-11-18
---

You can tell us what you need when you book online, or contact the box office and we will book for you. We keep wheelchair spaces back from general sale until shortly before each performance.

When you choose tickets we hold them for :setting{name="box-office.baskets.hold-for"}, so that seats are not locked away from everyone else. If you need longer, contact the box office and we will book for you.
