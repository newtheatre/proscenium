---
title: When you book or reserve tickets
order: 60
revised: 2026-11-18
---

We hold your name and email address, what you booked, what you paid, and any tickets you exchanged or cancelled. When your ticket is scanned at the door we record that it was used and when. We need this to supply what you bought, and the law requires us to keep records of sales.

If you book without an account, we hold the same details against your email address. You can claim them into an account later.

If a member of staff books for you at the desk, they enter your details for you. They can search for you only by your email address.
