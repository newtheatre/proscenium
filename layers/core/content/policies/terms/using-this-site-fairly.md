---
title: Using this site fairly
order: 50
revised: 2026-11-18
---

Please do not:

- use robots, scripts or other automated tools to book tickets, hold seats or copy the site in bulk;
- book tickets in order to sell them on at a profit;
- try to get into parts of the site or other people's accounts that you have no right to;
- interfere with the site, test it for weaknesses without our agreement, or send it anything harmful;
- pretend to be someone else, or give false details;
- use the site for anything unlawful.

::when{has="core.organisation.contact-email"}
If you find a security problem, please tell us at :setting{name="core.organisation.contact-email"} before telling anyone else. We will thank you, not chase you.

#otherwise
If you find a security problem, please tell us before telling anyone else. We will thank you, not chase you.
::
