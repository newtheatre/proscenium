You need an account for some things, such as keeping your tickets in one place.

- You must be :setting{name="core.accounts.minimum-age"} or over. If you are younger, ask a parent or guardian to book for you.
- An account is for one person. Give us your real name and an email address you can read.
- You sign in with a passkey, or with a link we email you, which works for :setting{name="core.sign-in.link-lifetime"}. Do not share either. Treat a sign-in link like a key.
- You stay signed in on a device for up to :setting{name="core.sessions.lifetime"}. Sign out when you use a shared computer. You can see and sign out your devices in My account.
- Tell us straight away if you think someone else has been in your account.
- You can delete your account at any time in My account. Our privacy notice explains what happens to your data.

We may suspend or close an account that is being used to break these terms, to commit fraud or to abuse our staff or volunteers. Unless there is an urgent reason, we will tell you first and give you a chance to reply. Closing an account does not cancel tickets you have paid for.
