---
title: Filming and photographs at our events
order: 155
revised: 2026-11-19
---

We sometimes film our own performances for our archive, where the licence for the show allows it. We do not publish these recordings. Members of the audience may appear in them. We tell you with a sign at the entrance and an announcement before the show, and you can ask a member of staff not to be included. We keep archive recordings for as long as the archive is useful to the theatre, and they are not used for anything else. Photographs taken for our website or social media are described on the sign in the same way. See our house rules, "Visiting us", for what you may do yourself.
