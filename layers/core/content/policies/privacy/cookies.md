We use a handful of cookies, all of them to make the site work. We use none for advertising and none to follow you around the web. That is why you were not asked to accept cookies when you arrived.

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep. Websites also keep small notes in your browser's own storage. Both are listed here.

If we ever add something that needs your consent, such as visitor statistics shared with another company, we will ask you first and this section will say so.
