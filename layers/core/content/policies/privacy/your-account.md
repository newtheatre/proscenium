---
title: When you create an account
order: 40
revised: 2026-11-18
---

We hold your name and email address, and how you sign in: a passkey (we hold its public half, which is useless on its own) or a sign-in link sent to your email.

::when{setting="core.sign-in.google" is="true"}
You can also sign in with your Google account. If you do, Google tells us your name and email address and that it is really you.
::

We keep a list of the devices you are signed in on, so that you can see them and sign them out. We need all of this to give you an account at all.

You must be :setting{name="core.accounts.minimum-age"} or over to hold an account. If you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian to book for you.
