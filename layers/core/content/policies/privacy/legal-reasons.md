Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Where we rely on legitimate interests, which we have weighed against your privacy, you can object, and we will stop unless we have a strong reason to continue.

We do not make decisions about you by computer alone that have a legal or similarly serious effect on you.
