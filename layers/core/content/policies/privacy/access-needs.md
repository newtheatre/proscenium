---
title: When you tell us about access needs
order: 50
revised: 2026-11-18
---

You can tell us what you need to enjoy a visit, such as a wheelchair space, level access or a companion seat. This is information about your health, which the law protects more strongly. We use it only with your clear permission (explicit consent), only to arrange your visit, and only the staff who need it can see it. You can change or remove it at any time in My account, and removing it does not affect bookings you have already made.

::when{setting="core.access-scheme.enabled" is="true"}
You can also apply to our :setting{name="core.access-scheme.name"}, which lets you tell us once what you need. If we turn down an application, we delete it after :setting{name="core.access-scheme.keep-rejected"}.
::
