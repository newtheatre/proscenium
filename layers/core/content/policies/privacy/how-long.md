We keep personal data only for as long as we have a reason to. The table above gives the time for each kind of record.

::when{setting="core.retention.armed" is="true"}
We remove accounts that were created but never used after :setting{name="core.retention.unclaimed-after"}, and accounts nobody has signed in to for :setting{name="core.retention.dormant-after"}. We email you before removing a dormant account.
::

When you delete your account we erase what we can and strip your name from records we must keep, such as sales records, so that they no longer identify you.
