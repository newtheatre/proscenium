revised: 2026-11-18

---

You have the right to:

- **see** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
- **correct** anything that is wrong;
- **delete** your data, where we have no legal duty to keep it;
- **limit** how we use it while a complaint is looked into;
- **object** to uses based on our legitimate interests;
- **take it elsewhere** in a format another service can read;
- **withdraw consent** you gave us.

The quickest way to use most of these is **My account, Data**:

- **Download my data** prepares a file of everything we hold about you. The link to it works for :setting{name="core.privacy.export-lifetime"}.
- **Delete my account** starts erasure. For :setting{name="core.privacy.cooling-off"} you can change your mind by signing in. After that it cannot be undone.

::when{has="core.privacy.contact"}
You can also write to :setting{name="core.privacy.contact"}.
::

We will reply within :setting{name="core.privacy.respond-within"}. The law gives us one month at most. It is free. We may ask for proof of who you are before sending anything.

We cannot delete something while we are required to keep it, for example while an incident you were involved in is still open. We will tell you if that applies.
