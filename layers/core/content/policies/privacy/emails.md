---
title: Emails we send you
order: 140
revised: 2026-11-18
---

We send emails you need: confirmations, sign-in links, and reminders about things you have booked or signed up for. These are part of the service, so they are not marketing and you cannot unsubscribe from them, though you can turn most reminders off in My account. We record whether an email was delivered or bounced.

We send news and offers only to people who have asked for them, and every such email says how to stop.
