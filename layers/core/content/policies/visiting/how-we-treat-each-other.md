---
title: How we treat each other
order: 60
revised: 2026-11-18
---

Our staff are often volunteers. We ask you to treat them, the performers and other audience members with respect.

We may refuse to let someone in, or ask them to leave, if they:

- are abusive, threatening or violent, or harass anyone;
- are too affected by drink or drugs to be safe;
- put anyone's safety at risk, or refuse a reasonable instruction from staff;
- keep disturbing the performance after being asked to stop.

We will not refund the ticket of someone asked to leave for these reasons. We never refuse entry for a reason connected with disability, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender reassignment, age or any other protected characteristic.
