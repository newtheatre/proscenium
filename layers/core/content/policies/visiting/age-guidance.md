Each show's page gives its age guidance and any content warnings, such as strong language, flashing lights or loud noises. Please read them before you book, and ask us if you are unsure.

::when{setting="core.visiting.accompanied-under" above="0"}
Children under :setting{name="core.visiting.accompanied-under"} must come with an adult who stays with them.
::

Everyone needs a ticket, whatever their age, unless the show's page says otherwise.
