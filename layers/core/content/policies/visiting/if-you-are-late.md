---
title: If you are late
order: 30
revised: 2026-11-19
---

Whether latecomers can be let in depends on the show, and we decide that show by show. A play with a single long scene, strobe lighting or a very quiet opening is different from a musical with a lively first number. There are four possibilities:

- **Let in at any time.** You can go straight in. Staff will show you to a seat as quietly as they can, which may not be the seat on your ticket.
- **Let in at a suitable point.** Front of house staff choose a moment that will not disturb the audience or the performers, such as a scene change or a blackout. You will wait in the foyer until then, and may be shown to a different seat.
- **Let in at the interval.** You can come in when the interval begins, and not before. This only applies to shows that have one.
- **Not let in.** Once the performance has started nobody can come in. This is usually for the safety of the performers or because the show cannot be interrupted.

Each show's page, the booking pages and your tickets say which of these applies to that show.

::when{setting="core.visiting.latecomers" is="any-time"}
Unless we tell you otherwise, our usual practice is to let latecomers in at any time.
::

::when{setting="core.visiting.latecomers" is="suitable-point"}
Unless we tell you otherwise, our usual practice is to let latecomers in at a suitable point, chosen by front of house staff.
::

::when{setting="core.visiting.latecomers" is="interval"}
Unless we tell you otherwise, our usual practice is to let latecomers in at the interval, and where a show has no interval, not at all.
::

::when{setting="core.visiting.latecomers" is="not-admitted"}
Unless we tell you otherwise, our usual practice is not to let latecomers in.
::

If you think you may be late, ask us before you set off. The same applies if you leave during the performance and want to come back. The front of house team's decision on the night is final, and we do not refund tickets for people who arrive too late to be admitted.
