---
title: If you are late
order: 30
revised: 2026-11-18
---

::when{setting="core.visiting.latecomers" is="suitable-break"}
If you arrive after the performance has started, we will let you in at the first suitable moment. That may not be until the interval, and you may be shown to a different seat so as not to disturb others. For some shows there is no suitable moment.
::

::when{setting="core.visiting.latecomers" is="not-admitted"}
Once a performance has started we cannot let anyone in, for the safety of the performers and so as not to disturb the audience.
::

::when{setting="core.visiting.latecomers" is="staff-decide"}
If you arrive after the performance has started, the front of house manager will decide whether and when you can be let in.
::

The same applies if you leave during the performance and want to come back. We do not refund tickets for people who arrive too late.
