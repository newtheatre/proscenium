If the fire alarm sounds, follow the instructions of our staff and leave by the nearest exit. If you would need help to leave in an emergency, tell us when you arrive so we can plan for it.

If you are hurt or feel unwell, tell a member of staff straight away.

::when{has="core.organisation.contact-email"}
If you lose something, ask a member of staff or contact :setting{name="core.organisation.contact-email"}.

#otherwise
If you lose something, ask a member of staff.
::

We look after belongings while they are with us, but we cannot accept responsibility for things left unattended.
