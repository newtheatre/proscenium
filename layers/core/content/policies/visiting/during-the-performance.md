revised: 2026-11-19

Switch phones and watches to silent, and keep screens dark.

Whether you may take photographs or record can differ from performance to performance, so look for signs and listen to the announcement before the show.

::when{setting="core.visiting.recording-allowed" is="true"}
Our usual practice is to welcome photographs without flash. Please do not record sound or video.

#otherwise
Our usual practice is that you may not take photographs or record sound or video during the performance. Performances are protected by copyright, and recording distracts performers and audience.
::

We sometimes film our own performances, where the licence for the show allows it, for our own archive and not to publish. When we do, there is a sign at the entrance and we announce it before the show starts. If you would rather not be in the recording, tell a member of staff before the performance begins. We may also photograph events for our website and social media, and will say so in the same way.
