---
title: During the performance
order: 50
revised: 2026-11-18
---

Switch phones and watches to silent, and keep screens dark.

::when{setting="core.visiting.recording-allowed" is="true"}
You are welcome to take photographs without flash, unless we announce otherwise for a particular show. Please do not record sound or video.

#otherwise
Do not take photographs or record sound or video during the performance. Performances are protected by copyright, and recording distracts performers and audience.
::

We sometimes photograph or film performances and events ourselves. We will put up a sign when we do. Tell a member of staff if you do not want to appear.
