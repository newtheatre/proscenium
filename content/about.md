The Nottingham New Theatre is a student-run theatre at the University of Nottingham, and a
student-run service of the University of Nottingham Students' Union. Every show we stage is
directed, produced, designed, built, lit, stage managed and sold by students.

## What we do

Our year runs through the In-House and Studio seasons, with Creatives alongside them, and we take
work to the Edinburgh Festival Fringe and, when the membership chooses, the National Student Drama
Festival. We also run StuFF, the Student Fringe Festival. Every member can take part, whatever
their experience.

## Our history

Theatre at the university goes back to the 1920s and Dramsoc. The New Theatre's own building,
converted from the old engineering block, opened in the 1960s; the raked seating and the
proscenium arch followed in the 1970s, and studio spaces were added in 2013.

There is a little more on [our history](/history), and the
[Nottingham New Theatre History Project](https://history.newtheatre.org.uk/) keeps the full
record of our shows and the people who made them.

## Our building

Our building on University Park has been closed since 2023. We currently perform in the Portland
Studio, opposite Mooch on University Park, which has step-free access. The campaign to save the
building is at [savennt.com](https://savennt.com/).

## Getting in touch

For tickets, bookings and anything else, write to
[boxoffice@newtheatre.org.uk](mailto:boxoffice@newtheatre.org.uk) and we will pass your message to
the right person.

- Technical questions and specifications: see [technical information](/technical-specification).
- The theatre's past, and anything to add to the record: write to the history project at
  [history@newtheatre.org.uk](mailto:history@newtheatre.org.uk).
- Members with a concern about the theatre or its activities: the Secretary and Welfare Officer
  is your first point of contact.
