---

Membership is sold by the Students' Union, not by us: we only record it on your account once
you have bought it (A-117).

The current annual fee is {{MEMBERSHIP_FEE_PENCE}}, set by the SU. Nothing in this system takes
payment for it, so that figure is quoted for information only.
