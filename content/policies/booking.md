Every number on this page is read from the system's own settings as you load it, so this page and
the rules the box office applies are the same thing. Where a rule is stated but the system does not
yet apply it, the page says so beside the rule rather than letting you assume it does.

## Booking

One online order holds up to {{PUBLIC_ORDER_SEAT_CAP}} seats. The box office itself is not capped,
so a larger group can be booked in person or by asking us.

## Paying

**Payment is in person only.** There is no online card payment on this site and there never will
be: the Students' Union takes card payments on its own reader, at the desk, so we hold no card
details of yours at any point.

Because of that, a booking is a reservation until you pay for it at the desk. An unpaid
reservation is released {{HOLD_RELEASE_MINUTES_BEFORE}} before curtain, and the seats go back on
sale. You are reminded {{HOLD_REMINDER_MINUTES_BEFORE}} before that happens.

## Changing your mind

Cancelling a booking you have not paid for is free: {{REFUND_UNPAID_CANCELLATION_FREE}}.

Refunding a booking you have already paid for needs a manager: {{REFUND_PAID_REQUIRES_MANAGER}}.
Refunds are handled in person, at the desk, for the same reason payments are.

## Membership

Member pricing follows your membership. A membership that has run out still counts for
{{MEMBERSHIP_GRACE_DAYS}} afterwards, so a renewal you have already arranged is not a refusal at
the desk.
