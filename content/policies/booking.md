Every number on this page is the number the box office applies. When the committee changes a
rule, it changes here at the same moment.

## Booking

One online order holds up to {{PUBLIC_ORDER_SEAT_CAP}} seats. The box office itself is not capped,
so a larger group can be booked in person or by asking us.

## Paying

**Payment is in person only.** There is no online card payment on this site and there never will
be: the Students' Union takes card payments on its own reader, at the desk, and we hold no card
details of yours at any point.

A booking is held unpaid until you pay for it at the desk. An unpaid booking is released
{{HOLD_RELEASE_MINUTES_BEFORE}} before curtain, and the seats go back on sale. You are reminded
{{HOLD_REMINDER_MINUTES_BEFORE}} before that happens. Online booking closes at the same moment,
or earlier if the performance says so; after that, tickets are on the door.

## Changing your mind

Cancelling a booking you have not paid for is free: {{REFUND_UNPAID_CANCELLATION_FREE}}.

Refunding a booking you have already paid for needs a manager: {{REFUND_PAID_REQUIRES_MANAGER}}.
Refunds are handled in person, at the desk, as payments are.

## Membership

Member pricing follows your membership. A membership that has run out still counts for
{{MEMBERSHIP_GRACE_DAYS}} afterwards, so a renewal you have already arranged is not a refusal at
the desk.
