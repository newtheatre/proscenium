Membership is bought at the Students' Union, not here. This screen records what somebody bought
and when it runs out, so that member prices and member-only screens unlock without paperwork.
It is at **Manage, People, Members** and needs the `members.read` permission, which the IT
Manager, Manager, Theatre Manager and Training Manager roles hold. Recording, confirming and
deciding claims need `members.write`, which the IT Manager and Manager hold.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A membership counts from the moment it is recorded.** Checking it against the SU's own list
happens afterwards and never holds up a member price: a volunteer at a desk should not be
arguing with a member about paperwork neither of them controls. What a check may gate, later,
is participation.
::

## How a membership is counted

A membership is a term of one or three years running from the day it was bought, so two people
who joined a month apart lapse a month apart. It is:

- **current** while today is inside the term;
- **in grace** for {{ MEMBERSHIP_GRACE_DAYS }} days after the term ends, when it still counts,
  so a renewal in hand is not a refusal at a desk;
- **lapsed** after that. Lapsing needs no sweep: current is worked out from the dates every time
  it is asked, so a membership that ran out overnight stops counting on its own.

Each membership is reminded {{ MEMBERSHIP_RENEWAL_NOTICE_DAYS }} days before its own end date,
once. The system never charges the fee; the SU's own records remain the truth, and this screen
is the theatre's copy of them.

## Finding a member

![The register with the search field (1), the Filters button (2), Record one (3), Export (4) and the table (5)](/images/docs/people/members.png)

1. **Search** by any part of a name, an email address or a student number. The student number
   is how the committee finds somebody on the SU's list, because a name may not match the SU's
   record and the address is often personal.
2. **Filters** opens the **Show** choice, which picks the view: **Current** (the default,
   including the grace window), **Awaiting record** (the claims queue, with a live count),
   **Awaiting a check** (recorded but not yet confirmed against the SU's list), **Lapsed**, and
   **Everyone ever**.
3. **Record one** opens the recording form.
4. **Export** downloads the current view as CSV.
5. **The table** shows each member with their student number or address, the term's dates (with
   an **In grace** badge where it applies), the source, and whether it has been checked. The
   arrow at the end of a row opens the account.

## Recording a membership

1. Press **Record one**.
2. Choose **who bought it**. A person is chosen from the picker, never typed; it searches by
   name, address or student number.
3. Give the day it was **bought on**. The term runs from this day and it cannot be in the
   future.
4. Choose the **term**, one year or three years.
5. Optionally add the **student number**, which is stored on the account, and the **evidence**,
   the SU's own reference for the purchase.
6. Press **Record it**. The membership counts from now, whether or not anybody has checked it.

## Checking against the SU's list

A recorded membership shows **No** in the Checked column until an officer confirms it. Use the
**Awaiting a check** view, find each person on the SU's list by their student number, and press
**Confirm** on the row. Confirmation records who checked and when; it does not change whether
the membership counts.

## The claims queue

A member who has just bought a membership at the SU can tell the theatre from their own account
page, giving their student number, the purchase date and the term. A claim creates no
membership: it waits here until an officer records it or declines it.

![The claims queue with the Filters button (1), the queue table (2) and the total line (3)](/images/docs/people/members-claims.png)

1. Under **Filters**, set **Show** to **Awaiting record**. The count beside it is how many are
   waiting.
2. Each claim shows the member, the student number they gave (with a **Differs** badge if the
   account already holds a different number), the purchase date, the term and how long they have
   waited. A **Holds one until** badge means the account already has a current membership, which
   usually means somebody already recorded it: decline the claim and say so.
3. Check the person against the SU's list, then press **Record** to write the student number to
   the account, record the membership with the claim as its evidence and close the claim in one
   step, or **Decline** and say why. The member reads what you write, so say what to put right.
   After each decision the focus moves to the next claim, so a long queue can be worked from the
   keyboard.

## Exporting the register

**Export** downloads the view you are looking at (the Show filter and the search both apply) as
`membership-register.csv` with the columns name, email, student number, from, until, source and
confirmed. It is capped at 5,000 rows, and taking a copy is recorded in the audit trail. The
queue view has no export.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This role needs an authenticator app before it can be used"**: your role is privileged and
  you have not enrolled an authenticator. The alert links to where to set one up.
- **"You do not have permission to do that"**: reading the register is wider than writing to
  it. Recording, confirming and deciding claims are the IT Manager's and the Manager's.
- **"That purchase date has not happened yet"**: a purchase is something that has happened.
  Check the date.
- **"Another account already holds that student number"**: a student number belongs to one
  account. The number was typed against the wrong person, or two accounts exist for one person
  and should be merged first.
- **"No such account"** or **"That account has been erased"**: the person chosen has no account,
  or has been anonymised. A membership needs an account to sit on.
- **"That membership is already confirmed"**: somebody else confirmed it while you were looking.
  Reload.
- **"That claim has already been answered"**: another officer recorded or declined it, or the
  member withdrew it, while you were deciding. The queue reloads.

## What happens next

Recording writes a `membership.granted` entry naming the membership and its expiry; confirming
writes `membership.confirmed`; an export writes `membership.exported` with the view and the row
count. Recording a claim also writes `membership.claim.recorded` and, if the student number was
new, `account.student-id.recorded`; declining writes `membership.claim.declined`. The student
number and the reason never reach the trail. The member is told when their claim is recorded or
declined, with the reason in the second case, and their account page shows the membership from
that moment.
