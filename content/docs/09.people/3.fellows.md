A fellowship is a permanent honour the theatre awards to alumni who have made a significant
contribution to it, and a Fellow has free tickets for life. The roll is at **Manage, People,
Fellows**, and the IT Manager, the Manager and the Theatre Manager read it. Recording an award
and revoking one are the IT Manager's and the Manager's.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**The roll is the theatre's own record.** No database held it before this one, so the existing
Fellows were entered here by hand through the same form as a new award. A fellowship is neither
a role (roles expire and carry authority) nor a membership (bought, and lapsing): it is awarded
once, for life, and carries no permission at all.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"This role needs an authenticator app before it can be used"**: your role is privileged and
  you have not enrolled an authenticator. The alert links to where to set one up.
- **"That person already holds a fellowship"**: one per person, for life. If theirs was revoked
  and is being restored, that is a question for the committee; the screen cannot award a second.
- **"That account is no longer here."** or **"That account has been erased"**: the person chosen has no account,
  or has been anonymised. An award needs an account to hold the pass.
- **"That fellowship is already revoked"**: somebody else revoked it while you were looking.
  Reload.

## Reading the roll

![The roll with the search field (1), the Filters button (2), Record an award (3) and the table (4)](/images/docs/people/fellows.png)

1. **Search** by any part of a name, an email address or a citation.
2. **Filters** opens the **Show** choice: **Current Fellows** (the default), **Revoked**, or **Everyone ever**.
3. **Record an award** opens the form.
4. **The table** lists when each fellowship was awarded, the Fellow (badged **Erased** if their
   account has since been anonymised, **Revoked** if the fellowship has), the meeting that
   resolved it, and the citation. The person icon opens their account; the ban icon, shown only
   to a writer and only on a current fellowship, revokes it.

## Recording an award

A person needs an account to hold the entitlement, so add one from
[Accounts](/docs/people/accounts) first if they have none.

1. Press **Record an award**.
2. Choose **who is being honoured** from the picker.
3. Give the **date awarded**.
4. Give who it was **resolved by**: the meeting, not a person, for example "Committee, 12 June
   2019".
5. Write the **citation** in the wording the theatre published. It is public wording and is
   shown exactly as written.
6. Press **Record the award**.

Recording issues the Fellow's lifetime pass in the same step: a pass of the reserved fellowship
type, at no charge and with no expiry, which the door scans like any other pass. A Fellow can
never exist without the pass, or the pass without the award.

## Revoking a fellowship

A revocation adds a second fact; it does not correct the first. The award, its date and the
citation all stand on the roll, the fellowship is badged Revoked, and the pass stops admitting
from now on. Every admission already taken under it stands.

1. Press the ban icon on the Fellow's row.
2. Give the **reason**. It is kept on the record and scrubbed if the person is later erased.
3. Press **Revoke the fellowship**.

## What happens next

The audit trail reads **Fellowship awarded** and **Fellowship revoked**, each naming the
fellowship and never the citation or the reason. The pass is issued or cancelled in the same
step, and neither can happen without the other. If the Fellow's account is later erased, the award stays on
the roll as part of the theatre's record, with the closed account's name in place of theirs.
