Announce is where an officer writes one message to many people without ever seeing, pasting or
disclosing a recipient list. It is at **Manage, Communications, Announce** and needs the
`comms.announce` permission, which at present only the IT Manager holds. The audience is
resolved from live data at the moment you send, so what the preview counted is what is reached,
and each person receives their own copy: no header or body ever names anyone else.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**An announcement is not an emergency channel.** A plain announcement carries the Committee
announcements topic: a member who has switched that topic's email off is not emailed (they
still get the in-app entry), and everyone else receives it inside the next announcements digest
rather than the moment you press Send. Tick **This is a safety notice** only when the message
must reach everyone regardless, and immediately.
::

## Choosing the audience

![The composer with the audience (1), the subject (2), the message (3), the safety notice tick (4) and the preview button (5)](/images/docs/communications/announce.png)

1. Pick an **Audience**. There are four, and each is answered from the database when you
   preview and again when you send:
   - **All current members**: everyone whose membership has started and has not expired by
     more than {{ MEMBERSHIP_GRACE_DAYS }} days, the same grace the desk allows.
   - **Holders of a role**: a second field asks which role. Anyone with an unexpired grant of
     it, so a lapsed committee member is not reached.
   - **Tonight's rota**: everyone holding a claimed or confirmed shift on a performance in
     the current show night, which runs 04:00 to 04:00. An open or declined shift names
     nobody.
   - **A session's sign-ups**: a second field searches training sessions by what they teach
     or their date; choose one from the list. Everyone signed up who has not cancelled.
2. Anonymised accounts are excluded from every audience at the query itself, and an address
   that can never be delivered to (a placeholder, or a reserved domain) is dropped at send
   time and logged. Nothing you choose can reach either.

## Writing and previewing

1. Give the message a **Subject** (up to 150 characters) and a **Message** (up to 10,000). Blank
   lines separate paragraphs in the email; there is no formatting beyond that, and anything
   that looks like HTML is shown as typed.
2. Tick **This is a safety notice** if the message must arrive whatever the recipient's
   preferences say. The screen describes this as reaching the audience "the same as a ticket
   or a refund does".
3. Press **Preview**. It is disabled until the subject, the message and the audience are all
   complete. The preview card shows the recipient count, "resolved just now from live data",
   and the message rendered as a recipient will read it, addressed to you by name.
4. Change anything, the audience included, and the preview and the Send button disappear:
   preview again. A stale count naming yesterday's audience is worse than none.

## Sending

1. Press **Send to N recipients**. The button appears only once a preview stands.
2. The screen confirms **"Sent to N recipients"** and clears the subject and the message,
   ready for the next one.

The count is the size of the resolved audience, not the number of emails delivered. A member
with announcement email off, or an address that could not be sent to, is inside the count and
recorded in the send log with the outcome that explains it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Give it a subject"**, **"Say what the announcement is about"**, **"Say which session
  you mean"**: a field the server needs is empty. The Preview button stays disabled until all
  three are filled, so you will normally only see these if the page and the server disagree.
- **"You do not have permission to do that"**: your session does not hold `comms.announce`.
  Roles lapse on 31 July; ask the IT Manager.
- **The Send button has vanished**: something changed since the preview. Press Preview again.
- **The count is lower than you expected**: the audience is live. A membership past its
  grace, a role grant that has expired, a shift that was declined or a session sign-up that
  was cancelled is not there to be reached, and an anonymised account never is.

## What happens next

Each recipient is sent to individually through the notification centre, and every outcome is
its own line in the send log. What arrives depends on which type you sent:

- A **plain announcement** is type `admin.announcement`, under the Committee announcements
  topic. Each recipient gets an in-app entry at once. The email is held and goes out with the
  next announcements digest, from The New Theatre (`hello@newtheatre.org.uk`), once
  {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_ANNOUNCEMENTS_MINUTES }} minutes have passed since the earliest
  unsent announcement for that person; the sweep that sends digests runs every ten minutes. The
  email's subject is the count ("1 announcement", "3 announcements") and your subject heads
  the entry inside it. A member with announcement email switched off gets no email and a
  `SUPPRESSED_PREFERENCE` line in the log. In the send log the email itself appears as
  `digest.announcements`, not as `admin.announcement`.
- A **safety notice** is type `admin.safety-notice`, transactional: no preference applies, it
  is not held for a digest, and it is emailed at once under your own subject line, from NNT
  Accounts (`accounts@newtheatre.org.uk`), with an in-app entry beside it.

The audit trail records who sent it, the audience definition (the kind, and the role or the
session where there was one), the recipient count and whether it was a safety notice. It does
not record the subject or the body, which are your own words: the send log's subject column is
where "what did this actually say" is answered, one line per recipient.
