Every outbound message carries a type from one registered catalogue; a type that is not in it
cannot be sent at all (0013). This page is that catalogue as an operator reads it: the type
name as the send log shows it, what the subject line says, where it lands, whether a member can
switch it off, and which digest it can be folded into. Use it beside the
[send log](/docs/communications/send-log) to turn a type name into a sentence.

## How to read the columns

- **Says**: the subject line, with the parts that vary in angle brackets.
- **Channel**: **Email**, **Email and in-app**, or both. An in-app entry is written before the
  email is judged, so it exists even when the email was suppressed or skipped; only an
  anonymised account gets neither.
- **Switch off**: the topic a member can turn off on their Notifications screen, or **No** for a
  transactional message, which answers something the person did or something they need to know
  and is not asked about. Transactional messages have no preference row at all, so nothing can
  suppress one.
- **Digest**: a message with a topic normally joins the next digest for that topic instead of
  being emailed on its own: one email per person per topic once the topic's window has passed
  (the sweep runs every ten minutes; the windows are {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_BOOKINGS_MINUTES }},
  {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_SHIFTS_MINUTES }}, {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_TRAINING_MINUTES }},
  {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_ROOMS_MINUTES }} and {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_ANNOUNCEMENTS_MINUTES }}
  minutes for bookings, shifts, training, rooms and announcements). A message sent once by a
  scheduled sweep, or one carrying a file, is emailed on its own instead and says so. A
  transactional message never joins one.
- **From**: the sender identity on the email (0020).

## Identity

Sent from NNT Accounts (`accounts@newtheatre.org.uk`). All transactional. The first five may
reach an address nobody has verified, because each is how an address gets verified or how an
account is first reached.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `account.verify` | Confirm your email address | Email | No | No |
| `account.exists` | You already have an account | Email | No | No |
| `password.reset` | Reset your password | Email | No | No |
| `account.magic-link` | Your sign-in link | Email | No | No |
| `account.set-password` | An account has been made for you at the New Theatre | Email | No | No |
| `account.method-removed` | A sign-in method was removed from your account | Email | No | No |
| `membership.expiring` | Your membership is running out | Email | No | No |
| `membership.claim.recorded` | Your membership is recorded | Email and in-app | No | No |
| `membership.claim.declined` | We could not record your membership | Email and in-app | No | No |
| `role.expiring` | Your <role> role lapses on <date>, or N of your roles lapse soon | Email | No | No |
| `role.expiry.digest` | Role expiry digest, <period> (to administrators, monthly) | Email and in-app | No | No |

## Show night

The shift reminder carries the **Shifts** topic and is sent from The New Theatre
(`hello@newtheatre.org.uk`). Every other shift message is transactional, because it is an
outcome of a claim or a change the holder must know about before the night, and goes from NNT
Accounts (`accounts@newtheatre.org.uk`).

| Type | Says | Channel | Switch off | Digest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `shift.approved` | Your <role> shift on <show> is confirmed | Email | No | No |
| `shift.declined` | Your <role> shift on <show> was declined | Email | No | No |
| `shift.assigned` | You have been assigned a <role> shift on <show> | Email | No | No |
| `shift.removed` | Your <role> shift on <show> has been reassigned | Email | No | No |
| `shift.released` | A <role> shift on <show> has been released | Email | No | No |
| `shift.reminder` | Tomorrow: <role> on <show> (with a calendar file) | Email and in-app | Shifts | No: sent once per shift, the day before |
| `shift.performance-cancelled` | A performance you were working has been cancelled | Email | No | No |
| `shift.venue-changed` | <show> has moved venue | Email | No | No |
| `shift.role-not-needed` | Your <role> shift on <show> has gone | Email | No | No |
| `shift.rota-unstaffed` | N performances unstaffed inside seven days (to rota administrators, daily) | Email | No | No |
| `incident.follow-up-required` | Follow-up required: a <severity> incident (to safety officers) | Email | No | No |
| `board.reset` | The backstage board at <venue> was reset | Email | No | No |
| `night.auto-closed` | Night auto-closed, no signatory: <venue>, <night> | Email | No | No |

Night reports themselves, signed off or auto-closed, are emailed to the configured recipients
outside this catalogue and are recorded with the report rather than in the send log.

## Communications

What the [announce](/docs/communications/announcements) screen sends, and the digests that
carry everything above which was held.

| Type | Says | Channel | Switch off | Digest | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `admin.announcement` | The officer's own subject | Email and in-app | Committee announcements | Announcements | The New Theatre |
| `admin.safety-notice` | The officer's own subject | Email and in-app | No | No | NNT Accounts |
| `digest.bookings` | N booking updates | Email | No | Is the digest | NNT Box Office |
| `digest.shifts` | N shift updates | Email | No | Is the digest | The New Theatre |
| `digest.training` | N training updates | Email | No | Is the digest | NNT Training |
| `digest.rooms` | N room booking updates | Email | No | Is the digest | NNT Room Bookings |
| `digest.announcements` | N announcements | Email | No | Is the digest | The New Theatre |

A digest lists every held message individually, the subject of each in bold and its body
beneath, so five changes in an hour is one email with five entries. No message is registered
under the **Bookings** topic yet, so `digest.bookings` has nothing to carry at present.

## System

drift report and a retention warning are not things a preference may silence.

| `retention.warning.window` | Your account has been quiet for a while | Email | No | No |
| `retention.warning.final` | Your account will be anonymised soon | Email | No | No |
| `retention.digest` | Retention sweep digest (to administrators, monthly) | Email and in-app | No | No |
