A member declares their access requirements from their own account. Somebody has to sight the
evidence and agree, in operational words, what the door should know. That is this screen, at
**Manage, Box office, Access profiles**, and it needs the `access.verify` permission, which
only the Accessibility officer role holds. General box office cannot open it.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-shield" color="warning"}
**Special category data.** What is declared here is encrypted at rest and never leaves this
screen. The door sees the agreed wording only, once a profile is verified and the member has
consented. The desk shows that wording only on a booking that holds an access or companion
ticket. Nobody else sees what was declared, and neither does any report or export.
::

## Reviewing a declaration

![The access profiles queue with the search and filter (1), the table (2) and a Review button (3)](/images/docs/box-office/access-profiles.png)

1. The queue opens on **Pending** declarations. The **Show** filter offers every status
   (Pending, Verified, Expired, Declined, Withdrawn) or **Everyone**; **Search** takes a name or
   an email address. Nothing sensitive is on the list: the booker, the status and how many
   companions they asked for.
2. Press **Review** on a row. The dialogue reads the declaration: the **Access Card number**
   if one was given, the **needs** ticked from the standard list, the number of
   **companions**, and anything they wrote **in their own words**.
3. Sight the evidence. The card number is shown so you can check it and is cleared the moment
   you decide, either way.
4. Type the **agreed wording for the door**: operational only, for example "aisle seat,
   assistance dog". Never a need, a diagnosis or their own words.
5. Press **Verify**, or **Decline** if the evidence did not check out or could not be checked.
   Declining asks for the word first: press **Decline the declaration** in the dialogue, or
   **Back**. The Access Card number is cleared and the door is given no wording for them; they
   may declare again.

A declaration can be decided while it is pending or after a verification has expired. A verified
profile stays current for {{ ACCESS_PROFILE_VALIDITY_MONTHS }} months; after that the door stops
seeing it and the member re-declares.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Agreed wording is what the door reads out"**: the wording is required to verify. Decline
  needs none.
- **"This declaration is already verified"** (or declined, or withdrawn): the member withdrew
  it, or another officer decided it first. The queue has caught up.
- **"That access profile is no longer here."**: the account was closed or anonymised while the dialogue was
  open.

## What happens next

Verifying stores the agreed wording inside the encrypted profile, stamps who verified it and
when it expires, and unlocks the access and companion ticket types for that member's own
bookings, which is how the door and the desk come to show the wording. Declining records the
decision and clears the card number. Both are in the audit trail as a decision on the account,
with no detail of what was declared.
