A show warns from this list, never in its own words, so two shows warning about the same thing
say it the same way and a theatregoer can weigh it. The vocabulary is managed at **Manage, Box
office, Content warnings**; reading it needs `ticketing.read` and changing it
`ticketing.write`, both held by the Box office role. A show chooses from it on its own Content
warnings tab.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
Two kinds. A **staging** warning is a fact about the room, strobe, haze, a blackout: either the
show does it or it does not, so it is not graded. A **content** warning is a theme, which each
show that carries it grades **mentioned**, **discussed** or **depicted**.
::

## Adding a warning

![The content warnings list with the search and filter (1), the Add a warning button (2) and the table (3)](/images/docs/box-office/content-warnings.png)

1. **Search** by title, or filter to archived warnings, from the toolbar.
2. Press **Add a warning**.
3. Give it a **Title**: what the show page prints. Strong language, Death, Strobe lighting. The
   **Address** is written from the title until you change it.
4. Choose the **Kind**, Staging or Content. A content warning can be **grouped under** a
   heading the show's editor offers it under: pick one of the usual headings or type a new one.
5. Optionally say **what it means**, pick an **Icon** for the badge, and set the **Order**:
   lower comes first within its kind.
6. Press **Save**.

**Edit** on a row opens the same form, with an **Archived** switch. A warning's kind cannot
change once a show carries it, because grading follows from it.

## Archiving or deleting a warning

- **Archived** (on the edit form) stops the warning being offered to a show. A show already
  carrying it keeps it, marked archived in its editor.
- **Delete** appears only on a row that no show carries. It cannot be undone.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The vocabulary already holds Strobe lighting"**: titles are held once.
- **"A slug is lowercase words joined by hyphens"**: fix the address.
- **"Strobe lighting is carried by 3 shows, so its kind cannot change"**: leave the kind, or
  add a new warning of the other kind.
- **"Strobe lighting is carried by 3 shows, so it can only be archived"**: archive it instead.

## What happens next

A new warning is offered on every show's Content warnings tab at once, under its heading. A show
that carries it prints its title, its icon and, for a content warning, its grade on the public
show page beside the show's own notes. Every change to the vocabulary is in the audit trail.
