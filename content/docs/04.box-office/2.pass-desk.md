A pass is bought once and admitted against many performances. It is issued here, at
**Manage, Box office, Pass desk**, to a named account; the buyer then shows its QR at the
door. The Box Office Manager issues them.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
A pass is paid for on the Students' Union's SumUp reader like a ticket. The screen only records
that the money was taken, under the same cross-check the desk uses.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"This pass is not on sale yet."** or **"This pass is no longer on sale."**: the pass type
  is a draft, closed, or outside its sales window. Its window and status are set on
  [Pass types](/docs/box-office/pass-types).
- **"The screen said £40.00; the desk now reads £45.00. Nothing has been charged: check the
  price and try again."**: the price point changed while the screen was open. Reload, read the
  new figure into the reader and try again.
- **"This pass is capped at 50 issues, and that many are already out."**: the cap was reached,
  possibly by another desk a moment ago. Nothing is issued and nothing is charged.
- **"That request is no longer pending"**: the request was already fulfilled or has lapsed.
  Issue the pass without it; the buyer field is still filled.
- **"That price point is no longer here."**: the price points were edited while the screen was
  open. Reload the page.

## Issuing a pass

![Issuing a pass with the pass picker (1), the price point (2), the buyer (3), the amount due (4) and the Issue button (5)](/images/docs/box-office/desk-passes.png)

1. Choose the **Pass**. Only pass types on sale right now are offered: published, inside their
   sales window, and not closed. If none is, the screen says **Nothing is on sale.** A capped
   pass shows how many are left of its cap, for example **12 left of 50**.
2. Choose the **Price** point. A pass type can carry several, each with its own label, and the
   one chosen is what **Due now** shows.
3. Find the **Buyer** by name or email. A buyer is chosen from the picker, never typed: a pass
   belongs to an account, so the door can read it back.
4. Key **Due now** into the reader and take the payment.
5. Press **Issue**. The screen confirms **Pass issued** and clears the buyer, ready for the
   next.

## Fulfilling an online request

A member can ask for a pass online without paying; nothing is reserved by the request. When
they come to the desk:

1. Choose the pass they asked for. Its **Pending requests** are listed by name under the price.
2. Press **Choose** beside their name. The buyer field is filled from the request.
3. Pick the price point they are paying, take the payment on the reader and press **Issue**.

The request is marked fulfilled in the same write as the pass, so it cannot be fulfilled twice.
A request lapses on its own once the pass type's sales window closes.

## What happens next

The pass is active at once, with its own reference, and its holder is emailed the confirmation
with the QR the door scans. One ledger entry is written, source DESK, tender CARD, for the price
point paid; the issue, and the request it fulfilled, are in the audit trail. The pass admits its
holder to a performance of any show it covers by seating them under the house's own
pass-admission ticket type, which counts against capacity like any other seat.
