A show category is the heading a show is filed under: a main house production, a studio piece,
a festival entry, a hire. It is what the shows list filters by and what a show's Details tab
offers. Categories are managed at **Manage, Box office, Show categories**; reading the list
needs `ticketing.read` and changing it `ticketing.write`, both held by the Box office role.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A category is retired, never destroyed.** A category a show belongs to can only be retired:
it stops being offered for a new show and still names every show that already carries it. A
category nothing has ever used can be deleted outright.
::

## Adding a category

![The show categories list with the search and filter (1), the Add a category button (2) and the table (3)](/images/docs/box-office/show-categories.png)

1. **Search** by name, or filter to archived categories, from the toolbar.
2. Press **Add a category**.
3. Give it a **Name**, held once, and an **Order**: lower numbers show first.
4. Press **Add a show category**.

**Edit** on a row opens the same form. The table shows whether a show belongs to each category.

## Retiring or deleting a category

- **Retire** stops the category being offered on a show's Details tab. A show already in it
  keeps it, and publishing that show is then refused until it moves to a current category.
  **Bring back** reverses it.
- **Delete** appears only on a row marked **Nothing yet**. It cannot be undone.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A category is already called Drama"**: names are held once.
- **"Drama can only be retired: a show belongs to it"**: retire it instead.
- **"Drama is already retired"**, **"Drama is not retired"**, **"Drama changed while you were
  editing it"**: somebody else changed it first. Reload.

## What happens next

A new category is offered on every show's Details tab at once. Every add, change, retirement
and deletion is in the audit trail.
