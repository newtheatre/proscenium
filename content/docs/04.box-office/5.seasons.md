A season is one of the theatre's seasons: Autumn or Spring of an academic year, or StuFF or the
Edinburgh Fringe, which sit outside both. It is never the whole committee year, which the money
screens call a year. A show belongs to the season it plays in; the shows list filters by it and
its season line counts it, and the Treasurer's [money dashboard](/docs/money) reports on it.
Seasons are managed at **Manage, Box office, Seasons**, by the Box Office Manager, who reads the
list and changes it.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert" color="warning"}
**Date seasons so they do not overlap.** The money dashboard counts everything taken between a
season's first and last day, bar takings included, so a day inside two seasons is counted in
both. Nothing refuses an overlap: end StuFF before the Fringe begins, and Spring before StuFF.
::

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A season is retired, never destroyed.** A season a show belongs to can only be retired: it
stops being offered for a new show and still names every show that already carries it. A season
nothing has ever used can be deleted outright.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"A season needs a name"**, **"A season needs a start day"**, **"A season needs an end
  day"**: all three are required.
- **"A season ends after it starts"**: the end day is on or before the start day.
- **"A season is already called Autumn 2026"**: names are held once.
- **"Autumn 2026 can only be retired: a show belongs to it"**: retire it instead.
- **"Autumn 2026 is already retired"**, **"Autumn 2026 is not retired"**, **"Autumn 2026
  changed while you were editing it"**: somebody else changed it first. Reload.

## Adding a season

![The seasons list with the search and filter (1), the Add a season button (2) and the table (3)](/images/docs/box-office/seasons.png)

1. **Search** by name, or filter to archived seasons, from the toolbar.
2. Press **Add a season**.
3. Give it a **Name**, held once, such as "Autumn 2026", "Spring 2027", "StuFF 2027" or
   "Fringe 2027", and the days it **Starts** and **Ends**, both included, London. These days are
   the ones the money dashboard reports on, so give the season's real first and last day.
4. Set the **Order**: lower numbers show first in pickers and lists.
5. Press **Add a season**.

**Edit** on a row opens the same form. The table shows each season's run and whether a show
belongs to it.

## Retiring or deleting a season

- **Retire** stops the season being offered on a show's Details tab. A show already in it keeps
  it, and publishing that show is then refused until it moves to a current season. **Bring
  back** reverses it.
- **Delete** appears only on a row with a cross in the In use column (**Nothing yet**). It cannot
  be undone.

## What happens next

A new season is offered on every show's Details tab at once. Every add, change, retirement and
deletion is in the audit trail.
