A season is the committee year a show belongs to. It is what the shows list filters by and what
its season line counts. Seasons are managed at
**Manage, Box office, Seasons**, by the Box Office Manager, who reads the list and changes it.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A season is retired, never destroyed.** A season a show belongs to can only be retired: it
stops being offered for a new show and still names every show that already carries it. A season
nothing has ever used can be deleted outright.
::

## Adding a season

![The seasons list with the search and filter (1), the Add a season button (2) and the table (3)](/images/docs/box-office/seasons.png)

1. **Search** by name, or filter to archived seasons, from the toolbar.
2. Press **Add a season**.
3. Give it a **Name**, held once, and the days it **Starts** and **Ends**. The committee year
   runs from {{ SEASON_START }} to {{ SEASON_END }} (month and day, London), the boundary the
   configuration holds for reporting and for role expiry; a season normally matches it.
4. Set the **Order**: lower numbers show first in pickers and lists.
5. Press **Add a season**.

**Edit** on a row opens the same form. The table shows each season's run and whether a show
belongs to it.

## Retiring or deleting a season

- **Retire** stops the season being offered on a show's Details tab. A show already in it keeps
  it, and publishing that show is then refused until it moves to a current season. **Bring
  back** reverses it.
- **Delete** appears only on a row marked **Nothing yet**. It cannot be undone.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A season needs a name"**, **"A season needs a start day"**, **"A season needs an end
  day"**: all three are required.
- **"A season ends after it starts"**: the end day is on or before the start day.
- **"A season is already called 2026/27"**: names are held once.
- **"2026/27 can only be retired: a show belongs to it"**: retire it instead.
- **"2026/27 is already retired"**, **"2026/27 is not retired"**, **"2026/27 changed while you
  were editing it"**: somebody else changed it first. Reload.

## What happens next

A new season is offered on every show's Details tab at once. Every add, change, retirement and
deletion is in the audit trail.
