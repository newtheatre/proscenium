The desk is where a booking made online is paid for, in person, before the show. It is at
**Manage, Box office, Desk**. Opening it and searching needs the `ticketing.read` permission;
collecting, requesting a comp, refunding and cancelling need `ticketing.write`. The Box office
role holds both. The screen's **Tonight** card names the duty managers confirmed for the
performance on screen, because they are who decides a comp request.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**The theatre takes no cash and no online payment.** Every collection is CARD on the Students'
Union's physical SumUp reader, or COMP. The screen records what the reader took; it never
charges anything itself.
::

## Finding a booking

The desk opens on tonight, with the previous and the next night an arrow either side.

![The desk with the night (1), the performance picker (2), the summary tiles (3), the camera button (4), the search field (5) and the results (6)](/images/docs/box-office/desk.png)

1. **The night** is shown at the top with arrows to the previous and the next night, for
   somebody who has come on the wrong day, and a **Today** button to come back. A night runs
   04:00 to 04:00, so a performance after midnight is still on the night it belongs to. When a
   night has nothing on it the screen says **Nothing on this night.**
2. **The performance** picker lists the night's performances as show and venue. Everything else
   on the screen is scoped to the one chosen: the search, the tiles and the Tonight card.
3. **The summary tiles** count the house on screen: **Capacity** (or Uncapped for a general
   admission house with no cap), **Reserved**, **Collected**, **Door** (walk-ups admitted with
   no booking) and **Walk-up headroom**, which is capacity less reserved and door. Above the
   search, an amber notice counts the unpaid bookings and what they owe, and names the time they
   release for walk-ups.
4. **Scan with the camera** opens the phone's or the laptop's camera, the same scanner the door
   uses, and closes it again once a code is read. Where there is no camera, the browser refuses
   it, or it will not start, the screen says so and the field beside it still takes a hardware
   scanner's keystrokes or a typed reference: press **Open**.
5. **Search** takes a booking reference or part of the booker's name, then :kbd{value="Enter"}
   or **Search**. A reference is matched exactly; a name matches anywhere in it. Under the
   search, the **All**, **Pending**, **Collected** and **Door** pills narrow the list by
   status: pending is reserved and unpaid, collected is reserved and paid, door is a walk-up
   with no booking.
6. **The results** list every matching booking for the performance on screen: reference,
   booker, status and what it currently costs. Press **Open** on one.

The **Tonight** card on the right counts the **access bookings** (with a link to the door
screen), the **pass admissions** seated on this performance, the time **reservations release**,
and who is **on shift**: the confirmed duty managers, or **Nobody confirmed yet**.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lightbulb" color="info"}
A scanned pass is refused here with **"That is a pass; scan it at the door."** Passes are
admitted, never collected.
::

## Taking payment

Collection is the payment boundary. Before it a booking can still be changed by the booker; after
it, a change is a refund, never both at once.

1. Open the booking. The dialogue is headed by its reference, the show and the booker, and lists
   each ticket with its price. A booking holding an access or companion ticket shows the agreed
   door wording above the list.
2. Read **Due now** off the screen, key that figure into the SumUp reader and take the payment.
3. Leave the tender on **Card** and press **Collect**.

The desk always sends the total it believes the booking costs. If that disagrees with what the
server computes, collection is refused quoting both figures, because a human is about to type
one of them into the reader. Nothing is charged by the refusal; reopen the booking and try again.

Unpaid online bookings are released to walk-ups shortly before curtain: {{ HOLD_RELEASE_MINUTES_BEFORE }}
minutes before unless the performance states its own, and the Tonight card names the exact time.
One online booking holds at most {{ PUBLIC_ORDER_SEAT_CAP }} seats.

## Comps

Being on the desk is not comp authority. Anybody with the desk can ask for a comp; only tonight's
duty manager or a ticketing manager can approve it, never the person who asked, and only then
does the COMP tender go through.

1. Open the booking and choose the **Comp** tender. **Due now** drops to £0.00.
2. Type the **reason for the comp** and press **Ask tonight's duty manager**. The screen
   confirms **Comp requested** and shows **Waiting on tonight's duty manager to approve this**
   until it is decided.
3. Once approved, the booking shows **Approved by** and the approver's name. Press **Collect**.
   The button stays disabled until then.

The approval happens on the duty manager's phone, not here: a pending request appears in the
**Comp requests** block of [Tonight at a glance](/docs/show-night/at-a-glance), where **Approve**
and **Decline** sit beside the reason you typed. A ticketing manager decides it from that same
block. Tell them there is one waiting; nothing here nudges them.

A request lapses {{ COMP_REQUEST_EXPIRY_MINUTES }} minutes after it was made; the screen then says
**That request lapsed; ask again** and offers the reason field afresh. A declined request shows
**Declined** and the decliner's reason. Each approval pays for exactly one collection.

## Refunds

Money goes back the way it came in: in person, on the reader, one ticket at a time.

1. Open a collected booking. Each unrefunded ticket has a **Refund** button beside its price.
2. Refund that figure on the reader, then press **Refund**. A dialogue asks for the word and
   names the amount: press **Refund £12.50**, or **Back** to leave the ticket alone.
3. The ticket leaves the list the moment the refund is recorded.

Anything the desk refuses is said inside that dialogue, so the figures stay in front of you.

Where the configuration requires a manager for a refund of paid money (`REFUND_PAID_REQUIRES_MANAGER`
is {{ REFUND_PAID_REQUIRES_MANAGER }}), the person pressing **Refund** must hold `money.refund`,
which the Manager role carries, or be the confirmed duty manager for this performance tonight.
A refund is its own ledger line: the original collection is never edited.

## Cancelling a collected booking

Once every ticket on a collected booking has been refunded, the dialogue says **Every ticket on
this booking has been refunded** and offers **Cancel booking**. Press it, then **Cancel the
booking** in the dialogue, and the booking is cancelled by staff. It cannot be brought back; a
booker who changes their mind again makes a new booking. An unpaid booking is not cancelled from here: the booker cancels it themselves from
their confirmation, or it lapses at the release time.

## Bookings that cannot be collected

A booking that is neither pending nor collected opens saying why in its own words: it was
cancelled, its hold lapsed, it was admitted at the door as a walk-up, or it was recorded as a
no-show. A walk-up admission and a no-show are the end of it; there is nothing to do with either
at the desk.

### Reinstating a lapsed or cancelled booking

A booker who turns up after their hold released is helped, not turned away, when the house
allows it. A lapsed hold and a booking the booker cancelled themselves both open with a
**Reason for reinstating** field and a **Reinstate booking** button.

1. Open the booking, by reference or from the results.
2. Type why it is being brought back. The reason is recorded against the booking with your name
   and the time.
3. Press **Reinstate booking**. The booking goes back to pending with a fresh hold expiry, keeps
   its reference and its original QR code, and the dialogue turns into the collection form, so
   payment can be taken there and then.

The seats are re-checked as the reinstatement is written, never as the screen last read them, so
a house that filled in the meantime refuses it and nothing is changed.

A booking cancelled by staff is never reinstated here. Staff cancellation only ever follows a
refund, so bringing it back would bring back a sale that was already handed back: take a new
booking instead. Such a booking opens with no reinstate field at all.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The screen said £12.00; the desk now reads £10.00. Nothing has been charged: check the
  booking and try again."**: the price changed between the screen loading and you pressing
  Collect. Close the dialogue, open the booking again and read the new figure into the reader.
- **"This booking has already been collected."** or **"This booking is no longer available to
  collect: it may already have been collected."**: somebody else took it first. Search again
  and the status badge says so.
- **"This booking was already admitted at the door."**: the person walked up and paid at the
  till, so there is nothing to take.
- **"This hold has lapsed. Box office can reinstate it if the seats are still free."** or
  **"This booking was cancelled and cannot be collected. Box office can reinstate it if the
  seats are still free."**: use the reinstate field on the same dialogue. The till shows these
  same words, and reinstating is a desk job, so send the booker to the box office.
- **"This booking was refunded and cancelled at the desk: reinstating it would bring back a sale
  that was already handed back. Take a new booking instead."**: the cancellation followed a
  refund, so this is a new sale.
- **"This performance no longer has room for this booking"**: the freed seats were resold
  between the booking lapsing and the reinstatement. Nothing has changed; offer what is left.
- **"That request has not been approved yet"**, **"That request was declined"**, **"That
  request has lapsed; ask again"**, **"That comp has already been given"**: the comp tender
  needs an approved, unspent, unexpired request. Ask again or take card.
- **"You cannot decide your own request"**: a duty manager who is also on the desk cannot
  approve their own comp; a second person decides it.
- **"This ticket has already been refunded, or is not on this booking"**: reopen the booking;
  the list only ever shows what is still unrefunded.
- **"The screen said £10.00; the ticket reads £12.00. Nothing has been refunded: check the
  ticket and try again."**: the same cross-check as collection, for a refund.
- **"£24.00 is still unrefunded on this booking. Refund every ticket first, then cancel."**:
  cancelling is refused while any money is stranded.
- **"That code is not one of ours."**, **"That code is not recognised"**, **"No such
  booking"**: the scan was not a booking's code, or the reference was mistyped. Try the name
  instead.
- **"No camera on this device"**, **"Camera access refused"**, **"The camera would not
  start"**: scan into the field or type the reference; allow the camera in the site settings
  to use it next time.

## What happens next

A collection writes one ledger entry, source DESK, with a line per ticket at the price it sold
for (or nought for a comp, with the reason and the approver on the entry), and marks the booking
collected, so the door reads PAID the moment it goes through. The booker's QR code stays valid.
A refund writes a negative line against the same ticket and frees its seat, which is offered to
the waiting list at once. A reinstatement writes no money at all: it returns the booking to
pending with a fresh hold, and its own record of who reinstated it, why and when survives
alongside the original expiry, which the no-show statistics still count. Every collection, comp
request, comp decision, refund, reinstatement and staff cancellation is in the audit trail.
