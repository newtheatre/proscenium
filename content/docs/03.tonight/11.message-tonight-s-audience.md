**Message tonight's audience** is the duty manager's way to tell a house something on the night:
the doors are late, the car park is shut, the second half is cancelled. It opens from the tile of
that name on [the hub](/docs/tonight), to whoever holds tonight's confirmed duty manager shift
inside its hours, and to the Front of House Manager without one. A Front of House Manager who
previews or sends is recorded on the night report as standing in; opening the screen and reading
the count is not. It writes to one of tonight's performances and nobody else: its ticket
holders, or its rota. Anything for another night, or for the membership, is
[Announce](/docs/communications/announce).

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**It goes at once, to everyone.** A message from this screen ignores preferences: a ticket holder
who has switched bookings email off still gets it, since tonight's news cannot wait for a digest.
Write only what the house needs to know tonight.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"This needs a confirmed duty manager shift on one of tonight's performances …"**: you hold no
  duty manager shift tonight that is running now. The screen shows it as one card, with who to
  ask.
- **"Give it a subject"** or **"Say what the message is about"**: a field is empty; **Preview**
  stays greyed out until both are filled.
- **"Nobody is in this audience"**: nobody holds a live booking for this performance, or nobody
  is on its rota yet.
- **The connection dropped while sending**: press **Send to N people** again. Nobody who already
  has it gets it twice; the screen then says who else it reached, or **Everyone in this audience
  already has it**. Changing the message first makes it a new one, which everybody gets.

## Writing and sending

![The composer with who it goes to and the count (1), the subject (2), the message (3) and Preview (4)](/images/docs/show-night/message.png)

1. **Who it goes to**: **Ticket holders** (everyone with a live booking for this performance,
   guests included) or **Tonight's rota** (everyone with a claimed or confirmed shift on it). The
   count under it is worked out as you choose. On a matinee day the hub hands over the house you
   were looking at, and a switcher above lets you choose the other.
2. **Subject**: what the email is headed.
3. **Message**: in a sentence or two. Blank lines separate paragraphs.
4. **Preview** shows the message as it will arrive. Change anything and the preview goes: preview
   again. Then press **Send to N people**.

The screen then says **Sent to N people** and keeps what went out on screen; **Write another**
clears it.

## What happens next

Each person gets their own copy, by email and in their inbox, and every outcome is a line in the
send log. A message to ticket holders comes from NNT Box Office; a message to the rota comes from
The New Theatre. The audit trail records who sent it, the performance, which audience, how many
and whether a shift or an officer sent it, never the words themselves.
