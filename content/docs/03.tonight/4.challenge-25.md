The **Challenge 25 register** is tonight's record of every ID check, accepted or refused. It opens
from the **Challenge 25** button on [the door](/docs/tonight/door) to any of tonight's
three shifts (door, bar, duty manager), and to the Front of house manager or the Bar manager
without one. [Till](/docs/tonight/till) writes to the same register on its own: it
asks as an age-restricted drink goes into the basket, and the outcome lands here either with the
sale or, for a refusal, the moment it is confirmed. A check made at the point of sale never needs
logging twice.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-shield-alert" color="warning"}
**Describe who you checked, never by name.** "Tall man, grey coat" is the record; a name is
not. The register is handed to the licensing authority and kept as a licensing record. The timestamp and the person checking are added automatically.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Nothing tonight authorises the register."** with the refusal beneath it: none of tonight's
  three shifts is yours and you hold neither officer role. The refusal names who does.
- **"That entry already has a correction: correct the correction instead"**: two people
  corrected the same entry. Find the newer one on the list.
- **"Nothing logged yet tonight."**: an empty register, not a fault.

## Reading the register

![The register with the Last synced line (1), tonight's entries (2) and the Log a check button (3)](/images/docs/show-night/age-checks.png)

1. **Last synced** is when the list was fetched.
2. **Tonight's entries**, newest first: the outcome (**ID accepted** or **Refused**), the time,
   the description, the ID type or the reason, and who checked. An entry that corrects an earlier
   one says **corrects an earlier entry**; the one it replaced stays on the list marked
   **corrected later**. Nothing is ever edited or removed.
3. **Log a check** opens the form, and sits at the foot of the screen.

## Logging a check

![The log form with the outcome (1), the ID shown (2), the description (3) and Log the check (4)](/images/docs/show-night/age-checks-log.png)

The usual check is two presses and a sentence: **ID accepted**, the ID you were shown, and who you
checked.

1. **Outcome** is two buttons. **ID accepted** asks for the ID shown; **Refused** asks for the
   reason instead. Whichever you pick, the other is not asked for and is not recorded.
2. **ID shown**: passport, photocard driving licence, PASS card or other, one button each. On a
   refusal this becomes **Why refused**: no ID shown, ID looked false, appeared underage even
   with ID, or other.
3. **Who you checked**: appearance, never a name.
4. **Log the check**. It stays unavailable until the outcome, its answer and the description are
   all there.

**The house, the product, a note** folds the rest away. Open it to change the performance (it
defaults to the house running now, and **No performance (checked outside a show)** covers a check
made when nothing is on), to name the product, or to add a note.

## Correcting an entry

Press **Correct this entry** under the entry. The form opens filled with what was recorded, with
the same two outcome buttons; change what was wrong and press **File the correction**. This files
a new entry. An entry already corrected has no button: correct the correction instead.

## What happens next

Every entry is on tonight's register the moment it is logged, and the night report counts
accepted and refused checks separately. The register is exported for the licensing authority from
the console; see [the age-check register](/docs/rota/age-check-register).
