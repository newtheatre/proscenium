The **Night report** is the duty manager's last screen of the evening. It opens from the **Night
report** tile on [the hub](/docs/tonight), to whoever holds tonight's duty manager shift and to the
Front of house manager without one. The report fills itself in from the evening's data; your part
is to read it, write a closing note and sign it off once [the checklist](/docs/tonight/checklist)
is closed.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This performance's checklist has not been closed yet"**: the post-show checklist is still
  open. Press **Open the checklist**, answer what is left, press **Close the night** there, then
  come back and sign off.
- **"This performance has already been signed off"**: somebody signed it first. The screen reloads
  and shows the signed report with their name and closing note.
- **"Say how the night went"**: the closing note was empty.
- **"Nothing is running tonight, so there is no report to compile"**: no performance at this venue
  tonight.
- **"The connection dropped, so it may or may not have gone through."**: reload. A signed report
  shows its banner; an unsigned one shows the closing note field again.

## Reading the report

The report reads as a draft until it is signed, and each visit shows the evening as it stands
now. It carries:

1. **Attendance**: sold, in, no-shows and walk-ups. A no-show is a paid booking nobody
   admitted; nothing marks it, so the figure falls as the door admits people and is final once
   the house is in.
2. **Takings**: the box office and the bar, with comps and discounts given shown as money
   forgone, and the number of bar items sold.
3. **Incidents**: each one logged tonight with its time, category and severity, marked when it
   needs a follow-up.
4. **Challenge 25**: how many checks were accepted and how many refused.
5. **Staffing**: each slot and who held it, or **Unfilled**. A line above the slots reading **An
   officer opened the duty manager's screens without the shift** means just that, for the night
   as a whole: it names no slot, so it says nothing about who held the duty manager shift.
6. **Backstage**: the milestones called on the backstage board, with their times.
7. **Checklist exceptions**: every item closed with a reason, and the reason.
8. **Access**: a count of verified access bookings, and nothing more about them.

On a matinee day the hub hands over the house you were looking at. Opened without one, and with
more than one performance running, the screen asks **More than one performance is running
tonight. Choose the one you are signing off.**, with a button per house. Each performance has its
own report. If you are an officer and the screen cannot offer the houses, it shows why instead;
open the report from the hub, which hands over the house it is showing.

## Signing off

1. Write a **Closing note**: how the night went, in a sentence or two.
2. Press **Sign off**.

The report freezes with your name, the closing note and the time, and is emailed to the standing
recipients and to you. The screen then shows **Signed off by …** above the report, and the sign-off
action goes away. A second sign-off for the same performance is impossible.

If you are the Front of house manager with no duty manager shift tonight, the screen says so above
the note before you sign: **"You hold no duty manager shift tonight. Your sign-off is recorded as
standing in for the duty manager."** The signed report then reads **Signed off by …, standing in
with no duty manager shift.**

## What happens next

A signed report never changes. A correction found later is an addendum, emailed the same way, and
shows under **Addenda** at the foot of the report. A performance nobody signs off within 24 hours
of its show night ending closes on its own, reading **Closed automatically, with nobody signing.**
