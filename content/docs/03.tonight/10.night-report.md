The **Night report** is the duty manager's last screen of the evening. It opens from the **Night
report** tile on [the hub](/docs/tonight), to whoever holds tonight's duty manager shift and to the
Front of House Manager without one. The report fills itself in from the evening's data. After the
curtain your part is to answer what is left of [the checklist](/docs/tonight/checklist), read the
report, write a closing note and press **Sign off and close**, which ends the night in one go.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The checklist cannot close while … is still open: tick or make an exception"**: a required
  item is unanswered. It is under **Still to do** at the top of the report: tick it, or press
  **Can't do this?** and say why, then sign off and close again. **Sign off and close** stays
  greyed out while one is open.
- **"… incident logged since you opened the report: read it again and sign off"**: somebody
  logged an incident while you were reading. The report reloads with it; read it and press
  **Sign off and close** again. Nothing was closed or signed.
- **"This performance has already been signed off"**: somebody signed it first. The screen reloads
  and shows the signed report with their name and closing note.
- **"Sign off and close opens at 22:10, once the curtain is down"**: the sign-off was sent before
  the curtain came down, from a screen left open or one whose clock is wrong. Nothing was signed;
  wait until that time and sign off again.
- **"Say how the night went"**: the closing note was empty.
- **"Nothing is running tonight, so there is no report to compile"**: no performance at this venue
  tonight.
- **"The connection dropped, so it may or may not have gone through."**: reload. A signed report
  shows its banner; an unsigned one shows the closing note field again.

## Reading the report

The report reads as a draft until it is signed, and each visit shows the evening as it stands
now. It carries:

1. **Attendance**: sold, in, no-shows and walk-ups, each counted in seats, so a party of four is
   four on every line. A no-show is a paid seat nobody admitted or refunded, or a pass holder's
   seat booked ahead and never used; nothing marks it, so the figure falls as the door admits
   people and is final once the house is in. At close, sold is in, plus no-shows, plus any unpaid
   seats still held.
2. **Takings**: the box office and the bar, with comps and discounts given shown as money
   forgone, and the number of bar items sold, all for this performance alone. **Drinks on card,
   whole night** is the one figure that is not this performance's own: the card reader serves every
   performance that night, so it is the night's drinks-on-card total, the same figure the till close
   lists as Drinks on card. A tab settlement belongs to no performance, so it is not in either
   performance's bar takings; the till close counts it.
3. **Incidents**: each one logged tonight with its time, category and severity, marked when it
   needs a follow-up. **Sign off and close** marks each one reviewed, so none needs marking on
   [Contacts and incidents](/docs/tonight/contacts-and-incidents) first.
4. **Challenge 25**: how many checks were accepted and how many refused.
5. **Staffing**: each slot and who held it, or **Unfilled**. A claim no officer confirmed reads
   as the claimant's name followed by **claimed, not confirmed**. Above the slots, one line for each
   role an officer acted in without the shift, such as **Door: Alex Morgan stood in by officer
   role, with no confirmed door shift**. The line is for the night as a whole and names no slot;
   **beside a confirmed door shift** means the rota had somebody confirmed on that role anyway,
   which is worth asking about. A quieter line such as **Door: Rowan Ellis covered it from the
   duty manager's shift** says tonight's duty manager worked the door; that is a shift, not an
   officer standing in. Each role gets its own line (duty manager, door or bar), so an officer
   who opened the till and admitted at the door appears twice. The lines come from what the
   officer did, not from the rota, so they appear even when **No shifts were stamped for this
   performance.** A bar opened on a night with no performance at that venue has no night report;
   an officer standing in there is listed on the **Performances** tab of
   [Night reports](/docs/reports) instead.
6. **Backstage**: the milestones called on the backstage board, with their times.
7. **Checklist exceptions**: every item closed with a reason, and the reason.
8. **Access**: a count of verified access bookings, and nothing more about them.

On a matinee day the hub hands over the house you were looking at. Opened without one, and with
more than one performance running, the screen asks **More than one performance is running
tonight. Choose the one you are signing off.**, with a button per house. Each performance has its
own report. If you are an officer and the screen cannot offer the houses, it shows why instead;
open the report from the hub, which hands over the house it is showing.

## Signing off and closing

Before the curtain comes down the report is a draft to read, and nothing that ends the night is on
the screen: it says **Sign off and close opens at 22:10, once the curtain is down**. The curtain is
the performance's running time and intervals after curtain up, or curtain up itself where no
running time is recorded. After it:

1. **Still to do**, at the top, lists every post-show item still open, and any required pre-show
   one. Press **Tick**, or **Can't do this?** to answer one with a reason, exactly as on the
   checklist.
2. Write a **Closing note**: how the night went, in a sentence or two.
3. Press **Sign off and close** at the foot of the screen.

In one go the checklist closes, every incident the report listed is marked reviewed, and the report
freezes with your name, the closing note and the time. It is emailed to you and to everybody who
holds one of the roles the committee chose for it on Settings, such as the Front of House Manager.
It goes to whoever holds the role on the night: once a role changes hands at the committee year end,
the new holder receives the report and the old one does not. The screen then shows
**Signed off by …** above the report, **Sign off and close** goes away, and the checklist reads
**Tonight is closed, by …**. A second sign-off for the same performance is impossible.

If you are the Front of House Manager with no duty manager shift tonight, the screen says so above
the note before you sign: **"You hold no duty manager shift tonight. Your sign-off is recorded as
standing in for the duty manager."** The signed report then reads **Signed off by …, standing in
with no duty manager shift.**

## What happens next

A signed report never changes. A correction found later is an addendum, emailed the same way, and
shows under **Addenda** at the foot of the report. A performance nobody signs off within 24 hours
of its show night ending closes on its own, reading **Closed automatically, with nobody signing.**

A card sale the Treasurer records after the bar's till closed (a charge nobody answered on the
night) is counted in the bar's takings and listed under **Late additions**, with who recorded it
and when. A late sale counts toward the night's card figure, so it shows on every report that
night. Once a report is signed, one recorded later is added to it as an addendum
instead, saying the amount and when the reader took it; that addendum is not emailed.

A report signed or closed before 27 September 2026 counted in, no-shows and walk-ups by booking,
so a party of four read as one in; its figures stay as they were signed, so compare it with a
later report with that in mind.
