An evening ends in a fixed order, each step waiting on the one before it: the bar answers
every card charge still waiting, closes the till against the reader, the duty manager works the
post-show checklist and closes the night, and the night report freezes. The bar's steps need
tonight's bar shift or the Bar Manager; the duty manager's need tonight's duty manager shift or
the Front of House Manager.

## If something goes wrong

- **"… card charges are still waiting for an answer. Answer them on the till first."**:
  answer them, below. A charge at any bar tonight counts, since every bar shares the one reader.
- **"The reader read £…; it should show £… (bar £…, box office £…). That difference needs a
  note before it can be recorded."**: the variance field was left blank. The figure is the
  whole night's, the desk's takings included, since the desk and the bar share the one reader.
- **"That session is already closed"**: somebody closed it before you opened this screen.
- **"Somebody else closed this session first, so this reading was not recorded. Read the till
  before recording anything."**: two people confirmed at once and the other one landed. Their Z
  figure and their note are the ones on the record, not yours.
- **"A card charge started while this close was in flight, so nothing was recorded. Answer it on
  the till and close again."**: a charge started in the moment between the check above and the
  close itself. Answer it, then close.
- **"The checklist cannot close while … is still open: tick it or record an exception"**: a required checklist
  item is unanswered.

## Answering card charges

[Till](/docs/tonight/till) lists every card charge that nobody has answered for under
**Unanswered card charges**, with the figure, the time, who started it and its state: a
hand-off to the SumUp app, or a figure keyed into the reader by hand. The list is every bar's
tonight, since every bar shares the one reader, and no till closes while any of them is waiting.
Answer one from your own till, whichever bar started it; what went through is recorded against
the bar that started it.

1. For each one, find out whether the reader took the money.
2. For a figure keyed by hand, press **Reader took it** or **Card declined**. For a hand-off,
   press **Payment went through** (with the SumUp code if you have it) or **Payment did not**.
3. One marked **Taken on the reader, not recorded** is a mismatch: the reader has money the
   ledger could not record. Fix the cause and answer again, or refund on the reader and abandon
   it with a note saying what happened to the money. A mismatch does not block the close; it is
   a reconciliation fact.

## Closing the till

1. On the till, open the overflow menu (top right) and press **Close till**. It is not a
   per-sale action, so it is not one of the pinned buttons. A show night has one SumUp reader
   and one login, shared by the desk and the bar, so the dialogue leads with what that reader
   should show for the whole night: **The reader should show £46.90**, with where it was taken
   under it (**bar £18.90, box office £28.00**). Under that, **This bar tonight** lists this
   session's own figures: drinks on card, tickets taken at the bar, tab settlements, comps
   (count and value forgone), discounts given, refunds and tab charges (credit extended, no
   money moved). A figure at nought is left out.
2. Read the Z figure off the reader and key it into the field, to the penny.
3. If it differs from what the reader should show, the dialogue says by how much, over or
   under, and asks why. Write the note.
4. Press **Confirm close**, or **Back** to leave the session open. The expected figure, the
   actual figure, the variance and the note are kept with the session and never edited after.

A session forgotten past 04:00 does not appear on the till the next day, and no screen lists
it yet. Tell the Bar manager, whose role is the one thing that can still close it.

## The post-show checklist

Open [the checklist](/docs/tonight/checklist) from **Close the night** on
[Tonight at a glance](/docs/tonight/tonight-at-a-glance). Tick each post-show item, or close it over
with a reason, and press **Close the night**. The screen confirms **Tonight is closed, by …**.

## The night report

The report compiles itself from the evening's data; nobody types it. It carries attendance
against sold (admitted, no-shows, walk-ups), takings by tender split between desk and bar, the
incidents and near misses with their severities, the Challenge 25 counts, the milestone
timeline from the backstage board, the staffing (each shift's holder, unfilled slots, and any
officer who opened a screen with no shift, flagged), the bar summary from the same computation
the till close used, the checklist with every exception's reason, and a count of verified access
bookings and nothing more about them.

Once the checklist is closed, open [the night report](/docs/tonight/night-report) from the
**Night report** tile on the hub, write a closing note and press **Sign off**. The report freezes
with your name and is emailed to the standing recipients and to you. Any performance still unsigned
24 hours after its show night ends closes on its own: the report freezes marked as closed
automatically, with no human signatory, listing any checklist items left unmet, and is emailed the
same way. A second report for the same performance is impossible.

## What happens next

The till close, the checklist close and the report are each in the audit trail. The frozen
report is immutable; a later correction is an addendum, distributed the same way. The
reconciliation figures feed [the finance reports](/docs/money/daily-reconciliation).
