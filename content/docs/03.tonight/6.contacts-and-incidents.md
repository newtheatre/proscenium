**Contacts and incidents** opens from the hub tile of that name to any of tonight's three shifts
(door, bar, duty manager), and to the Front of house manager or the Bar manager without one. The
top half is who is on tonight; the bottom half is the incident log.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-shield-alert" color="warning"}
**People by role, never by name.** An incident entry is read by the committee and lands in the
night report in full. "The person on the door" and "a member of the audience" are the record;
names are not.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Nobody is on the rota tonight yet."**: the rota for tonight has no confirmed shift.
- **"That entry already has a correction: correct the correction instead"**: find the newer
  entry on the list and correct that one.
- **Log something is greyed out**: nothing tonight authorises you, and the warning above the log
  says which shift or role would.
- **Log it is greyed out**: the sheet still needs a kind, a category or a sentence.
- **"Nothing logged yet tonight."**: an empty log, not a fault.

## Ringing somebody

![The screen with the On tonight roster (1), the log (2), and Log something at the foot (3)](/images/docs/show-night/incidents.png)

1. **On tonight** lists the duty manager, door and bar shifts for tonight's performances. A call
   button appears only where that member has chosen, on their own profile, to share their number
   with the shift; pressing it opens the phone's dialler. An unfilled slot stays on the list as
   **unfilled** so it reads as a gap in the rota, not as somebody to ring. A claim no officer has
   confirmed yet reads the same quiet way, as the claimant's name and **claimed, not confirmed**.
2. **The log** is tonight's entries, newest first, each with its time, who logged it, its
   category and its severity. An entry the duty manager has acknowledged reads **reviewed**.
3. At the foot of the screen, under the thumb: **Log something**, for anything worth writing
   down, from a near miss to something serious.

The first time you open the screen on a phone, and while the log is empty, a line under the
header says every entry is timed, named and printed in the night report; after that the screen
starts with the roster.

## Logging something

**Log something** opens a sheet from the foot of the screen, one choice at a time, each a tile:

![The Log something sheet with the kind (1), what it is about (2), the sentence (3) and Log it (4)](/images/docs/show-night/incidents-log.png)

1. **What kind**: **Near miss** is chosen already, since it is the one most often worth a line.
   Tap **Note**, **Incident** or **Serious** instead where it was more than that.
2. **About**: medical, behaviour, safety, security, property or other.
3. **Performance**, on a matinee day only; a single performance is chosen for you.
4. Write **What nearly happened** (or **What happened**) and press **Log it**, or **Back** to
   leave it. The time is now and the reporter is you.

A near miss is three taps and a sentence: open, a category, the sentence, **Log it**. It has no
severity to triage and nothing else waits on it.

## Marking an entry reviewed

**Mark reviewed** under an entry acknowledges it before the night closes. It shows to
tonight's duty manager and to the Front of house manager without a shift, and only while the
entry is not already reviewed. It is an acknowledgement, not a resolution: a follow-up the
committee owes is tracked on [Safety](/docs/rota/safety), not here.

## Correcting an entry

Press **Correct this entry** under the entry. The sheet opens on the entry's kind, category and
account; change what was wrong and press **File the correction**. The original stays on the list marked **corrected later**, and the new entry says
**corrects an earlier entry**. Nothing is edited in place.

## What happens next

An entry at a severity the committee has marked for follow-up notifies every holder of the
Safety Officer's permission at once. Every entry lands in the night report, corrected ones
included. **Sign off and close** on [the night report](/docs/tonight/night-report) marks every
entry the report listed as reviewed as it closes the night, so marking each one here first is
optional; an entry already marked gains no second review. The button shows to the duty manager
alone, and the entry then reads **reviewed**.
