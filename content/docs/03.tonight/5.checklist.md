The **Checklist** is the duty manager's: the venue's pre-show items before the house opens, its
post-show items after, and the **Close the night** action that needs every required item
answered. It opens from the **Checklist** tile on [the hub](/docs/tonight), and from **Close
the night** at the foot of [Tonight at a glance](/docs/tonight/tonight-at-a-glance), to whoever holds
tonight's duty manager shift and to the Front of house manager without one. The items themselves
are configured per venue on [the console](/docs/rota/checklists); this screen only works through
them.

On a matinee day the hub and the glance both hand over the house you were looking at, so the
screen opens on it. Opened without one, and with more than one performance running, the screen
asks which: **More than one performance is running tonight. Choose the one you are closing.**,
with a button per house naming its show and curtain time. Each performance keeps its own
checklist and its own close.

## Working through the list

![The checklist (1) and the Close the night action (2)](/images/docs/show-night/checklist.png)

1. **The checklist**, in two phases, **Pre-show** and **Post-show**. Each item is one of:
   - **Hand-ticked**: press **Tick**. The list shows who ticked it.
   - **Ticks itself**: it reads the live data and shows **clear** or **not yet clear**. Nobody
     can tick it by hand. Two exist: **No-show holds released** clears once no unpaid hold is
     left on this performance, and **Tonight's incidents reviewed** clears once every incident
     logged against this performance carries a review. An item that ticks itself cannot be made
     an exception either, so on a night with an incident logged, mark each entry reviewed on
     [Contacts and incidents](/docs/tonight/contacts-and-incidents) before closing.
   - **(optional)**: a tick is welcome but the night closes without it.
2. **Close the night** is greyed out while any required item is neither ticked nor an exception,
   with the count beside the list (**2 required items still open. Tick or make an exception.**),
   and greyed out again once the night is closed.

The list is taken from the venue's own items the first time it is read tonight, so a change made
during the evening does not move the goalposts under you.

## Recording an exception

An item that cannot be done tonight is an **exception**, not a blank: press **Make an
exception**, say why, and press **Make the exception**. The list then shows **Exception:** with
the reason, the reason prints in the night report, and the item counts as answered for closing.

## Closing the night

Press **Close the night**, which comes back to life once every required item is ticked or an
exception. The screen then says **Tonight is closed, by …** and the action greys out. A close is once
per performance: a second press, or another duty manager's, reads back the first.
The whole order of an evening's end is on [Closing the night](/docs/tonight/closing-the-night).

## If something goes wrong

- **"The checklist cannot close while … is still open: tick or make an exception"**: the items
  named are required and unanswered. Tick them, or make each one an exception.
- **"This performance is already closed"**: somebody closed it first. Reload and the banner says
  who.
- **"That item cannot be ticked: it may already be ticked, an exception, or one that ticks
  itself"**: reload; the list has moved on since it was read.
- **"Say why this item is an exception"**: the reason field was empty.
- **"Nothing is running tonight, so there is no checklist"**: no performance at this venue.

## What happens next

Every tick, exception and close is in the audit trail with who and when. From house open, the
hub shows **Pre-show checklist incomplete** naming any required pre-show item still open. The
close is the gate the night report's sign-off stands behind.
