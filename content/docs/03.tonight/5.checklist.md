The **Checklist** is the duty manager's: the venue's pre-show items before the house opens and its
post-show items after. The night itself closes with **Sign off and close** on
[the night report](/docs/tonight/night-report), which needs every required item answered and
carries the post-show items still open, so nothing that ends the night is on this screen. It opens
from the **Checklist** tile on [the hub](/docs/tonight), to whoever holds tonight's duty manager
shift and to the Front of house manager without one. The items themselves are configured per
venue on [the console](/docs/rota/checklists); this screen only works through them.

On a matinee day the hub and the glance both hand over the house you were looking at, so the
screen opens on it. Opened without one, and with more than one performance running, the screen
asks which: **More than one performance is running tonight. Choose the one you are closing.**,
with a button per house naming its show and curtain time. Each performance keeps its own
checklist and its own close.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The checklist cannot close while … is still open: tick or make an exception"**, on the night
  report: the items named are required and unanswered. Tick them, or press **Can't do this?** on
  each and say why.
- **"That item cannot be ticked: it may already be ticked, an exception, or one that ticks
  itself"**: reload; the list has moved on since it was read.
- **"Say why this item is an exception"**: the reason field was empty.
- **"That item cannot be made an exception: it may already be ticked or an exception"**:
  reload; somebody answered it first.
- **"Nothing is running tonight, so there is no checklist"**: no performance at this venue.

## Working through the list

![The checklist (1) and the way on to the night report (2)](/images/docs/show-night/checklist.png)

1. **The checklist**, in two phases, **Pre-show** and **Post-show**. Each item is one of:
   - **Hand-ticked**: press **Tick**. The list shows who ticked it.
   - **Ticks itself**: it reads the live data and shows **clear** or **not yet clear**. Nobody
     can tick it by hand. Two exist: **Unpaid holds released** clears once no unpaid hold is
     left on this performance, and **Tonight's incidents reviewed** clears once every incident
     logged against this performance carries a review. A paid booking nobody used does not hold
     the first one open: it is counted as a no-show in the night report instead. The second never
     holds the close and reads **Clears at Sign off and close** until then: **Sign off and close**
     marks every incident on the report reviewed as it closes, so there is nothing to mark
     beforehand. If the first cannot clear tonight, for example an unpaid hold nobody released, it
     offers **Can't do this?** like any other item.
   - **The till is closed**, at the foot of **Post-show**: it ticks itself once this venue's till
     is closed tonight and no earlier night left a till open, or a card charge unanswered, at any
     bar. Until then it says what is open and who closes it. Only the bar can close a till, so
     this line never holds the close and takes no exception.
   - **(optional)**: a tick is welcome but the night closes without it.
2. **Night report**, under the list, with the count of required items still open
   (**2 required items still open. The night closes with Sign off and close on the night
   report.**). Once the night is closed the list says **Tonight is closed, by …** instead.

The list is taken from the venue's own items the first time it is read tonight, so a change made
during the evening does not move the goalposts under you.

## Recording an exception

An item that cannot be done tonight, or one that ticks itself but cannot clear, is an
**exception**, not a blank: press **Can't do this?** under it, say why, and press **Make the
exception**. The list then shows **Exception:** with the reason, the reason prints in the night
report, and the item counts as answered for closing.

## Closing the night

The night closes on [the night report](/docs/tonight/night-report), after the curtain, with one
**Sign off and close**: it closes this checklist, marks tonight's incidents reviewed and freezes the
report together. This screen then says **Tonight is closed, by …**. A close is once per
performance. The whole order of an evening's end is on
[Closing the night](/docs/tonight/closing-the-night).

## What happens next

Every tick, exception and close is in the audit trail with who and when. From house open, the
hub shows **Pre-show checklist incomplete** naming any required pre-show item still open.
