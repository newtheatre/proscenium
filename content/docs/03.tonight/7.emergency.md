The **Emergency** card is the venue's own: the address, what3words, exits and assembly point,
first aid, isolation points and any notes, written by the Front of House Manager or the Theatre
Manager on [the console](/docs/rota/emergency-cards) and read here under pressure by somebody who has never
read it before. Anyone signed in can open it, shift or none: it opens from the hub's red
**Emergency** tile, or at `/tonight/emergency`. It shows one card for every venue running
tonight, each headed by the venue's name, with the venues you are working tonight first.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-wifi-off" color="info"}
**It works with no signal.** The cards are saved on the phone the moment any show-night screen
opens tonight, not only when this one is visited, so they open instantly afterwards, even from the
hub with poor signal, and survive a dropped connection. The line at the foot of each card says when the copy you are reading was
filed. On a shared phone, the duty manager's number shows only to whoever was signed in when it
was saved, and signing out takes the saved cards off the phone.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"No venue is running tonight, so there is no card to show."**: nothing is on the programme
  tonight. If you are in a building anyway, ring 999 and give the address you are at.
- **"No emergency card is saved on this phone yet, and none could be read just now."**: the
  first visit on this phone had no connection. Any show-night screen opened with signal, once,
  fixes it for the rest of the night.
- **"No address has been filed for …"**: that venue has no card, or a card without an address.
  Ask the duty manager, and tell the Front of House Manager afterwards.
- **"No duty manager has shared a number tonight."**: either the shift is unfilled or only
  claimed (a claim is not on the call list until an officer confirms it), or the person holding
  it has not chosen to share their number with tonight's team. Find them in the foyer, and make
  the card's first call (999, or estates security where the card names them) either way.
- **No number under After 999 at all**: you are not on tonight's team at that venue, so the
  duty manager's number is not shown to you. Ring 999 first, then find the duty manager. At a
  campus venue the heading names estates security instead of 999, and the same applies.
- **"No trained first aider is on tonight's rota"**: nobody on tonight's confirmed team at that
  venue holds a current first aid record. The line under it, where there is one, is the
  committee's own advice for that venue.

## Reading the card

![The card with the address to read to 999 (1), who to ring after it (2), evacuation (3), first aid (4) and when it was filed (5)](/images/docs/show-night/emergency.png)

1. **Read to 999** is the address in large type, with the what3words underneath where one is
   filed. If no address has been filed the card says so and tells you to ask the duty manager.
   At a campus venue whose card names estates security, the heading is **Read to Estates
   Security** instead: they are who you call first there.
2. **After 999** says the order to ring in: 999 (or whoever the card names first) first, then
   the duty manager. If you are on
   tonight's team at that venue, tonight's duty manager is listed with their number and a
   **Call** button where they have chosen to share it; where nobody has, the card says to find
   them in the foyer.
3. **Evacuation**: the exits, and the assembly point.
4. **First aid**: where the kit is, where the defibrillator is, and who is first-aid trained
   tonight. Once the committee has named the first aid course (the **Training that makes a first
   aider** setting), that is tonight's own confirmed team holding a current record of it, by
   first name with the job each is doing, such as "Sam (door)". Until then it is the line the
   card records.
5. **Filed** on the date and time the card was last written. Below it come **Isolation
   points** (with the fire panel) and **Notes** where the card carries them.

**Call 999** sits at the foot of the screen, under the thumb. Where tonight's card names someone
to call first, such as **Call Estates Security** at a campus venue, that comes first and
**Call 999** stays beside it, so the emergency services are always one step away. On a night
with more than one venue, the calls follow the cards, your own venue's first, and a call that is
only some venues' names them, such as **Call Estates Security (Studio)**.

Neither dials on the first tap, so a phone knocked in a pocket or tapped by mistake in the dark
rings nobody. The tap opens a sheet naming who you are about to call and their number; press the
red button carrying the number to ring from the phone you are holding, or **Back** to close it.
The duty manager's **Call** button on the card is not an emergency call and rings straight away.
The rest of the screen is for reading aloud.

## What happens next

Nothing is written from this screen. Each edit on the console is a new version of the card; the
phone shows the newest it has managed to fetch, and says when that was. The duty manager's number
and tonight's first aiders come from tonight's own rota, so they change with the shifts, with
each person's contact choice and with their training records, never from a standing list.
