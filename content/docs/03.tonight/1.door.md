The door is where a booking becomes a person in a seat. It opens from the hub's **Door** tile to
whoever holds tonight's door shift, and to the Front of house manager without one. Tickets and
passes come through the same screen. It is door mode and nothing else: no prices, no email
addresses, no booking history. Admit, or send them to the bar.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**The door never sells.** An unpaid booking and a walk-up both go to
[the till](/docs/tonight/till), which takes the money and marks the booking paid; the
customer walks back to a green scan. Changes to a booking stay on
[the desk](/docs/box-office/desk).
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"WRONG PERFORMANCE: This ticket is for … Ask the Front of House Manager."**: a live booking
  for a different performance. Check the picker at the top; if the ticket really is for another
  night, the Front of House Manager decides.
- **"WRONG PERFORMANCE: Admitted for … Ask the Front of House Manager."**: this booking came in
  at a different performance, such as the matinee. It is not tonight's re-entry; the Front of
  House Manager decides.
- **"ALREADY IN: Already admitted at …"**: this booking has been through already, at the time
  shown.
- **"LAPSED: The hold was released. Send to the bar for a ticket."**: an unpaid booking that
  reached its release time. The bar can sell them a walk-up if there is room.
- **"CANCELLED"** or **"EXCHANGED: Moved to …"**: the booking no longer stands, or was swapped
  for another performance; the line names which, and what to do.
- **"NOT FOUND"**, in amber: nothing tonight matches what was scanned or typed. Nothing has been
  refused. Check the spelling, ask for the reference again (the alphabet has no `0`, `1`, `I` or
  `O`), or try the name.
- **"This pass was already admitted tonight, at …"**, **"This pass has expired."**, **"This
  pass does not cover this show."**, **"This pass is not active."**: send them to the bar.
- **"This performance no longer has room for that admission"**: the house is full, so a pass not
  yet redeemed for tonight cannot take a seat. Send them to the bar.
- **"No camera. Type the reference or a name."**, **"Camera not allowed. Type the reference or a
  name."** and **"Camera did not start. Type the reference or a name."**: the field is ready on
  its own. Allow the camera in the browser's site settings to scan next time.
- **"NO ANSWER: The connection dropped, so nothing was checked. Try again."**: the phone could not
  reach the theatre at all, so this is not a verdict on the ticket. Wait a moment and scan it
  again; if the foyer signal is gone, the bar can check the reference on the laptop.

## Scanning a ticket

![The door with the performance picker (1), the camera (2), the one field (3) and the back arrow (4)](/images/docs/show-night/door.png)

1. **Performance** shows only when the venue runs more than one tonight. It starts on the house
   whose doors are open now. Check it on a matinee day: a ticket for the other performance is
   refused as the wrong one.
2. **The camera** opens by default and stays open all evening. Hold the code inside the gold
   frame; a torch button appears on phones that have one. Where there is no camera, or the
   browser refuses it, one line says which and the field below is the whole door.
3. **QR, reference or name** is the one field. Type the six-character reference, or part of the
   booker's name, and press **Check** or :kbd{value="Enter"}. A hardware scanner plugged in as a
   keyboard types the whole URL out of a QR, and that is accepted too. The field empties after
   each check, so two people in a row are two checks and no presses in between.
4. **The back arrow** returns to the hub.

The verdict is big enough to read at arm's length in the dark. With the camera open it sits over
the live view rather than replacing it, so the next person is scanned without touching anything:

- **PAID: Paid, admit.** The booking is marked admitted, with the booker's first name and the
  party size underneath.
- **UNPAID: Send to the bar with this ticket.** The booking is real but nobody has taken its
  money. The bar collects it and the door then reads PAID.
- **PASS: Pass, admit.** A pass holder is in, for their own seat.
- **REFUSED**, or the reason itself as the heading, in red, with what to do next beneath.
- **NOT FOUND**, in amber: nothing matched, and nothing was refused.

The phone buzzes as well as showing the card: once for PAID or PASS, twice for UNPAID, one long
buzz for a refusal, two longer ones for NOT FOUND, and nothing where the browser cannot buzz at
all.

Over the camera, the card clears itself after about three and a half seconds, or sooner when the
next code is read, or on a press. A refusal, a **NOT FOUND** and a **NO ANSWER** card hold for
eight seconds, which is long enough to read the reason out. With no camera the card stays until
the next check. The camera stops whenever the screen is left or hidden, so a phone in a pocket is
not filming.

## Finding someone by name

A name in the field lists tonight's bookings and passes under it, below the camera, which keeps
running.

![The field after a name (1), what it found (2) and the Admit button (3)](/images/docs/show-night/door-search.png)

1. **The field** takes any part of the booker's or holder's name, and empties once it is checked.
   Two characters is the least it takes.
2. **What it found**: each booking for the performance chosen, as a first name, the party size
   and **Paid**, **Unpaid** or **In**; and each pass under that name, as its card.
3. **Admit** on a paid booking admits it and shows the verdict. An unpaid one says **Send to the
   bar with this ticket** instead, and one already in says when.

A pass is never admitted blind: its card shows the holder, whether the pass is **Active**, what
it covers (a fellowship covers every in-house show), tonight's state (**Not yet redeemed**,
**Redeemed, not yet in**, **Already admitted tonight**) and when it was last used. Where the pass
would be refused, the card says so instead of offering the button. Otherwise press **Admit one**:
a pass admits its holder and nobody else, and creates tonight's £0 pass-admission ticket for the
seat. The camera reading a pass QR lists that pass's card the same way. The hint under the
heading carries the standing rule: a refused pass goes to the bar, never to an argument at the
door.

## What happens next

An admission sets the booking's status to admitted, which is what the hub's **In** figure and the
glance's **to come** count. A pass admission writes a £0 ticket against tonight's performance in
the same moment. Nothing about the person leaves this screen: the first name and party size are
the whole of what door mode may show. [Challenge 25](/docs/tonight/challenge-25) is its own tile
on the hub.
