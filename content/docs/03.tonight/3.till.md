The till is the bar's screen and the one money-taking point on a show night. It opens from the
hub's **Till** tile to whoever holds tonight's bar shift, to whoever holds a confirmed slot on
tonight's bar opening, and to the Bar Manager without either. A door shift does not open it, and
neither does the Front of House Manager's role. Everyone at the venue sells against one session for
the whole night, and the catalogue is kept on the phone so the grid stays up when the venue's
Wi-Fi drops.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**The theatre takes no cash and no online payment.** Every sale is on the SU's physical SumUp
reader, on an authorised tab, or a comp. The till records what the reader took; it never
charges anything itself. The screen sends the total it believes the basket costs with every
sale, and a mismatch with what the server computes refuses, quoting both figures.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"The screen said £…; the till now reads £…. Nothing has been charged: check the basket and
  try again."**: a price moved between pricing and charging. The total on screen has already
  caught up; press charge again.
- **"No till session is open here: open one before ringing anything up"**: the session was
  closed under you. Open it again.
- **"That size is not on the till right now"**: its price ended or the product was retired.
  Remove the line.
- **"… needs a Challenge 25 outcome before this can be charged"**: a restricted line with no
  outcome, usually a prompt closed rather than answered. The prompt opens again on the next
  press.
- **"The connection dropped, so it may or may not have gone through. Write this refusal up on
  the age checks screen."**: the refusal did not reach the register. The lines stay out of the
  basket; log the check on [Challenge 25](/docs/tonight/challenge-25).
- **"The connection dropped, so it may or may not have gone through. Look under Unanswered card
  charges before charging again."**: the charge left the till but nothing came back. If it
  reached the server it is listed under **Unanswered card charges**; answer it there rather than
  starting a second one.
- **"That charge has already been answered, so it cannot be declined now. Read the till
  again."**: somebody answered it on another device first, or it had already been recorded.
- **"That booking is in a charge still waiting for its answer. Answer that one first."**: the
  booking is in a charge nobody has answered yet, on this till or another. Answer that one.
- **"That member is not authorised to charge to a tab"**: the holder is not named on the list
  and holds no role on it, or their role has lapsed. Take it on the reader.
- **"…'s tab is at £…; this charge of £… …would take it past the … cap. Nothing has been charged:
  a duty manager or Bar Manager can override."**: over the cap, and either the override is
  switched off or you are not one of those roles. Ask a duty manager or the Bar Manager to press
  charge; the till overrides it for them, on the spot.
- **"…'s tab reached the cap while this was being charged. Nothing has been charged: …"**: another
  till charged the same tab in the same moment and their charge is the one that fits. Read the tab
  and charge again.
- **"…: This booking is …"** or **"One of those bookings was collected elsewhere just now.
  Nothing has been charged: look it up again."**: the desk got there first. Remove the line.
- **"… no longer has room for that order. Nothing has been charged."**: the house filled while
  the walk-up sat in the basket.
- **"Not enough left in stock for this sale: nothing has been charged."**: the stock register
  has run out of an ingredient. Tell the Bar Manager. On **Charge £… on SumUp** this is checked
  before the SumUp app opens, so the reader is never asked for money the till could not record.
- **"That is a pass; the door admits it"**: a pass QR at the bar. Passes are admitted at the
  door and never collected.
- **"Nothing matching "…" on tonight's performances here."**: the name search covers tonight's
  performances at this venue only; a reference finds a booking wherever it is.
- **"Both a name and an email, or neither."**: a walk-up with half an identity.
- **"The SumUp hand-off is not switched on here. Key the figure into the reader."**: the typed
  path is the only one on this installation.
- **"Taken on the reader, not recorded"**: the reader has money the ledger could not record,
  usually a booking collected at the desk meanwhile. Put the cause right and answer again
  (**Record it again**, or **Payment went through** for a hand-off), or refund on the reader and
  give it up with a note saying what happened to the money.
- **"Showing what was last loaded; the till could not refresh just now."**: the catalogue on the
  phone is being used. Selling continues; prices are still checked by the server on every charge.
- **"Nothing is on the till yet. Price a size in the catalogue, and it appears here."**: the
  catalogue has no priced size; see [Products](/docs/bar/products).
- **"That request has not been approved yet"**, **"That request was declined"**, **"That request
  has lapsed; ask again"** and **"That comp has already been given"**: the request moved on
  while the modal sat open. Ask again if it lapsed; otherwise nothing more to do here.
- **"The screen said £… was being given away; the till now reads £…. Nothing has been given:
  check the basket and try again."**: the same price-moved check a charge makes, on a comp.

## Opening the till

1. Open the till from the hub. If nobody has opened tonight's session the screen says **The
   till is not open yet.**
2. If the night does not say which bar on its own, the screen asks **Which bar are you opening
   tonight?** and lists the venues you may open one at: a venue with a performance tonight, a
   venue whose bar opening you are working, and, for the Bar manager, every venue in the
   building. Press one and the screen reloads on it. This is what a hire night or a society social
   looks like, and it is also what a night running two houses looks like. The phone remembers the
   bar you chose until 04:00, so opening the till again tonight, from the hub or from the SumUp
   app, goes straight back to it rather than asking. To go to a different bar, press **Change
   bar** (under the three dots once the till is open) and the question comes back.
3. Press **Open till**. There is one session per venue per night: a second person pressing it
   joins the session already open rather than starting another. A bar opening is a night's work
   like any other, so the till, the stock and the Challenge 25 register all behave as they do on
   a show night, and nothing on it belongs to a performance, since there is none.
4. The screen shows **Open since** the time the session opened, and the grid.

## Selling drinks

![The till with the Open since line (1), the Bar pane (2), the Tickets pane (3), a product tile (4), the allergen button (5) and the overflow menu (6)](/images/docs/show-night/till.png)

1. **Open since** is tonight's session.
2. **Bar** is the drinks grid, grouped by category.
3. **Tickets** is the second pane over the same basket, below.
4. **A product**: one tile each, with the name and the price under it. A drink that comes in one
   size goes into the basket on that press. A drink that comes in several reads **From £…** and how
   many sizes there are: pressing it opens the sizes, each with its own price, and the one you
   press goes into the basket. A size that offers a choice (a mixer, say) asks for it next; the
   choice depletes stock at no extra charge. **Back**, or a press outside, closes either without
   adding anything. An age-restricted drink carries **Check ID** on its tile, so you can see
   which ones need a Challenge 25 check before you touch them. A size the stock register says has
   none left reads **Out of stock** in the sizes, a drink with every size gone reads it on the
   tile, and a mixer with none left reads it in the choice; see [Out of stock](#out-of-stock).
5. **Allergens** sits under each tile's name and opens the product's allergen note over the
   basket, without leaving the sale.
6. The **overflow menu** holds **Close till**: it is the end of the night, not a per-sale
   action, so it sits here rather than under the thumb; see
   [Closing the night](/docs/tonight/closing-the-night).

A row of category chips sits above the grid on a phone, sticky as the grid scrolls, with **All**
first: press a category and the grid shows that category alone; press **All** and everything is
back. The chip you pressed stays pressed from one sale to the next, until you press another.
Tiles sit two to a row for a one-handed reach, and once a size is in the basket its allergen
note is one press from the basket line too, not only from the tile above.

### Out of stock

The grid reads the stock register when the till loads, again after every sale (on the reader, on
a tab, through the SumUp app or as a comp), and again whenever you come back to the till from
another app (the SumUp app, say). There is no need to reload the page. A size with no servings
left reads **Out of stock**, and its button is greyed out. A size that offers a choice stays live
while any of its options is left, and in the choice an option with none left (a mixer that has
run out, say) reads **Out of stock** and is greyed out the same way. The label is advice, not a
live count: the catalogue is kept on the phone, so between sales it can trail the shelf in either
direction, for instance just after a delivery is booked in. Whatever the grid says, every charge
checks the stock register again, and the reader is never asked for a sale the register cannot
cover.

Until the bar's first stocktake has been applied (the cutover count), the stock register is not
yet a trusted balance, so a size or an option with nothing on hand reads **Out of stock** but its
button stays live, and the charge is what refuses. The count is on [Stocktakes](/docs/bar/stocktakes).

**Bar** and **Tickets** are two views of one basket, and both stay put when you switch: the
camera in the Tickets pane is not torn down and started again every time you look at the drinks.
The hint above the grid shows while the basket is empty and goes once there is something in it.

The bar under your thumb carries the count, the comp chip and the total on one line, and the
charge buttons below it.

The **Basket** lists every line with plus, minus and remove. The total is recomputed by the
server on every change, never trusted from the screen. Two choices sit under the lines:

- **Discount**: one of the active percentage discounts, or **None**. A discount takes at most
  {{ BAR_DISCOUNT_MAX_PERCENT }}% off, applies to the bar subtotal only, and its percentage is
  kept on the sale as it stood at that moment.
- **Charge to**: **The reader**, or a named holder's tab. Only members the committee has
  authorised appear: people named in Settings, and everybody currently holding a role named
  there. A tab is capped at {{ BAR_TAB_CAP_PENCE }} outstanding; a charge that would
  take it past the cap is refused, quoting the balance, the charge and the cap. The override is
  on the till itself: a duty manager or the Bar Manager pressing charge takes
  it past the cap without a separate approval step, and the confirmation says so. Credit never
  marks a booking paid, so the picker is hidden and any holder already chosen clears the moment
  ticket money joins the basket.

## Challenge 25 before you pour

**Challenge 25** opens on the press, not at the charge, so nobody pours a drink and then finds it
cannot be sold. The first age-restricted line to go into a basket opens the prompt, naming the
drink.

Two presses for the routine case. If the customer is plainly over 25, press **Visibly over 25**:
no ID was asked for, and the register entry says so. If you asked for ID, press the type that was
shown (passport, photocard driving licence, PASS card, other). Either way you are back at the
grid with the line in the basket. The rest of that sale does not ask again, however many
restricted drinks go in, and charging does not ask either.

Press **Refused** to record a refusal: choose the reason, describe who you checked (never by
name), and **Confirm refusal**. The restricted lines come straight back out of the basket, the
screen reads **ID refused. Not sold:** with the drink named, and the rest of the basket
is still yours to charge. A refusal is not a pass, so a restricted drink pressed after one asks
again.

Whichever you press, the outcome lands in [the register](/docs/tonight/challenge-25)
automatically: **Visibly over 25** and an accepted ID go with the sale, and a refusal goes on the
register as you confirm it, whether or not there is anything left to charge.

Closing the prompt without answering leaves the drink in the basket, unchecked; the charge button
then asks before it takes any money, and no route sells a restricted drink without an outcome.

## Tickets at the bar

The door never sells: an unpaid or walk-up customer is sent here, and the **Tickets** pane is
where their money is taken.

1. Press **Tickets**. Under **Find a booking**, type the reference or a name and press
   **Find**, or press **Scan** to read the booking's QR with the camera.
2. A found booking shows its reference, the booker's first name, the party, the show and the
   time, flagged **not tonight** when it is not. A booking that owes money offers **Add £… to
   the basket**; a collected, admitted, cancelled, lapsed or no-show booking says so and offers
   nothing. The till never edits a booking: swaps, refunds and comps stay on
   [the desk](/docs/box-office/desk).
3. The booking sits in the basket beside the drinks, and one charge takes the lot in one reader
   transaction. The basket line says how the total splits between bar and tickets.

## Walk-ups

1. Under **Walk-up**, choose the performance on a matinee day, then set a quantity against each
   ticket type on sale for it (access ticket types are not sold here) and press **Add to the
   basket**.
2. **Name** and **Email** are optional, and worth asking for: with them the booking's QR is
   emailed; without them the screen shows a door pass after the sale. Give both or neither.
3. After the charge, each walk-up shows a **door pass**: the reference, the QR and the party
   size, to photograph off the screen, or **Print the pass** from the counter laptop. Only
   the passes go on the paper, light on white; the rest of the screen does not print. The door
   scans or types it exactly as it does an emailed one.

## Comps

A bar-only basket, on neither a discount nor a tab, offers **Ask for a comp** alongside the
charge buttons. Nobody gives one away from the till on their own say-so.

![Asking for a comp: what is being given away (1), the reason (2) and Ask (3)](/images/docs/show-night/till-comp.png)

1. Press **Ask for a comp**, say why, and press **Ask**. The basket is priced live, exactly as a
   sale would be, so what is asked for is what would actually be given away.
2. The rest of the screen locks until the request is settled, so the basket it named cannot be
   sold or comped twice; closing the dialogue to wait does not cancel it, and a **Comp
   pending** chip reopens onto the same request, wherever it has got to.
3. **Declined** shows the reason on the spot, and frees the basket straight away. **Approved**
   offers **Give the comp**: press it, answering Challenge 25 first if a line needs it, and the
   sale posts at 0 with the full price kept on the line, exactly as
   [Comps and discounts](/docs/money/comps-and-discounts) reads it back later; the screen only
   unlocks once **Next sale** is pressed. A request nobody decided in time reads
   **lapsed**; ask again.

## Charging

Nothing is recorded until the reader has answered: a declined card leaves no sale, no stock
used and no booking marked paid.

On the counter laptop, or where the SumUp hand-off is not configured, the charge button reads
**Charge £…**. Press it, and the till shows **Key this into the reader** over the figure, set
large so it reads across a bar. Key it into the reader and take the card, then answer under your
thumb:

- **Reader took it** records the sale. The till checks the basket again as it does so; if a
  price moved or a booking was collected at the desk in the meantime, it says **Taken on the
  reader, not recorded** with the reason. Tell the duty manager, then either put the cause
  right and press **Record it again**, or refund on the reader, write a note saying what
  happened to the money, and press **Give up, with note**.
- **Card declined** records nothing and brings the basket back, ready to charge again.

On a phone with the hand-off configured there is one charge button. **Charge £… on SumUp** opens
the SumUp app with the total already keyed; the app takes the payment on the reader and comes
back to the till, and only then is the sale recorded. If the app will not open on this phone,
press **Key in by hand** under it, which is the typed charge above. A tab charge
never goes to the reader: with a holder chosen the button reads **Charge the tab**, and the
charge is recorded straight away.

While the app has the screen the till shows **Waiting for SumUp** and asks **Did the payment go
through on the reader?** Answer **Payment went through** (with the SumUp code if you have it),
**Payment did not**, or **Check again**. A failure or a cancellation from the app
brings the basket back, with **Try SumUp again** on the message: it starts a new hand-off for the
basket as it now stands, priced and checked exactly as the first was, so if a price moved in the
meantime the till says so rather than sending the old figure.

Any charge left waiting tonight, typed or handed to SumUp, is listed under **Unanswered card
charges** so the laptop can answer for a phone that left one behind: **Reader took it** or
**Card declined** for a typed one. An unanswered one is given up on after
{{ SUMUP_ATTEMPT_TIMEOUT_MINUTES }} minutes, and the till will not close while one is waiting.

Coming back from the app lands on a **Payment** screen, which says **Recorded: £…** and "The till
has it. Close this page.", **Not taken** where SumUp says the payment did not go through and
nothing was recorded, **Already given up on** where the hand-off had been abandoned before SumUp
answered, or **Taken on the reader, not recorded**, which tells you to tell the duty manager.
**Back to the till** is the way on from all of them, and opens the till at the bar the charge
was rung up at with the basket back on it. On Android the SumUp app comes back in a new browser
tab rather than the one the till was in; the basket comes back once, in whichever tab you look at
first. The tab the charge started in, if you go back to it, empties its basket and says **its
basket was restored in another tab on this phone**, so the same round is never on two screens.
Carry on in the tab that has it. A basket SumUp turned down is kept on the phone until 04:00 and
no longer.

After every sale the confirmation reads **Taken on the reader**, **Taken on SumUp** or the tab it
went on, over the figure, and lists which bookings were paid, the discount applied, or the tab's
new balance. Press **Next sale**. What an ID refusal took out of the basket is said above the
basket as you confirm the refusal, not on the confirmation.

## When the connection drops

The catalogue is on the device, so the grid, the sizes and the allergen notes all keep working
and the basket keeps taking presses. Pricing is not: a total is never worked out on the phone,
so with nothing to reach the till cannot say what the basket costs.

The bar under your thumb reads **No connection. Charging waits for it.** in place of the total,
and the charge buttons stay where they are, greyed out. Nothing is lost: the moment the
connection comes back the total is worked out again on its own and the buttons come alive. Keep
serving and charge when it returns.

## What happens next

One sale is one write: a ledger entry in pence with a line per basket line, a stock movement
per ingredient, any ticket collections and walk-up bookings, and the Challenge 25 outcome, all
together or not at all. A booking collected here reads PAID at the door the moment the charge
goes through. Every sale is attributed to you and to tonight's session. The desk and the bar share
one SumUp reader and one login on a show night, so the close compares the reader's Z with the whole
night, the desk's takings included; this session's own figures are listed under it.
