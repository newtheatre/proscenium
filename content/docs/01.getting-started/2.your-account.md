Every signed-in person has the same account screens, whatever roles they hold. **My NNT** is
what is yours right now: your next shift, your membership, your training, a room booking, your
passes and recent notifications. The four settings pages, **Profile**, **Access requirements**,
**Security** and **Email settings**, are how the theatre knows you, and sit in the account menu
under your name. Membership and passes are in the list of your own screens rather than in
settings: they are things you have rather than things about you.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
Anything on these screens that could lock you out or give your account away asks you to prove
it is you again in a **Confirm it's you** box, unless you signed in within the last
{{ REAUTH_WINDOW_MINUTES }}. It offers whatever you signed in with: your passkey, Google, or
your password and its authenticator code. Confirm, and the action you were taking runs.
Removing something asks first, then asks you to confirm it is you if it needs to, and never
asks either twice.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Confirm it is still you before this goes ahead"**: the box opens for you to confirm; the
  action runs by itself once you have. "There is no way to confirm this here. Sign out and sign
  in again." means the account has no method strong enough to reassert with, so a fresh sign-in
  is the confirmation.
- A refusal to removing or withdrawing something appears inside the box that asked, and
  nothing changes.
- **"That is the only way into this account: add another before removing it"**: add a passkey
  or another method first.
- **"That address signs in with Google and cannot hold a password"**: a committee address was
  put on a password account. Ask the IT Manager to link the Google identity instead.
- **"That code did not match. Try the next one."**: while setting up the app, the six digits
  were wrong or already used. Wait for the app to change and type the new code.
- **"This account already has an authenticator app"**: one is already confirmed. Remove it
  before setting up another.
- **"This account holds a role that needs an authenticator app. Give up the role first."**: the
  authenticator cannot be removed while the role stands.
- **"That is not the address on this account"**: the confirmation typed while closing the
  account did not match. Type the address exactly as the box shows it.
- **"Your membership could not be read."**, **"Your passes could not be read."**, **"Your
  overview could not be read."**: the page could not reach what it needed. This is not the same
  as nothing being recorded, and the screen says so rather than showing you an empty one. Press
  **Try again**, and if it keeps happening, say so.

## My NNT

Open it from the account menu, or from **My NNT** in the list of your own screens: the strip
under the header on a wider screen, or the header's menu button on a phone.

![My NNT on a phone, with the account menu (1), the help button (2), the menu button (3) and your name and membership line (4)](/images/docs/getting-started/my.png)

1. **Your name** opens the account menu: settings, the parts of the site you can reach, and
   **Sign out**.
2. **The help button** opens the page of this documentation that describes the screen you are on.
3. **The menu button** opens the member's own screens: My NNT, Rota, Rooms, Training, Passes
   and Membership, with Tonight while you work a show tonight and Bar tab if you keep one. On a
   wider screen they are a strip under the header instead.
4. **The heading** names you and says where your membership stands, in sentences: who you are
   signed in as, and then your membership running until a date, having run out with a date you
   can renew by, having run out, or not being there yet.

The tiles lead with what is soonest. A shift, a room booking, a performance you have booked and a
training session you are signed up to each take their place by when they happen, so the nearest
is first; the rest follow in the same order every time: membership, passes, recent
notifications, the next show on sale.

Each tile links to its own screen: **Next shift** to your rota (or to Tonight, while you are on
shift, which is a confirmed shift inside its own hours), **Membership** to the membership page,
**Training** to your training, **Next room booking** to your bookings, **Tickets** to your
bookings for performances, **Passes** to your passes, **Recent notifications** to your notifications,
and **Next show on sale** to what's on. The link is at the foot of every tile, full or empty. An
empty tile says what would fill it and offers the one action that does.

**Tickets** shows your next booking still to come, from tonight on: the show, the night and the
venue, with **Open booking** and its reference, which opens the booking page with its QR code.
**See all your bookings** lists your bookings still to come; with none, the tile offers what's on.

## Profile

**Profile** lists what the theatre holds about you, and under every field says who can see it:
either only you, or you and the officers who can already look up accounts.

1. Change your **name**, **pronouns** (optional, in your own words) or **phone number**.
2. Switch on **Share my phone with tonight's team** if you want the duty manager running a
   performance you hold a confirmed shift on to be able to ring you. It is shown to nobody else,
   and only while the shift stands.
3. Fill in an **emergency contact** if you want one on record: their name, their phone number
   and how you know them. The card above the fields says who can see it.
4. Press **Save**. The screen says "Profile saved".

**Email address**, below the profile, names the address you sign in with above an empty field,
so the box is only ever the new one. Type it and press **Change the address**. Your other devices
are signed out, and the new address receives a link to confirm itself. Until it is confirmed,
nothing but that confirmation is sent to it. A committee address cannot be put on an account
that holds a password: those sign in with Google only.

## Security

Security starts with the ways into your account, then puts the one step you should take next
straight below them, then the rest, with your data and closing the account at the foot.

![The Security page with the ways in (1), your next step (2), the authenticator app card (3) and the recovery codes button (4)](/images/docs/getting-started/security.png)

1. **Ways in** lists every method on the account (password, Google, each passkey) with when it
   was added and last used. Each has **Remove this way in**, except the last one, which reads
   "Your only way in": the theatre never removes your last way in, so add another before taking
   one away. A box asks first, naming the method; press **Remove this way in** in it to go ahead,
   or **Back** to keep it. Removing a method signs out every other device and emails your address
   to say it happened.
2. **Your next step**, when there is one. If a role you hold needs an authenticator app and you
   sign in with a password, it is **Your role needs an authenticator app**, with **Set up an
   authenticator app**: the screens that role opens refuse you until it is done. Otherwise, where
   the browser can hold a passkey and the account has none, it is **Add a passkey**, which enrols
   this device's passkey. A passkey sign-in never asks for an authenticator code, but adding one
   does not change a password sign-in on another device: that still asks for its code. A browser
   that cannot hold one says "This browser cannot hold a passkey."
3. **An authenticator app** is a second step at sign-in, so a stolen password is not enough on
   its own. When it is not your next step it has a card of its own further down.
4. **Show a new set of recovery codes**, once an authenticator app is set up.

Under the ways in is **Password**: type one and press **Save the password**. It adds a password where
the account has none, and replaces the one it has otherwise, so a member who removed their
password after adding a passkey, or who has only ever used an emailed link, has a way back to
one. Your other devices stay signed in; ending them is what a reset from the sign-in screen is
for. A committee address sees "Theatre addresses sign in with Google and cannot hold a password"
in place of the field: no password may ever exist on a theatre address.

### Setting up an authenticator app

1. Press **Set up an authenticator app**.
2. Scan the code with your app, or type the text below it in by hand.
3. Type the six digits the app shows. The factor is not active until you do. **Stop setting
   this up** leaves at this point and puts nothing on the account.
4. **Save your recovery codes.** They are shown once and never again; each works a single time,
   for the day you do not have your phone. Press **I have saved them** when you have.

Confirming the app signs out every other session on your account. Once active, the card says
how many recovery codes remain, offers **Show a new set of recovery codes** (the old set stops
working) and **Remove the authenticator**. Removing asks first, in a box saying that signing in
goes back to your password alone and your recovery codes stop working. When a role you hold
needs an authenticator app the card says so, and removal is refused until you give up the role.

### Your data

- **Download my data** saves one file holding everything the theatre holds about you. It is
  downloaded here and never emailed: an attachment is a copy nobody can take back. The
  download is noted in the audit trail.
- **Close my account** removes your name and address and the account cannot be used again.
  Type your email address to confirm. Your bookings and attendance stay on our own count of the
  theatre's own records still add up. Download your data first if you want it; this cannot be
  undone, and you are signed out the moment it goes through.

## Notifications

**Notifications**, opened from **See all notifications** on My NNT, is everything the theatre
has sent you, newest first. Everything lands here, even when its email is switched off, and a
notification that points somewhere links there. **Email settings** at the top opens the
settings below.

## Email settings

**Email settings**, one of your account settings, is five switches, one for each topic:
bookings, shifts, training, room bookings and committee announcements. Switch one off and its
news stops arriving by email; it still lands in your notifications. The topics are what a
notification is about rather than which part of the theatre sends it, so a setting holds however
the committee rearranges itself. Tickets, receipts, security emails and safety notices always
arrive by email whatever you choose here. Each answers something you have just done, and no
setting silences one.

A switch saves as you set it, and the switch itself is the confirmation: nothing pops up to be
dismissed. A change takes effect on the next notification.

## Membership

**Membership**, in the list of your own screens, shows one of four states, worded the same here, on the My NNT tile
and in any refusal that turns on it: **Current**, **In grace**, **Lapsed** and **None**, each with
the sentence that goes with it. Membership is bought at the Students' Union, not here; the current fee
is {{ MEMBERSHIP_FEE_PENCE }}, and a membership that has run out still counts for
{{ MEMBERSHIP_GRACE_DAYS }}, which keeps a renewal in hand from being a refusal at a desk.
Where the committee has named the SU's own page for buying one, the membership page, the
running-out and declined emails and a room booking refused for want of a membership each link
to it; until then they say where to buy it without a link.

1. Once you have bought one, fill in **Tell us about a membership**: your student number (how
   the committee finds you on the SU's list, since names do not always match), the day you
   bought it and the term, one or three years. **Bought on** starts empty: give the date on your
   SU receipt, and the term runs from that day. If your account already holds a student
   number, or you have claimed before, the number is filled in for you.
2. Press **Send the claim**. The screen says "Claim sent" and shows it as waiting to be
   recorded. An officer records it, usually within a day, and you hear either way. The committee
   checks memberships against the SU's list later; that check never holds up a member price.
3. **Withdraw the claim** if you made a mistake. A box asks first; once it is withdrawn, send
   another. A withdrawal is recorded against the claim, never with your student number.

Renewing works the same way. If you buy the next term while your current one is still running,
claim it as usual: once recorded, the new term starts the day after the current one ends, so
renewing early loses you nothing.

A declined claim shows the officer's reason under "Your last claim was not recorded", with the
form ready for another. Booking or asking for a room needs a current membership, and so does a
member price on a ticket; nothing else on the member screens does, and nothing is hidden from
you while it lapses. The refusal says so and links back here.

## What happens next

Every change to how you sign in is written to the audit trail and, for a removal, told to your
address. Changing your email or removing a method signs out your other devices; so does
confirming an authenticator app. A membership claim goes to the officers who hold the register at
**Manage, People, Members**, and their decision is sent to you as a notification. Closing the
account anonymises it in one step and ends your session.
