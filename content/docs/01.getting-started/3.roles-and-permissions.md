Authority in this system comes from two places, and the difference matters more than the list
of roles. A **role** is a standing grant the IT Manager makes against your account, and it
opens the sit-down screens under **Manage**: the programme, the rota, the bar's catalogue, the
accounts. Everything operational (the door, the till, tonight's duty manager screen, running a
training session, reading a department's catalogue) comes from a **fact** instead: tonight's
confirmed shift, a current training record, a department you lead. Nothing operational is
granted in advance, so there is nothing to remember to revoke.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
Roles are granted and revoked at **Manage, People, Accounts**: open an account and use its
**Roles** card. Granting and revoking a role is the IT Manager's alone.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"That is not yours to open"**: the account does not hold the permission the screen needs.
  If you think it should, ask the IT Manager; the role may have lapsed at the year end.
- **"Set up your authenticator app"**: the role is there and only lacks its second factor.
  Follow the link to Security and enrol one.
- **"This account holds a role that requires a second factor. Give up the role first."**: an
  authenticator app cannot be removed while such a role stands.
- **A screen is missing from the sidebar**: a group with nothing you can open is not shown at
  all. Check the table above, then check the role's expiry on your account.

## What each role opens

The console sidebar shows only the groups a role can open, so two officers see two different
sidebars. The wording below is the one the screens use.

| Role | Holds | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IT Manager | Every permission, including granting roles, settings and backups | Everything under Manage |
| Manager | Accounts, the audit trail, members and Fellows, rooms, training, refund approval, narrowing a pass type | People, Spaces, Training, and the refund and pass-type decisions the box office cannot make alone |
| Theatre Manager | Reads accounts, the audit trail, members and Fellows; writes rooms and the trail's notes; reads training | People (read), Spaces, Training (read) |
| Training Manager | The training catalogue, records, requests and department leads; reads accounts, members and rooms | Training, plus People and Spaces to read |
| Front of House Manager | The committee's Box Office Manager too, so one role: the programme (shows, venues, seasons, ticket types, pass types, content warnings), the desk and the ticket export; the rota, checklists, emergency cards, the age-check register, the backstage board's setup, reports; opens the door and duty manager screens without a shift, refund approval for tonight's performance included | Box office, Tonight (the console group), Reports and, on the night, the door and the duty manager screen |
| Front of house | Nothing standing | Nothing under Manage: this role exists so a person can be found, and the night derives from their shift |
| Bar manager | The bar's catalogue, stock and reports; opens the till without a bar shift | Bar and, on the night, the till |
| Accessibility officer | Verifying access profile declarations, and nothing else | Box office, Access profiles only |
| Safety Officer | Configuring which incident severities route to them, the open-items list, reports | Tonight, Safety; Reports |
| Treasurer | The ledger and every report built on it, Z readings, the SU export | Money |
| Committee | The money dashboard's aggregate figures and cross-season reports | Money, Money dashboard; Reports |

Three permissions are the named exception to "standing means administrative": the front of house
manager's door and duty manager bypass, and the Bar Manager's till bypass. They open tonight's
screens without a shift, and every use is recorded in the audit trail as an officer bypass.

## Authority that derives from a fact

- **Tonight's shift.** A confirmed shift on tonight's performance (the show night runs 04:00 to
  04:00) opens the door, the till or the duty manager screen for that role and that night. The
  **Next shift** tile on My NNT links to Tonight while it stands.
- **A training record.** Somebody is a trainer if and only if they currently hold a record on a
  module marked as granting it; a record that is expiring still counts. Revoking the record is
  the whole of taking the standing away.
- **Leading a department.** A department lead reads its catalogue without holding a training
  permission.
- **Membership.** Current, or inside its grace window, decides whether you may book a room or
  use a member price. It is read from the SU's recorded term, never from a grant.
- **Your own rows.** You can always read and change what is yours: your bookings, your claims,
  your profile.

A missing training record is never fixed by granting a role. The fix is the record.

## The committee year

Every role expires at the end of the committee year, 31 July, unless the IT Manager marks
it permanent when granting it, which is exceptional and reported. Expiry is checked at the
moment you act, so a role that lapsed overnight stops working with nothing to be run.
Holders are warned {{ ROLE_LAPSE_NOTICE_DAYS }} before a role lapses, so a handover is planned
rather than discovered. A lapsed grant is tidied away after {{ ROLE_GRANT_PRUNE_DAYS }}; the
audit trail keeps the history.

The last IT Manager is guarded: no action may leave the theatre without a usable one.

## Roles that need an authenticator app

Any role touching money, personal data or safety records requires a second factor: at present
{{ PRIVILEGED_ROLES }}. Holding such a role with a password sign-in and no authenticator app
refuses every console screen with "This role needs an authenticator app before it can be used.
Set one up, then come back and try again." and a link to **Security**. The sidebar still shows
the links, so you can see what the role would open. A passkey sign-in counts as both steps for
that session.

## What happens next

Granting, revoking and every officer bypass is in the audit trail. A lapsing role is notified
to its holder ahead of time, and a grant that has lapsed grants nothing from the instant it
expired.
