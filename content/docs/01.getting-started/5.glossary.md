The screens, the emails and this documentation use one word for each thing. Where the old
estate used two, the one kept is in the first column and the one dropped in the second. The
words the screens use are the ones written here, so if a screen says "performance" it means the
dated slot and never the production.

## People and accounts

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member | Membership, for the person | The person. "A member books", never "a membership books". |
| Membership | Member, for the state | Being current with the SU, bought there and recorded here; checked when a room is booked or a member price used, and shown on Membership. Current, in grace, lapsed or none. |
| The SU | SU, the union | The Students' Union, abbreviated after the first full mention on a page. |
| Officer | Committee, crew | A person holding a standing role with a permission, expiring each committee year. |
| Committee | Officer, crew | The collective body: "committee direction", "the committee's own words". |
| Crew | Officer | Backstage participants who join a show night's board with a short code and no account. |
| Fellow | Honorary member, life member | Somebody the theatre has awarded a fellowship: one per person, for life, recorded on the roll at People, Fellows with a public citation. A fellowship issues a lifetime pass at no cost in the same step, and revoking it stops future admissions without rewriting anything. |
| Duty manager | Manager, FOH lead | The one shift on a performance that runs the night. Every venue template has exactly one; the duty manager's screen shows the house numbers and the checklist, and tonight's confirmed duty manager approves comps without holding a standing permission. Refunds are the box office's. |
| Sign in, sign out | Log in, login, logon | Getting into and out of your account. |
| Email | E-mail | The address on an account, and the messages sent to it. |
| Postcode | Post code, post-code | One word, wherever an address is collected. |
| Access profile | Access needs, disability record | What a member declares once about what they need at a performance and how many companions come with them, verified in person by the Accessibility Officer. The door sees only the agreed wording, never the declaration. |

## Tickets and the programme

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show | Performance, for the production | The production itself: its title, category, content warnings and the performances under it. |
| Performance | Show, for a dated slot | A dated, timed slot a booking is made against. Everything record-like keys to a performance, never to a day or a venue. |
| Booking | Reservation | The reader's word for seats held or bought against a performance, paid or not. |
| Hold | Booking, reservation | The state of a booking before it is released or paid. An unpaid booking is released before curtain and the seats go back on sale. |
| Ticket | Reservation, booking, hold | A seat that has been sold: paid, or to be paid at the box office. |
| Pass | Ticket, season ticket | An entitlement bought once and admitted against many performances. A Fellow's is issued with the fellowship. |
| Comp | Free ticket, freebie | A ticket or a bar sale at no charge. It is requested with a reason and approved by tonight's duty manager or the Bar Manager, never by the person who asked; the ledger records the value forgone. |
| Walk-up | Door sale, on-the-door | Somebody with no booking who buys at the bar's till on the night. The door never sells: unpaid and walk-ups go to the bar. |
| Door pass | Ticket, for a walk-up | A walk-up's own QR code, printed from the counter laptop or photographed off the screen, which the door scans like any booking. |
| Waiting list: offer, claim, lapse | Queue, reserve list | For a sold-out performance: you join with a party size, seats that free up are offered in join order, an offer is claimed through the same booking step as any other, and an offer not claimed in its window lapses and passes to the next person. An offer never outlives its performance. |
| Content warning | Trigger warning, advisory | A warning chosen from a vocabulary rather than written fresh, so two shows warn about the same thing in the same words. Each is general or technical (staging), and a general one is mentioned, discussed or depicted. |
| Venue | Room | Where a performance happens: its own row, an address and an emergency card. It may point at a room. |

## Rooms

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Room | Venue, space, in body copy | A bookable part of the building, with opening hours, a policy and a calendar of closures. |
| Space | Room, venue, outside navigation | Only the console group's umbrella word. Body copy says room or venue. |

## The night

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shift | Rota, for one slot | One role on one performance: door, bar, duty manager. |
| Rota | Shift, for the whole schedule | The schedule and its screens. "Your rota", never "your shifts". |
| Show night | Day, date | Runs 04:00 to 04:00 London time and is labelled by the day it began, so a sale at 01:00 is still tonight's. |
| Challenge 25 | ID check, under-age check | The policy the bar works to, and the name of the register it is written in: **Challenge 25** on Tonight, **Age-check register** at Manage, Rota. The wording on both screens is this one. |
| Age check | Challenge 25, for one entry | One recorded check: the ID that was accepted, or the refusal and its reason, and who was checked described without a name. The Front of House Manager exports the register for a licensing inspection. |
| Emergency card | Evacuation plan, safety sheet | The venue's one card: the address read to a 999 handler, the assembly point, exits, isolation points, first aid kit, defibrillator, first aiders, fire panel and what3words. Written at Manage, Rota, Emergency cards; cached on every phone that opens a tonight screen. |
| Backstage board | Chat, group message | Tonight's shared board between front of house and backstage: milestones such as house open and clearance, called and acknowledged from either end. Crew join it without an account by typing the backstage code. |
| Backstage code | Show code, join code, PIN | The six-digit code for tonight's backstage board, derived fresh for each night and venue and shown to the duty manager. It is never stored, and resetting the board changes it. |
| Officer bypass | Override, admin access | A Front of House Manager opening the door or duty manager screen, or a Bar Manager opening the till, without holding a shift tonight. Every use is recorded. |
| Sold, in, seats left | Admitted, attendance, remaining | The three house numbers, in these words on the hub, the glance, the door and the till. "In" is through the door; "seats left" is the cap less what is sold. |
| Exception | Closed over, exempted, waived | A checklist item answered with a reason instead of a tick. The control is **Make an exception**, the entry reads "Exception: …", and the night report prints the reason. |

## The bar

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stocktake | Stock check, count | A count of stock captured at a moment and then applied, in one step, as movements that bring the register to what was counted. It is open until applied; a blank and a counted 0 are different things. |
| Movement | Adjustment, transaction | One change to a stocked item's quantity: a delivery, a sale, a stocktake correction. Nothing is edited afterwards: a correction is another movement. |
| Serving size | Variant, option | A size a product sells at. The catalogue row says **Serving size**, a sentence says size, and the till says size. |
| Z reading | End-of-day total, cash-up | The SumUp reader's own total for a calendar day, recorded by the Treasurer at Money, Daily reconciliation and compared with what the ledger expected for the same day. A variance is resolved with a note, never by editing either figure. |

## Money

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ledger | Accounts, the books | The record of every payment, comp, discount and refund, in pence. Nothing in it is ever edited: a correction is a new line that replaces an old one. |
| Period close | Month end, locking | A closed period refuses any new ledger line inside its dates. Closing is a fact appended, never a change to the entries it covers; only the IT Manager reopens one. |
| Reader | Card machine, terminal | The SU's physical SumUp reader, the one way any money is taken. Nothing here charges a card. |

## Training

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Module | Session, record, certification | The catalogue entry: a course, of kind module, certification or brief. |
| Session | Module | One scheduled delivery of a module, signed up for and attended or not. |
| Record | Session, certification | What a member holds once a module is awarded: current, expiring or lapsed. |
| Certification | Record, module as a loose word | Not a separate thing: a module of kind certification. A finished course is always a record. |

## Taking something away

The five words for it, each meaning something different. A button names which of them it does, so
"Delete" on its own never appears beside a row other things point at.

| Use | Not | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retire | Archive, delete | A catalogue thing comes off the till or out of the building's list, and its history is kept. **Bring back** reverses it. |
| Hide | Retire | Something goes off the till for now. **Put on the till** reverses it. |
| Close | Retire, lock | A period or a night stops taking entries. |
| Remove | Delete, retire | A template, a lead, a note or a poster goes: there is nothing to keep. |
| Revoke | Remove, withdraw | A grant, a record or a fellowship is taken away, and the fact that it was is kept. |
