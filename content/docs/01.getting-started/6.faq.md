The questions a new committee member asks in their first fortnight, answered from what the
screens actually do. Where an answer has a longer page, it links there.

::accordion

::accordion-item{label="The sign-in form says my details do not match an account, and I am sure they do"}
The wording is the same whether the address is unknown, the password is wrong, the address has
never been confirmed, or the account has been closed, so that the form cannot be used to find
out who has an account. If you registered recently, press **I did not get my confirmation
email** and follow the new link. If you are a committee member with an `@newtheatre.org.uk`
address, you have no password: use **Sign in with Google**. Otherwise use **I have forgotten my
password**.
::

::accordion-item{label="A link in an email says it has expired"}
Every emailed link works once and for a fixed time: a sign-in link for
{{ MAGIC_LINK_MINUTES }}, a password reset for {{ PASSWORD_RESET_HOURS }}, a link the theatre
sent when it made your account for {{ ADMIN_TOKEN_HOURS }}. The screen that says so offers a
form to send a fresh one, or **Ask for a new one** takes you back to sign in. If your address
was changed twice, only the newest confirmation link works.
::

::accordion-item{label="I have lost my phone and cannot get an authenticator code"}
At the code step press **I do not have my authenticator** and type one of the recovery codes
you saved when you set the app up; each works once. If you have none left, the IT Manager can
reset the second factor on your account from Manage, People, Accounts, after which you sign in
with your password alone and set the app up again. A passkey on another device also gets you in
without a code.
::

::accordion-item{label="The console refuses to open and tells me to set up an authenticator app"}
Your role is one that touches money, personal data or safety records, so it needs a second
factor before it can be used. Go to **Security** in the account menu, press **Set up an
authenticator app**, save the recovery codes, and try again. The sidebar shows the screens
your role would open in the meantime so you know it is the factor, not the role, that is
missing.
::

::accordion-item{label="My membership reads lapsed, but I renewed at the SU last week"}
The theatre only records what the SU sells, and the SU's list reaches us in batches. Go to
**Membership** on the My NNT strip and fill in **Tell us about a membership** with your student
number, the day you bought it and the term. An officer records it and you hear either way. A
membership that has run out still counts for {{ MEMBERSHIP_GRACE_DAYS }} after its end, so a
renewal in hand is not a refusal at the desk.
::

::accordion-item{label="I tried to book a room and was told it needs a current membership"}
That is the one thing on the member screens a lapsed membership refuses, and the refusal links
to Membership so you can tell us about the one you bought. Nothing is hidden from you while a
membership lapses: every screen still opens, and only the booking itself is refused.
::

::accordion-item{label="Where is the screen I was told to use?"}
Console screens are under **Manage** in the account menu, grouped by domain: Tonight, Box
office, Bar, Spaces, Training, People, Money, Communications, System. A group you cannot open is
not shown at all, so if a whole group is missing the account does not hold that permission, or
the role has lapsed at the committee year end. [Finding your way](/docs/getting-started/finding-your-way)
lists every group and its screens.
::

::accordion-item{label="Tonight is not in my account menu"}
Tonight's screens open from a confirmed shift on tonight's performance, or from an officer's
bypass. The menu entry appears once your shift is confirmed and the night has begun at 04:00,
and goes again when it ends. The **Next shift** tile on My NNT links to Tonight while your shift
stands; an officer whose role opens the night without a shift goes straight to `/tonight`. If you
claimed a shift but it has not been confirmed, the tile says "Claimed" and Tonight stays closed.
::

::accordion-item{label="Somebody removed a sign-in method from my account, or I did not"}
Removing a method sends a message to the account's address, so you would have heard. Any change
to how you sign in is in the audit trail with who made it. If you did not make it, change your
password from the sign-in screen's forgotten-password link, which signs out every other device,
and tell the IT Manager.
::

::accordion-item{label="Can I change my email address to my theatre one?"}
Only if the account has no password. Committee addresses on `@newtheatre.org.uk` sign in with
Google and can never hold a password, so the change is refused on a password account. Ask the
IT Manager, who can merge accounts from Manage, People, Accounts; a Google sign-in on an
address that already has a password-less account claims it with everything on it.
::

::accordion-item{label="I registered but never received the confirmation email"}
On the sign-in screen press **I did not get my confirmation email** and type the address. The
answer is the same whatever you type; if the address needs confirming, a fresh link is on its
way. Check the address was typed correctly when you registered: an address no message could
reach was never given an account. Resends are limited per address over a short window.
::

::accordion-item{label="How do I get a copy of what the theatre holds about me?"}
**Security**, then **Download my data**: one file, downloaded there and then rather than
emailed, and noted in the audit trail. **Close my account** on the same card removes your name
and address for good; bookings and attendance stay as anonymous rows so the theatre's own
records still add up.
::

::accordion-item{label="What does the help button do?"}
The question-mark next to your name opens the page of this documentation for the screen you
are on. If the page and the screen disagree, press **Report as out of date** at the top of the
page: the IT Manager is told at once.
::

::accordion-item{label="Why does everything ask me to confirm it is me again?"}
Changing a security setting, removing a sign-in method or closing the account asks for a fresh
proof unless you signed in within the last {{ REAUTH_WINDOW_MINUTES }}. The box offers whatever
you signed in with: passkey, Google, or password and code. It is a confirmation of the action
you were taking, not a second sign-in, and the action runs by itself once you have confirmed.
::
