You have been given a role and an address, and there is a season to run. This is the short list
for the first two weeks. Everything on it has a page of its own, linked from the step.

## If something goes wrong

Whatever is refused, the refusal on screen says what to do next. If it does not, or if a page
here disagrees with the screen in front of you, press **Report as out of date** at the top of
the page and the IT Manager is told.

## The first two weeks

1. **Sign in.** A committee address on `@newtheatre.org.uk` uses **Sign in with Google** and
   never holds a password. Anything that goes wrong at this step, and the questions people ask
   about it, are on [Signing in](/docs/getting-started/signing-in).
2. **Set up an authenticator app**, under **Security** in the account menu. Any role that
   touches money, personal data or safety records needs one before it opens a console screen,
   and the sidebar shows you what your role would open in the meantime. Save the recovery codes
   somewhere you can reach without your phone: [Your account](/docs/getting-started/your-account).
3. **Tell us about your membership**, if you hold one and it has not been recorded. Booking a
   room and a member price on a ticket need a current one; nothing else on your own screens
   does. The form and the four states are on
   [Your account](/docs/getting-started/your-account).
4. **Find your screens.** Console screens are under **Manage** in the account menu, grouped by
   domain, and a group you cannot open is not shown at all. If a whole group is missing, your
   role does not hold it or it has lapsed at the committee year end.
   [Finding your way](/docs/getting-started/finding-your-way) lists every group and its screens;
   [Roles](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions) says what each role opens.
5. **Read your own area's pages.** Each section of this documentation opens with who uses the
   screens and what they are for, and every screen's page is one press from the screen itself:
   the question-mark button beside your name opens the page for what you are looking at.
6. **Learn what tonight does.** Tonight is a shift, not a role: it appears in the account menu
   once your shift on tonight's performance is confirmed, from 04:00 to 04:00, and goes again
   after. Read [Tonight](/docs/tonight) before your first show night rather than during it.
7. **Say what you cannot find.** Nobody is expected to have inherited a folder. The IT Manager
   answers a page that is wrong and a screen that will not open, and a question asked in week
   one is cheaper than a guess in week six.

## What happens next

Your role runs until 31 July, when every standing grant ends and the incoming committee is
granted its own. What you can do tonight comes from tonight's confirmed shift and your current
training records rather than from the grant, and a shift opens a screen your role does not:
[Roles and permissions](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
