The theatre's screens have four faces, one for each way of working, and the same account moves
between them. Which one you are looking at is plain from the top of the page: a photograph and
a footer, a calm strip of your own things, a sidebar of groups, or a dark screen with big
buttons. The account menu is the one thing every face shares, and it is how you get from one
to another.

## The four faces

| Face | For | How it looks | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The public site | Anybody: what's on, booking a ticket, the theatre's own pages | The full-width site with its photographs and footer | `/`, `/whats-on`, `/shows/...`, `/about`, the policy pages |
| My NNT | You, about your own things: a booking, your rota, your training | The site header, a strip of member screens, no sidebar | `/my`, `/rota`, `/rooms`, `/training`, `/account/...` |
| Manage | Sit-down work done for the theatre | A sidebar of groups, each split into Every day and Set-up | `/admin`, `/box-office/...`, `/bar/...`, `/people/...`, `/money/...`, `/reports`, and the `manage` routes under rooms, rota and training |
| Tonight | Standing up, on a phone, on a show night | A dark screen, two words per button, the hub as the way back | `/tonight` and its screens |

An address names the part of the theatre it belongs to, not which face it wears. Where a part
has both a member side and a console side, the member's screens sit at the top and the
console's under `manage`: `/rooms` is where you book a room, `/rooms/manage/requests` is where
an officer decides one.

## The account menu

Press your name at the top right of any screen (in the sidebar's foot on a console screen).

![The console overview with the account menu open (1), the menu itself (2) and Sign out (3)](/images/docs/getting-started/account-menu.png)

1. **Your name** opens the menu. Under it is your membership line: member until a date, in
   grace until a date, or a link to put a lapsed or unrecorded membership right.
2. **The menu** is three groups: the settings pages (Profile, Security, Notifications); the
   places you can reach (My NNT for everybody, Tonight while you hold a confirmed shift
   tonight, Manage for anybody holding a standing permission, Documentation for everybody) and
   **Back to the site**; and **Sign out**.
3. **Sign out** ends the session on this device only. Other devices stay signed in unless you
   change a password or a sign-in method.

Somewhere you cannot open is not listed. Tonight appears from 04:00 on a night you hold a
confirmed shift and goes again at 04:00 the next morning: it comes from the shift and never
from a standing grant. It is also reached from the **Next shift** tile on My NNT, and directly
at `/tonight` by an officer whose role opens the night without a shift.

## Manage

The sidebar is nine groups in a fixed order, the same for everybody: Rota, Box office, Bar,
Spaces, Training, People, Money, Communications, System. A group with nothing you can open is
not shown. A group opens when you go to a screen inside it and stays open until you close it,
so Box office and Bar can both be open at once. **Overview** at the top is the operations
dashboard: messages that did not arrive and shows nobody has assessed for content warnings.
The **NNT** at the top of the sidebar goes back to it.

Inside a group the screens are in two parts. **Every day** is the work of a week: the desk, the
board, the stock, the requests that came in. **Set-up** is what a committee sets when it takes
over or when a season is built, and then leaves alone. A group whose screens are all one kind,
such as People or Money, has no headings at all.

The groups and their screens, in sidebar order:

- **Rota**. Every day: Rota board, Approvals, Bar openings, Safety, Age-check register. Set-up:
  Shift templates, Checklists, Emergency cards, Backstage board.
- **Box office**. Every day: Desk, Pass desk, Shows. Set-up: Ticket types, Pass types, Venues,
  Seasons, Show categories, Content warnings, Access profiles.
- **Bar**. Every day: Stock, Movements, Stocktakes, Order list, Tabs, Reports. Set-up: Products,
  Product categories, Discounts.
- **Spaces**. Every day: Room requests, Bookings, Closures, Utilisation. Set-up: Rooms, Other
  rooms.
- **Training**. Every day: Records, Sessions, Requests. Set-up: Catalogue, Departments.
- **People**: Accounts, Roles, Members, Fellows.
- **Money**: Money dashboard, Revenue by show, Comps and discounts, Daily reconciliation,
  Ledger entries, Periods, Exports.
- **Reports**: Reports.
- **Communications**: Announce, Send log.
- **System**: Settings, Audit trail, Backups.

The group is called Rota and the show-night screens are called Tonight, and they are not the
same thing: the Rota group is planned at a desk days ahead, and Tonight is the phone screen you
work a show night on.

A deep link to a console screen you cannot open is refused with "That is not yours to open"
rather than shown empty. The sidebar and the guard read the same declaration, so they never
disagree.

## My NNT

The strip under the header lists your own screens, in this order: My NNT, My rota, Book a room,
My room bookings, My training, Training sessions, My tab, Passes, Access requirements,
Membership. On a phone the strip scrolls sideways. The same list is in the header's menu button
on a narrow screen.

## Tonight

The hub is the navigation. Its tiles, in the order it shows them, are Scan ticket, Admit pass
holder, Till on a bar shift, Tonight at a glance, Checklist, Challenge 25, Backstage, Contacts
and incidents, and Emergency last of all. Every other tonight screen has a back arrow to the
hub, and the header names the show, the venue and the time, with a badge saying which shift you
are on. Opening any tonight screen also caches the emergency card, so it opens without a signal
later.

## The help button

A question-mark button at the top right of a screen (next to your name, or in the console's
top bar) opens the page of this documentation that describes that screen. A screen with no
page has no button.

## This documentation

Every page here is signed-in only and needs no role. The person on a shift tonight needs the
page for the screen in front of them more than anybody does.

![The documentation with the sidebar (1), the search (2) and Report as out of date (3)](/images/docs/system/docs.png)

1. **The sidebar** is the whole tree, one section per area of the theatre, in the order the
   console shows them. On a phone it folds into the header's menu button.
2. **Search the documentation** finds a page or a heading by its words.
3. **Report as out of date** is on every page, for anybody signed in.

Under every title is the area the page belongs to and **Last updated** with a day and a name.
Where a page quotes a number the committee has set, the number is the live setting, read as the
page is drawn, so a change on Settings shows here with nothing to edit.

If a page and a screen disagree, the screen has changed and the page has not. Press **Report as
out of date**: the IT Manager is told at once and a line goes in the audit trail naming the
page. There is no editor here, and a page is changed by changing its file in the repository and
merging it, the way a public page is; how that is done is in the repository, in
`docs/operator-documentation.md`.
