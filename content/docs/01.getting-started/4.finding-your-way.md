The system has four faces, one for each posture of work, and the same account moves between
them. Which one you are looking at is obvious from the chrome: a photograph and a footer, a
calm strip of your own things, a sidebar of groups, or a dark screen with big buttons. The
account menu is the one thing every face shares, and it is how you get from one to another.

## The four shells

| Shell | For | How it looks | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The public site | Anybody: what's on, booking a ticket, the theatre's own pages | The full-width site with its photographs and footer | `/`, `/whats-on`, `/shows/...`, `/about`, the policy pages |
| My NNT | You, about your own things: a booking, your rota, your training | The site header, a strip of member screens, no sidebar | `/my`, `/rota`, `/rooms`, `/training`, `/account/...` |
| Manage | Sit-down work done for the theatre | A sidebar of groups, one open at a time | `/admin`, `/box-office/...`, `/bar/...`, `/people/...`, `/money/...`, and the `manage` routes under rooms, rota and training |
| Tonight | Standing up, on a phone, on a show night | A dark screen, two words per button, the hub as the way back | `/tonight` and its screens |

A prefix names the domain, not the shell. Where a domain has both a member side and a console
side, the member's screens sit at the top and the console's under `manage`: `/rooms` is where
you book a room, `/rooms/manage/requests` is where an officer decides one.

## The account menu

Press your name at the top right of any screen (in the sidebar's foot on a console screen).

![The console overview with the account menu open (1), the menu itself (2) and Sign out (3)](/images/docs/getting-started/account-menu.png)

1. **Your name** opens the menu. Under it is your membership line: member until a date, in
   grace until a date, or a link to put a lapsed or unrecorded membership right.
2. **The menu** is three groups: the settings pages (Profile, Security, Notifications); the
   shells you can reach (My NNT for everybody, Manage for anybody holding a standing permission,
   Documentation for everybody) and **Back to the site**; and **Sign out**.
3. **Sign out** ends the session on this device only. Other devices stay signed in unless you
   change a password or a sign-in method.

A shell you cannot open is not listed. Tonight is reached from the **Next shift** tile on My NNT
while you hold a confirmed shift tonight, or directly at `/tonight` by an officer whose role
opens the night without one.

## Manage

The sidebar is nine groups in a fixed order, the same for everybody: Tonight, Box office, Bar,
Spaces, Training, People, Money, Communications, System. A group with nothing you can open is
not shown, and only the group holding the screen you are on is open, so the sidebar stays short
however many screens there are. **Overview** at the top is the operations dashboard: messages
that did not arrive and shows nobody has assessed for content warnings.

The groups and their screens, in sidebar order:

- **Tonight**: Shift templates, Approvals, Unfilled shifts, Checklists, Emergency cards, Safety,
  Age-check register, Backstage board.
- **Box office**: Desk, Issue passes, Shows, Venues, Seasons, Show categories, Ticket types,
  Passes, Content warnings, Access profiles.
- **Bar**: Products, Categories, Stock, Movements, Stocktakes, Order list, Reports.
- **Spaces**: Rooms, Room requests, Closures, Other rooms, Utilisation.
- **Training**: Catalogue, Departments, Records, Sessions, Requests.
- **People**: Accounts, Members, Fellows.
- **Money**: Season dashboard, Revenue by show, Comps and discounts, Daily reconciliation,
  Ledger entries, Periods, Exports.
- **Communications**: Announce, Send log.
- **System**: Settings, Audit trail, Backups.

A deep link to a console screen you cannot open is refused with "That is not yours to open"
rather than shown empty. The sidebar and the guard read the same declaration, so they never
disagree.

## My NNT

The strip under the header lists the member's own screens: My NNT, My rota, Book a room, My
bookings, My training, Training sessions, Passes, Access requirements, Membership. On a phone
the strip scrolls sideways. The same list is in the header's menu button on a narrow screen.

## Tonight

The hub is the navigation. Its tiles are Scan ticket, Tonight at a glance, Admit pass holder,
Backstage, Emergency, Contacts and incidents, and Till for a bar shift. Every other tonight
screen has a back arrow to the hub, and the header names the show, the venue and the time,
with a badge saying which shift you are on. Opening any tonight screen also caches the
emergency card, so it opens without a signal later.

## The help button

A question-mark button at the top right of a screen (next to your name, or in the console's
top bar) opens the page of this documentation that describes that screen. A screen with no
page has no button.

## This documentation

Every page here is signed-in only and needs no role, because the person on a shift tonight
needs the page for the screen in front of them more than anybody. The tree on the left is the
sections; **Search the documentation** at the top right finds a page or a heading by its words.
Each page opens with a badge naming the module it belongs to and when it was last updated and
by whom.

If a page and a screen disagree, the screen has changed and the page has not. Press **Report as
out of date** at the top of the page: the IT Manager is told at once and a line goes in the
audit trail naming the page. Pages are edited by editing the file and merging, the way the
public pages are; there is no in-app editor.
