Revenue by show is at **Manage, Money, Revenue by show** and needs `finance.read`, which the
Treasurer holds. It reads one season at a time, because a treasurer comparing shows compares
them within a season.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
A show's revenue is money the theatre actually holds: tickets collected and paid for, less
anything refunded. An online booking that has not yet been paid for at the desk contributes
nothing, however many seats it holds. Bar takings and pass sales are never in a show's figure.
::

## Reading the season

![Revenue by show with the season (1), a show's row (2) and pass utilisation (3)](/images/docs/money/revenue-by-show.png)

1. Choose the **season** from the list, which reads as the two years it spans, "2025/26". A
   season starts on {{ SEASON_START }} and ends on {{ SEASON_END }} (month and day, London).
   The range it resolved to is printed above the table. Choosing another season reads the
   ledger again at once; there is nothing else to press.
2. Each show has one row:
   - **Gross**: every ticket collected or sold at the door for the show's performances.
   - **Refunded**: the money handed back on those tickets.
   - **Net**: gross less refunded.
   - **Walk-up** and **Pre-booked**: the gross split by how the seat was sold. A walk-up is a
     seat reserved and paid for in one go at the desk or the bar; pre-booked is an online
     booking collected later.
   - **Pass admissions**: how many seats a pass covered. These carry no money, because the
     money was taken when the pass was sold.
3. **Pass utilisation** lists every pass in the season: its reference, its type, what was
   paid for it, how many shows it admitted the holder to and how many shows its type covers.
   Pass revenue stays here, at pass level; it is never folded into a show's own row.

## The unattributed row

A row named **Unattributed (no performance link)** appears only when the ledger holds ticket
money that names no performance. That is older history from before the link was made reliable.
It is real money, so it is shown rather than quietly left out of every show's figure, and the
per-show rows plus this one always add up to the season's whole ticket revenue.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The report could not be read."**: the ledger query failed or you no longer hold
  `finance.read`. Choose the season again to read it again.
- **A show is missing**: it had no collected ticket, walk-up, refund or pass admission with a
  performance inside the season you chose. Check the year.
- **The figures moved since you last looked**: nothing is stored, so a collection or a refund
  made since is already counted. To freeze a range, [close the period](/docs/money/periods).

## What happens next

Nothing is written by reading this screen. The refunds it shows were posted from the desk, one
ledger entry per ticket, each naming the collection it reverses.
