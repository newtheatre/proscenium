Ledger entries is at **Manage, Money, Ledger entries**, and the Treasurer reads it. It is the list
every figure on the money dashboard drills down to: press **Entries** on a revenue row, or a
refunds, comps or discounts figure, and you land here with the same range and the filters that
figure is made of. Open variance is a reading rather than an entry, so it opens
[daily reconciliation](/docs/money/daily-reconciliation) instead.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Nothing posted to the ledger on this day."**: no filter is set beyond the day, and the day
  was quiet. Widen the **When** range.
- **"No entry matches that."**: the filters exclude everything. Press **Clear**.
- **"The entries could not be read."**: the list did not come back, or your role no longer
  reads the ledger. Reload the page.
- **"That entry could not be read."**: opening one entry failed. Close the panel and open it
  again.
- **"This period is closed: post a correction in the open period instead."**: seen on a
  money-taking screen, not here. Somebody tried to post money dated inside a
  [closed period](/docs/money/periods); the correction goes in today's open period.

## What an entry is

An **entry** is one money event: one collection at the desk, one sale at the till, one refund.
It carries where it happened (the **source**: Desk, Till, Self-serve, Import or System), how it
was paid (the **tender**: Card, Comp, Tab or None), who did it, when, and any entry it
corrects. Its total is never stored: it is the sum of its lines, read at the moment it is asked
for.

A **line** is one thing inside the entry, in integer pence: a ticket collection, a walk-up
sale, a pass sale, a pass admission, a bar item, a tab settlement, a refund or a row of imported
history. A line names the performance, ticket, product or pass it belongs to. A comp line
carries an amount of 0 and keeps the full price beside it; a pass admission is 0, the money
having moved when the pass was sold.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lock" color="info"}
**Nothing in the ledger is ever edited.** An entry cannot be changed or deleted, and neither can a
line. A mistake is corrected by a new entry with negative amounts that names the one it
reverses; both stay visible, and what is owed is the sum across them. A refund is exactly that:
a new entry, one per ticket, reversing the collection.
::

Every entry is stamped in UTC and also carries its plain London calendar day, which is what a
day, a month or a year total groups by. The one exception is
[daily reconciliation](/docs/money/daily-reconciliation), which reads the show night (04:00 to
04:00): the reader is read once a night.

## Finding an entry

![Ledger entries with the search (1), the table (2), an entry's Open button (3) and the count (4)](/images/docs/money/ledger-entries.png)

1. **Search** finds an entry by its booking reference or by the show it was for.
2. The table lists each entry's time, **What** it was, its source, its tender and its amount.
   **What** names the kinds of its lines, how many items it held and its show, such as "Ticket
   collection (2 items), Hamlet", with the booking reference beneath. Opened from the
   navigation, the list shows **today's London day** rather than everything the ledger has ever
   held. Change the **When** filter to another day or a range. Filter by **Source**, **Tender**
   or **What** as well, one value or any of several, or by whether a discount reduced it. The
   active filters show in the toolbar; **Clear** drops them all. Sort by **When**, newest first
   by default.
3. **Open** shows the whole entry in a panel beside the list.
4. The count beneath the table is the whole match, and the list pages through it.

## Opening an entry

![An entry open in its panel, with who and how (1) and its lines (2)](/images/docs/money/ledger-entry.png)

1. The panel says when the entry happened, where and how it was paid, its amount, and each
   fact it carries: who took it, a comp's reason and who approved it, a discount, whose tab it
   was on and, for a voided charge, why.
2. **Lines** lists every line: what it was and how many, its show and performance, its booking
   reference, any discount and its amount.

Nothing in the panel can be changed. For the money behind one show, use
[revenue by show](/docs/money/revenue-by-show); for the value given away, use
[comps and discounts](/docs/money/comps-and-discounts); for every line in a range in a
spreadsheet, take the [SU export](/docs/money/exports).

## What happens next

Nothing is written by reading this screen. Entries are written by the screens that take money,
in the same transaction as the thing they paid for, so an entry cannot exist without its sale
nor a sale without its entry.
