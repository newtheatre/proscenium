Comps and discounts is at **Manage, Money, Comps and discounts** and needs `finance.read`, which
the Treasurer holds. A giveaway is a figure here, never a silent gap: a comp posts to the ledger
at zero with the full price kept on its line, and a discount keeps the amount it took off, so
what was foregone can be added up later without reconstructing any price history.

## Choosing a scope

![Comps and discounts with the scope (1), the date range (2), Refresh (3), foregone value (4) and access admissions (5)](/images/docs/money/comps-and-discounts.png)

1. Choose the **scope**: **PERIOD** for a date range, or **SHOW** for one production across all
   its performances.
2. For a period, set **from** and **to** (London dates; the screen opens on today). For a show,
   pick it from the list; until you do, the screen says "Choose a show to see its figures."
3. Press **Refresh** to read the ledger again.
4. **Foregone value** has two rows. **Comps**: how many comp lines were given and what they would
   have sold for. **Discounts**: how many lines carried a discount and how much was taken off.
5. **Access and companion admissions** lists each access kind of ticket with a count and the
   value taken. It reads "None in this scope." when there were none.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-shield" color="info"}
Access admissions are counts and value only. Nothing on this screen, or in the report behind it,
reaches a person's declared needs or their name; only the Accessibility Officer sees those.
::

## Where a comp comes from

The treasurer reads comps; nobody gives one from here. At the desk a comp needs a request with
a reason, approved by a manager before the COMP tender goes through. At the bar a comp needs a
request approved by tonight's duty manager or the bar manager, never the person who asked.
Refunding a paid ticket at the desk needs a manager (the `money.refund` permission) where the
configuration says so. The approver's identity travels on the ledger entry.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The report could not be read."**: the ledger query failed or you no longer hold
  `finance.read`. Press **Refresh**.
- **"Choose a show to see its figures."**: the scope is SHOW and no show is chosen yet.
- **The value looks too high for a comp**: the value column is what the seat or the drink would
  have sold for, not what was taken (which was nothing). That is what foregone means.

## What happens next

Nothing is written by reading this screen. The same comp and discount totals appear on the
season dashboard for the same range and in the [daily reconciliation](/docs/money/reconciliation)
split for each night, where they are already netted out of the figure the reader is expected to
show.
