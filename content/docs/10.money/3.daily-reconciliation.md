Daily reconciliation is at **Manage, Money, Daily reconciliation**, and the Treasurer reads it
and records a reading on it. Nothing comes back from the card reader on its own, so this is a
person's job: once a night, read the Z total off the SU's SumUp reader and type it in. What it
should say is already worked out for you.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
A **night**, not a calendar day. The show night runs from 04:00 to 04:00 London time, so a sale
rung through at 00:30 belongs to the evening before, the same night the reader was read for. The
month and season totals elsewhere group by the plain London calendar day instead; that is why a
night's figure and a day's figure can legitimately differ.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"The reader read £…; we expect £…. That difference needs a note before it can be
  recorded."**: the two disagree and the note is empty. Say why, then record.
- **"A write-off must name the variance it resolves, and cannot write off a figure that now
  matches"**: you ticked write-off on a first reading, or the figure you typed equals the
  expected total. Untick it, or record a plain correction.
- **"Somebody else just recorded this night's first reading; resolve it instead of adding
  another"**: two people reconciled the same night at once. Reload the screen and, if the
  other reading is wrong, correct it.
- **"That reading already has a correction or a write-off resolving it"** or **"That reading is
  no longer the live one for this night"**: the screen was stale. Reload the screen and resolve
  the reading that is now current.
- **"That reading could not be recorded."**: any other failure. Reload and try once more;
  if it persists, report the page.

## Reading what the ledger expects

![Daily reconciliation with the night (1), the expected figure (2), the current reading (3), the reader figure (4) and Record (5)](/images/docs/money/reconciliation.png)

1. Pick the **night**. The screen opens on the current show night. If any earlier night ran with
   no reading, or still carries an open variance, a **Nights needing attention** warning lists
   them all at the top; the list is never cut short. Nights with no reading are listed from
   **1 September 2026**, the first night that had to be reconciled here: nights imported from
   the old system never had a Z reading. A reading somebody did record for an earlier night
   still appears if its variance is open.
2. **Expected for the night** is built from the ledger alone: the desk's card takings by kind
   (ticket collections, walk-up sales, pass sales), the desk total, the bar's card sales and its
   tab settlements, and the **Expected total**. Comps, discounts and bar refunds are printed
   beneath the table for information, and are already netted out of the total: a comp never
   moved money through the reader.
3. **Current reading** says whether a reading exists for the night, and if so what the reader
   showed, the variance (reader less expected), who entered it and whether it was written off.

## Recording the night's reading

1. Read the Z total off the reader.
2. Type it into **Reader figure**, in pounds and pence, as the reader shows it: `123.45`. A
   pound sign and a thousands comma are both read through, and the line beneath the field
   repeats the figure being recorded so you can check it before pressing Record.
3. If it matches the expected total, press **Record**. Nothing else is needed.
4. If it does not match, write a **Note** saying why, then press **Record**. The screen refuses
   a differing figure with no note, quoting both figures.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert" color="warning"}
A bare number is pounds, so `123` records £123.00. Anything the field cannot read as pounds and
pence is refused before Record can be pressed. The expected figure is worked out again at
the moment you press Record, never taken from what the screen showed, so a sale rung through
while you were reading the reader is already counted.
::

## Resolving a variance

A reading with a variance stays open until it is resolved. Once a reading exists, the section
is headed **Resolve the variance** and works the same way, with one more control:

1. To **correct** the reading (the first figure was mistyped, or a sale was found afterwards),
   type the right reader figure in pounds and pence and a note, and press **Record**. The new reading names the one
   it supersedes; both stay in the history.
2. To **write it off** (the difference is real and accepted), tick **Write off instead of
   correct**, type the same reader figure and a note, and press **Record**. A write-off must
   name the reading it resolves, and cannot write off a figure that now matches.

The **History** table shows every reading for the night in order, oldest first, with the reader
figure, the variance, who entered it and the note. Readings are never edited or deleted.

## What happens next

Each reading is a row in the reconciliation record with the night, the reader figure, the
expected figure, the variance, who entered it, when, the note and what it supersedes. The
same step puts **Daily Z reading recorded** on the audit trail. A night with an open
variance stays in the **Nights needing attention** list, adds to the dashboard's **Open
variance** figure, and is named as a warning when somebody tries to
[close a period](/docs/money/periods) covering it.
