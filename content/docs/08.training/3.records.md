A record is what somebody holds once a module has been awarded to them. This screen, at
**Manage, Training, Records**, shows one person's whole history and is where a record is made
outside a session. Anybody who can read the catalogue can open it; a department lead sees only
records on their own departments' modules. Signing off and recording a certificate need
`training.write` or a lead's standing in the module's department; revoking needs
`training.revoke`, which the Training Manager holds.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
Nothing here is ever deleted. A correction is a revocation with a reason, and then a fresh
award. Every gate in the system (a shift that needs a module, a session with a prerequisite)
reads the records live, so a change here takes effect on the next request.
::

## Reading somebody's history

![The records screen with the person picker (1)](/images/docs/training/records.png)

1. Choose the person under **Whose records**. The search takes a name, an email address or a
   student number.
2. The table lists every record they hold or have held, including what has been revoked or
   superseded. **Search** narrows it by a module id or its title.

Each row shows the module, its department and kind, and how the record came to be: **Session
award**, **Signed off**, **External certificate**, **Self-registered** or **Brought over** from
the old system. Under **Held** is the award date and either the date it runs to or **Never
expires**. Under **State**:

- **Valid**, **Renew soon** or **Expired**, worked out from today's date. A record shows
  **Renew soon** within {{ TRAINING_EXPIRY_WARNING_DAYS }} days of its expiry, and still counts
  as held until the day it expires.
- **Attended** for a brief, which has no expiry.
- **Revoked**, which stops counting everywhere.
- **Superseded**, when a later award of the same module stands in its place.

## Signing a module off

A sign-off records competence proven outside a session: an experienced technician who never
needed the course, or a certification issued on experience gained backstage.

1. Choose the person, then press **Sign something off**.
2. Choose the module by its title. Briefs and retired modules are not offered.
3. Press **Sign it off**.

The award is dated today. Every direct prerequisite has to be held already, and the refusal
names any that are not. The expiry is stamped from the module's policy as it stands today and
is never recomputed afterwards.

## Recording an external certificate

An external certificate is competence earned elsewhere that the theatre never assessed: a
first aid at work certificate, for instance. The theatre records it rather than assesses it,
so the paper and its dates are the evidence.

1. Choose the person, then press **Record a certificate**. The button is disabled unless some
   module accepts outside evidence.
2. Choose the module by its title. Only modules whose department has said they accept an external
   certificate are offered, and the **Evidence** field then names what that module asks to see.
3. Enter **Awarded on**, the day the issuing body granted it, which cannot be in the future.
4. Enter **Runs to**, the issuing body's own expiry date. A certificate never takes the module's
   policy, and it cannot run more than ten years from the award.
5. Enter the **Evidence**: a certificate number, the issuing body or a document reference.
6. Press **Record the certificate**.

## Revoking a record

1. Find the record and press **Revoke**. A revoked record has no button. The dialogue names the
   module being revoked.
2. Say **Why**. The reason is required, is never shown on the audit trail, and is scrubbed if
   the person is ever erased.
3. Press **Revoke the record**.

The record stays on the history and stops counting at every gate. If the person should hold
the module after all, sign it off again: the new award is dated today.

## Expiry

Each module's policy decides when a record awarded under it runs out, and the date is stamped
on the record at award. A record is warned about {{ TRAINING_EXPIRY_WARNING_DAYS }} days before
it expires and again {{ TRAINING_FINAL_WARNING_DAYS }} days before, by the daily sweep, which
sends only once the training sweep has been armed in [Settings](/docs/system/settings) and
reports what it would send until then. The academic year, for modules that end with it, turns
over on {{ ACADEMIC_YEAR_BOUNDARY }} (month and day).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You do not yet hold ..., which this module needs first"**: the module needs something the
  person does not currently hold. The
  refusal names each missing module; sign those off first, or record the certificate that
  covers them.
- **"An award cannot be dated in the future"**: the awarded-on date is ahead of today.
- **"A retired module takes no new sign-offs"** or **"A retired module takes no new external
  certificates"**: the module has been retired in the catalogue.
- **"A brief is attended, not signed off"**: a brief is only ever awarded by a session's
  register.
- **"This module does not accept external certificates"**: the module's editor has the switch
  off. A lead of its department can turn it on.
- **"An expiry falls after the award, not on or before it"** and **"An expiry cannot run more
  than 120 months from the award"**: the certificate's dates do not fit.
- **"That account has been erased"**: nothing can be recorded against a tombstone.
- **"That record is no longer here."**: the record was not found, most likely because the page is stale. Reload.
- **"You do not have permission to do that"**: a lead is acting on a module outside their own
  departments, or somebody without `training.revoke` is revoking.
- **"They hold no training records yet."**: the person has nothing on file; this is not an
  error.

## What happens next

A sign-off, an external certificate and a revocation are each in the audit trail with the
actor, the person and the module; the evidence reference and the revocation reason are not.
The person sees the change on their own training page the next time they open it, and any
shift or session that depends on the module reads the new state at once.
