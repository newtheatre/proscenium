Every module belongs to a department, and a department's leads steward its part of the
catalogue without holding an officer role. The screen is at **Manage, Training, Departments**.
Anybody who can read the catalogue can open it; adding or editing a department needs
`training.write`, and assigning or removing a lead needs `training.leads`, which the Training
Manager holds.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
A lead's standing is read on every request and lapses at the committee handover on 31 July, so
nothing has to be remembered in July. It is renewed by assigning the same person again.
::

## The list

![The departments screen with the search field (1), the Filters button, where the retired switch lives (2), the Add a department button (3), the table (4) and a row's Assign a lead button (5)](/images/docs/training/departments.png)

1. **Search** by a department's name or its code.
2. **Including retired departments** shows the ones no longer in use.
3. **Add a department** opens the form.
4. Each row shows the name, the code, a **Retired** badge where it applies, what it covers, and
   under **Led by** the current leads, each with a cross to remove them.
5. **Assign a lead** and **Edit** act on that row.

## Adding a department

1. Press **Add a department**.
2. Give it a **Code**: uppercase letters, digits and hyphens, in the style TECH, BACKSTAGE or
   FOH. The code is what modules reference, so it is fixed once the department exists.
3. Give it a **Name** and, optionally, **What it covers**.
4. Leave **Still in use** on. A retired department keeps its modules readable and takes no new
   ones.
5. Press **Add a department**.

**Edit** changes the name, the description, the order and whether it is still in use; the code
cannot change.

## Assigning a lead

1. Press **Assign a lead** on the department's row.
2. Choose the person under **Who leads it**. They need an account, and a person may lead more
   than one department.
3. Press **Assign**.

The assignment lapses at handover on 31 July unless somebody renews it, and the screen says so.
Assigning somebody who already leads the department renews their assignment to the next
handover.

To remove a lead, press the cross beside their name under **Led by**, then **Remove Alex Morgan**
in the dialogue. It takes effect on their next request: nothing is cached.

## What a lead may do

A lead reaches four console screens, each scoped to the departments they lead: the
[Catalogue](/docs/training/catalogue), this screen, [Records](/docs/training/records) and
[Requests](/docs/training/requests). Within their departments they may:

- add and edit modules, including drafts, and declare prerequisites;
- sign a module off for somebody and record an external certificate;
- read a person's records for those modules;
- answer requests on the demand board.

A lead may not add departments, assign other leads, revoke a record, or see records and
requests belonging to other departments. Running a session is not a lead's standing at all: it
comes from holding a trainer-granting certification, and is described under
[Sessions](/docs/training/sessions).

## If something goes wrong

- **"A department already has that code"**: codes are unique. Choose another, or edit the one
  that exists.
- **"That account is no longer here."** or **"That account has been erased"**: the person chosen cannot be
  assigned. An erased account is a tombstone and nothing may be attached to it.
- **"That lead assignment is no longer here."**: the assignment was already removed, probably by somebody else.
  Reload.
- **"You do not have permission to do that"**: assigning and removing leads needs
  `training.leads`; adding and editing departments needs `training.write`.
- **"The departments could not be read."**: the list failed to load. Reload, and if it keeps
  happening say so.

## What happens next

A new or changed department is in the audit trail. Assigning a lead records the actor, the
person and the department, and removing one records the same. The lead sees the Training
group in their console the next time they load a page, and loses it the same way.
