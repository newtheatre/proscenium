Any signed-in member with a current membership can book one of the theatre's rooms. **Book a
room** under **My NNT** is the calendar; a free slot on it opens the booking form. A booking
inside the rules is held the moment it is made; one outside them becomes a request that an
officer decides on, with the slot held while they do.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
The rules are configuration, quoted live here. The shortest booking is
{{ ROOM_MIN_BOOKING_MINUTES }} minutes and the longest {{ ROOM_MAX_BOOKING_HOURS }} hours; a
booking needs {{ ROOM_AUTO_APPROVE_NOTICE_HOURS }} hours' notice to be held without a decision;
bookings open {{ ROOM_BOOKING_HORIZON_WEEKS }} weeks ahead; one member may hold
{{ ROOM_ACTIVE_BOOKINGS_PER_MEMBER }} active bookings, counting each week of a series.
::

## The calendar

![The rooms calendar on a phone, with the day controls (1), the room chooser (2), the link for a room not listed (3) and a quarter-hour slot to press (4)](/images/docs/members/rooms.png)

1. **Earlier**, **Today** and **Later** move a day at a time, or a week at a time in the week
   view.
2. **Every room** or one room. Across every room the columns are rooms and the question is
   "what is free tonight"; within one room the columns are days and the question is "when is the
   studio free".
3. **Book a room not listed here** goes to the form for a room the theatre does not manage:
   see [Other rooms](/docs/members/other-rooms).
4. **The grid** shows quarter-hour slots. Free, Yours, Booked, Awaiting a decision, Not open
   and Closed are told apart by colour and by the legend at the foot of the page, and each slot
   says which it is when you reach it by keyboard or screen reader. A slot somebody else holds
   reads as booked and nothing more. **Not open** is outside the room's hours; **Closed** is a
   closure an officer set, and every closure on the days shown is listed in words under the
   grid with its reason and its times. On a touch screen the slots are taller, so a finger can
   hit one.

On a phone the calendar is always the day view, which is the picture above, and there is no
**Day** or **Week** to press. On a wider screen those two switch between them, and a week
across every room is a table of counts, one cell per room per day; pressing a cell opens that
room on that day.

If the calendar cannot be read at all, the page says so in red with **Try again**, and says
nothing about which rooms exist. "No rooms are bookable yet" means exactly that, and only ever
appears when the calendar loaded.

1. Find a free slot. Click it to book an hour from there, or drag across several. On a touch
   screen, tap where the booking starts and then tap where it ends; the line above the grid
   says which tap it is waiting for.
2. The booking form opens with the room, day and times already filled in.

## Booking a slot

![The booking form on a phone, with the room (1), what it is for (2), the day (3) and the start time (4)](/images/docs/members/book-a-room.png)

1. **Room**. A room that always asks says so under it: "Every booking for this room is agreed by
   a person before it is held."
2. **What it is for** is shown to officers, and to nobody else looking at the calendar.
3. **Day**.
4. **From** and **Until**.

Then:

1. **How many people** is optional. The field will not go above what the room holds, and the
   room's capacity is quoted under it.
2. **What the room is for** is a fixed list (rehearsal, meeting, workshop and so on). It is what
   a room we do not manage is judged suitable for, so it is required.
3. **Priority if the slot is contested** decides who keeps the room if a higher priority needs
   it, not what it is used for. An officer may change it.
4. Press **Book it**. **My bookings** opens, and "Booked" names the room, the day and the
   times that were made. A confirmation email follows with a calendar file attached. A series
   and a request land on the same screen and say the same way what was made.

## Repeating a booking

A term of rehearsals is one series, booked from the same form.

1. Open **Repeat this booking**.
2. Choose **Every week** or **Every day**. For a weekly series, pick the days of the week; the
   day already chosen above is preselected.
3. Set **How many times**, up to {{ ROOM_SERIES_MAX_OCCURRENCES }}. The line under the fields
   reads the series back in words.
4. Press **Book N dates**.

Every date is checked before any of them is held, so a week somebody else has stops the whole
run rather than leaving you with half of it. "Some of those dates cannot be booked" then lists
each refused date with its reason, and **Book the other N dates** resubmits without them. A
series inside the rules is held at once; one that needs a decision is asked for as one request.

## Asking for an exception

When a booking fails a rule that can be bent (too short a notice, too long, too far ahead, over
your cap, outside the room's hours), the form says "This one needs somebody to agree to it",
lists exactly which rules failed, and adds a field:

1. Say **Why this one is worth an exception**. It is shown to whoever decides.
2. Press **Ask for it**. "Asked for" confirms it, the slot is held while somebody decides, and
   **My bookings** shows it as "Waiting on a decision".

A room marked as always asking, and a member on the no-show ladder (see
[Your room bookings](/docs/members/your-room-bookings)), go this way whatever the rules say;
the form says so before anything is typed.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Booking a room needs a current membership. Renew it at the Students' Union."**: refused
  outright, never diverted to a request. **Sort out your membership** opens the membership page.
- **"That slot has already happened"** and **"That room is no longer in use."**: refused
  outright.
- **"The room is closed that day"** and **"That is outside the hours the room opens."**: the
  room's opening hours. Both can be asked for as an exception.
- **"The room is closed then: ..."**: a closure an officer has set, quoting its reason. Nothing
  to ask for; pick another time.
- **"The shortest booking is N minutes."**, **"The longest booking is N hours."**, **"Booking
  needs N hours' notice. Ask, and somebody will decide."**, **"Bookings open N weeks ahead."**,
  **"You already hold N bookings, which is the limit."**: each can be asked for as an exception.
- **"Somebody booked that slot first"**: two people went for the same slot and the other won.
  Nothing was booked; find another.
- **"A series runs to N occurrences at most"** and **"That leaves no occurrences to book"**:
  the series is too long, or every date was left out.
- **"That is more people than the room holds"**: a warning on the booking, never a refusal. The
  booking stands.

## What happens next

A booking inside the rules is written as Confirmed and a confirmation email goes out with the
calendar file attached. A request is written as Waiting on a decision with its slot held, the
member is told it was received, and every approver is told there is something to decide; a
request left {{ ROOM_REQUEST_EXPIRE_HOURS }} hours lapses and the member is told. The decision,
either way, arrives by email and on [Your room bookings](/docs/members/your-room-bookings). What
officers do with a request is on [Room requests](/docs/spaces/room-requests).
