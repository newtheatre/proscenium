A tab charge is owed to the bar by an authorised holder, not money taken: the till
posts it against the holder's own account, and it is cleared later by paying it off. **My tab**,
under **My NNT**, is for a signed-in member: it shows what is owed right now and every charge
that made it up.

![Your bar tab with the outstanding balance (1) and the charges it is made up of (2)](/images/docs/members/tab.png)

1. **Outstanding** is the live balance: every charge run up, less anything settled or voided.
2. **Charges** lists every charge ever made to the tab, itemised: what it was for, when, what it
   came to, and whether it is still outstanding, settled, or was voided.

Only an authorised holder can be charged in the first place, a setting the bar manager controls;
this page shows the same figures and charges to anybody signed in, empty if nothing has ever been
charged.

## Settling a tab

Settlement is taken in person on the Students' Union's SumUp reader, at the till, never online:
the till shows the outstanding charges and takes payment against them directly. This page reads
what is owed; it does not take payment.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your tab could not be read"**: the page failed to load, which is not the same as owing
  nothing. Reload, and if it keeps happening say so.

## What happens next

A charge posts the moment it is rung up, so the balance here is live. A void, always the bar
manager's own call and always with a reason, credits the charge back and it reads Voided rather
than disappearing.
