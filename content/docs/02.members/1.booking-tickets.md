Anybody can book tickets: the what's on listing, the show page and the booking form are public,
and no account is needed. A signed-in member's booking attaches to their account and can use a
pass or a verified access profile; a guest gives a name and an email address instead. Either
way the seats are held online and paid for at the theatre. Signed in, **My NNT** carries a
"Next show on sale" tile that opens the show page.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Nothing is paid online, ever.** Booking holds the seats; the box office takes payment in
person, on the night, on the Students' Union's card reader. The order stub says "Paid online
£0.00, ever" for exactly this reason.
::

## Finding a show

1. Open **What's on** from the public site. Every show on sale is listed as a poster card, with
   its dates and whether it is still bookable. When shows are on at more than one venue, a row
   of venue buttons filters the list.
2. Choose a show to open its page.

![What's on with the venue filter (1), a show's poster card (2) and its book button (3)](/images/docs/members/whats-on.png)

1. **Filter by venue** narrows the list to one venue. It only appears when more than one venue
   has something on.
2. **A show's card** carries the poster, the dates and the state of the run.
3. **Book** opens the show page, or the waiting list when the run is full.

## The show page

The show page carries what somebody decides on: dates, running time, ticket prices and age
guidance at the top, then the description, the interval and latecomer policy, the content
warnings and the list of performances.

![A show page with the dates (1), the ticket prices (2), the book button (3) and the performances (4)](/images/docs/members/show.png)

1. **Dates** span the first and last night still on offer.
2. **Tickets** lists every price on the run, cheapest last, with its condition ("members", for
   instance) beside it.
3. **Book tickets** goes straight to the first night still bookable here.
4. **Performances** lists every night with its own state and its own button: **Pick** for a
   night on sale, **Join the waiting list** for a full one, **Book elsewhere** where the tickets
   are sold by another box office, and nothing at all for a cancelled night.

Content warnings come in three states, and the page says which: somebody has been through the
show and found nothing, nobody has been through it yet, or there is a list. "Nobody has been
through this show yet" means nothing has been checked, not that there is nothing to say.

## Holding the seats

1. Pick a performance. When the run has more than one night on sale, the booking page lists them
   and the chosen one is highlighted; picking another night is a link, so the address always
   names the night.
2. Choose how many of each ticket type. The stub on the right (below the form on a phone) adds
   up what is to pay at the theatre as you go. One online booking holds up to
   {{ PUBLIC_ORDER_SEAT_CAP }} seats, counted across every line; a larger party talks to the box
   office.
3. Not signed in, give your name and the email address the booking should go to. Signed in,
   there is nothing to give: the booking attaches to the account.
4. Press **Reserve your seats**. The screen confirms with a reference and the amount to pay at
   the box office on the night, and **View your booking** opens the booking page. The same
   goes to the email address as a confirmation carrying the QR code.

The stub also says when the hold ends: seats hold until {{ HOLD_RELEASE_MINUTES_BEFORE }}
minutes before curtain, then go back on sale for walk-ups. A reminder is emailed
{{ HOLD_REMINDER_MINUTES_BEFORE }} minutes before that happens.

## Access and companion tickets

A signed-in member with a verified access profile sees a banner at the top of the form saying
how many access and companion tickets are still available to them for this performance, and the
Access and Companion ticket types cannot be set higher than that. Without a verified profile
those types do not appear. Recording the profile is on [Access requirements](/docs/members/access-requirements).

## Using a pass

A signed-in member holding an active pass that covers the performance sees **Your pass** above
the ticket lines, naming the pass and its reference, with **Use my pass**. Redeeming it is a
separate action from choosing tickets: it makes a zero-value booking of its own, with its own
reference and QR code, and nothing is due. A pass admits once per performance. The rest is on
[Passes](/docs/members/passes).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Choose at least one ticket"**: every quantity is still nought.
- **"Tell us the name the booking is under"** or **"Tell us where to send your booking
  reference"**: a guest field is empty. **"That does not look like an email address"** means the
  address has no @ or no domain.
- **"Online orders are capped at N tickets; for a larger party, contact the box office
  directly"**: the order, or one line of it, is over {{ PUBLIC_ORDER_SEAT_CAP }}. The button
  stays disabled until it is under.
- **"Sold out"** with **Join the waiting list**: the house is full. The form does not open;
  [Waiting list](/docs/members/waiting-list) explains what happens next.
- **"Booking is not open"**: the performance is cancelled, the show is not on sale, the online
  booking window has closed ("Tickets are on the door, subject to availability"), or the tickets
  are sold elsewhere, in which case **Book elsewhere** goes to that box office. The banner quotes
  the reason.
- **"Online booking cannot hold seats this close to curtain"**: the hold would already have
  released. Come to the theatre; tickets may still be on the door.
- **"This performance no longer has room for that order"**: the seats went while the form was
  open. Try fewer, or join the waiting list.
- **"Only N companion tickets may be held for this performance"**: the access entitlement is
  used up on an earlier booking.

## What happens next

The booking is written with its tickets at the price shown, status unpaid, and no money moves.
The confirmation email carries the reference, the amount due, and the QR code the door reads.
The same QR code stays valid through any change to the booking. On the night, the desk finds
the booking by reference, name or scan and takes the payment; see [The desk](/docs/box-office/the-desk).
An unpaid booking whose hold passes is released, and its seats go to the waiting list first.
