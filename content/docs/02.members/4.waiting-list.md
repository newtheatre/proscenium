A full performance offers a waiting list instead of a booking form. Anybody can join: a
signed-in member joins as themselves, a guest gives a name and an email address. When seats free
up (a hold lapses, a booking is cancelled, a refund, or the box office raises the capacity) the
list is offered in the order people joined, by email, one entry at a time. There is no picture
of these pages here: each one is opened from a link minted for one entry.

## Joining the list

1. On the show page, press **Join the waiting list** beside the full performance, or press it
   on the booking page's "Sold out" banner.
2. Set the **Party size**, up to 10 including yourself. This is how many seats you will be
   offered together, so ask for what you need: an offer is for the whole party.
3. Not signed in, give your **Name** and **Email address**.
4. Press **Join the waiting list**. "You're on the list" confirms it, and a confirmation email
   follows carrying a link to your entry and a link to leave.

Joining does not depend on the house being full at that moment: the only refusal is a duplicate
email address on the same performance.

## Claiming an offer

When enough seats free up for your party, an offer email arrives with a link to your entry.

1. Open the link. "A seat is free" says how long the offer is held for you: an offer stands for
   {{ WAITING_LIST_OFFER_WINDOW_MINUTES }} minutes, or until the performance starts if that is
   sooner.
2. Choose ticket types to make up exactly your party size; the button stays disabled until the
   count matches ("2 of 2 chosen").
3. Press **Claim these seats**. "Seats claimed" gives the reference and the amount to pay at the
   box office on the night, and **View your booking** opens the booking page.

An offer not claimed in time lapses and passes to the next person. The entry page then says so:
"This offer lapsed and passed to the next person on the list. Contact the box office if you
still want to attend."

## Leaving the list

Every email carries a leave link, and the entry page has **Leave the waiting list** on it. Both
are buttons, not automatic: opening the link does nothing until it is pressed, so an email
scanner cannot remove anybody. "You have left the waiting list" confirms it. Rejoining later
puts you at the back, in the order you join.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A name and an email address are required to join as a guest"**: one of the guest fields is
  empty.
- **"This email address is already on the waiting list for this performance"**: one entry per
  address per performance. The earlier entry stands.
- **"You're on the list, but we could not email you"**: the entry was written but the
  confirmation did not go out. The place is held in order; ask the box office to check the
  entry before the performance, since there is no link to it otherwise.
- **"This offer is no longer open. Contact the box office if you still want to attend."**: the
  offer lapsed or was claimed already.
- **"This performance no longer has room for that party. Contact the box office directly."**:
  the seats went between the offer and the claim. The entry is not lost; ask the desk.
- **"That link has already been used or is no longer valid."**: the leave link was forged,
  replaced or the entry has been purged after the performance. Nothing to do.
- **"These seats have already been claimed"** or **"You already left this waiting list"**: the
  entry is settled.

## What happens next

An entry is recorded with its party size and its place in the order. Each offer and each lapse
is recorded against it. A claim makes an ordinary unpaid booking through the same path as a
fresh booking, capacity-checked, so it is paid for at the desk on the night like any other and
appears on [Your booking](/docs/members/your-booking). Entries are purged after the performance's
night.
