This page is for whoever answers "did we send that, and can somebody turn it off": the
Communications Officer, and anybody fielding a question about a message a member did or did not
get. Every message the theatre sends has a type, and the type is the first thing the
[send log](/docs/communications/send-log) shows against a line. Find the type here and the row
says what the message says, how it arrives, whether a member can switch it off, and whether it
waits for a digest.

Nothing outside this list can be sent at all, so a message a member says they had that is not
here did not come from us.

## If something goes wrong

- **A member says they never got something on this list**: find the type and their name on the
  [send log](/docs/communications/send-log). The outcome there says whether it was sent, held
  back by their own preference, or never had an address to go to.
- **A member asks to stop something whose Switch off column says No**: it cannot be stopped. It
  answers something they did, or something they have to know before a night, and there is no
  preference row for it to turn off.
- **A member has the topic switched off and wants one message anyway**: send it from
  [Announce](/docs/communications/announce) as a safety notice, which no preference silences.
  Use that for something urgent and not to get around a choice they made.

## How to read the columns

- **Says**: the subject line, with the parts that vary in angle brackets.
- **Channel**: **Email**, **Email and in-app**, or both. An in-app entry is written before the
  email is judged, so it exists even when the email was suppressed or skipped; only an
  anonymised account gets neither.
- **Switch off**: the topic a member can turn off on their Notifications screen, or **No** for a
  transactional message, which answers something the person did or something they need to know
  and is not asked about. Transactional messages have no preference row at all, so nothing can
  suppress one.
- **Digest**: a message with a topic normally joins the next digest for that topic instead of
  being emailed on its own: one email per person per topic once the topic's window has passed
  (digests go out every ten minutes; the windows are {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_BOOKINGS_MINUTES }},
  {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_SHIFTS_MINUTES }}, {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_TRAINING_MINUTES }},
  {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_ROOMS_MINUTES }} and {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_ANNOUNCEMENTS_MINUTES }}
  minutes for bookings, shifts, training, rooms and announcements). A message sent once by a
  run on a clock, or one carrying a file, is emailed on its own instead and says so. A
  transactional message never joins one.
- **From**: which of the theatre's addresses the email comes from.

## Ticketing

Sent from NNT Box Office (`boxoffice@newtheatre.org.uk`). All transactional, email only, and
every one may reach an unverified address: a guest who booked with an address has never
verified it, and this is what they gave it for.

| Type | Says | Channel | Switch off | Digest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reservation.hold-expiring` | Your hold on <show> releases soon | Email | No | No |
| `reservation.confirmed` | Your booking for <show> | Email | No | No |
| `reservation.walk-up-paid` | Your tickets for <show> (a walk-up paid at the bar, with the QR) | Email | No | No |
| `reservation.cancelled` | Your booking for <show> is cancelled | Email | No | No |
| `pass.issued` | Your <pass type> | Email | No | No |
| `waiting-list.joined` | You are on the waiting list for <show> | Email | No | No |
| `waiting-list.offered` | Seats have come free for <show>, or A seat has come free for <show> | Email | No | No |

The hold reminder goes by itself to anybody whose unpaid hold is about to release, and it is the
only reminder a booker gets: nothing is sent before a performance to a booking that is paid for.
Cancelling a performance tells the people working it, not its ticket holders; tell them from
[Announce](/docs/communications/announce).

## Communications

What the [announce](/docs/communications/announce) screen sends, and the digests that
carry everything above which was held.

| Type | Says | Channel | Switch off | Digest | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `admin.announcement` | The officer's own subject | Email and in-app | Committee announcements | Announcements | The New Theatre |
| `admin.safety-notice` | The officer's own subject | Email and in-app | No | No | The New Theatre |
| `admin.ticket-holders` | The officer's own subject, to ticket holders | Email and in-app | Bookings | Bookings | NNT Box Office |
| `admin.ticket-holders.safety-notice` | The officer's own subject, to ticket holders | Email and in-app | No | No | NNT Box Office |
| `digest.bookings` | N booking updates | Email | No | Is the digest | NNT Box Office |
| `digest.shifts` | N shift updates | Email | No | Is the digest | The New Theatre |
| `digest.training` | N training updates | Email | No | Is the digest | NNT Training |
| `digest.rooms` | N room booking updates | Email | No | Is the digest | NNT Room Bookings |
| `digest.announcements` | N announcements | Email | No | Is the digest | The New Theatre |

A digest lists every held message individually, the subject of each in bold and its body
beneath, so five changes in an hour is one email with five entries. The one message under the
**Bookings** topic is a plain announcement to ticket holders, and `digest.bookings` carries
nothing else at present. Both ticket-holder types may reach an address nobody has verified, as the
ticketing messages do: a guest who booked is reached at the address they booked with.

holiday copy that keeps failing, a drift report and a retention warning are not things a
preference may silence.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `bank-holidays.sync-failed` | The bank holiday list has not updated from gov.uk since <time> (to the IT Manager, once a failing weekly copy has lasted six days) | Email and in-app | No | No |
| `docs.drift-reported` | Documentation flagged: <path> (to the IT Manager, with the page link) | Email | No | No |
| `retention.warning.window` | Your account has been quiet for a while | Email | No | No |
| `retention.warning.final` | Your account will be anonymised soon | Email | No | No |
| `retention.digest` | What retention would close next, monthly (to the IT Manager) | Email and in-app | No | No |
