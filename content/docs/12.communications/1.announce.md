Announce is where an officer writes one message to many people without ever seeing, pasting or
disclosing a recipient list. It is at **Manage, Communications, Announce**, and at present the
IT Manager is the only person who opens it. The audience is worked out at the moment you send,
so what the preview counted is what is reached, and each person receives their own copy: no
header or body ever names anybody else.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**An announcement is not an emergency channel.** A plain announcement carries the Committee
announcements topic: a member who has switched that topic's email off is not emailed (they
still get the in-app entry), and everyone else receives it inside the next announcements digest
rather than the moment you press Send. A message to ticket holders works the same way under the
Bookings topic instead. Tick **This is a safety notice** only when the message must reach
everyone regardless, and immediately.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Give it a subject"**, **"Say what the announcement is about"**, **"Say which session
  you mean"**, **"Say which show you mean"**, **"Say which performance you mean"**: a field the
  server needs is empty. The Preview button stays disabled until the audience, the subject and
  the message are all filled, so you will normally only see these if the page and the server
  disagree.
- **"You do not have permission to do that"**: announcing is not yours to do. Roles lapse on
  31 July; ask the IT Manager.
- **The Send button has vanished**: something changed since the preview. Press Preview again.
- **The count is lower than you expected**: the audience is live. A membership past its
  grace, a role grant that has expired, a shift that was declined, a session sign-up that
  was cancelled, or a booking that was cancelled, expired unpaid or refunded in full is not
  there to be reached, and an anonymised account never is.
- **A performance was cancelled and the audience has not heard**: cancelling a performance
  tells the people working it, not the people booked for it. Send an announcement to
  **Ticket holders for a performance**, choosing the cancelled performance, and tick **This is
  a safety notice** so it goes at once.

## Choosing the audience

![The composer with the audience (1), the count it resolves to (2), the subject (3), the message (4), the safety notice tick (5) and the preview button (6)](/images/docs/communications/announce.png)

1. Pick an **Audience**. There are six, and each is counted afresh when you
   preview and again when you send:
   - **All current members**: everyone whose membership has started and has not expired by
     more than {{ MEMBERSHIP_GRACE_DAYS }} days, the same grace the desk allows.
   - **Holders of a role**: a second field asks which role, listed by title (Theatre Manager,
     Front of House Manager, and the rest). Anyone with an unexpired grant of it, so a lapsed
     committee member is not reached.
   - **Tonight's rota**: everyone holding a claimed or confirmed shift on a performance in
     the current show night, which runs 04:00 to 04:00. An open or declined shift names
     nobody.
   - **A session's sign-ups**: a second field searches training sessions by what they teach
     or their date; choose one from the list. Everyone signed up who has not cancelled.
   - **Ticket holders for a performance**: a **Show** field searches shows by title; choose
     one, then choose the night from **Performance**, which lists what is left of the run from
     tonight on, with its venue, a cancelled night marked as cancelled. Everyone whose booking
     for that night is held, collected or admitted at the door, with at least one ticket not
     refunded.
   - **Ticket holders for a show**: the same **Show** field, and everyone with such a booking
     for any night of the run still to come.

   Only shows that are published and still have a night to come are offered. A night that has
   already happened has no audience here, so a show from a past season cannot be reached.

   Changing the **Audience** clears the show and the night, so choose them again if you come
   back. A ticket holder who booked as a guest, with an address and no account of their own, is
   reached at the address they booked with. Somebody with several bookings is one person in the
   count and gets one copy.
2. Under the audience, the screen says how many people it comes to: "412 people will get this",
   or "Nobody is in this audience". The count arrives as soon as the audience is complete, before
   a word of the message is written, and it is read again every time the audience changes.
3. Anonymised accounts are excluded from every audience at the query itself, and an address
   that can never be delivered to (a placeholder, or a reserved domain) is dropped at send
   time and logged. Nothing you choose can reach either.

## Writing and previewing

1. Give the message a **Subject** (up to 150 characters) and a **Message** (up to 10,000). Blank
   lines separate paragraphs in the email; there is no formatting beyond that, and anything
   that looks like HTML is shown as typed.
2. Tick **This is a safety notice** if the message must arrive whatever the recipient's
   preferences say. The screen describes this as reaching the audience "the same as a ticket
   or a refund does".
3. Press **Preview**. It is disabled until the subject, the message and the audience are all
   complete. The preview card shows the recipient count, "resolved just now from live data",
   and the message rendered as a recipient will read it, addressed to you by name.
4. Change anything, the audience included, and the preview and the Send button disappear:
   preview again. A stale count naming yesterday's audience is worse than none.

## Sending

1. Press **Send to N recipients**. The button appears only once a preview stands.
2. The screen says **"Sent to N recipients"**, or **"Queued for N recipients"** where the message
   is waiting for the next digest, and keeps the subject and the message exactly as
   they went out, so you can read back what was sent.
3. Press **Write another announcement** to clear the form for the next one. Nothing empties itself
   under you.

The count is the size of the resolved audience, not the number of emails delivered. A member
with announcement email off, or an address that could not be sent to, is inside the count and
recorded in the send log with the outcome that explains it.

## What happens next

Each recipient is sent to individually through the notification centre, and every outcome is
its own line in the send log. What arrives depends on which type you sent:

- A **plain announcement** sits under the Committee announcements topic. Each recipient gets an
  in-app entry at once. The email waits and goes out with the next announcements digest, from
  The New Theatre (`hello@newtheatre.org.uk`), once
  {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_ANNOUNCEMENTS_MINUTES }} minutes have passed since that
  person's earliest unsent announcement; digests go out every ten minutes. The email's subject
  is the count ("1 announcement", "3 announcements"), and your subject heads the entry inside
  it. A member with announcement email switched off gets no email, and the send log reads
  **Held back by a preference** for them. The email itself appears in the log as the digest
  rather than as your announcement.
- A **safety notice** answers something urgent: no preference applies to it, it never waits for
  a digest, and it is emailed at once under your own subject line, from The New Theatre
  (`hello@newtheatre.org.uk`), with an in-app entry beside it.
- A message to **ticket holders** is about their booking rather than a committee
  announcement, so it comes from NNT Box Office (`boxoffice@newtheatre.org.uk`). A plain one
  sits under the Bookings topic: a booker with bookings email switched off is not emailed, and
  everyone else receives it in the next bookings digest, once
  {{ NOTIFICATION_DIGEST_WINDOW_BOOKINGS_MINUTES }} minutes have passed. Ticked as a safety
  notice, it is emailed at once to every ticket holder whatever their preference.

The confirmation says which of the two happened: **Queued for N recipients** for a plain
announcement, so nothing is expected in the send log until the digest goes out, and **Sent to N
recipients** for a safety notice, which is in the log straight away.

The audit trail records who sent it, the audience definition (the kind, and the role or the
session where there was one), the recipient count and whether it was a safety notice. It does
not record the subject or the body, which are your own words: the send log's subject column is
where "what did this actually say" is answered, one line per recipient.
