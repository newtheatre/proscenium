**My room bookings**, under **My NNT**, lists every room booking a signed-in member holds and what
became of the ones they held. Cancelling from here frees the slot straight away. The same screen
carries any request for a room the theatre does not manage, the member's own no-show standing,
and the link for subscribing to their bookings from a calendar app.

![My room bookings on a phone, with the coming up and past switch (1), the list (2) and the calendar feed card (3)](/images/docs/members/room-bookings.png)

1. **Coming up** and **Past** switch the list.
2. **The list**: room, state, span, what it is for, and for a series which week of how many.
3. **Your bookings in your own calendar** creates a private feed link.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That is not your booking"**: the booking belongs to somebody else, or no longer exists.
- **"That booking is already cancelled"** (or turned down, or moved): it was decided before you
  pressed the button. The list refreshes.
- **"That booking is part of a series, so say whether you mean this one or all of them"**: the
  choice was not made.
- **"That booking has already been decided"**: somebody else acted on it at the same moment.
- **"That booking is already confirmed, so it cannot be changed"** (or turned down, or
  cancelled): an officer answered the request before you saved. A confirmed booking can still
  be cancelled and booked again.
- **"Somebody already holds that slot, so your request is as it was"**: the room or time you
  moved to is taken. Your request still holds the slot it had; choose another.
- **"Changing the whole series at once is not possible yet. Change the weeks one at a time, or
  cancel the series and ask again."**: choose **Just this one** and change each week in turn.
- A change can also meet any refusal the booking form gives, such as a slot in the past or a
  room that is closed then; see [Book a room](/docs/my-nnt/book-a-room).
- **"Nothing booked yet"**: the list is empty. **Find a free slot** opens the calendar.
- **"Your bookings could not be read."**: the list did not arrive, which is not the same as
  holding nothing. Press **Try again**.

## Reading the list

Each booking carries one of these states:

- **Confirmed**: held. **Add to calendar** downloads it as a calendar file.
- **Waiting on a decision**: a request, with its slot held, that an officer has not yet decided.
  **Change** alters it while it waits.
- **Cancelled**: by you, or by an officer.
- **Turned down**: an officer declined the request; the reason is shown under it.
- **Given to another booking**: a booking with a higher priority tier took the slot. The
  reason is shown, and "A replacement is held for you below" when the officer found one.
- **Moved to a room we do not manage**: an officer turned the request into one for a room the
  theatre does not manage; it now appears under **Rooms we do not manage** on the same screen.

## Changing a request

A request waiting on a decision is still yours to change; once an officer has answered it, it
is not.

1. Press **Change** beside a booking that is waiting on a decision.
2. For a week of a series, say **Just this one**. **The whole series** is offered and not yet
   possible: the screen says so and **Save the change** stays disabled.
3. Change the room, what it is for, the day, the times, how many people or why it is worth an
   exception. The form opens with what you asked for last time.
4. Press **Save the change**, or **Leave it as it is**.

Your request is checked again exactly as a new one would be, and it keeps its slot until the
change lands. Moving it to another room or another day starts the wait for a decision again,
as if you had asked today; changing anything else leaves it where it was.

## Cancelling a booking

1. Press **Cancel** beside a confirmed booking or one waiting on a decision.
2. The screen asks "Cancel this booking?", naming the room and the span, and warns that the slot
   frees straight away and cannot be got back.
3. For a booking in a series, say **Just this one** or **The whole series**. Neither is
   preselected: the button stays disabled until you choose. The whole series means every date
   still standing; ones already cancelled or turned down are left alone.
4. Press **Cancel the booking** (or **Cancel the whole series**), or **Keep it**.

## Rooms we do not manage

A request for a room the theatre does not manage (see [Book a room not listed here](/docs/my-nnt/book-a-room-not-listed-here))
is listed here separately: nothing is held until somebody else answers. Its states are
"Not yet requested", "Requested, awaiting a room", "Confirmed", "Turned down", "Cancelled" and
"Moved to one of our rooms". **Withdraw the request** asks "Withdraw this request?": if the Theatre Manager
has already put the request in, they are told to withdraw it by hand. Our booking for it
stands until a person cancels it with them.

## Your standing

Bookings that were not turned up to count against you, over a rolling window. At
{{ ROOM_NO_SHOW_RECORD_AT }} the screen says "N bookings were not turned up to" and how many more
it takes; at {{ ROOM_NO_SHOW_PREAPPROVAL_AT }} it says "Every booking you make is checked by a
person first", and every booking from then on goes through the request queue whatever the
rules say. The booking form warns of this before anything is typed. The count clears at the
committee year end.

## Your bookings in your own calendar

1. Press **Create my calendar link**. The link appears once, in a field, with **Copy the link**
   beside it.
2. Copy it now and subscribe to it from your calendar app. It is yours alone; do not share it.
3. To revoke it, press **Replace my calendar link**: a new link is made and the old one stops
   working straight away. The screen cannot show an existing link again, only replace it.

The feed carries bookings up to {{ ROOM_FEED_WEEKS }} weeks ahead.

## What happens next

A cancellation frees the slot at once, is recorded in the audit trail, and sends a
confirmation; a series cancelled whole sends one message naming every date. A change to a
request is recorded in the audit trail as "Room request changed", naming what moved but never
your own words, and sends nothing: the officers see the request as it now stands. Decisions on
requests, bumps and closures each arrive by email under the one "room bookings" topic, and a
reminder goes out the day before each confirmed booking with the calendar file attached.
