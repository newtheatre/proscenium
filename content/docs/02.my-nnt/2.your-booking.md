Every booking has one page, opened from the link in the confirmation email or from **View your
booking** straight after booking. It is public in the sense that no sign-in is needed: the link
in the email is the key, and whoever holds it holds the booking. The page shows the live state
of the booking, its QR code, and, while the booking is unpaid, the buttons to change it. There
is no picture of this page here: every booking has its own address and nobody else's.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
The QR code never changes. It is a key to the booking, not a copy of it: paid, unpaid,
exchanged or cancelled is whatever the door reads when the code is scanned, so a code saved
on night one still works on night three.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"That link isn't valid"**: the link was mistyped, or the cookie it set has gone. Use the
  resend form underneath.
- **"This booking can no longer be changed here"** (or cancelled, or exchanged): it is no longer
  unpaid. Everything after payment is a box office conversation.
- **"A booking must keep at least one ticket. Cancel it instead if none are wanted."**: every
  quantity was set to 0.
- **"This performance no longer has room for that change"**: the extra seats went while the
  form was open. The booking is unchanged.
- **"Online orders are capped at N tickets"**: the new total is over {{ PUBLIC_ORDER_SEAT_CAP }}.
- **"This performance has already started, so it can no longer be cancelled online"**: contact
  the box office.
- **"A booking with an access or companion ticket cannot be exchanged online"**: the
  entitlement is per performance, so the box office moves it by hand.
- **"This performance does not offer one of the ticket types on this booking"**: a type on this
  booking is not sold on the other night. The box office can rebuild it.
- **"That performance no longer has room for this booking"**: the other night filled up. Try
  another, or contact the box office.

## Reading the page

While the booking is unpaid or paid, **Before you come** repeats what the booking form said
before booking: the show's age guidance and each content warning with its level, or that it has
none or has not been assessed yet, read from the same rows as the show page, with a link to the
show page for what each warning means. The confirmation email carries the same lines. The link
is left out once the show is no longer on the site.

The page names the show, the night and the reference, then a status badge with a line under it:

- **Unpaid**: "£X due at the box office on the night."
- **Paid**: "Collected at the box office."
- **Admitted**: "Already checked in at the door."
- **Lapsed**: "This hold was released. Contact the box office if you still want to attend."
- **Exchanged**: names the performance it was exchanged for.
- **Cancelled**: "by the booker" or "by the box office".
- **No-show**: "Recorded as not attended."

Under the badge are the ticket lines and the QR code. "Save this image to keep the code, or
show this page at the door."

## Changing the tickets

While the booking is unpaid:

1. Press **Change tickets**. Each ticket type on sale for the performance appears with its price
   and the quantity currently held.
2. Set the quantities you want. They are totals, not additions: setting Standard to 3 leaves the
   booking holding three Standard tickets. No field goes above the online order cap of
   {{ PUBLIC_ORDER_SEAT_CAP }}, which is what the write path allows.
3. Press **Save changes**, or **Back** to leave the booking as it is.

A booking must keep at least one ticket. Adding seats runs the same checks a fresh booking
would (the house still has room, the online window is still open); giving seats back never does.

## Exchanging for another night

1. Press **Exchange for another night**. Every other performance of the same show is listed by
   its date and time ("Wed 14 Oct, 19:30"), with the venue and the state under it; a full one or
   one no longer on sale is greyed out.
2. Choose a night and press **Exchange booking**, or **Back** to leave the booking as it is. The
   tickets move as they are, priced at that performance's prices, which may differ.

Only another performance of the same show can be exchanged into. A different show is a cancel
and a new booking.

## Cancelling

1. Press **Cancel booking**. A confirmation opens, headed "Cancel this booking": "Nothing has
   been charged, so nothing needs refunding. The seats go back on sale straight away."
2. Press **Cancel this booking**, or **Back** to keep it.

Cancelling is the only thing on this page that undoes something. The way out of the change and
exchange forms is **Back**, so nothing labelled Cancel means "stop filling this in".

Cancelling online stops at curtain-up. After that, and after the booking has been paid, the
page offers nothing to press: "You have paid for this booking. Refunds are handled in person
at the box office; bring your reference."

## Getting the link back

Without the email, the page (at `/qr`) is headed "Open your booking from your email": give the
booking reference and the email address the booking was made with, press **Resend it**, and a
fresh copy with the same QR code is sent. The answer is the same whether or not the pair matched, so the page can never be
used to find out whether an address has a booking. A resend goes out only while the booking is
unpaid: the email says UNPAID and quotes what is due. Asking again and again for the same
address is turned down for a short while.

## What happens next

A change rewrites the ticket lines and the amount due, and the same QR code carries on. A
cancellation records that the booker cancelled, frees the seats at once and offers them to the
waiting list. An exchange marks this booking exchanged, pointing at the new one. The desk sees
the current state the moment it opens the booking.
