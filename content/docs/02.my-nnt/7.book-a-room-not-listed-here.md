When nothing of ours suits, a signed-in member with a current membership can ask for a room the
theatre does not manage. **Book a room not listed here** sits at the top of the calendar under
**Rooms**. It is a request, not a booking: the Theatre Manager fills in the Students'
Union's form, the SU answers in its own time, and nothing is held for anybody in the meantime.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="warning"}
**Nothing here is held for you.** You may say which room you would like, but the SU may give us
a different one. Ask early: the request needs {{ EXTERNAL_REQUEST_NOTICE_WORKING_DAYS }} working
days, and weekends and bank holidays do not count. The form names the earliest day you can ask
for under **Day**, and the calendar there starts from it.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Asking for a room needs a current membership."**: said in place of the form when your
  membership has run out, and refused outright if it runs out while the form is open. **Tell us
  about your membership** opens the membership page.
- **"That slot has already happened"**: the day or time is in the past.
- **"This needs N working days, because a person fills in a form and waits for an answer.
  Weekends and bank holidays do not count. The earliest day you can ask for is ..."**: too
  little notice, naming the first day that has enough. There is no exception path for a room we
  do not manage; pick that day or a later one.
- **"We cannot count the notice for that date: bank holidays are only known up to ..."**: the
  bank holidays are copied from gov.uk, which publishes about two years ahead, and the list does
  not reach that far yet. Until it does the request is refused rather than judged; ask for a
  nearer date, or tell the IT Manager if the date is less than a year away.
- **"That room is not one we have listed"**: the preferred room was removed while the form was
  open. Leave the preference empty.
- **"This room is no good for that: ..."** or **"This room may not suit that: ..."**: a warning
  on the request, quoting the officer's note, never a refusal. The request stands with the
  preference in it.

## Asking for a room

![The form for a room not listed here on a phone, with what the room is for (1), a name for it (2), the day (3) and the start time (4)](/images/docs/members/other-rooms.png)

1. **What the room is for** is a row of buttons, one per purpose; press one, and it is marked
   with a tick. It decides whether a room the SU offers will suit.
2. **A name for it** is optional, shown to the Theatre Manager and written on the SU's form.
   Left empty, the request is called by what the room is for.
3. **Day**, with the earliest day you can ask for named under it: "The earliest day you can ask
   for is Thursday 1 October", say. The calendar does not offer an earlier one.
4. **From** and **Until**, on the same 24-hour time field as every other screen. The form checks
   what you typed before it sends anything, and says under a field what is wrong with it.

Then:

1. **How many people** is optional.
2. **A room you would like** is optional and a preference, not a promise. Choose a purpose
   first: where an officer has noted that a room is no good for that purpose, or may not suit
   it, the note appears under the picker as you choose.
3. **Anything else whoever manages it should know** is optional.
4. Press **Ask for it**. "Asked for" confirms it, adding "The Theatre Manager fills in the form
   for it next", and **My bookings** opens with the request under **Rooms we do not manage**.

## What happens next

The request is recorded as "Not yet requested" and the member is told by email; the Theatre
Manager and every other approver is told there is a request to put in. Once it has been put to
the SU it reads "Requested, awaiting a room"; a room being assigned makes it "Confirmed",
naming the room, and a refusal makes it "Turned down" with the reason. Withdrawing after it has
been put in tells the Theatre Manager to withdraw it with the SU by hand. What the officer does
is on [Other rooms](/docs/spaces/other-rooms) in the Spaces section.
