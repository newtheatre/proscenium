A full performance offers a waiting list instead of a booking form. Anybody can join: a
signed-in member joins as themselves, a guest gives a name and an email address. When seats free
up while online booking is open (a booking is cancelled, a refund, an offer lapses, or the box
office raises the capacity) the list is offered in the order people joined, by email, one entry
at a time. Online booking stops when unpaid bookings are released,
{{ HOLD_RELEASE_MINUTES_BEFORE }} minutes before curtain, or earlier where the performance has
its own booking window; seats that free after that, including every unpaid booking released at
that moment, are sold on the door. There is no picture of these pages here: each one is opened
from a link made for one entry and nobody else's.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Tell us the name to hold the place under"** or **"Tell us where to send the offer"**: a
  guest field is empty. **"That does not look like an email address"** means the address has no
  @ or no domain.
- **"This offer is for 2 seats; the request asked for 1."**: a claim takes the whole party.
- **"This email address is already on the waiting list for this performance"**: one entry per
  address per performance. The earlier entry stands.
- **"Online booking has closed"**, or **"Online booking closed at ... Tickets are on the door,
  subject to availability."**: nothing more is offered or claimed online for this performance,
  so the list takes no more names and a standing offer can no longer be claimed. Come to the
  box office.
- **"You're on the list, but we could not email you"**: the entry was written but the
  confirmation did not go out. The place is held in order; ask the box office to check the
  entry before the performance, since there is no link to it otherwise.
- **"This offer is no longer open. Contact the box office if you still want to attend."**: the
  offer lapsed or was claimed already.
- **"This performance no longer has room for that party. Contact the box office directly."**:
  the seats went between the offer and the claim. The entry is not lost; ask the desk.
- **"That link has already been used or is no longer valid."**: the leave link was not the one
  we sent, or the entry has gone with the performance. Nothing to do.
- **"These seats have already been claimed"** or **"You already left this waiting list"**: the
  entry is settled.

## Joining the list

1. On the show page, press **Join the waiting list** beside the full performance, or press it
   on the booking page's "Sold out" banner.
2. Check the line under the heading: it names the show, the night and the venue the list is for,
   so a link opened days later is unambiguous.
3. Set the **Party size**, up to 10 including yourself. This is how many seats you will be
   offered together, so ask for what you need: an offer is for the whole party.
4. Not signed in, give your **Name** and **Email address**. Each field answers on itself if it
   is empty or malformed, before anything is sent.
5. Press **Join the waiting list**. "You're on the list" confirms it, and a confirmation email
   follows carrying a link to your entry and a link to leave.

Joining does not depend on the house being full at that moment. The refusals are a duplicate
email address on the same performance, and a performance whose online booking has already
closed: nothing would ever be offered from that list, so the page says "Online booking has
closed" instead of showing the form.

## Claiming an offer

When enough seats free up for your party, an offer email arrives with a link to your entry.

1. Open the link. "A seat is free" says until when you have first refusal: an offer stands for
   {{ WAITING_LIST_OFFER_WINDOW_MINUTES }} minutes, or until online booking stops if that is
   sooner. An offer is first refusal, not a hold: nothing is kept for you until you claim.
2. Choose ticket types to make up exactly your party size. The page says so before you start
   ("An offer covers the whole party, so choose all 2 tickets to claim it"), and a claim for a
   different number is refused naming both figures. Give seats back afterwards from
   [Your booking](/docs/my-nnt/your-booking) if the party shrinks.
3. Press **Claim these seats**. The booking page opens, headed "Booking made", with the
   reference, the amount to pay at the box office on the night and the QR code.

An offer not claimed in time lapses and passes to the next person, or to the door once online
booking has stopped. The entry page then says so: "This offer lapsed and passed to the next
person on the list, or to the door. Contact the box office if you still want to attend."

## Leaving the list

Every email carries a leave link, and the entry page has **Leave the waiting list** on it. Both
are buttons, not automatic: opening the link does nothing until it is pressed, so an email
scanner cannot remove anybody. On the entry page it asks first, and says what follows: with an
offer standing, your first refusal on the seat passes to the next person on the list, or to the
door, and the link stops working;
otherwise the place goes and the emails stop. **Back** leaves the entry alone. "You have left the
waiting list" confirms it. Rejoining later puts you at the back, in the order you join.

## What happens next

An entry is recorded with its party size and its place in the order. Each offer and each lapse
is recorded against it. A claim makes an ordinary unpaid booking through the same path as a
fresh booking, capacity-checked, so it is paid for at the desk on the night like any other and
appears on [Your booking](/docs/my-nnt/your-booking). An entry is deleted once the
performance's night has passed.
