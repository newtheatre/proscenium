**Rota**, under **My NNT**, is where a signed-in member takes on a show night. It lists the
shifts they already hold, the open shifts they can take, and a card for each role they cannot
take yet. A shift is one role on one performance: duty manager, door or bar. Qualifying is not a
role anybody grants; it is read live from the member's training records each time the page
loads, and the committee names which module unlocks which role.

![Rota on a phone, with the shifts held (1), the week chips (2), the shifts you can take (3) and a role you could take (4)](/images/docs/members/rota.png)

1. **What you hold**: each shift with its role, venue, night, show and state (Claimed,
   Confirmed or Declined), and a button to release or dismiss it.
2. **Coming up**, **This week**, **Next week** and **Later** choose which weeks are shown.
   A week runs from Monday's show night to Sunday's, and a night runs until 04:00, so a shift
   starting at 00:30 on a Monday is in the week of the Sunday night it belongs to.
3. **Shifts you can take**: only roles you hold the training for, grouped by night, each with
   a **Claim** button. A performance you already work is left out, since nobody holds two
   shifts on one performance.
4. **Roles you could take**: one card for each role you cannot take yet, saying how many of its
   shifts are open and which module opens it, with the one thing to do about that module.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You do not currently qualify for a door shift"** (or duty manager, or bar): the list was
  stale, or a record expired between loading the page and pressing the button. The claim
  re-checks at the moment of writing.
- **"That shift has already been taken"**: somebody else claimed it first.
- **"You already hold a shift on this performance"**: one shift per person per night.
- **"That show night has already begun, so the shift can no longer be released"**: tell the
  duty manager in person. A refusal to a release appears inside the box that asked, and the
  shift stays yours.
- **"Nobody holds this shift, so there is nothing to release"**, **"This shift was declined,
  not held, so there is nothing to release"**, **"This shift has been cancelled"**: the shift
  changed under you. The list refreshes.
- **"This shift has been reassigned since you opened this page"**: an officer moved it.
- **"That is not your shift to release"**: the shift belongs to somebody else.
- **"Nothing you can take is open."**: no performance in the chosen weeks has an open shift in
  a role you hold the training for, or you already work the ones that do.
- **"The shifts you hold could not be read."**, **"The shifts you can take could not be
  read."**: the list did not arrive, which is not the same as there being none. Press **Try
  again**.

## Claiming a shift

1. Find it under **Shifts you can take**, choosing the week if it is not coming up soon.
2. Press **Claim**. One of two things happens, and the message says which: "Shift confirmed"
   means it is yours; "Claim sent for approval" means the FOH officer will confirm or decline
   it and you will be told either way. Which one depends on the committee's auto-confirm
   setting.
3. The shift moves to **What you hold**.

A member holds at most one shift on a performance. Two people claiming the same shift at the
same moment resolve to one winner; the other is told it has already been taken.

## A role you could take

Each card names the module that opens the role, as a link to its catalogue page, and offers
the one thing to do about it:

- **Sign up**, or **Join the waiting list** when it is full, for the soonest session teaching
  it that you can join. Pressing it signs you up there and then.
- **You have a place**, or **On the waiting list, number n**, once you have signed up.
- **Ask for it** only when no session teaching it is open; **Asked for** once you have. Asking
  tells the department there is demand, and you are emailed when a session opens.

A record that is expiring still counts as held, so a role does not lock before the record's
date. "Not open for claiming yet: ask …, the Front of House Manager" means nobody can claim that
role yet: the module that unlocks it has not been named, or has not been published in the
catalogue. The line names whoever holds the Front of House Manager role, and they can open it.

## Releasing a shift

1. Press **Release the shift** beside a claimed or confirmed shift, or a bar opening slot.
2. A box asks first, saying the shift goes back on the open list for anyone who qualifies and
   that having it back means claiming it again. Press **Release the shift** in the box to go
   ahead, or **Back** to keep it. Nothing is released until you do.
3. "Released" confirms it, and the shift is back on the open list.
4. A shift declined by the officer stays in **What you hold** marked Declined until you press
   **Dismiss**, which clears it.

Releasing stops once the show night has begun. A duty manager shift released at any time, or
any shift released inside {{ SHIFT_RELEASE_NOTICE_HOURS }} hours of the performance, tells the
rota officers at once rather than waiting for their digest.

## What happens next

A claim writes the shift as Claimed or Confirmed and is audited. Once confirmed, the shift is
what gives the member authority on the night: the door, the till and the duty manager's screens
open for whoever holds tonight's confirmed shift, and for nobody else without a standing role.
A reminder goes out the day before. A release puts the shift back on the open list and, where
it is short notice, tells the officers; an officer's approval, decline or reassignment arrives
by email.
