Any signed-in member with a current membership can book one of the theatre's rooms. **Book a
room** under **My NNT** is the calendar; a free slot on it opens the booking form. A booking
inside the rules is held the moment it is made; one outside them becomes a request that the
Theatre Manager decides on, with the slot held while they do. Whoever looks after the rooms (the
Theatre Manager and the IT Manager) books without a membership of their own, since they book for
productions rather than for themselves.

Under the heading, one line gives the rules for the room chosen (or the house's, before one is)
and how many of your cap you already hold, for example "Bookings run 30 minutes to 4 hours, made
48 hours to 12 weeks ahead. You hold 3 of your 10." Waiting requests count towards the cap.

Without a current membership the calendar still opens, with "Booking a room needs a current
membership. Renew it at the Students' Union." above it and **Tell us about your membership**
beside it, so you know before pressing a slot. An officer who manages rooms is not shown it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Booking a room needs a current membership. Renew it at the Students' Union."**: said in
  place of the form when your membership has run out, so nothing is filled in for nothing, and
  refused outright if it runs out while the form is open; never diverted to a request. **Tell us
  about your membership** opens the membership page, and **Buy a membership from the Students'
  Union** sits beside it where the SU's page is known.
- **"That slot has already happened"** and **"That room is no longer in use."**: refused
  outright, and said alone: a rule that could otherwise be asked about (too little notice, say)
  is not listed beside them, since there is nothing to ask for.
- **"The room is closed that day"** and **"That is outside the hours the room opens."**: the
  room's opening hours. Both can be asked for as an exception.
- **"The room is closed then: ..."**: a closure an officer has set, quoting its reason, or a
  performance ("... is on") in the house the room belongs to, from before doors until after the
  curtain comes down. Nothing to ask for, in a room that always asks as in any other: the form
  says so in red and **Ask for it** cannot be pressed. Pick another time.
- **"The shortest booking is N minutes."**, **"The longest booking is N hours."**, **"Booking
  needs N hours' notice. Ask, and the Theatre Manager will decide."**, **"Bookings open N weeks ahead."**,
  **"You already hold N bookings, which is the limit."**: each can be asked for as an exception.
- **"You already have that room booked then. It is on My bookings."**: the slot is yours
  already, often from pressing Back and booking again. Nothing new was booked.
- **"That room is already booked then. Choose another time or room."**: somebody else holds
  it, perhaps since you opened the calendar. Nothing was booked; find another.

A refusal stays on the form under the fields, with **Book it** still there to try again once
something is changed.
- **"Up to N"** under **How many times**: the series is longer than
  {{ ROOM_SERIES_MAX_OCCURRENCES }}. The number you typed is left as it is; make it smaller.
- **"A series runs to N occurrences at most"** and **"That leaves no occurrences to book"**:
  the series is too long, or every date was left out.
- **"That is more people than the room holds"**: a warning under **How many people** and on the
  booking, never a refusal. The number you typed stands, and so does the booking.

## The calendar

![The rooms calendar on a phone, with the day controls (1), the room chooser (2), the link to ask for a Students' Union room (3) and a quarter-hour slot to press (4)](/images/docs/members/rooms.png)

1. **Earlier**, **Today** and **Later** move a day at a time, or a week at a time in the week
   view. In the day view a strip of the coming fortnight sits under them, one button a day, so
   next Wednesday is one tap; **Or another day**, the date field beneath it, goes to any day
   further off.
2. **Every room** or one room. Across every room the columns are rooms and the question is
   "what is free tonight"; within one room the columns are days and the question is "when is the
   studio free".
3. **Ask for a Students' Union room** goes to the form for a room the theatre does not manage:
   see [Ask for a Students' Union room](/docs/my-nnt/ask-for-a-students-union-room).
4. **The grid** shows quarter-hour slots. Free, Yours, Booked, Awaiting a decision, Not open
   and Closed are told apart by colour and by the legend at the foot of the page, and each slot
   says which it is when you reach it by keyboard or screen reader. A slot somebody else holds
   reads as booked and nothing more. **Not open** is outside the room's hours, though a booking
   an officer agreed there as an exception is still drawn over it; **Past** has
   already happened and cannot be pressed; **Closed** is a closure an officer set or a
   performance in the house, and every closure the grid shows is listed in words under it with
   its reason, its days and its times. One that runs past midnight names both days. On a touch screen the slots are taller, so a finger can
   hit one. The grid scrolls in its own box with the room names pinned along its top and the
   times down its left edge, so a long day, or a phone scrolling across many rooms, never loses
   which slot is which. The day starts at the earliest hour any room shown opens, at 06:00 when
   one of them never shuts, earlier still for a booking that starts before that, and never later
   than 08:00; it runs to midnight. A time before the first slot can still be typed on the form.

On a phone the calendar is always the day view, which is the picture above, and there is no
**Day** or **Week** to press. On a wider screen those two switch between them, and a week
across every room is a table of counts, one cell per room per day: the bookings that day, and
"Closed in part" (or "Closed") where a closure or a performance takes some (or all) of the
hours the day view shows, so the table and the day view never disagree. A day gone by reads
"Past" rather than "Free". A day the room does not open reads "Not open", unless an exception
is booked on it, when it counts that booking like any other. Pressing a cell opens that room on
that day.

The view, the room and the day are kept in the page's address, so **Back** from the booking form
returns to the calendar as you left it, and a copied link opens the same view for somebody else.

If the calendar cannot be read at all, the page says so in red with **Try again**, and says
nothing about which rooms exist. "No rooms can be booked yet" means exactly that, and only ever
appears when the calendar loaded; an officer who manages rooms is told to add them under
**Rooms** instead.

1. Find a free slot. Press it to book an hour from there (less, if somebody holds the slot
   before the hour is up), or drag across several. On a touch
   screen, press where the booking starts and then press where it ends; the line above the grid
   says which press it is waiting for.
2. The booking form opens with the room, day and times already filled in.

## Booking a slot

![The booking form on a phone, with the room (1), what the room is for (2), a name for it (3) and the day (4)](/images/docs/members/book-a-room.png)

1. **Room**. A room that always asks says so under it: "Every booking for this room is agreed by
   the Theatre Manager before it is held."
2. **What the room is for** is a row of buttons (rehearsal, meeting, workshop and so on); press
   one, and it is marked with a tick. It is what a Students' Union room is judged suitable for,
   so it is required. It also sets the booking's priority if the slot is contested: a rehearsal
   is **Rehearsal** and anything else **General**. Only an officer can give a booking
   **Production** or **Committee** priority, and only an officer sees a **Priority if the slot is
   contested** field to do it.
3. **A name for it** is optional, and shown to officers and to nobody else looking at the
   calendar. Left empty, the booking is called by what the room is for, such as "Rehearsal".
4. **Day**.

Then:

1. **From** and **Until**.
2. **How many people** is optional. The room's capacity is quoted under it. A number above it
   is kept as typed, with "That is more people than the room holds" under the field.
3. Press **Book it**. **My bookings** opens, and "Booked" names the room, the day and the
   times that were made, with the year when the day is not in this calendar year. A confirmation email follows with a calendar file attached. A series
   and a request land on the same screen and say the same way what was made.

## Repeating a booking

A term of rehearsals is one series, booked from the same form.

1. Open **Repeat this booking**.
2. Choose **Every week** or **Every day**. For a weekly series, pick the days of the week; the
   day already chosen above is preselected.
3. Set **How many times**, up to {{ ROOM_SERIES_MAX_OCCURRENCES }}; a larger number is kept and
   refused with "Up to N" rather than changed for you. The line under the fields reads the series
   back in words.
4. Press **Book N dates**.

Every date is checked before any of them is held, so a week somebody else has stops the whole
run rather than leaving you with half of it. "Some of those dates cannot be booked" then lists
each refused date ("Thursday 5 November: another member already has it", or "you already have
this slot" when the clash is with your own booking or request), and **Book the other N dates**
resubmits without them. A series inside the rules is held at once, and the dates are numbered
by the ones booked.

A series that needs a decision is not sent straight away. "These dates need the Theatre Manager
to agree to them" lists each date that breaks a rule and which rules, and **Why these dates are
worth an exception** appears. Say why once for the whole series and press **Ask for N dates**:
it is asked for as one request, with your reason on every date, so changing one week later on
**My bookings** opens with it filled in. The message afterwards says how many were made or
asked for, and how many dates were left out.

## Asking for an exception

When a booking fails a rule that can be bent (too short a notice, too long, too far ahead, over
your cap, outside the room's hours), the form says "This one needs the Theatre Manager to agree
to it", lists exactly which rules failed, and adds a field:

1. Say **Why this one is worth an exception**. It is shown to the Theatre Manager.
2. Press **Ask for it**. "Asked for" confirms it, the slot is held while the Theatre Manager
   decides, and
   **My bookings** shows it as "Waiting on a decision".

A room marked as always asking, and a member on the no-show ladder (see
[My room bookings](/docs/my-nnt/my-room-bookings)), go this way whatever the rules say.
The form says so before anything is typed, and is the request from the start: it asks
**What the Theatre Manager should know** and offers **Ask for it** rather than a **Book it**
that could only fail. Repeated, it asks the same once for the whole series and offers **Ask for
N dates**, and the series is asked for as one request.

## What happens next

A booking inside the rules is written as Confirmed and a confirmation email goes out with the
calendar file attached. A request is written as Waiting on a decision with its slot held, the
member is told it was received, and every approver is emailed at once that there is something to
decide; a request left {{ ROOM_REQUEST_EXPIRE_HOURS }} lapses and the member is told. Every rooms
email gives the day and both ends of the slot, "Thursday 29 October 2026, 18:00 to 19:00". The decision,
either way, arrives by email and on [My room bookings](/docs/my-nnt/my-room-bookings). What
officers do with a request is on [Room requests](/docs/spaces/room-requests).
