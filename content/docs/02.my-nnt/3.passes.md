A pass is bought once and admits its holder to many performances. The **Passes** screen, under
**My NNT**, is for a signed-in member: it lists the passes they hold, any request still with an
officer, and the pass types on sale that can be requested. A pass is paid for at the box office
desk, on the Students' Union's card reader, when it is collected; the screen only asks for one.

![Passes on a phone, with the passes held (1) and the pass types that can be requested (2)](/images/docs/members/passes.png)

1. **Your passes** lists each pass with its type, its price point, what was paid, its reference
   and its state: Active, Cancelled or Expired.
2. **Request a pass** lists every pass type on sale with what it costs and what it covers,
   each with a **Request** button. Where a type has more than one price point, all of them are
   shown, and which one you pay is settled at the desk. The card only appears while something is
   on sale.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your passes could not be read"**: the list failed to load, which is not the same as
  holding nothing. Reload, and if it keeps happening say so.
- **"This pass is not on sale yet"** or **"This pass is no longer on sale"**: the pass type's
  sales window has not opened, or has closed, since the screen was loaded.
- **"This pass is capped at N issues, and that many are already out"**: said at the desk, at
  payment, not here. A request may be made past the cap; an issue may not.
- **"This pass does not cover this show"**, **"This pass has expired"**, **"This pass is not
  valid yet"**, **"This pass is not active"**: the pass cannot be used for this booking. The
  form only offers a pass it believes covers the performance, so this means something changed.
- **"This pass was already redeemed for this performance, at ..."**: once per performance.
- **"This performance no longer has room for that admission"**: the house filled while the
  form was open. Join the waiting list.

## Requesting a pass

1. Open **Passes** under **My NNT**.
2. Under **Request a pass**, read what it costs and what it covers, then press **Request**
   beside the pass type wanted. The screen confirms
   "Pass requested" and the request appears under **Your requests** as Pending.
3. Go to the box office desk and pay. The desk sees pending requests by name and issues the pass
   against the request at payment; the request becomes Fulfilled and the pass appears under
   **Your passes** as Active.

A request reserves nothing and admits nobody. It lapses on its own if the pass type's sales
window closes before it is paid for, and then reads Expired. An officer may also decline it,
and then it reads Declined.

## Reading a pass

The confirmation email carries a link to the pass's own page, with its QR code, which the door
scans. "Save this image to keep the code, or show this page at the door." The same page says
"That link isn't valid" when the link is wrong or has gone; the pass is still listed under
**Passes** while signed in.

## Using a pass to book

Booking a performance the pass covers, while signed in, shows **Your pass** above the ticket
lines with **Use my pass**. Pressing it makes a zero-value booking with its own reference and QR
code, and nothing is due at the desk. A pass admits once per performance; the seat still needs
to exist, so a full house refuses the redemption like any other booking. The rest of the
booking form is on [Booking tickets](/docs/my-nnt/booking-tickets).

## What happens next

A request is recorded against the account and shown on the desk. Issuing a pass at the desk
writes a pass-sale ledger line at the moment of payment, attaches the pass to the account and
emails it with its QR code. Redeeming a pass online writes a zero-value ticket and a zero-value
ledger line referencing the pass, so the pass's use is counted.
