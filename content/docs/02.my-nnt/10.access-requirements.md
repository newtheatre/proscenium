**Access requirements**, under **My NNT**, is where a signed-in member records what they need
once, instead of explaining it at every booking. What is recorded is verified in person by an
Accessibility Officer before any other screen sees it. Once verified, the booking form offers
access and companion tickets, and the door sees only the agreed wording, never what was
declared.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
Only the Accessibility Officer sees the declaration itself. General box office sees whether
the profile is verified and the door wording that follows from it, and nothing else. What you
write in your own words is never shown to the door.
::

## Recording your needs

![Access requirements on a phone, with the needs (1), the companions (2), the note in your own words (3) and the save button (4)](/images/docs/members/access.png)

1. **What do you need?**: tick each that applies. The list runs from standing and crowds
   through level access, distance, prompt toilet access, an essential companion, visual and
   audible information, to anything the categories do not cover.
2. **Companions**: 0, 1 or 2 people admitted alongside you at no charge, once verified.
3. **Anything else, in your own words**: optional, and read only by the officer verifying the
   profile.
4. **Save your requirements**, or **Save changes** for a profile already recorded.

Between them:

- **Access Card number** is optional and the quickest way to verify, if you have one. It is
  cleared the moment it is checked.
- **Let the door see my agreed wording once verified** is the consent the door wording depends
  on.

"Access profile saved. An Accessibility Officer will verify it before the door sees anything."
confirms it. The badge at the top of the card then reads Pending; after verification it reads
Verified, and the booking form starts offering the Access and Companion ticket types up to the
number verified.

## Changing or withdrawing

Any change lands the profile back at Pending until it is verified again. **Withdraw my
requirements** removes it: "Withdrawing deletes this after 30 days. The people on the door are
shown nothing in the meantime." The
badge reads Withdrawn, and the form stays so a new profile can be submitted.

A verified profile stays current for {{ ACCESS_PROFILE_VALIDITY_MONTHS }} months, after which
it reads Expired and the door stops seeing it until it is verified again. A profile the officer
could not verify reads Declined.

## Booking with a verified profile

On the booking form a banner says how many access and companion tickets are still available to
you for that performance, and the Access and Companion ticket types cannot be set above it. The
desk sees the agreed wording on the booking, so you are met with the right words rather than a
question. A booking carrying an access or companion ticket is not exchanged online; the box
office moves it by hand. The rest is on [Booking tickets](/docs/my-nnt/booking-tickets).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only N companion tickets may be held for this performance"**: the verified entitlement is
  already used on another booking for that night.
- The Access and Companion ticket types do not appear on the booking form: the profile is not
  yet verified, has expired, or was declined. The badge on this screen says which.
- The door does not have the wording: the consent box was not ticked, or the profile is not
  verified.

## What happens next

A saved profile is recorded as Pending and appears on the Accessibility Officer's screen. The
officer sights the evidence in person, or checks the Access Card number, and marks the profile
verified or declined; the card number is cleared at that moment. Verification sets the door
wording and the companion entitlement that every later booking reads. Withdrawal tombstones the
profile for 30 days and then deletes it outright. How the officer verifies is on
[Access profiles](/docs/box-office/access-profiles).
