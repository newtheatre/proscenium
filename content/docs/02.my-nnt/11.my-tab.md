A tab charge is owed to the bar by an authorised holder, not money taken: the till
posts it against the holder's own account, and it is cleared later by paying it off. **Bar
tab**, under **My NNT**, shows what is owed right now and every charge that made it up. It is in
the list only for an authorised tab holder, or for anybody still owing on a tab after their
authority lapsed; nobody else has a tab to see.

![Bar tab with the outstanding balance (1) and the charges it is made up of (2)](/images/docs/members/tab.png)

1. **Outstanding** is the live balance: every charge run up, less anything settled or voided.
2. **Charges** lists every charge ever made to the tab, itemised: what it was for, when, what it
   came to, and whether it is still outstanding, settled, or was voided.

Only an authorised holder can be charged in the first place: somebody named in Settings, or
somebody holding a role named there while the role lasts;
this page shows the same figures and charges to anybody signed in, empty if nothing has ever been
charged.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your tab could not be read"**: the page failed to load, which is not the same as owing
  nothing. Reload, and if it keeps happening say so.
- **"That bar's till is not open tonight…"**: the till closed since the page loaded. Nothing was
  added.
- **"… is served at the bar, since it needs a Challenge 25 check…"**: ask whoever is on the till.

## Tonight's tab code

Nobody else can charge your tab while you are not there. When somebody else is serving, the till
asks for your code: open this page and show them **Tonight's tab code**, as a QR code to scan or
eight characters to read out. It changes at 04:00 each night, so a code seen last week charges
nothing. Charging your own tab when you are serving needs no code. The card appears only while
you are authorised to run a tab.

## Adding to your own tab

While a bar's till is open tonight, **Add to my tab** on this page lets you put a drink on your own
tab without anyone serving you. Pick the bar if more than one is open, set how many of each, and
press **Add £… to my tab**. It goes on the till's night like any other tab charge, and the cap
applies: an addition that would take you past it is refused, and nobody waives the cap on their
own tab. Anything that needs a Challenge 25 check is not offered here; ask whoever is on the till.

## Settling a tab

Settlement is taken in person on the Students' Union's SumUp reader, at the till, never online:
the till shows the outstanding charges and takes payment against them directly. This page reads
what is owed; it does not take payment.

## What happens next

A charge posts the moment it is rung up, so the balance here is live. A void, always the Front of
House Manager's own call and always with a reason, credits the charge back and it reads Voided rather
than disappearing.
