Rooms is the list of every room we control, which is what a member sees on the calendar. It is
at **Manage, Spaces, Rooms**, and the Theatre Manager sees it and adds, edits and retires a
room.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A room is retired, never deleted.** A booking made last term still has to name something, so
a room you no longer want leaves the calendar and keeps its history. Every room here is one we
control; a room the Students' Union lends us lives under [Students' Union rooms](/docs/spaces/students-union-rooms).
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Give the room a name people would recognise"**: the name is blank.
- **"Closing time must be later than opening time"**: a day's closing time is not later than
  its opening time.
- **"What the room still holds could not be counted"**: the Retire dialogue could not count the
  waiting requests and bookings, so **Retire** stays off. Close the dialogue and try again.
- **"That room is no longer here."**: the room was removed between your opening the form and saving it.
  Reload the list.
- **"The rooms could not be read."**: the list did not load. This is not the same as there
  being no rooms; reload, and if it keeps happening say so.

## The rooms we control

![The rooms screen with the search field (1), the Students' Union rooms button (2), Add a room (3), the table (4) and a row's Edit button (5)](/images/docs/spaces/rooms.png)

1. **Search** by any part of a room's name. The filter beside it shows retired rooms, which are
   hidden until you ask.
2. **Students' Union rooms** is a shortcut to the catalogue of rooms the Union lends us.
3. **Add a room** opens the same form as editing one.
4. **The table** shows each room's capacity (**Uncapped** where none is recorded) and when it
   is open: **Always open** where no hours are recorded, or the days and times, such as
   **Mon to Fri, 09:00 to 22:00**. Days with different times are listed a run at a time:
   **Mon to Thu, 09:00 to 22:00; Fri, 09:00 to 18:00**. A room whose every booking goes to the
   queue carries a **Needs approval** badge, a retired one **Retired**, and one with rules of
   its own **Own rules**, with each rule listed under it (**Longest booking 2 hours, No notice
   needed**).
5. **Edit** opens the room. While the room is in use, **Close for a spell** and **Retire** sit
   beside it. **Close for a spell** opens [Closures](/docs/spaces/closures) with the closure form
   already open on this room.

## Adding or editing a room

Left alone, a room is open whenever and follows the house rules. Fill in only what is true
of this room.

1. Press **Add a room**, or **Edit** on a row.

![The room form with the name (1), the capacity (2), the opening hours section (3), the approval switch (4), the room's own rules (5) and the save button (6)](/images/docs/spaces/room-form.png)

2. **Name** is required, and is what a member sees on the calendar. A description is optional
   and shows beneath the name in the list.
3. **Capacity** is optional. Left blank the room is uncapped. A booking for more people than
   the room holds is a warning to the member, never a refusal: the room still fits the
   booking somebody has already agreed to.
4. **Opening hours** start from a choice of three. **Always open**, the default, records no
   hours at all, which is true of most rooms. **Weekdays** asks for one opening and one closing
   time for Monday to Friday, and the weekend is closed. **Set each day** shows a switch and two
   times for every day, starting from whatever the first two gave; a day switched off is closed
   all day. With any hours recorded, a booking has to sit wholly inside them, and a closed day
   refuses outright.
5. **Every booking needs approval** sends every booking for this room to the
   [request queue](/docs/spaces/room-requests), whatever the policy says. It is for a space
   where a request is a conversation: the auditorium in a production week is the obvious one.
6. **This room's own rules** override the house rules for this room only. Each box shows the
   house default it falls back to and the smallest it accepts, such as **House default: 30
   minutes. One or more.** Leave a box blank to follow the house. **Notice needed** is the one
   rule where 0 is a real answer, meaning none needed; the other four take one or more. The five
   rules are the shortest booking (house default {{ ROOM_MIN_BOOKING_MINUTES }}), the longest
   ({{ ROOM_MAX_BOOKING_HOURS }}), the notice needed to confirm without a decision
   ({{ ROOM_AUTO_APPROVE_NOTICE_HOURS }}), how far ahead booking opens
   ({{ ROOM_BOOKING_HORIZON_WEEKS }}) and how many bookings one member may hold, waiting
   requests included ({{ ROOM_ACTIVE_BOOKINGS_PER_MEMBER }}).
7. When editing, **In use** is the switch that retires a room or brings one back. Switched off
   here, it settles nothing the room holds; **Retire** on the list offers to.
8. Press **Add the room** or **Save changes**.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lightbulb" color="info"}
The house defaults themselves are settings, changed under
[Settings](/docs/system/settings), not here. Change a default and every room that follows it
changes with it; a room with its own number keeps it.
::

## Retiring a room

Press **Retire** on the row. The dialogue first counts what the room still holds from now on:
requests waiting on a decision, and confirmed bookings yet to start. For each it has found, it
offers a ticked box:

- **Turn down N waiting requests**: each member is told the room is no longer in use, as if the
  request had been turned down from [Room requests](/docs/spaces/room-requests). Left unticked,
  the requests wait in the queue, and approving one into this room is refused. To move a request
  to another room instead, decide it on Room requests before retiring.
- **Cancel N bookings yet to come**: each member is told the room has closed, with the calendar
  file that takes the booking off their calendar, and nothing is rebooked. Left unticked, the
  bookings stand.

Then press **Retire Studio**. It stays off until the count has come back. The room leaves the
calendar and the booking form at once, stays on every booking and report that names it, and
comes back by editing it and switching **In use** on. Switching **In use** off retires a room
without the count and settles nothing. Bookings already under way or past are never touched.

## What happens next

Adding a room writes an audit entry naming it. Editing writes the entry with what changed,
from and to, and setting hours writes a second entry counting the days now open and closed
rather than fourteen times. Retiring is an edit of **In use**, and its entry counts any requests
turned down and bookings cancelled with it. Only those members are emailed: otherwise a room's
rules apply to the next booking judged against them, and a member sees the change on the
calendar.
