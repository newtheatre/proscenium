Rooms is the list of every room we control, which is what a member sees on the calendar. It is
at **Manage, Spaces, Rooms**. Seeing it needs `rooms.read`; adding, editing or retiring a room
needs `rooms.write`, and without that permission the buttons are simply not there.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A room is retired, never deleted.** A booking made last term still has to name something, so
a room you no longer want leaves the calendar and keeps its history. Every room here is one we
control; a room the Students' Union lends us lives under [Other rooms](/docs/spaces/other-rooms).
::

## The estate

![The rooms screen with the search field (1), the Other rooms button (2), Add a room (3), the table (4) and a row's Edit button (5)](/images/docs/spaces/rooms.png)

1. **Search** by any part of a room's name. The filter beside it shows retired rooms, which are
   hidden until you ask.
2. **Other rooms** is a shortcut to the catalogue of rooms we do not manage.
3. **Add a room** opens the same form as editing one.
4. **The table** shows each room's capacity (**Uncapped** where none is recorded) and when it
   is open: **Always open** where no hours are recorded, **Open every day**, or the days it
   opens. A room whose every booking goes to the queue carries a **Needs approval** badge; a
   retired one carries **Retired**.
5. **Edit** opens the room. **Retire** appears beside it while the room is in use.

## Adding or editing a room

Left alone, a room is open whenever and follows the estate settings. Fill in only what is true
of this room.

1. Press **Add a room**, or **Edit** on a row.

![The room form with the name (1), the capacity (2), the opening hours section (3), the approval switch (4), the room's own rules (5) and the save button (6)](/images/docs/spaces/room-form.png)

2. **Name** is required, and is what a member sees on the calendar. A description is optional
   and shows beneath the name in the list.
3. **Capacity** is optional. Left blank the room is uncapped. A booking for more people than
   the room holds is a warning to the member, never a refusal: the room still fits the
   booking somebody has already agreed to.
4. **Opening hours** are per weekday. Leave every day closed and the room is open whenever,
   which is true of most rooms. Switch one day on and the rest become closed days; a booking
   then has to sit wholly inside the hours you give, and a closed day refuses outright.
5. **Every booking needs approval** sends every booking for this room to the
   [request queue](/docs/spaces/room-requests), whatever the policy says. It is for a space
   where a request is a conversation: the auditorium in a production week is the obvious one.
6. **This room's own rules** override the estate settings for this room only. Each box shows
   the estate default it falls back to. Leave a box blank to follow the estate; type nought
   where a real answer is "none needed", for example no notice at all. The five rules are the
   shortest booking (estate default {{ ROOM_MIN_BOOKING_MINUTES }} minutes), the longest
   ({{ ROOM_MAX_BOOKING_HOURS }} hours), the notice needed to confirm without a decision
   ({{ ROOM_AUTO_APPROVE_NOTICE_HOURS }} hours), how far ahead booking opens
   ({{ ROOM_BOOKING_HORIZON_WEEKS }} weeks) and how many bookings one member may hold
   ({{ ROOM_ACTIVE_BOOKINGS_PER_MEMBER }}).
7. When editing, **In use** is the switch that retires a room or brings one back.
8. Press **Add it** or **Save**.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lightbulb" color="info"}
The estate defaults themselves are settings, changed under
[Settings](/docs/system/settings), not here. Change a default and every room that follows it
changes with it; a room with its own number keeps it.
::

## Retiring a room

Press **Retire** on the row, or edit the room and switch **In use** off. The room leaves the
calendar and the booking form at once, stays on every booking and report that names it, and
comes back the same way. Retiring a room does not cancel what is booked in it; to do that,
[close it](/docs/spaces/closures) first.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Give the room a name people would recognise"**: the name is blank.
- **"A room closes after it opens"**: a day's closing time is not later than its opening time.
- **"No such room"**: the room was removed between your opening the form and saving it.
  Reload the list.
- **"The rooms could not be read."**: the list did not load. This is not the same as there
  being no rooms; reload, and if it keeps happening say so.
- **The buttons are missing**: you hold `rooms.read` without `rooms.write`. The screen is
  read-only for you rather than refusing three times over.

## What happens next

Adding a room writes an audit entry naming it. Editing writes the entry with what changed,
from and to, and setting hours writes a second entry counting the days now open and closed
rather than fourteen times. Retiring is an edit of **In use**. Nothing is emailed: a room's
rules apply to the next booking judged against them, and a member sees the change on the
calendar.
