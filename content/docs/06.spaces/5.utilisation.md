Utilisation is the report the end-of-year review is written from: how many hours each room
was used, cancelled, and booked but not turned up to, against how many hours it was open. It
is at **Manage, Spaces, Utilisation** and needs `rooms.read`. It reads and changes nothing,
so there is nothing here that needs `rooms.write`.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A room with no opening hours recorded is always open**, so there is no denominator to divide
by. Its share reads **no opening hours recorded** rather than 0 per cent. Give a room
hours under [Rooms](/docs/spaces/rooms) and the share appears.
::

## Reading the report

The report opens on the committee year to date: from 1 August to today, London time, which is the
span a review is written about. On 31 July the span is still the year that is ending; on 1 August
it has turned over.

![The utilisation report with the Filters button holding the From and Until dates (1), the Export button (2), the table (3) and the totals (4)](/images/docs/spaces/utilisation.png)

1. **From** and **Until** set the span. Changing either goes back to the first page.
2. **Break down by** chooses **Room** or **Kind of booking**, which is the booking's priority
   tier. Open hours only exist per room, so a breakdown by kind shows the hours used and lost
   without a share.
3. **Export** downloads the same figures as CSV.
4. **The table** shows, for each row, **Used** (confirmed hours and the share of open hours
   they took), **Open** (the hours open in the span, or **Always open**), **Not used**
   (hours cancelled, and hours not turned up to) and the count of bookings. Busiest first,
   always. Search matches a room or a kind.
5. **The totals** describe the whole span, not only what the search matched.

Rooms we do not manage are not here: nothing about their hours is ours to count. A member who
has closed their account still counts, because the hours are counted per room rather than
per person.

## Exporting

Press **Export** with the span and breakdown you want. The file is named
`room-utilisation-<from>-to-<to>.csv`, one row per room or kind, with the confirmed,
cancelled, no-show and open hours, the share used, and the count of bookings. It carries the
whole report rather than the page on screen, up to 5,000 rows.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A report ends on or after the day it starts"**: **Until** is before **From**.
- **"Choose a day to report from"**, **"Choose a day to report to"**: a date is blank.
- **"Nothing was booked in that span."**: the report ran and found nothing, which is an answer
  rather than a failure.
- **"The utilisation report could not be read."**: the report did not load. This is not the
  same as nothing being booked; reload, and if it keeps happening say so.

## What happens next

Nothing. Reading the report writes nothing and tells nobody; the figures come from the
bookings, the recorded no-shows and each room's opening hours as they stand.
