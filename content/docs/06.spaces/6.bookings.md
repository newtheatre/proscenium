Bookings lists every member's room bookings, whoever made them, and is where a booking is bumped
for something that ranks higher or marked as a no-show. It is at **Manage, Spaces, Bookings**. The
Manager, the Theatre Manager and the Training Manager all see the list; bumping and no-shows are
the Manager's and the Theatre Manager's.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert" color="warning"}
**Bumping takes a member's booking away.** They are told at once, with your reason word for word,
and the booking is not given back. Say why as you would to their face.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"The bookings could not be read."**: the list did not load. Reload, and if it keeps
  happening say so.
- **A booking you expected is not listed**: bookings that have ended are hidden. Turn on the
  **Past** filter to see them.
- **"No booking matches that."**: nothing fits the search and filters together. Press **Clear all**
  to start again.
- **"A booking may only be bumped by a higher priority than its own"**: the kind you chose does
  not rank above the booking's own. Choose one that does, or leave the booking where it is.
- **"Only a confirmed booking can be bumped"** or **"That booking has already happened"**: the
  booking changed or ended while the dialogue was open. Reload the list.
- **"That booking changed while this was being worked out"**: somebody else bumped or cancelled it
  first. Reload the list; nothing was written.
- **"Choose what the room is for from the list"**: pick an entry under **What it is for**.
- **"That account does not exist"**: the person chosen under **Taken for** was removed. Choose
  again.
- **"Only a confirmed booking can be marked as a no-show"** or **"That booking has not happened
  yet"**: only a confirmed booking that has ended can be marked.
- **"That booking is already marked as a no-show"**: somebody marked it first. Reload the list.
- **"That record has already been superseded"**: the no-show was withdrawn by somebody else
  first. Reload the list.

## The list

The table shows each booking's title and room, the member who made it, when it runs, what kind
of booking it is, and its state. A booking marked as a no-show carries a **No-show** badge
beside its state. On a phone the member and the time sit under the title.

The list starts with what is still to come, soonest first, a page at a time. The button at the end
of a row is the one thing that booking allows: **Bump** before a confirmed booking ends, **Record
a no-show** after it ends, and **Withdraw the no-show** once one is marked. Anything else offers
nothing.

## Finding a booking

- **Search** matches a booking's title, the member's name or the room.
- **Filters** narrow by **State**, **Room**, **Member**, **Kind of booking** and the day it
  **Starts**. **Past** shows bookings that have ended, and **Marked as a no-show** shows only
  those marked, however long ago.
- **Sort** is by when the booking starts; change the direction to put the latest first.

Search, filters, sort and page all live in the address, and a link to a filtered list opens the
same list for whoever you send it to.

## Bumping a booking

Bumping gives a confirmed booking's slot to a need that ranks higher, such as a production in its
show week. It is never automatic, and an equal or lower kind can never bump.

1. Find the booking and press **Bump** on its row.
2. The top of the dialogue says what the member would be offered instead: the same room at the
   nearest free time, or a room at least as big, closest in time. If nothing equivalent is free
   it says so, and the bump still goes ahead with no offer.
3. **Taken for**: choose the person the room is being taken for.
4. **Title**, **Kind of booking** and **What it is for** describe the new booking. The kind has to
   rank above the booking it takes.
5. **Reason** is required. The member reads it word for word.
6. Press **Bump the booking**. The member is told at once with your reason and the offer, and the
   screen says what they were offered.

The bumped booking stays on the list as **Given to another booking**; it is never deleted. The
offer, if there was one, is already booked in the member's name.

## Recording a no-show

1. Turn on the **Past** filter and find the booking.
2. Press **Record a no-show**. A reason is optional.
3. Press **Record the no-show**. The row gains its **No-show** badge, and the screen says how many
   bookings the member has now missed.

Missed bookings count over a rolling window that never reaches back past the start of the
committee year. At {{ ROOM_NO_SHOW_RECORD_AT }} the member is told where they stand; at
{{ ROOM_NO_SHOW_PREAPPROVAL_AT }}, every booking they make goes to
[Room requests](/docs/spaces/room-requests) first. Below the first step, a mark tells the member nothing.

## Withdrawing a no-show

A mark made in error is withdrawn, never deleted.

1. Find the booking (the **Marked as a no-show** filter lists every one) and press **Withdraw the
   no-show**.
2. **Reason** is required; the button stays off until there is one.
3. Press **Withdraw the no-show**. The badge goes, and the mark stops counting. The original stays
   on record beside your reason.

## What the list does not show

Only the member's name appears. Their address, the notes they left on a booking and the reason
they gave for a request are not on this screen; a request that needs a decision is in
[Room requests](/docs/spaces/room-requests).

## What happens next

Every bump, no-show and withdrawal is on the audit trail with who did it. The reasons you type
reach the member and the booking, not the trail.
