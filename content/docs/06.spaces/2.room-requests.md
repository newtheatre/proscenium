Room requests is the one queue for everything a member has asked for that needs a person's
answer, whoever manages the room. It is at **Manage, Spaces, Room requests** and needs
`rooms.write`, which the Manager and the Theatre Manager hold; a role with `rooms.read` alone
does not see it in the navigation. Whoever holds the permission is an approver: there is no
separate approver role.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A request for one of our rooms holds its slot while it waits**, so leaving one here keeps the
room blocked for everybody else. A request for a room we do not manage holds nothing: the
Students' Union has hundreds of rooms and decides which one we get, so two members may ask for
the same evening.
::

## What lands here

A booking that sits inside the policy for an ordinary room confirms on its own and never
appears. You see a request for one of our rooms when:

- the policy would not confirm it: too short, too long, less than
  {{ ROOM_AUTO_APPROVE_NOTICE_HOURS }} hours' notice, further ahead than
  {{ ROOM_BOOKING_HORIZON_WEEKS }} weeks, or the member already holds
  {{ ROOM_ACTIVE_BOOKINGS_PER_MEMBER }} bookings. A room's own rules replace these where it
  has them. The member was asked why the exception is worth making, and their reason is on
  the row;
- the room is marked **Every booking needs approval**, which shows as an **Always asks** badge;
- the member has missed {{ ROOM_NO_SHOW_PREAPPROVAL_AT }} bookings and is under pre-approval,
  so every booking of theirs asks whatever the policy says.

A request that nothing could make right never arrives: a lapsed membership, a slot in the
past, a retired room or a closed span are refused at the form, because there is nothing an
approver could agree to.

A term of rehearsals that breaches the policy queues as a whole, one row per week. Each week is
an ordinary booking with its own row, so tick them together to decide the term at once, or
decide one week on its own.

A request for a room we do not manage carries a **Not ours** badge. It has three stages rather
than one decision: **Not yet requested** (nobody has filled in the SU's form), **Requested,
awaiting a room** (the form is in) and **Confirmed** (the SU told us which room).

## Reading the queue

![The queue with the select-all box (1), the table (2), and a row's Approve (3), move (4), Reject (5) and Not one of ours (6) buttons](/images/docs/spaces/room-requests.png)

1. **Select every request shown** ticks every request for our rooms on the page. Rows for
   rooms we do not manage have no box: nothing about them is decided in bulk.
2. **The table** shows who asked and what for, the span and the room (or **No room yet**), and
   **Status**: the rules the request breaks, judged as you read it and not as it
   was written, so one that ran out of notice since Monday says so today. Open work comes
   first, soonest first; **Show, Everything** adds what has been answered, most recent first,
   for looking something up. **Kind** narrows to our rooms or to rooms we do not manage, and
   **Room** to one of ours. Search matches a name, a room, a title or a reason.
3. **Approve** confirms the booking in the room it asked for.
4. **The arrows** approve it into another room.
5. **Reject** asks for a reason.
6. **Not one of ours** turns it into a request for a room we do not manage.

## Deciding on one of our rooms

1. Read the row: the rules it breaks and the reason the member gave.
2. Press **Approve** to confirm it as asked. The clash rule is asserted on the approving write
   itself, so if somebody took the slot while the request waited the approval is refused and
   the booking is never doubled.
3. Or press the arrows to **Approve into another room**. Choose a room that is in use; the
   slot has to be free there too, and this is one request at a time.
4. Or press **Reject** and **Say why**. The requester is shown your words word for word, in
   their bookings and in the message they get, so write it to them.
5. Or press **Not one of ours** when the room they asked for is the wrong kind of room. Say
   why; the slot is freed straight away, and the request becomes an ask for a room we do not
   manage, at the top of this same queue. It is refused when there is no longer time to fill
   in the form.

To decide several at once, tick the rows and use **Approve N requests** or **Reject** in the
toolbar. A batch takes up to 100 requests. Each member gets one message however many of
theirs were in it, and a batch that partly failed says how many could not be decided and why,
leaving those ticked.

## Handling a request for a room we do not manage

Nothing here holds a slot, and every step tells the member where things stand. The row shows
**Form in by** and a date: the SU needs {{ EXTERNAL_REQUEST_NOTICE_WORKING_DAYS }} working
days, and Saturdays, Sundays and the bank holidays listed in the settings do not count. The
booking itself may fall on any of them; only the gap before it is judged.

1. Fill in the SU's form yourself, then press **Form is in**. Add **Their reference** if they
   gave one, so the two sides can be reconciled later. The member is told it is with the SU.
2. When the SU answers, press **Record room** and pick the room from the catalogue. Recording
   the room confirms the request and tells the member which room they have, and where it is.
   If we have marked that room **No good for** this purpose, the screen stops you with what we
   know and offers **Record the room anyway**; a room marked **May not suit** is a warning on the
   picker rather than a stop.
3. If the room they gave us is wrong, press **Send back** instead. Name the room they
   offered and what was wrong with it; leave **Remember this about the room** ticked and pick
   **May not suit** or **No good for**, and the next person asking for that room for that
   purpose is warned before anybody is troubled. The request goes back to waiting and the
   member is told we are asking again.
4. **Change room** does the same as **Record room** on a request already confirmed, because
   being moved room to room after an answer is ordinary.
5. **Turn down** ends it, with a reason the member reads word for word.
6. **Use one of ours** moves it into one of our rooms instead. This claims the slot straight
   away, so it can fail if somebody holds the room for that span. It lands confirmed if the
   span passes the policy and waiting on a decision if it does not: choosing the room is not
   a licence to skip the rules.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lightbulb" color="info"}
Every room the SU offers is kept against the request, suitable or not, so asking again never
overwrites what we were given first. What we learn about a room is kept under
[Other rooms](/docs/spaces/other-rooms).
::

## The morning sweep

Approvers are emailed the moment a request arrives. Every day at 08:00 a sweep then looks at
what is still waiting:

- A request for one of our rooms that has waited {{ ROOM_REQUEST_ESCALATE_HOURS }} hours is
  chased: every approver is emailed once that it is waiting.
- One that has waited {{ ROOM_REQUEST_EXPIRE_HOURS }} hours lapses. It is rejected with the
  reason **"Nobody answered this in time, so it lapsed."**, the slot is freed and the member
  is told. An approver deciding at the same moment wins, and the sweep leaves that decision
  alone.
- A request for a room we do not manage is chased the same way, saying whether the form is
  in yet, but it never lapses: lapsing exists to free a held slot, and this holds none, so it
  would tell the member nothing while the SU may still answer.
- Nobody is chased about a date that has passed.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Somebody took that slot while this was waiting"**: the approval was beaten to the room.
  The request stays open; approve it into another room, or reject it and say so.
- **"That request was already rejected"**, **"already cancelled"**: a colleague decided, or
  the member withdrew it. Rejected and cancelled are final for everybody, officers included,
  because the slot may be somebody else's by now.
- **"That booking is not waiting for a decision"**: it was confirmed already. Reload.
- **"That room is no longer bookable"**: the room you chose to move into was retired.
- **"Say why, because the requester is shown it word for word"**: a rejection with no reason.
- **"Moving a request to another room is one at a time"**: the arrows do not work on a batch.
- **"There is no longer time to ask: the form would have had to go in by ..."**: the working
  days have run out, so the request cannot become an ask for a room we do not manage. Decide
  it as it stands.
- **"Bank holidays are not known that far ahead, so the notice cannot be counted"**: the
  bank holiday list in the settings does not reach the booking's date. Extend the list rather
  than guessing; a date it does not cover is refused instead of being counted wrongly.
- **"That request has already moved on"**: a colleague acted first. The modal shows this
  rather than the page, so the button does not merely appear to do nothing. Reload.
- **"That room is not one we have listed"**: record the room under Other rooms first.
- **"Somebody already holds ... for that span"**: moving into one of ours found the slot
  taken. Choose another room.
- **"That request is confirmed, so it cannot be turned down"**: a confirmed ask is the
  member's to withdraw. Send it back or change the room instead.
- **"N requests could not be: ..."**: a batch partly failed. The rows that failed stay ticked
  with the first reason shown.

## What happens next

Approving confirms the booking, and the member sees it on the calendar and in their bookings.
Rejecting is final and carries your reason. Every decision is in the audit trail with the
room it went into, and never with the reason the member gave. A moved request is superseded
rather than deleted: it reads **Moved to a room we do not manage** or **Moved to one of our
rooms**, never as cancelled, and points at what replaced it. One message per member per
action carries every request of theirs you decided, and it links to their bookings.
