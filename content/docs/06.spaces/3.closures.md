Closures is where a room is shut for a span: a get-in, a repair, a hire, or a building closure
that takes every room at once. It is at **Manage, Spaces, Closures**, and the Theatre Manager
reads the list and closes or reopens a room.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert" color="warning"}
**Closing a room cancels what is booked in it.** Everybody affected is told, with your reason.
Reopening restores nothing: a cancelled booking has to be made again, and the slot may be
somebody else's by then. Say why before you say when.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"That room does not exist"**: the room was retired or removed while the form was open.
  Reload and choose again.
- **"That closure is no longer here."**: somebody reopened it first. Reload the list.
- **"The closures could not be read."**: the list did not load. Reload, and if it keeps
  happening say so.
- **The close button stays off**: no room is chosen yet, the span ends before it starts, or
  what closing would cancel has not been counted yet. Nothing is chosen for you: a closure
  cannot be undone.
- **"What closing would cancel could not be counted"**: the count failed, so the room cannot be
  closed from here yet. Change the room or the days to count again, or reload.
- **A member says their booking was refused for a closure**: that is the closure working. The
  refusal quotes your reason, and a closed span cannot be asked for as a request either:
  nothing an approver could agree to would make it open.
- **"The room is closed then: ... is on"**: a performance closing its venue's room. See
  [Closed for performances](#closed-for-performances); it cannot be reopened from this screen.

## Closing a room

1. Press **Close a room**, or **Close for a spell** on the room's row on
   [Rooms](/docs/spaces/rooms), which opens the form with that room already chosen.

![The closure form with the room (1), the reason (2), the days (3), the whole days switch (4) and the close button (5)](/images/docs/spaces/closure-form.png)

2. **Which room** starts empty, and nothing is chosen for you: a closure cannot be undone.
   Choose one room, or **Every room**, which is what a building closure or a fire alarm test
   means. Retired rooms are not offered.
3. **Why** is required. It is shown to everybody on the calendar, and to whoever loses a
   booking, so write it to them. The form says how many characters it takes.
4. **Day** and **Until day** name the days, in London time. **Until day** follows **Day** until
   you move it later, so an afternoon needs nothing typed twice and a five-day get-in is one
   closure rather than five.
5. **Whole days** closes from midnight on the first day to midnight after the last. It is on by
   itself once **Until day** is later than **Day**, and off for a single day; switch it off to
   give **From** and **Until** times instead, which on a single day must run forwards.
6. The button counts before you press it: **"Close The Studio: cancels 3 bookings"**, or
   **"cancels nothing"**. It reads **"Choose a room to close"**, and stays off, until a room is
   chosen. Press it, and the screen says what it did: **"Nothing was booked in that span."**, or
   how many bookings were cancelled and how many people were told.

A member whose booking is in the span loses that booking only. For a term of rehearsals, only
the weeks that overlap are cancelled, and the rest of the series carries on; if the first week
went, the next one becomes the head of the series.

## The list

![The closures screen with the Close a room button (1), the table (2) and a row's Reopen button (3)](/images/docs/spaces/closures.png)

1. **Close a room** opens the form above.
2. **The table** shows the room (or an **Every room** badge), the span, the reason and who
   closed it. A span that ends on a different day carries that date as well, so a get-in reads
   as the several days it is. Search matches a room or a reason. Closures that have passed are hidden until
   the **Past** filter asks for them.
3. **Reopen** removes a closure.

## Closed for performances

A venue attached to a room (see [Venues](/docs/box-office/venues)) closes that room for each of
its performances, draft or on sale, from before doors until after the curtain comes down: by
default {{ SHIFT_START_BEFORE_DOORS_MINUTES }} before doors to
{{ SHIFT_END_AFTER_CURTAIN_DOWN_MINUTES }} after, the same window a shift is worked in. Nobody
types a show week in as a closure. A member trying to book it is told "The room is closed then:"
and the show's name, and the calendar shows it to everybody; a show not yet published reads only
"A performance is on", since nothing about it is public. Approving a request, or bumping a
booking, over a performance is refused in the same words.

**Closed for performances**, under the list, shows each of these up to the booking horizon: the
room, the show (marked **Not yet published** where it is not), the span and the venue. They have no **Reopen**: cancelling the performance, or
detaching the room from the venue, is what opens the room again.

A booking already in the room when the performance was scheduled, or when the room was attached,
is **not** cancelled. It is listed under its performance as a booking to settle, with its title,
who made it and when, and it stays until you or they cancel it.

## Reopening a room

Press **Reopen** on the row and confirm with **Reopen the room**. The room is bookable again from
that moment. Bookings the closure cancelled stay cancelled and have to be made again, which
the confirmation says in as many words.

## What happens next

The closure is written with who made it, and the audit trail records it with the count of
bookings it cancelled. Each member who lost a booking gets one message listing what went and
why, however many of theirs fell in the span. The calendar shows the closure with its reason
to everybody; it is never masked as merely booked. Reopening writes its own entry, recording
that nothing was restored.
