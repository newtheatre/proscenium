A tab charge is credit extended to an authorised holder, not money taken: the till puts it on
the holder's own account, and it is cleared later by a settlement at the till or by a member
paying it off. This register is at **Manage, Bar, Tabs**, and the Bar Manager opens it.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Voiding is the manager's own call, never the till's.** A settled charge is corrected by refund
policy, not a void: voiding is refused once a charge has been settled. Every void carries a
reason, written to the audit trail alongside it.
::

## Reading the register

![The tab register listing every holder with a balance and the view tab action](/images/docs/bar/tabs.png)

Only a holder currently carrying a balance appears. A settled or voided charge is never a debt,
so its holder drops off the list once nothing of theirs is outstanding.

**View tab** opens every charge run up against that holder, itemised: what it was for, when it
happened, what it came to, and whether it has since been settled or voided. **Void** appears only
on a charge that is neither settled nor already voided.

## Voiding a charge

1. Press **Void** on the charge.
2. Give a **Reason**: why the charge is being undone, kept on the audit record.
3. Press **Void the charge**.

The void posts as a reversing credit against the same holder, and reverses any stock the charge
depleted. It is written once and never edited; a mistaken void is corrected with a fresh charge,
not by trying again.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That charge is already settled: correct it by refund policy, not a void"**: settlement has
  already cleared it. A settled charge is never voided from here.
- **"That charge has already been voided."**: somebody else voided it first. Reload and look
  again.
- **"That charge is no longer voidable: it may have just been settled."**: a settlement landed
  between opening the tab and pressing Void. Reload and look again.
