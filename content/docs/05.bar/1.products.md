A product is what the till shows: House red, Lager, Crisps. The list is at **Manage, Bar,
Products** and needs `bar.read` to open and `bar.write` to change, both of which the Bar manager
role holds. Each product has its own screen for serving sizes, reached from the list, where what
it depletes and what it costs are set.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**A product is retired, never destroyed.** Once anything has been sold as a product it can only
be retired: a retired product leaves the till and still resolves for every line, report and
export behind it. A product nothing has ever been sold as can be deleted outright.
::

## Adding a product

Categories come first: both buttons stay disabled until at least one category exists, because a
product has to sit somewhere on the till.

**Set up a product** is the way in for anything new: it asks what shape the thing is and then
writes the product, its stocked item, its serving sizes, what each one pours and its opening
prices in one go. **Add a product** is still there for the bare record when the sizes and the
recipe are being built up by hand afterwards.

### Setting one up by shape

![The three shapes a product is set up by: sold as itself (1), sold by measure (2) and made from several things (3)](/images/docs/bar/product-setup.png)

1. **Sold as itself** is a can, a bottle or a packet: one of them leaves the shelf with every
   sale. It asks for the name, the category, allergens, the age flag, what it is sold as (Can,
   Bottle or Each), the price, and optionally an opening delivery so the stock figure starts
   true.
2. **Sold by measure** is wine, spirits or draught: one stocked container pours several sizes.
   Pick the stocked item or create it here, and the **Measures** list fills in the sizes that
   kind of drink is usually poured at, chosen from the category's name where it can be. Untick
   anything the bar does not pour, change a label or a measure if the bar differs, and the prices
   come pre-filled from the category's defaults wherever one is set.
3. **Made from several things** is a cocktail or a mixed drink. Add a line per ingredient with
   the quantity it pours, set one price, and switch on **It comes with a choice** to create the
   mixer or garnish group in the same submission.

One **Set it up** finishes it. Nothing is written until that press succeeds, so a name somebody
else takes first leaves nothing behind and the form is still in front of you.

The shape is not stored anywhere: it is read from the serving sizes the product ends up with, so
a product set up one way and edited later is the same as one built by hand from the start. Every
size, recipe line and price is editable afterwards on the product's own screen.

A size nothing prices does not stop the set-up. The screen says so before you press, and the
product arrives hidden with the reason on it: fill the price in, or set a category default, and
put it on the till after.

### Adding the bare record

1. Press **Add a product**.
2. Give it a **Name**. This is what the till and every report call it.
3. Choose its **Category** and, if the order within the category matters, an **Order in the
   category**: lower comes first on the till.
4. Say what you know about **Allergens**. *Confirmed no allergens* is an answer; *No information
   recorded* is the absence of one, and the till says which. Choosing *Allergens recorded* opens
   an **Allergen note** that staff read out at the bar, and it has to cover the ingredients, not
   only the bottle.
5. Switch on **Age restricted** for anything alcoholic. A basket holding one of these asks for
   a Challenge 25 outcome before it can be paid for.
6. Switch on **Staffed only** to keep it off self-serve tabs, so it is sold across the bar by a
   person.
7. Press **Add it**. The product is hidden until you put it on the till.

![The products list with the set-up button (1), the add button (2), the serving sizes link (3), the edit button (4) and the retire button (5)](/images/docs/bar/products.png)

1. **Set up a product** opens the guided set-up above.
2. **Add a product** opens the bare form above.
3. **Serving sizes** opens the product's own screen.
4. **Edit** changes the name, category, order, allergens and switches. Status is a separate
   decision and is not on this form.
5. **Retire** takes the product off the till for good. It asks first: press **Retire Kopparberg**
   in the dialogue, or **Back**.

The toolbar searches by name and filters by category and by whether a product is retired; the
list sorts by category order, category name, order in the category or name.

## Hiding, showing and retiring

A product is in one of three states, and the badge beside its name says which when it is not on
the till.

- **On the till** is live: the till draws its buttons.
- **Hidden** is off the till for now and can come back. A new product starts here, and **Hide**
  puts one here for a night the bar is not selling it.
- **Retired** is off the till for good. Retiring is one way for anything that has been sold;
  a product that has never sold can be retired and brought back, but has no reason to be.

**Put on the till** refuses until the product has at least one serving size that is not retired,
and refuses while any of its sizes still depletes a stocked item that has since been retired: the
refusal names the ingredient, and the fix is to change the recipe or put the item back.

## Serving sizes

One stocked thing sells at many sizes: a bottle of wine as Bottle, 125ml, 175ml or 250ml; a
spirit as Single or Double. A size carries what pouring it depletes and its own dated price, and
no size is ever stored on the product itself.

![A product's serving sizes with the product name (1), the add button (2), the depletion button (3), the choice button (4), the prices button (5) and the Filters button, where the retired sizes switch lives (6)](/images/docs/bar/serving-sizes.png)

1. **The product name** and its category. **Every product** goes back to the list.
2. **Add a serving size** asks for a **Serving kind**, a **Label** and an **Order on the till**.
   A product sells each serving kind once, because the kind is what a category default price
   resolves on. The label is what the till button says.
3. **What it depletes** is the recipe: each line is a stocked item and a quantity in that item's
   own unit. A size with no lines depletes no stock when it sells. Depletion is independent of
   price, so a double may deplete twice a single without costing twice as much.
4. **Add a choice** (or **Change choice**, once one is attached) is a cocktail's mixer: a
   choice group the till prompts for when the size is added to a sale.
5. **Prices** opens the size's dated price history and the form for a new row.
6. **Including retired sizes** hides or shows retired sizes in the list.

The **Price today** column shows what the till charges now, with a badge saying whether that is
the size's **Own price** or a **Category default**. *Not priced* means neither exists, and the
till refuses to sell the size rather than guess.

Under each size, **sells N more at current stock** says what the stock register still supports:
the tightest ingredient decides, and a size with a choice counts whichever option is best stocked,
since the customer picks one. A size that depletes nothing says nothing. Beside it, a badge names
any ingredient that has been **retired** or is **out of stock**, which is what to fix before the
product can go back on the till.

### Setting a price

1. Press **Prices** on the size.
2. Enter the **Price** in pounds. It is held in pence and formatted only when shown.
3. Choose **In force from**: today for a correction that applies now, a future date for one that
   waits.
4. Press **Set this price**.

Prices are dated rows and nothing is ever overwritten: the latest row on or before today wins, so
a mistake is corrected with a new row dated today, and the history beneath the form shows every
row with **In force today** on the one the till is reading.

### Changing what a size depletes

1. Press **What it depletes**.
2. A size with a choice group attached (a cocktail's mixer, say) shows it as a read-only line: it
   is set from the size's own choice, on a screen of its own, not edited or removed here.
3. **Add an ingredient**, choose the **Stocked item** and enter the quantity in its unit. Take a
   line out with the cross beside it.
4. Press **Save it**.

Sales from now on deplete the new recipe. Movements already written stay as they are: the history
is never restated. Saving here never touches an attached choice group, whether it is shown or the
recipe list beside it is empty.

### Attaching a choice

A choice group is a named set of stocked items a size can offer a choice of, such as a spirit's
mixer or a cocktail's base. A size offers at most one; attaching a new one replaces the last.

1. Press **Add a choice** (or **Change choice**, once one is already attached).
2. Pick an existing **Choice group**, or press **Add a new choice group** to build one without
   leaving the form: give it a **Name**, add at least one option and its quantity in the item's
   own unit, then press **Add it**. The new group returns you to the form, already chosen.
3. Set the **Quantity** the chosen option depletes, and tick **Included in the price** for a free
   mixer, one that adds nothing to what the size already charges.
4. Press **Attach it**.

The till prompts for the choice the next time the size is added to a basket. **Clear choice**
removes it from the size without deleting the choice group itself, so it stays available to
attach elsewhere; press **Clear the choice** in the dialogue to go ahead.

### Retiring, restoring and deleting a size

**Retire** takes a size off the till without touching any line sold as it, any movement or any
price row, and asks first: press **Retire Pint** in the dialogue, or **Back**. **Put back**
restores it and asks nothing. **Delete** only appears on a size nothing has been sold as
and nothing has ever priced; once either is true, the size can only be retired. Editing a size
that has sold keeps its serving kind fixed: add a new size instead.

## Deleting a product

**Delete** appears only on a product that has never been sold. The confirmation says what goes
with it: the product, leaving its category and the rest of the list as they are. A category
with products in it cannot be deleted, so delete or move the products first.

## If something goes wrong

- **"cannot go on the till until it has a serving size"**: add one on the serving sizes screen,
  or put a retired one back, and try again.
- **"cannot go on the till while its recipe calls for ..., which is retired"**: a size depletes
  a stocked item that has been retired. Change that size's recipe, or put the item back on the
  stock register.
- **"has been sold, so it can only be retired"**: the delete is refused because lines in the
  ledger point at it. Retire it instead.
- **"has been sold, so it stays retired: add a new product rather than reviving this one"**: a
  retired product with sales behind it never comes back. Add it again as a new product.
- **"A product is already called ..."**: names are unique, ignoring case. Choose another.
- **"A stocked item is already called ..."** or **"A choice group is already called ..."**: the
  same rule on the other two names the guided set-up writes. Nothing was created; rename and press
  again.
- **"is measured, so it sells by a measure: say the size and how much it pours"**: a stocked item
  counted in millilitres was picked for a product sold as a whole thing. Choose a measure, or pick
  an item counted in whole items.
- **"A serving cannot pour more than a ... holds"**: a size pours more than the container size on
  the stocked item. Correct the measure, or the container size on the stock register.
- **"already sells as a ..."**: a product has one size per serving kind. Edit the existing size
  or pick a different kind.
- **"has been sold, so its serving kind is fixed: add a new size instead"**: the kind is what a
  sold line resolved on and cannot move under it.
- **"is retired, so nothing can be poured from it: put it back or choose another"**: a recipe
  line names a retired stocked item.
- **"has a price history, which is append-only, so it can only be retired"**: a priced size is
  never deleted.
- **"is retired, so it cannot be offered as a choice: put it back or choose another"**: an option
  in a new choice group names a retired stocked item.
- **"offers ..., which is retired: fix its options before attaching it"**: an existing choice
  group has a retired option. Fix the group before attaching it here.
- **"A choice group is already called ..."**: names are unique, ignoring case. Choose another.
- **"changed while you were editing it"**: somebody else changed the product's status first.
  Reload and look again.

## What happens next

Every change is written to the audit trail with what changed, from and to: a product added,
edited, deleted or moved between statuses, a size added, edited, retired or deleted, a recipe
changed and a price set. An allergen note is prose, so the trail records that it changed and
never what it says. A status change or a new price is read by the till the next time it draws
its buttons or prices a basket; nothing needs a deploy.
