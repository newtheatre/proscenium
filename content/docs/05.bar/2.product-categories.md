A product category groups products on the till and decides the order they appear in: Wine, Soft
drinks, Spirits. The list is at **Manage, Bar, Product categories**, and the Front of House Manager opens
it and changes it. A category can also carry default prices by serving
kind, so pricing a whole shelf is a handful of rows rather than one per bottle.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**The order here is the order on the till.** A category's place is read when the till draws its
buttons, so a move here is a save and never a release.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"A category is already called ..."**: names are unique, ignoring case. Choose another.
- **"has ... products in it: move or delete them first"**: the delete is refused while anything
  sits in the category.
- **"has a price history, which is append-only, so it cannot be deleted: rename it instead"**: a
  category that has ever carried a default price is kept for the sales that resolved on it.
- **"A colour is six hexadecimal characters after a hash"**: the colour field takes the form
  `#` followed by six characters from 0 to 9 and a to f.

## Putting the categories in order

**Order on the till**, at the top of the page, lists every category in the order the till shows
them, numbered from 1.

1. Drag a category by its row to where it belongs, or press its up or down arrow to move it one
   place. The arrows do the same from a keyboard or a phone.
2. The move saves as soon as it lands, and the rest renumber around it.

## Adding a product category

1. Press **Add a category**.
2. Give it a **Name**: what the till and every report call it.
3. Optionally give it a **Colour**, six hexadecimal characters after a hash, which the till uses
   to tell the groups apart. Clearing the field removes the colour.
4. Press **Add a product category**. It goes last on the till; drag it into place after.

![The categories list with the add button (1), the default prices button (2) and the edit button (3)](/images/docs/bar/categories.png)

1. **Add a category** opens the form above.
2. **Default prices** opens the category's dated price history by serving kind.
3. **Edit** changes the name and colour. The **Products** column counts what sits in the
   category, and **Delete** appears only when that count is 0.

The toolbar searches by name and sorts by order on the till or by name.

## Default prices by serving kind

A serving size with no price of its own resolves here, by its serving kind: every soft drink can
at one price, every spirit at one price as a single and another as a double. An explicit price on
the size always wins, and the serving sizes screen shows which level the till is reading with an
**Own price** or **Product category default** badge. Setting a product up with
[Set up a product](/docs/bar/products) copies these defaults in as each size's own price, so a
later change to a default reaches only sizes left without a price of their own: change an existing
product's price on its own serving sizes screen.

1. Press **Default prices** on the category.
2. Choose the **Serving kind** the default applies to.
3. Enter the **Price** in pounds and pence with a full stop, such as *2.50*. A comma, a third figure of pence or a minus is refused rather than guessed, and a price is at least 1p: a drink given away is a comp.
4. Choose **In force from**: today for a correction that applies now, a future date for one that
   waits.
5. Press **Set this default**. The serving kind stays chosen, so the next price goes on the same
   kind unless you change it. The message says whether the default is in force now, waiting for
   its date, or, for one dated before a later row, kept in the history only.

Prices are dated rows and nothing is ever overwritten. The history beneath the form lists every
row by serving kind with **In force today** on the one currently winning; a mistake is corrected
with a new row dated today.

## Deleting a category

**Delete** appears only on a category with no products in it. A category with products, or one
with a price history, cannot be removed: move or delete the products, or rename the category and
keep it.

## What happens next

A category added, moved, edited or deleted, and every default price set, is written to the audit trail
with what changed. The till reads the order, the colour and the default price the next time it
draws its buttons or prices a basket.
