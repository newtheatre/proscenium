The bar reports read the ledger and the movement history for a period and show what sold, what
it cost, what the stocktakes found, and what was given away. They are at **Manage, Bar,
Reports** and open for `bar.read` or `finance.read`: the Bar manager, the Treasurer and
administrators. The Treasurer reads them without seeing the catalogue or the stock register.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Nothing here is a stored total.** Every figure is queried fresh from the ledger and the
movement history when the screen asks, so a correction, a reversal or a late stocktake shows
the moment it lands.
::

## Choosing a period

![The reports screen with the period kind (1), the range fields (2), the refresh button (3), an export button (4) and the gross profit summary (5)](/images/docs/bar/reports.png)

1. **The period kind**: NIGHT, WEEK, SEASON or CUSTOM.
2. **The dates** the kind asks for. The screen opens on a custom range of today to today.
3. **Refresh** runs the report again; it also reruns whenever the period changes.
4. **Export CSV** on each section downloads that section for the same period.
5. **The gross profit summary**: revenue, cost and the difference.

Every period runs on the London calendar.

- **NIGHT** is one show night: from 04:00 on the date chosen to 04:00 the next morning, so a
  sale after midnight belongs to the night it was part of.
- **WEEK** is the seven days starting on the day chosen.
- **SEASON** is the committee year ending in the year typed: 1 August of the year before to
  31 July.
- **CUSTOM** is from the first date to the last, both included.

## The sections

- **Sales**: one row per category, product and serving size sold, with the quantity and the
  revenue.
- **Gross profit**: revenue against the delivered cost of what was depleted. The summary line
  gives revenue, cost and gross profit; the table beneath shows each stocked item, the quantity
  depleted and its cost, priced from the deliveries recorded on the stock register. An item whose
  deliveries carry no cost contributes nothing to the cost side.
- **Stocktake variance**: for every stocktake applied in the period, each item's quantity
  variance and its value.
- **Comps**: each comp given on the till, with its reason, who approved it and the value
  foregone.
- **Discounts**: each discount, its percentage, how many times it was applied and what was given
  away.

## Exporting

1. Choose the period.
2. Press **Export CSV** on the section you want.
3. The file opens in a new tab or downloads. Money is formatted as pounds in the file; the
   comps and variance exports also carry when each row happened.

The export is guarded against a spreadsheet treating a cell as a formula, so a product name
beginning with an equals sign arrives as text.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A custom range must end after it starts"**: the *to* date is before the *from* date. Swap
  them.
- **"Give the date as YYYY-MM-DD"**: the date field was cleared or typed in another shape. Pick
  the date again.
- **"That is not a day on the calendar"**: the date has the right shape but names no day, such as
  a 31st in a thirty-day month. Pick the date again.
- **The report could not be read**: the sign-in has lapsed or the account holds neither
  `bar.read` nor `finance.read`. Sign in again, or ask an administrator about the role.

## What happens next

Reading a report writes nothing. Each export is recorded in the audit trail with the section and
the period it covered, never the figures.
