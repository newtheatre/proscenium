The order list compares what is on hand with each stocked item's par level and lists the
shortfalls, so ordering before a show week starts from the register rather than a walk round the
store. It is at **Manage, Bar, Order list**, and the Bar Manager reads it. It is worked out fresh every time the screen opens: nothing is stored.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Advisory only.** This list is a comparison, not an order: nothing here places one. Sending it
to a supplier is a person's job.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Nothing is short against its par level"**: not a refusal. Everything with a par level is at
  or above it.
- **The list is empty and so is "No par level set"**: no stocked items exist yet, or every one
  is retired. Add what the bar counts on the stock register.

## Reading the list

![The order list with the export button (1) and a category group (2)](/images/docs/bar/order-list.png)

1. **Export CSV** downloads the same shortfalls as a file named for today's date.
2. **A group**, headed by the free-text category set on the stocked item. Items with no category
   sit under *Uncategorised*, and two spellings of one category that differ only in case fall
   into one group.

Each row shows the item, what is **On hand**, its **Par** and the **Shortfall**, all in the
item's own unit. An item that is at or above par is not listed; when nothing is short, the screen
says so.

**No par level set** lists every item left out of the comparison for want of a par level, so
a missing level is in front of you rather than silent. Each name links straight to that item, found
on the stock register, so giving it a par level is one press away rather than a search.

## Exporting

1. Press **Export CSV**.
2. The file opens in a new tab or downloads, one row per shortfall with the category, the item,
   on hand, par and the shortfall.

## What happens next

Nothing is written. The list changes as movements are recorded, a stocktake is applied, or a par
level is changed on the stock register.
