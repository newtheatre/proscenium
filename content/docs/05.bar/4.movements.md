The movement history is every change to stock the system has ever recorded, in the order it
happened: deliveries and wastage recorded on the stock register, sales and comps written by the
till, adjustments posted by an applied stocktake, and reversals. It is at **Manage, Bar,
Movements** and needs `bar.read` to open; reversing a movement needs `bar.write`. Both are held by
the Bar manager role.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Every movement stays exactly as it was written.** Nothing here can be edited or deleted. A
mistake is corrected with a reversing movement that names the original, and both rows remain, so
any on-hand figure can be traced to its causes.
::

## Reading the history

![The movement history with the search field (1), the filters (2) and a reverse button (3)](/images/docs/bar/movements.png)

1. **Search** by the stocked item's name.
2. **Filters** narrow the list to one stocked item or to one kind of movement, and the sort
   runs by when it happened, newest first, or by the order it was recorded in.
3. **Reverse** appears on a movement that can still be reversed.

Each row says when the movement happened, on the London clock, which stocked item it was
against, **What happened** with the reason where one was given, the signed **Quantity** in the
item's own unit, and for a delivery the **Cost a unit** that gross profit is measured against. A
movement written by something other than this screen says where it came from beneath the item's
name, so a sale line or a stocktake line can be found.

The kinds are: **Delivery**, **Sale**, **Comp**, **Stocktake adjustment**, **Wastage**,
**Transfer**, **Adjustment** and **Reversal**. Only a delivery, wastage, an adjustment and a
reversal are ever entered by hand; a sale, a comp and a stocktake adjustment are written by the
screen that owns them, and the history says so if anybody tries. A transfer is named in the
vocabulary but posts nowhere yet: it belongs to moving stock between two venue bars, which waits
for a multi-venue bar to exist. The movements route refuses one by design, the same "not by hand"
refusal a sale or a comp gets.

## Reversing a movement

A reversal writes a second movement cancelling the first: the same stocked item, the opposite
quantity, and a reason. The original stays exactly where it is, and on hand is the sum across
both.

1. Find the movement and press **Reverse**. The confirmation names the item, the quantity and
   when it was recorded.
2. Choose a **Reason** from the list. It starts on *Count correction*.
3. Press **Reverse it**.

A movement can be reversed once. A reversal cannot itself be reversed: if the reversal was the
mistake, record what actually happened as a new delivery, wastage or adjustment from the stock
register.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That movement has already been reversed"**: two people reached for the same row; the first
  reversal stands. Reload and look again.
- **"A reversal is not itself reversed: record what actually happened instead"**: undoing a
  correction would hide it behind another. Record the true movement from the stock register.
- **"is retired: put it back before moving stock against it"**: the movement's item has been
  retired since. Put the item back on the stock register, then reverse.
- **"movements are posted by ..., not by hand"**: a sale, comp, stocktake or transfer movement is
  written only by the screen that owns it.

## What happens next

The reversal is a new row in the history with its own time, actor and reason, and on hand for the
item moves at once everywhere it is shown: the stock register, the order list and the next
stocktake's expected figure. The row is the record; nothing else is written.
