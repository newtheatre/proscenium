The **Emergency** card is the venue's own: the address, what3words, exits and assembly point,
first aid, isolation points and any notes, written by the Front of house manager on
[the console](/docs/rota/emergency-cards) and read here under pressure by somebody who has never
read it before. It opens from the hub's red **Emergency** tile to any of tonight's three shifts,
and to either officer without one.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-wifi-off" color="info"}
**It works with no signal.** The card is saved on the phone the moment any show-night screen
opens tonight, not only when this one is visited, so it opens instantly afterwards and survives a
dropped connection. The line at the foot says when the copy you are reading was filed.
::

## Reading the card

![The card with the address to read to 999 (1), evacuation (2), first aid (3) and when it was filed (4)](/images/docs/show-night/emergency.png)

1. **Read to 999** is the address in large type, with the what3words underneath where one is
   filed. If no address has been filed the card says so and tells you to ask the duty manager.
2. **Evacuation**: the exits, and the assembly point.
3. **First aid**: where the kit is, where the defibrillator is, and who is first-aid trained
   tonight as the card records it.
4. **Filed** on the date and time the card was last written. Below it come **Isolation
   points** (with the fire panel) and **Notes** where the card carries them.

There is nothing to press: the screen is for reading aloud.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This venue has no emergency card yet"**: nothing has been filed for this venue. Tell the
  Front of house manager; the address must come from somebody who knows it.
- **"No emergency card is cached on this device yet, and none could be read just now."**: the
  first visit on this phone had no connection. Any show-night screen opened with signal, once,
  fixes it for the rest of the night.
- **"No address has been filed for …"**: a card exists without an address. Ask the duty
  manager, and tell the front of house officer afterwards.

## What happens next

Nothing is written from this screen. Each edit on the console is a new version of the card; the
phone shows the newest it has managed to fetch, and says when that was.
