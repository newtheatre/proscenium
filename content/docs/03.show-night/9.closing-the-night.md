An evening ends in a fixed order, because each step gates the next: the bar answers every SumUp
hand-off, closes the till against the reader, the duty manager works the post-show checklist
and closes the night, and the night report freezes. The bar's steps need tonight's bar shift or
the Bar manager; the duty manager's need tonight's duty manager shift or the Front of house
manager.

## Answering SumUp hand-offs

[The till](/docs/show-night/the-till) lists every hand-off to the SumUp app that nobody has
answered for under **Unanswered SumUp payments**, with the figure, the time, who started it and
its state. The till will not close while one is waiting.

1. For each one, find out whether the reader took the money.
2. Press **It went through** (with the transaction code from the SumUp app if you have it) or
   **It did not**.
3. One marked **Taken on the reader, not recorded** is a mismatch: the reader has money the
   ledger could not record. Fix the cause and answer again, or refund on the reader and abandon
   it with a note saying what happened to the money. A mismatch does not block the close; it is
   a reconciliation fact.

## Closing the till

1. On the till, open the overflow menu (top right) and press **Close till**. It is not a
   per-sale action, so it is not one of the pinned buttons. The dialogue works out what the
   ledger expects the reader to show for this session: card sales, tab settlements, comps
   (count and value foregone), discounts given, refunds and tab charges (credit extended, no
   money moved), with the desk's takings shown alongside for the whole-day figure. Only what was
   rung up on this session counts, so a second bar open in another venue reconciles separately.
2. Read the Z figure off the reader and key it into the field.
3. If it differs from the expected figure the dialogue says by how much, over or under, and asks
   why. Write the note.
4. Press **Confirm close**, or **Back** to leave the session open. The expected figure, the
   actual figure, the variance and the note are stored with the session, append-only.

A session forgotten past 04:00 does not appear on the till the next day, and no screen lists
it yet. Tell the Bar manager, whose role is the one thing that can still close it.

## The post-show checklist

Open [the checklist](/docs/show-night/checklists) from **Close the night** on
[Tonight at a glance](/docs/show-night/at-a-glance). Tick each post-show item, or close it over
with a reason, and press **Close the night**. The screen confirms **Tonight is closed, by …**.

## The night report

The report compiles itself from the evening's data; nobody types it. It carries attendance
against sold (admitted, no-shows, walk-ups), takings by tender split between desk and bar, the
incidents and near misses with their severities, the Challenge 25 counts, the milestone
timeline from the backstage board, the staffing (each shift's holder, unfilled slots, and any
officer who opened a screen with no shift, flagged), the bar summary from the same computation
the till close used, the checklist with every exception's reason, and a count of verified access
bookings and nothing more about them.

There is no screen for signing it off by hand yet. Any performance still unsigned 24 hours after
its show night ends is closed by the system: the report freezes marked as closed automatically,
with no human signatory, listing any checklist items left unmet, and is emailed to the standing
recipients the committee has configured. A second report for the same performance is impossible.

## If something goes wrong

- **"… SumUp payments are still waiting for an answer. Resolve them on the till first."**:
  answer them, above.
- **"The reader read £…; we expect £…. That difference needs a note before it can be
  recorded."**: the variance field was left blank.
- **"That session is already closed"**: somebody closed it before you opened this screen.
- **"Somebody else closed this session first, so this reading was not recorded. Read the till
  before recording anything."**: two people confirmed at once and the other one landed. Their Z
  figure and their note are the ones on the record, not yours.
- **"A SumUp payment landed while this close was in flight, so nothing was recorded. Answer it on
  the till and close again."**: a hand-off started in the moment between the check above and the
  close itself. Answer it, then close.
- **"The checklist cannot close while … is still open: tick it or record an exception"**: a required checklist
  item is unanswered.

## What happens next

The till close, the checklist close and the report are each in the audit trail. The frozen
report is immutable; a later correction is an addendum, distributed the same way. The
reconciliation figures feed [the finance reports](/docs/money/reconciliation).
