**Tonight at a glance** is the duty manager's screen. It opens from the hub tile of that name to
whoever holds tonight's duty manager shift, and to the Front of house manager without one. It
answers the questions a foyer asks at 19:20: how full are we, can we admit the pass holders, how
long is it, who do I ring.

## Reading the screen

![The glance with the performance switcher (1), the numbers (2), pass pressure (3), show info (4) and the Last synced line (5)](/images/docs/show-night/glance.png)

1. **The performance switcher** appears on a matinee day. The hub hands over the house you
   chose there; otherwise the clock picks the one whose doors are open now.
2. **The numbers**: reserved, collected (through the door), to come (reserved and not yet
   arrived) and walk-ups OK (seats nobody has reserved). A capped house shows the percentage
   collected or expected as a bar; an uncapped one says there is no percentage to read.
3. **Pass pressure** sets the passes covering tonight against the seats left and gives one of
   three answers: admit freely, admit and watch the walk-up queue, or more passes than seats,
   so admit in order of arrival and send walk-ups to the bar.
4. **Show info**: running time and intervals, the latecomer policy, the age guidance and content
   warnings, any notes, and the doors time.
5. **Last synced** is when the figures were fetched. The screen refreshes every 20 seconds; a
   dropped connection keeps the last figures on screen under a warning.

Below these:

- **Comp requests** appears only while something is waiting. Each row says whether it came from
  the box office (**Ticket**) or the till (**Bar**, with the money it gives away and the round
  asked for), the reason given and who asked. **Approve** decides it there and then; **Decline**
  asks for a reason, which goes on the record and is shown to whoever asked. Your own request
  carries no buttons: somebody else decides it. A bar ask records the house it was made at, worked
  out from the bar's own hours, and says so on the row: on a two-house day **Asked at** tells you
  which. The queue itself is the venue's whole night whichever house you are reading, because an
  ask lapses in minutes and one waiting under the other house would never be seen. An ask made
  while the bar was open with nothing running names no house, and the row says nothing extra. See
  [the desk](/docs/box-office/the-desk) and [the till](/docs/show-night/the-till) for the asking
  half.
- **Access tonight** lists, by first name and party size, the people whose verified access
  profile has agreed door wording, and the wording itself, so they are met with the right words.
  What the profile declares never appears here.
- **On tonight** is the rota for this performance: duty manager, door and bar, with a call
  button where the person has chosen to share their number. An unfilled slot says **Unfilled**.
- **Backstage code** is hidden until you press **Show tonight's code**, and **Hide** puts it
  away. It is read out loud to the crew and never emailed or notified; see
  [the backstage board](/docs/show-night/backstage-board).

**Close the night** at the foot of the screen opens [the checklist](/docs/show-night/checklists).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This needs a confirmed duty manager shift on one of tonight's performances, or the front
  of house manager's role"**: you hold a door or bar shift, which sees the hub's tiles but not
  this screen. The person named has it.
- **"Nothing running tonight."**: no performance at this venue tonight.
- **"Showing what was last loaded: …"**: the connection dropped. The figures stand until the
  next successful refresh.
- **The backstage code will not show**: the code needs duty manager authority, and the block
  says why it was refused.
- **"You cannot decide your own request"**: a comp you asked for yourself is decided by somebody
  else, whichever screen you ask from.
- **"That request has lapsed; ask again"**: the request sat longer than the window allows. The
  person who asked raises a fresh one.
- **A comp request disappears before you decide it**: somebody else decided it, or it lapsed.
  The queue is refreshed on the same 20 seconds as the figures.

## What happens next

Approving or declining a comp is written here, with the decision, the decider and a declined
request's reason on the record. Nothing else is written from this screen except the reveal of the
backstage code, which is served on request and never stored on the phone. The numbers are the same ones the night report compiles
from, so what you read here is what the report will say.
