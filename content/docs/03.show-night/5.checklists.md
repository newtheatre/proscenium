The **Checklist** is the duty manager's: the venue's pre-show items before the house opens, its
post-show items after, and the **Close the night** action that needs every required item
answered. It opens from **Close the night** at the foot of
[Tonight at a glance](/docs/show-night/at-a-glance), to whoever holds tonight's duty manager
shift and to the Front of house manager without one. The items themselves are configured per
venue on [the console](/docs/rota/checklists); this screen only works through them.

## Working through the list

![The checklist (1) and the Close the night action (2)](/images/docs/show-night/checklist.png)

1. **The checklist**, in two phases, **Pre-show** and **Post-show**. Each item is one of:
   - **Hand-ticked**: press **Tick**. The list shows who ticked it.
   - **System-verified**: it ticks itself from the live data and shows **clear** or **not yet
     clear**. Nobody can tick it by hand. Two exist: **No-show holds released** clears once no
     unpaid hold is left on this performance, and **Tonight's incidents reviewed** clears once
     every incident logged against this performance carries a review. A system-verified item
     cannot be closed over either, so on a night with an incident logged, mark each entry
     reviewed on [Contacts and incidents](/docs/show-night/incidents) before closing.
   - **(optional)**: a tick is welcome but the night closes without it.
2. **Close the night** is enabled until the night is closed, and refuses while any required item
   is neither ticked nor closed over.

The list is stamped from the venue's configuration the first time it is read tonight, so a change
to the configuration during the evening does not move the goalposts under you.

## Recording an exception

An item that cannot be done tonight is **closed over** rather than left blank: press **Close
over**, say why, and press **Record it**. The list shows **Closed over** with the reason, the
reason prints in the night report, and the item counts as answered for closing.

## Closing the night

Press **Close the night**. The screen says **Tonight is closed, by …** and the action greys out.
A close is once per performance: a second press, or another duty manager's, reads back the first.
The whole order of an evening's end is on [Closing the night](/docs/show-night/closing-the-night).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Cannot close: … still needs completing or an exception recorded"**: the items named are
  required and unanswered. Tick them or close over them.
- **"This performance is already closed"**: somebody closed it first. Reload and the banner says
  who.
- **"That item cannot be ticked: it may already be ticked, exempted, or system-verified"**:
  reload; the list has moved on since it was fetched.
- **"Say why, because an exception needs a reason on the record"**: the reason field was empty.
- **"More than one performance is running tonight: name the performance"**: a matinee day. This
  screen has no performance picker yet, so on such a day the checklist cannot be opened from the
  phone; the hub's warning banner still reads it.
- **"Nothing is running tonight, so there is no checklist"**: no performance at this venue.

## What happens next

Every tick, exception and close is in the audit trail with who and when. From house open, the
hub shows **Pre-show checklist incomplete** naming any required pre-show item still open. The
close is the gate the night report's sign-off stands behind.
