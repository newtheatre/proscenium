**Contacts and incidents** opens from the hub tile of that name to any of tonight's three shifts
(door, bar, duty manager), and to the Front of house manager or the Bar manager without one. The
top half is who is on tonight; the bottom half is the incident log.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-shield-alert" color="warning"}
**People by role, never by name.** An incident entry is read by the committee and lands in the
night report in full. "The person on the door" and "a member of the audience" are the record;
names are not.
::

## Ringing somebody

![The screen with the On tonight roster (1), the Log incident button (2), the log (3) and Report a near miss (4)](/images/docs/show-night/incidents.png)

1. **On tonight** lists the duty manager, door and bar shifts for tonight's performances. A call
   button appears only where that member has chosen, on their own profile, to share their number
   with the shift; tapping it opens the phone's dialler. An unfilled slot stays on the list as
   **unfilled** so it reads as a gap in the rota, not as somebody to ring.
2. **Log incident** opens the full form.
3. **The log** is tonight's entries, newest first, each with its time, who logged it, its
   category and its severity. An entry the duty manager has acknowledged reads **reviewed**.
4. **Report a near miss** is the one-tap version.

## Logging an incident

1. Press **Log incident**.
2. Choose the **Performance** on a matinee day (a single performance is chosen for you).
3. Choose a **Category**: medical, behaviour, safety, security, property or other.
4. Choose a **Severity**: note, near miss, incident or serious.
5. Write **What happened** and press **Log it**. The time is now and the reporter is you.

## Reporting a near miss

A near miss is a category and one sentence: press **Report a near miss**, pick the category,
say what nearly happened, press **Report it**. There is no severity to choose and nothing else
waits on it.

## Marking an entry reviewed

**Mark reviewed** under an entry acknowledges it for the post-show checklist. It shows to
tonight's duty manager and to the Front of house manager without a shift, and only while the
entry is not already reviewed. It is an acknowledgement, not a resolution: a follow-up the
committee owes is tracked on [Safety](/docs/rota/safety), not here.

## Correcting an entry

Press **Correct this entry** under the entry, change what was wrong and press **File the
correction**. The original stays on the list marked **superseded**, and the new entry says
**corrects an earlier entry**. Nothing is edited in place.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Nobody is rostered on tonight yet."**: the rota for tonight has no confirmed shift.
- **"That entry has already been corrected: correct the correction instead"**: find the newer
  entry on the list and correct that one.
- **The Log incident button is greyed out**: nothing tonight authorises you, and the warning
  above the log says which shift or role would.
- **"Nothing logged yet tonight."**: an empty log, not a fault.

## What happens next

An entry at a severity the committee has marked for follow-up notifies every holder of the
Safety Officer's permission at once. Every entry lands in the night report, superseded ones
included. The post-show checklist's **Tonight's incidents reviewed** item reads this log: press
**Mark reviewed** on each entry before closing, and the item clears once every one of tonight's
entries carries a review. The button shows to the duty manager alone, and the entry then reads
**reviewed**; see [Checklists](/docs/show-night/checklists).
