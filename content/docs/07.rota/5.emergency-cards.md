The emergency card is what somebody on a show-night phone reads when the worst happens: the address
to give a 999 call handler, the exits, the assembly point, where the first aid kit and the
defibrillator are. Every venue has one, and this screen is where it is written. It is at
**Manage, Rota, Emergency cards**. Seeing the cards needs the `emergency-card.read`
permission and changing them needs `emergency-card.write`; the Front of house manager role holds
both.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Every edit is a new version, never a rewrite.** What front of house reads tonight is whichever
version was filed last, and nothing here can lose an earlier one. The screen shows when the
current version was filed and by whom.
::

## Filing a venue's card

The table lists every venue with its address, its assembly point and the date of its current
version. A venue with no card yet reads "Not filed".

![The emergency cards screen with the search (1), the venue table (2) and a venue's Set it up or Edit button (3)](/images/docs/rota/emergency.png)

1. **Search** by venue name, or filter to venues that are filed or not yet.
2. **Each row** is one venue and its current card.
3. Press **Set it up** on a venue with no card, or **Edit** to file a new version.

The card is read in the dark, so keep every field short and plain.

![The card dialog with the address (1), the assembly point (2), the exits (3) and the isolation points (4)](/images/docs/rota/emergency-card.png)

1. **Address** is the one required field. Write it to be read aloud to a 999 call handler,
   including the postcode, because that is exactly what it is for.
2. **Assembly point**: where everybody goes.
3. **Exits**: which doors, and where they lead.
4. **Isolation points**: gas, electricity, water.

Below those are the optional fields: **First aid kit**, **Defibrillator**, **First aiders** (who to
find on a normal night), **Fire panel**, **what3words** and **Notes**. Press **Save as a new
version** when the card is right.

## If something goes wrong

- **"the address is what gets read to a 999 handler"**: the address is empty. A venue counts as
  filed only once its latest version carries one.
- **"No such venue"**: the venue was removed since the page loaded.

## What happens next

A save writes a new card version and a `emergency-card.updated` audit row against the venue. The
next time any show-night screen opens at that venue, the new version is cached on the device, so it
is readable there with no connection at all, and the phone shows the date it is as of.
