The emergency card is what somebody on a show-night phone reads when the worst happens: the address
to give a 999 call handler, the exits, the assembly point, where the first aid kit and the
defibrillator are. Every venue has one, and this screen is where it is written. It is at
**Manage, Rota, Emergency cards**, and the Front of House Manager and the Theatre Manager read
the cards and write them.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Every edit is a new version, never a rewrite.** What front of house reads tonight is whichever
version was filed last, and nothing here can lose an earlier one. The screen shows when the
current version was filed and by whom.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"the address is what gets read to a 999 handler"**: the address is empty. A venue counts as
  filed only once its latest version carries one.
- **"who to ring first needs both a name and a number, or neither"**: only one of **Ring first**
  and **Their number** is filled in. Fill in both, or clear both to leave the card calling 999.
- **"the number to ring first is digits and spaces, with a plus in front if it needs one"**: the
  number has something a phone cannot dial, such as a hyphen or a word, or has fewer than three
  or more than fifteen digits. Write it as you would dial it.
- **"the number to ring first is who you call before 999, so it cannot be 999 itself"**: the
  card always offers 999 (and 112 is the same call), so leave both fields blank instead.
- **"That venue is no longer here."**: the venue was removed since the page loaded.

## Filing a venue's card

The table lists every venue with its address to read to 999, its assembly point and the date of
its current version. A venue with no card yet reads "Not filed". An external venue is listed only once one of
its performances from tonight onwards has a shift on the [Rota board](/docs/rota/rota-board), or
while it already has a card: until we staff a night there, its own building's procedures apply.

![The emergency cards screen with the search (1), the venue table (2) and a venue's Set up the card or Edit button (3)](/images/docs/rota/emergency.png)

1. **Search** by venue name, or filter to venues that are filed or not yet.
2. **Each row** is one venue and its current card.
3. Press **Set up the card** on a venue with no card, or **Edit** to file a new version.

The card is read in the dark, so keep every field short and plain.

![The card dialogue with the address to read to 999 (1), the assembly point (2), the exits (3) and the isolation points (4)](/images/docs/rota/emergency-card.png)

1. **Address to read to 999** is the one required field. Write it to be read aloud to a 999 call
   handler, or to whoever the card rings first, the postcode included: that is exactly what it
   is for. A venue's first card starts from its address for audiences on
   [Venues](/docs/box-office/venues); change it wherever the door an ambulance should come to
   differs, such as a stage door. From then on the card keeps
   its own, and changing the venue's address never changes it.
2. **Assembly point**: where everybody goes.
3. **Exits**: which doors, and where they lead.
4. **Isolation points**: gas, electricity, water.

Under the address, **Ring first** and **Their number** say who to call before 999, both or
neither. At a campus venue (the Studio, Studio Live, the Trent Building and the Monica Partridge
Building) that is estates security: fill in the name volunteers should see, such as "Estates
Security", and the number as you would dial it. The card on the night then reads the address to
them, offers **Call Estates Security** first and keeps **Call 999** beside it. Left blank, the
card calls 999 first, as every card did before. Give a desk or service line, never somebody's own
mobile: the card is kept for good. The number is on each card rather than kept once, so if the
security line changes, edit each campus venue's card.

Below those are the optional fields: **First aid kit**, **Defibrillator**, **First aiders** (who to
find on a normal night), **Fire panel**, **what3words** and **Notes**. Press **Save as a new
version** when the card is right.

Once the IT Manager sets **Training that makes a first aider** to the first aid course in the
training catalogue, the card names tonight's first aiders itself: whoever on tonight's confirmed
team at the venue holds a current record of it. The **First aiders** line then shows only on a
night when nobody on the rota does, under "No trained first aider is on tonight's rota", so write
it as what to do then.

## What happens next

A save files a new version of the card and shows on the audit trail as **Venue emergency card
updated**, against the venue. The
next time any show-night screen opens at that venue, the new version is cached on the device, so it
is readable there with no connection at all, and the phone shows the date it is as of.
