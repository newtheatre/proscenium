This is the rota itself: one card per upcoming performance, one row per shift on it, and who holds
each. It is at **Manage, Rota, Rota board** and needs the `rota.write` permission, which
the Front of house manager role holds. A confirmed shift is what lights up the show-night screens
for its holder that evening, so a row that is still open the day before is the thing to fix here.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
Members fill most of the rota themselves. Every member sees the open shifts they currently qualify
for on **My rota**, claims one in a tap, and can release it up to the start of its show night. This
screen is for what self-service leaves behind: a gap close to the night, a claim to confirm, a
person to swap.
::

## Reading the board

The board shows the next run of performances that have not yet run, nearest first, and every shift
stamped on them. A performance with nothing stamped is not on the board at all; it shows on
[Shift templates](/docs/rota/shift-templates) as a venue with no template, and in the seven-day
digest.

![The rota board with a performance card (1), an open shift's Assign button (2), the confirmed count (3) and Add a shift (4)](/images/docs/rota/board.png)

1. **Each card** is one performance: its date, time and show, then a row per shift showing the
   role, who holds it and its status. An open or declined row is outlined in amber.
2. **Assign** appears on an open or declined shift.
3. **The footer** counts confirmed shifts against the total, and reads "Fully staffed" or "Needs
   people".
4. **Add a shift** adds a one-off shift outside the venue's template.

A shift is in one of five states. **Open** names nobody and is claimable. **Claimed** is waiting for
[Approvals](/docs/rota/approvals). **Confirmed** is tonight's shift. **Declined** keeps the
claimant's name on the record but nobody on the shift. **Cancelled** follows a cancelled
performance and is not shown.

## Assigning somebody

1. Press **Assign** on the open or declined shift.
2. Type at least two characters of a name or an email address. Up to ten matches are listed, each
   marked **Eligible** or **Locked** against the role's training module, checked live.
3. Choose an eligible person and press **Assign**.

![The assign dialog with the candidate search (1) and the Assign button (2)](/images/docs/rota/board-assign.png)

1. **The search** matches name or address, and shows each candidate's eligibility as a badge.
2. **Assign** stays disabled until an eligible person is chosen.

An officer's assignment is confirmed by definition: it never joins the approvals queue. The same
eligibility gate applies as when a member claims, so an assignment cannot put somebody a training
gap would refuse onto the door. Assigning onto a shift somebody already holds replaces them in one
write, so there is never a moment with two confirmed duty managers on a performance.

## Confirming and standing down

- A **Claimed** shift carries **Confirm**, the same decision the approvals screen makes.
- A **Confirmed** shift carries **Unconfirm**, which stands it back down to open. This is the
  officer's call, distinct from a member releasing their own shift, and the holder is told.

## Adding a one-off shift

**Add a shift** on a performance card opens a dialog for a shift outside the venue's template: a
second door on a busy night, or a bar on a performance whose venue does not normally staff one.

1. Choose the **role**.
2. Give the **slot**: the role's ordinal on this performance, so 1 for the first door shift, 2 for
   the second. Each role and slot exists once per performance.
3. Optionally choose a **person**. Naming an eligible person confirms the shift at once; leaving it
   empty leaves the shift open for a member to claim.

## The reminders that chase a gap

Two scheduled jobs run against this board, so a gap is chased without anybody watching the screen.

- **Every morning at 07:00**, everybody who holds `rota.write` gets one digest of every performance
  inside the next seven days with an open or declined shift, an unconfirmed duty manager, or no
  shifts at all. An unconfirmed duty manager is flagged distinctly, because the night cannot legally
  run without one. A fully staffed week sends nothing.
- **Every morning at 10:00**, every holder of a confirmed shift on tomorrow's show night gets one
  reminder per shift with a calendar attachment carrying the call time (the venue's door time where
  one is set, otherwise curtain up).

A member releasing a shift also reaches the officers straight away, rather than waiting for the
next digest, when the performance is within {{ SHIFT_RELEASE_NOTICE_HOURS }} of starting, and
always when the shift released is the duty manager's.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Type at least two characters"** and **"Nobody matches that"**: the search needs more, or
  matched nobody who is active. Disabled and erased accounts are never listed.
- **"That member does not currently qualify for a door shift"** (or bar, or duty manager): the
  person is locked on that role. Either they need the module named on
  [Shift templates](/docs/rota/shift-templates), or the role has no module set yet, which locks
  everybody.
- **"That member already holds a shift on this performance"**: one person, one shift a night.
- **"That account is disabled and cannot be assigned a shift"**: reinstate the account first, or
  choose somebody else.
- **"This performance already has a confirmed duty manager"**: only one may be confirmed at a time.
  Unconfirm or reassign the existing one rather than adding a second.
- **"That slot on this performance is already on the rota"**: the role and slot you typed exist
  already. Use the next ordinal.
- **"Only a confirmed shift can be unconfirmed"** or **"This shift is already open"**: the shift
  changed between the board loading and the press. Refresh.
- **"This performance has been cancelled"**: a cancelled performance takes no shifts.

## What happens next

Every action here is audited with the officer's account: `shift.reassigned`, `shift.confirmed`,
`shift.unconfirmed` and `shift.added`. The person assigned is told the show, venue, date and role;
a person replaced or stood down is told they are off it. A confirmed shift opens the show-night
screens for its holder from 04:00 on the day, and closes them at 04:00 the next morning with nothing
to revoke.
