The theatre opens the bar on evenings with nothing on: an external hire, a society social, a
get-in. Money is taken, stock moves and ages are checked exactly as on a show night, so the
evening is planned and staffed exactly as a show night is. It is not a performance, and nothing
about it pretends to be: no listing, no attendance figure and no night report comes from one.

The screen is at **Manage, Rota, Bar openings**. Reading it needs `rota.read`; planning,
cancelling and standing somebody down need `rota.write`, which the Front of house manager role
holds.

This is the third of the rota's four screens, which run in order: shift templates, the rota board,
bar openings, then approvals. **Approve the claims** at the top of this screen is the way on to
the last one.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
A bar opening names no show, because there is none. Its label is what stands in for a show title
everywhere the rota shows it: on the member's own **My rota**, on the open-slot list and on the
till's venue picker.
::

## Planning one

![The bar openings screen with the plan dialog open: the venue (1), the evening (2), the hours (3) and the label (4)](/images/docs/rota/bar-opening-plan.png)

1. **Venue**: only venues whose shift template staffs a bar are offered, because a venue with no
   bar row stamps nothing. Set the template up first on
   [Shift templates](/docs/rota/shift-templates).
2. **Evening**: the date the bar opens. A bar still open after midnight belongs to the evening it
   opened on, the same 04:00 to 04:00 night the rest of the system uses.
3. **Opens** and **Closes**: the hours the bar is worked. A closing time earlier on the clock than
   the opening one means the next morning. The database refuses an opening that closes before it
   opens.
4. **What it is**: the label. Write what somebody scanning the rota would recognise, such as
   "Drama society social" or "Get-in, The Crucible".

Pressing **Plan the opening** stamps one bar slot per head of the venue's bar count, all open and naming
nobody, in the same write as the opening itself.

## Staffing it

The **Staffing** column lists every slot, its status and who holds it. Slots fill the same way
shifts do:

- A member claims one from their own **My rota**, under **Bar openings** in the open list. The
  claim confirms itself or waits for [Approvals](/docs/rota/approvals), the same setting that
  governs a shift.
- Two people claiming one slot at the same moment settle to exactly one winner; the other is told
  the slot has gone.
- One person holds one slot on an opening. Claiming a second is refused.
- **Stand down** takes somebody off a slot and returns it to open naming nobody. They are told.
  It asks first: press **Stand Alex Morgan down** in the dialogue, or **Back**. It is also how a
  declined claim is reopened.
- The member can give a slot back themselves with **Release** on their own My rota, and clear a
  declined claim with **Dismiss**, exactly as they can for a shift.

A confirmed slot opens the till at that venue for that night, exactly as a confirmed bar shift on
a performance does, and only between the opening's own hours.

## Cancelling one

**Cancel** asks first: press **Cancel the Friday late bar opening** in the dialogue, or **Back**.
It then cancels the opening and every slot on it. Whoever held a slot keeps their
name on it and is emailed; an unclaimed slot still names nobody. A cancelled opening holds no
authority: its slots stop opening the till on the next request.

Cancelling cannot be undone. Plan a new opening if the evening comes back.
