Incidents are logged on the night by whoever is working it. Which of them reach the safety officer
afterwards, and what happens to each until it is closed, is decided here. The screen is at
**Manage, Rota, Safety**. Reading it needs the `safety.read` permission and changing anything
needs `safety.write`; the Safety officer role holds both, and no other role does. The Front of
house manager cannot open this screen.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Nothing routes until you opt it in.** A severity with follow-up off notifies nobody when an
incident is logged at it. The incident is still in the log; it just does not come to you.
::

## Severity routing

Every incident carries one of four severities: **Note**, **Near miss**, **Incident** and
**Serious**. Each has a switch.

![The safety screen with the severity routing card (1), the Serious switch (2) and the open follow-ups (3)](/images/docs/rota/safety.png)

1. **Severity routing** lists the four severities with a switch each.
2. **A switch on** means an incident logged at that severity notifies the safety officer at once and
   joins the open follow-ups below until it is closed. A near miss reported from a show-night
   screen lands at the near-miss severity, so its switch decides whether those come to you too.
3. **Open follow-ups** lists every routed incident, from any night, that has not been closed.

The switch saves as soon as it is flipped. It changes what happens to incidents logged from then on;
an incident already open stays open.

## Closing a follow-up

Each open item shows its severity, its category, when it happened, who reported it and their
account of it.

1. Press **Close** on the item.
2. Write a **resolution note**: what was done, and what changes.
3. Press **Close it**.

The closure is its own entry, linked to the incident. The incident itself is never edited: like the
rest of the log it is append-only, and a correction on the night is a new entry that supersedes the
old one rather than a change to it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Give a resolution note"**: the note is empty. The dialog will not close without one.
- **"That entry is already closed, or does not exist"**: somebody closed it first, or the incident
  was superseded. Refresh the list.
- **"That severity is no longer here."**: the page and the server disagree about the vocabulary. Reload.

## What happens next

A routing change is audited as `incident-severity.routing-changed`, and a closure as
`incident-followup.closed` against the incident. Whether or not its follow-up is closed, an incident
at a routed severity is flagged on that night's report.
