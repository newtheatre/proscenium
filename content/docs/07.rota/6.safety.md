Incidents are logged on the night by whoever is working it. Which of them reach the Theatre
Manager afterwards, and what happens to each until it is closed, is decided here. The screen is at
**Manage, Rota, Safety**, and the Theatre Manager reads it and changes it. The President reads the
same screen, with the switches off and no **Close** button. No other role reaches it, the Front of
House Manager included. Both roles hold safety records, so they are privileged by default,
and a holder signing in with a password needs an authenticator app before the screen opens
(see [Roles](/docs/people/roles)); which roles are privileged is a setting.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
**Serious routes from the start; the rest wait for you.** A new install has **Serious** switched
on and the other three off. A severity with follow-up off notifies nobody when an incident is
logged at it. The incident is still in the log; it just does not come to you. If every switch is
turned off, the screen says so in a warning above the switches, and the console overview lists it
under **Set-up still to do** for whoever can change it.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"This role needs an authenticator app before it can be used"**: your role is privileged and
  you have not enrolled an authenticator. The alert links to where to set one up.
- **The switches are off and there is no Close button**: you are the President, who reads the
  screen without changing it.
- **"Give a resolution note"**: the note is empty. The dialogue will not close without one.
- **"That entry is already closed, or does not exist"**: somebody closed it first, or the incident
  was superseded. Refresh the list.
- **"That severity is no longer here."**: the page and the server disagree about the vocabulary. Reload.

## Severity routing

Every incident carries one of four severities: **Note**, **Near miss**, **Incident** and
**Serious**. Each has a switch.

![The safety screen with the severity routing card (1), the Serious switch (2) and the open follow-ups (3)](/images/docs/rota/safety.png)

1. **Severity routing** lists the four severities with a switch each. A screen reader names each
   switch by its severity, for example "Serious routes to follow-up".
2. **A switch on** means an incident logged at that severity notifies the Theatre Manager at once and
   joins the open follow-ups below until it is closed. A near miss reported from a show-night
   screen lands at the near-miss severity, so its switch decides whether those come to you too.
3. **Open follow-ups** lists every routed incident, from any night, that has not been closed.

The switch saves as soon as it is flipped. Switching a severity on puts every earlier incident at
it that has no closure into the open follow-ups at once, but sends no email for them: the
confirmation says how many joined, so read the list. Switching one off takes its incidents off
the list. Either way an email only ever goes for an incident logged after the switch is on.

## Who receives the email

The follow-up email goes to everybody holding a role that can change this screen: the Theatre
Manager, and every administrator (the IT Manager among them), since the administrator's role
holds every permission. The President reads the screen but is not emailed. The email says the
severity, the category, the venue and when it happened, and never what was written: read that on
the list.

## Closing a follow-up

Each open item shows its severity, its category, when it happened, who reported it and their
account of it.

1. Press **Close** on the item.
2. Write a **resolution note**: what was done, and what changes.
3. Press **Close the follow-up**.

The closure is its own entry, linked to the incident. The incident itself is never edited: like the
rest of the log nothing edits it, and a correction on the night is a new entry that replaces the
old one rather than a change to it.

## What happens next

The audit trail shows a routing change as **Severity follow-up routing changed**, and a closure
as **Incident follow-up closed** against the incident. Whether or not its follow-up is closed, an incident
at a routed severity is flagged on that night's report.
