Every Challenge 25 check made on a show night, at the bar or the door, is in a register nothing
can edit. This screen exports it. It is at **Manage, Rota, Age-check register**, and the Front of
House Manager exports it. A shift alone reads tonight's entries on the phone and never the
history: that is an officer's job, and every export is recorded.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
The register never names the person checked. Each entry is a physical description, the outcome,
the ID type accepted or the reason refused, the product where there was one, and who checked.
That is what makes it safe to hand over.
::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick a range that starts before it ends"**: the from date is after the to date. Both buttons
  stay disabled until the range is right.
- **The download opens as a blank tab or refuses**: the session has expired, or the account has lost
  the permission since the page loaded. Sign in again and reload.

## Exporting the register

![The export panel with the from date (1), the to date (2), Export CSV (3) and Export PDF (4)](/images/docs/rota/age-checks.png)

1. **From** and
2. **To** are the range, inclusive, by London date. Both open on today, so a single night is one
   press away.
3. **Export CSV** downloads a spreadsheet: one row per entry, in time order, with every column the
   register holds.
4. **Export PDF** downloads a formatted document for an inspection, headed with the venue, the
   period and the date and time it was generated.

Both cover the same rows. Superseded entries are included alongside their corrections, each
pointing at the other, so nothing that was ever written is left out. The file is named for the
range, for example `challenge-25-register-2026-09-01-to-2026-09-30.pdf`.

## What happens next

Every export shows on the audit trail as **Challenge 25 register exported**, with the range, the
format and how many entries it carried, which is what matches an inspection to what was handed
over. A cell in the CSV can never be read as a spreadsheet formula.
