When a member claims an open shift, the claim either confirms itself or waits here. Which one is a
setting: with auto-confirm on, a claim becomes a confirmed shift at once and this screen stays
empty; with it off, every claim queues for an officer's decision. Auto-confirm is currently set to
**{{ SHIFT_CLAIM_AUTO_CONFIRM }}**. The screen is at **Manage, Rota, Approvals**, and the Front
of House Manager decides what is on it.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
Changing the setting affects only future claims. A claim already waiting stays waiting until it is
decided here, and a claim already confirmed stays confirmed.
::

## Deciding a claim

The list shows every claim waiting, oldest performance first: the role and venue, the show and its
date and time, and who claimed it.

![The approvals screen with the search (1), the waiting claims (2) and the count of claims waiting (3)](/images/docs/rota/approvals.png)

1. **Search** by the claimant's name or the show, or filter by role or by night.
2. **Each row** is one claim on one shift, with **Confirm** and **Decline** at its right-hand end.
3. **The count** of claims waiting, across every page.

Confirming needs nothing more than the press. Declining opens a dialogue that asks why, and
the claimant is shown that reason word for word. Say what they can do about it: "The bar shift
went to somebody with the licensing module; the door is still open" is more use than "No".

A declined shift keeps the claimant's name on the record but takes them off the shift, and it stays
off the open list until an officer reassigns it from the [rota board](/docs/rota/rota-board)
or the member dismisses the decline from their own My rota, which returns it to open.

## Confirming from the rota instead

The same confirmation is available inline on the [rota board](/docs/rota/rota-board), where
a claimed shift carries a **Confirm** button. Either route writes the same record; this screen is
the one to use when there are several claims to work through.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Say why, because the claimant is shown it"**: the decline has no reason. Write one.
- **"Nobody has claimed this shift"**: the member released the claim between the list loading and
  the decision. The row disappears on refresh.
- **"This shift is already confirmed"** or **"This shift was already declined"**: another officer
  decided it first. Two officers deciding at once confirm it once between them; nobody's press is
  lost, and nothing is double-written.
- **"This shift has been cancelled"**: its performance was cancelled, which cancels every shift on it.
- **"This performance already has a confirmed duty manager"**: two people claimed the duty manager
  slot in queue mode and the other was confirmed first. Decline this one with a reason that says so.

## What happens next

A confirmation shows on the audit trail as **Claimed shift approved** and tells the claimant,
naming the show, venue, date and role; from that moment the shift opens the show-night screens for
them on the night, and they get the day-before reminder with its calendar attachment. A decline
shows as **Claimed shift declined** and tells the claimant the reason. The reason is kept with the
shift and never on the trail, so closing an account can remove it and still leave the record that
the claim was decided.
