No, not to come and see a show. An account is for people who get involved with the theatre.

## Without an account

- Book tickets for any show on sale. You give a name and an email address, and your booking
  reference and QR code are sent there.
- Join the waiting list for a performance that has sold out.
- Hold a season pass. It is sent to your email address, and once you open its link this browser
  keeps it, so booking here uses it. On another phone or computer, open the link there, or ask
  for the email again on the [pass page](/passes).
- Read what's on, our policies, and what our training covers.

Nothing is ever paid online. Booking holds your seats, and you pay at the box office in person
on the night.

## With an account

- The next booking you have still to come is on **My NNT**, and the booking page lists all of
  them, each opening with its QR code; a season pass is on your account on every device.
- You can record access requirements once, for the box office to see on every booking.
- Members of the society can sign up for front of house shifts, book rehearsal rooms and see
  their training.

Membership of the society is bought through the Students' Union, not on this website. An account
is how we connect that membership to you.

## If you have booked before

If you booked as a guest and later create an account with the same email address, your earlier
bookings are already on it. Registering sends you a message headed "Finish setting up your
account" with a link to choose a password; follow it and the account, with your bookings, is
yours. See [Creating an account](/help/creating-an-account).
