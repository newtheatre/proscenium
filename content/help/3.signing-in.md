Press **Sign in** at the top of any page. If you followed a link to something that needs an
account, you return to it once you are in.

## With a password

1. Type your email address and password, and press **Sign in**.
2. If you have set up an authenticator app, type the six-digit code it shows. If you do not have
   it with you, press **I do not have my authenticator** and use one of your recovery codes.

## With a passkey

Where your browser supports it, **Use a passkey** appears under the form. Your device checks it
is you with a fingerprint, your face or a PIN, and no code is asked for afterwards. You can add
a passkey from your account settings once you are signed in.

## With an emailed link

1. Press **I have forgotten my password**, then **Email me a sign-in link instead**.
2. Type your address and press **Send a sign-in link**.
3. Open the link in the message. It lasts {{ MAGIC_LINK_MINUTES }} and works once.

## If you have forgotten your password

1. Press **I have forgotten my password**, type your address and press **Send a reset link**.
2. Open the link in the message. It lasts {{ PASSWORD_RESET_HOURS }} and works once.
3. Choose a new password and press **Set my password**. Any other device you were signed in on
   will need the new password too.

The screen says "Check your email" whatever address you type, and the same message covers a
wrong password, an unconfirmed address and a closed account. That way the form never tells
anyone whether somebody has an account here.

## Theatre addresses

Committee members sign in with **Sign in with Google**, choosing their `@newtheatre.org.uk`
account. A theatre address never has a password, so the password form and the reset form both
point you back to Google.

## Still stuck

- **"Too many attempts. Try again shortly."**: wait a few minutes, then try once more.
- **"That link has expired or has already been used. Ask for a new one."**: each emailed link
  works once. Ask for another from the sign-in screen.
- If you have lost your authenticator and your recovery codes, contact the theatre's IT Manager,
  who can reset it for you.
