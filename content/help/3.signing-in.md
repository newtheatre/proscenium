Press **Sign in** at the top of any page, type your email address and press **Continue**. If
you followed a link to something that needs an account, you return to it once you are in.

## With an emailed link

1. After your address, press **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. Open the link in the message. It lasts {{ MAGIC_LINK_MINUTES }}, works once, and takes you
   back to where you started.

The link needs no password, and it confirms your address if you have not confirmed it yet.

## With a password

1. After your address, type your password in the field under the link and press **Sign in**.
2. If you have set up an authenticator app, type the six-digit code it shows. If you do not have
   it with you, press **I do not have my authenticator** and use one of your recovery codes.

## With a passkey

Where your browser supports it, your passkeys are offered as you tap the address field, and
**Use a passkey** sits under the form. Your device checks it is you with a fingerprint, your face
or a PIN, and no code is asked for afterwards. You can add a passkey from your account settings
once you are signed in.

## If you have forgotten your password

1. After your address, press **I have forgotten my password**, then **Send a reset link**.
2. Open the link in the message. It lasts {{ PASSWORD_RESET_HOURS }} and works once.
3. Choose a new password and press **Set my password**. That signs you in, and any other device
   you were signed in on will need the new password.

You do not need a password at all: **Email me a sign-in link** works every time.

The screen says "Check your email" whatever address you type, and the same message covers a
wrong password, an unconfirmed address and a closed account. That way the form never tells
anyone whether somebody has an account here.

## Theatre addresses

Committee members type their `@newtheatre.org.uk` address and press **Continue with Google**. A
theatre address never has a password, so the screen offers Google alone.

## Still stuck

- **"Too many attempts. Try again shortly."**: wait a few minutes, then try once more.
- **"That link has expired or has already been used. Ask for a new one."**: each emailed link
  works once. Ask for another from the sign-in screen.
- If you have lost your authenticator and your recovery codes, contact the theatre's IT Manager,
  who can reset it for you.
