## Registering

1. Press **Sign in** at the top of any page, then **I do not have an account yet**.
2. Give your name, your email address and a password of at least {{ PASSWORD_MIN_LENGTH }}
   characters. The hint under the password field names every rule it has to meet.
3. Press **Create my account**. The screen says "Check your email".
4. Open the message we send and follow the link to confirm your address. The screen says
   "Address confirmed" and offers **Sign in**.

Registering does not sign you in on its own: confirm the address first, then sign in.

## If something does not arrive

- Check your spam or junk folder for a message from the New Theatre.
- On the sign-in screen, **I did not get my confirmation email** sends it again.
- If the address already has an account, you get a message saying so rather than a second
  account. The screen says "Check your email" either way.

An account whose address is never confirmed is removed after a while, so follow the link when
the message arrives.

## Theatre addresses

Committee members with an `@newtheatre.org.uk` address do not register here. They sign in with
Google, and their account is made the first time they do. See [Signing in](/help/signing-in).
