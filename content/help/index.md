These pages are for anybody visiting the New Theatre's website, whether or not you have an
account with us.

You can book tickets for any show without an account. Choose a show from **What's on**, pick your
tickets, and give a name and an email address for the booking. Your seats are held until the
night, and you pay at the box office in person when you arrive.
