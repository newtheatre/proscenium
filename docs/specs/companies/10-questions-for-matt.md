## 1. Is Companies on for NNT?

NNT rarely hosts visiting companies outside StuFF, and its room bookings are members' own. Turning it on costs nothing but a menu entry.

- **Off by default in `student-society`, on in the other presets (recommended).** NNT switches it on when StuFF planning starts; a fresh student society is not shown a module it may never use.
- **On everywhere.** Simpler, but a student theatre sees settlements and agreements it does not need.

## 2. Is "company" the right staff word?

A hirer running a birthday party is not obviously a "company".

- **Company (recommended).** Matches the catalogue, core's `company_name` and how theatres talk about visiting work; the "private person" switch covers the odd case.
- **Organisation.** Neutral, but longer, and still wrong for a private person.
- **Partner.** Friendly, but suggests a standing relationship and clashes with Fundraising's sponsors.

## 3. Is online acceptance enough?

- **Online acceptance counts, a signed file optional (recommended).** A signed-in contact clicking Accept, with the company's legal name shown, is recorded with who and when. Many small companies never sign paper.
- **A signed file is required.** Safer for large deals, slower for every act in a festival.

## 4. Should calls block room bookings?

- **Warn only now, block later (recommended).** Calls are Companies' own times so the module runs without Spaces; Spaces' amendment (CO-45) adds calls as a blocker the way performances and closures are, through `companies.hire-source` and an event.
- **Calls become Spaces bookings.** Blocks at once, but Companies would require Spaces, and a festival without room booking would need it switched on.

## 5. Is a split worked out before or after VAT?

UK visiting deals usually share "net box office receipts": after VAT and, by agreement, booking fees.

- **After VAT, booking fees per agreement (recommended).** The basis uses Finance's figures, already net of output tax where the theatre is VAT-registered; each agreement says whether fees come off.
- **Before VAT.** Rare, and the company would be paid tax the theatre owes HMRC.

## 6. Are the default numbers right?

Proposed: 2 guest places per performance, names due 24 hours before, reminder 48 hours before, settle within 14 days, approve without an answer after 14 days, contact links kept 2 years after they end, company people 90 days after the last date. All are settings; a theatre changes them on the module's page. They should go to the workshops like the other modules' numbers.

## 7. May an owner approve a settlement they prepared?

Your ruling of 23 November exempts owners from the "not your own record" rule.

- **No, even an owner (recommended).** A settlement pays money out; the second person is the control. A theatre with one finance person makes the settlement and a committee officer approves it.
- **Yes, owners are exempt here too.** Consistent with the ruling, weaker as a control.

## 8. How should NNT take hire deposits and fees?

- **Card reader or bank transfer, no online payment (recommended for NNT).** Deposits go through Payments with whatever methods NNT has turned on; a bank transfer is recorded against the settlement. Other theatres may enable online payment.
- **Online payment for hirers.** Convenient for the public, but NNT's payment rules would need checking first.

## 9. Is a private person hiring a room a company?

- **Yes, with a "private person" switch (recommended).** One hire flow; the switch makes the name and address personal data that follow the person on erasure.
- **A separate hirer record.** Cleaner words, but a second list, a second settlement path and a second set of screens.
