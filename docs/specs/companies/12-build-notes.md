# 12. Build notes

What the build has settled that the parts above leave open, slice by slice.

## The gate

- **CO-01, CO-02, CO-05.** Abilities are declared as the routes that need them land, since the conformance check refuses an ability nothing requires; part 6 stays the full list. The fact `companies.contact-of` is read from the contacts table, through the account scope, on every check. Moves a person makes go through one route, `POST /api/companies/engagements/<id>/<move>` (`confirm`, `cancel`). Confirming a hire needs at least one call until Spaces bookings carry the engagement's reference (CO-14).
- **CO-03.** Approval is `POST /api/companies/settlements/<id>/approve`. The preparer is refused on the statement (`prepared_by IS NOT` the approver) and by the table's check, so the rule binds owners too, whatever the own-record exemption says. With Invoices off, the posting's key is `companies:settlement:<id>:approved`, kind `recognition`, one debit and credit pair per line that posts (M1); a note never posts. With Invoices on, approval posts nothing and announces `companies.settlement.approved`; the half of the Invoicing case where issuing posts once waits for Invoicing's `invoicing.line-owner` point, and its test lands with it.
- **CO-04 and BO-75.** A contact names a guest at `POST /api/companies/allocations/<id>/guests` (`guest.name` through the fact, or `allocation.manage`). The allocation keeps a count of places named, and the statement that raises it is the guard, so of two contacts naming the last place one is told it has gone (`companies.allocation.full`). Box office's contribution to `companies.guest-issuer` so far has `issue` only: a confirmed zero-price order on the Complimentary ticket type, from the band with most room, made by the same code as an approved comp request (`issueCompOrder`), and so reported as complimentary (L6). Companies runs it as Box office, so the door codes it signs are Box office's own. `hold`, `withdraw` and `release` join the contract with CO-26 and CO-27, once Box office has staff holds; until then unnamed places are not held and stay on sale.

## Companies and contacts

- **CO-06.** A near match is two names with the same key: lower case, accents dropped, punctuation and "&" read as words, and a leading or trailing "The" left out ("The Splinter Theatre", "Splinter-Théâtre!" and "Splinter Theatre, The" are one key). The key is kept in `name_key` beside the name so the check is one indexed read, `GET /api/companies/matches?name=`. New company offers each match, archived ones too, as a link to open it, and still adds the new one on "Add anyway": only an exact name, ignoring case, refuses (G1), and a rename onto a name in use refuses on the statement.
- **CO-07.** A contact is added by email and name (`POST /api/companies/<id>/contacts`); the account is found or made by Identity's `findOrCreateAccount` with origin `invite`, so a new address makes an unclaimed account and an unverified address held by someone else never matches (G2). The API still takes an `accountId` for callers that have one. `PATCH .../contacts/<contact>` changes the role, the messages they are sent and the day phone, or makes them primary, stepping the old primary down in the same batch (G4). Addresses and phones are sent only to someone who may manage contacts; `company.view` sees names and roles.
- **CO-09.** Archive and restore are `POST /api/companies/<id>/archive` and `/restore`, each one guarded update offered with Undo. Archiving refuses while an engagement is not cancelled or settled; restoring refuses when another unarchived company has taken the name meanwhile.
- **Screens.** Companies is under Programme in the console. The list follows Collections (search by New, archived below the divider); a company's page has Contacts, Engagements and Details tabs. Engagement rows do not open a page yet; that comes with the engagement screens. Keeping a production's `company_name` in step on rename (G3) lands with linking, CO-12.

## Your companies

- **CO-08.** Adding a contact sends `companies.contact-added` (email and inbox), naming who added them and the company's public name. An account that has never been signed in to gets `companies.contact-added-claim` instead, by email only, carrying core's claim link (sealed): it signs them in once and lands on My account. Your companies (`/account/companies`, `GET /api/companies/mine`) and its card on My account show only to a live contact of an unarchived company (count `companies.mine`). Each engagement that is not cancelled is a section with its dates and what the contact owes, oldest deadline first: guests still to name before the deadline, a sent agreement to answer, a proposed settlement to agree. The pages that act on each (Your engagement, the guests sheet, the statement) come with their slices.

## Engagements

- **CO-12.** A production is linked when the engagement opens (`productionId`) or later, once, by `POST /api/companies/engagements/<id>/production`; either way core's `company_name` on the production becomes the company's public name in the same batch, and a change of the company's name or public name rewrites it on every production linked through an engagement that is not cancelled or settled (G3), through core's `setProductionCompanyName`. Cancelled productions are left alone, since core refuses to edit them. The warning in core when staff edit a linked production's company name is not built yet. A second engagement on a production refuses on the statement (E6). Searching productions in the New engagement and Link dialogs needs `core.production.view`, which `engagement.manage` cannot imply because core does not mark it impliable; the programmer role holds both.
- **CO-16.** Cancelling needs a reason (`companies.engagement.reason-needed`), releases the engagement's open allocations and withdraws a sent agreement, in the batch with the move. The production, and its company name, are left as they were. Guest tickets already issued stay issued until Box office's staff holds let `release` join the guest-issuer contract.
- **Screens.** Engagements (`/console/companies/engagements`, under Programme) lists live engagements first and folds settled and cancelled ones below the divider. An engagement's page has Overview (where it stands and why it was cancelled) and History (its moves, newest first); Confirm is its primary action once agreed, and Link a production and Cancel sit in its menu. New engagement is on the company's Engagements tab. The Agreement, Calls and people, Guests and Settlement tabs come with their slices.

## Agreements

- **CO-18 and CO-22.** New terms are always a new draft version (`POST /api/companies/engagements/<id>/agreements`), superseding a draft or sent version at once; an accepted version stays accepted, and the one a settlement applies, until a later version is accepted (A5). A field left out takes the theatre's setting (guest places, names due, settle within, clauses). Durations are taken as written (`24h`) or in seconds and stored in days, hours or minutes (`1d`). Sending (`.../agreements/<agreement>/send`) checks the deal type's own terms (A2) and that a live contact receives agreements, moves an enquiry to offered, and sends those contacts `companies.agreement-sent`. Renegotiating an agreed or confirmed engagement leaves its state where it is.
- **CO-19.** A contact answers at `.../agreements/<agreement>/answer` with `agreement.answer`, a fact default. The response's unique index decides two answers at once. Accepting moves an offer to agreed and makes one open allocation per performance still to come (A4), each due the version's notice before it starts, set-wise; a later accepted version changes the places on open allocations, never below the number named. Declining moves the offer back to enquiry. Contacts see sent versions only, never drafts. Without Box office's staff holds, accepted places are not yet held (L1).
- **CO-21.** `POST /api/companies/agreements/<id>/record` records a paper answer for a live contact; `agreement.record` acts on the version with owner: refuses, so its author cannot record it (owners exempt, by the 23 November ruling). The signed file is not attached yet.
- **A6.** Withdrawing (`.../withdraw`) discards a draft, or withdraws a sent version until it is answered and moves the offer back to enquiry.
- **Screens.** The engagement page gains an Agreement tab: versions newest first with their state and who answered, Change terms (prefilled from the latest version, fields by deal type), and Send, Discard, Withdraw and Record a paper answer in each version's menu. Your companies links each engagement to Your engagement (`/account/companies/<engagement>`), which so far shows the terms and clauses with Accept and Decline; calls, people and guests join it with their slices.
