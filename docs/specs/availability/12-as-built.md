# 12. As built

Where the build departs from, or is narrower than, parts 1 to 11. The build is the reference from here on.

## Gate, first part (AV-01 to AV-03, AV-05, AV-06, AV-08), 24 November 2026

- **Tables.** Six of the fourteen in the gate: profiles, patterns, pattern lines, exceptions, requests and the people a request asks. Polls add four, meetings two and calendar imports two (below).
- **Levels** are worked out on read, per day, in `layers/availability/server/levels.ts` (`resolveDay`), from one read per table for everyone asked (`server/reading.ts`). Rows of merged accounts read as their survivor's through the account scope, so the module has no merge handler yet; where two patterns of one survivor overlap after a merge, the one changed last wins.
- **Overlapping patterns** are refused twice: by a check that names the pattern in the way, and by the insert or update itself, which carries the no-overlap predicate and is guarded with `expect(1)`.
- **Removing an exception** that began before today keeps its past days by ending it yesterday (A3); one wholly in the past is refused.
- **Reasons.** A shared reason shows to a viewer only when the person is in a group the viewer plans for (A7); `availability.find.anyone` sees levels without reasons. People a viewer may not plan for are left out and counted in `hidden`.
- **Requests.** `openRequest` answers the open request for the same `sourceRef` instead of opening a second. The people asked are the groups' members by subquery plus those named, over live accounts and their survivors, written in one set and told once. Joiners, leavers and reminders are below, under Requests.
- **Event.** A change of entries announces `availability.entry.updated` with the account and the dates touched, no levels or reasons (A6). The kit's naming rule needs the past participle.
- **Settings** so far: `grid.step`, `entry.unmarked-default` and `entry.horizon`. `grid.step` and `entry.horizon` have audience signed-in, because My availability reads them; the rest stay staff.
- **Abilities** are declared with the first code that needs them: `find.anyone`, `request.send` and `settings.manage`. Entering one's own availability needs only a sign-in until `own.manage` has a screen to gate; `find.use` and `entry.on-behalf` arrive with Find a time and on-behalf entries.
- **Not yet:** the S1 screen (AV-04), the `core.person-busy` contribution (AV-07, once lane A's support module is on `next`), Productions' and Rehearsals' group contributions, and retention.

Tests: `layers/availability/tests/integration/gate.test.ts` (through the routes and services, with the fixture module `crews` contributing a group kind) and `layers/availability/tests/unit/levels.test.ts`.

## My availability (AV-04, S1 and S3), 24 November 2026

- **Screen.** `/members/availability`, in the members' menu for everyone signed in. A week from Monday on a computer, three days on a phone, moved with Earlier and Later; the first day in view is in the address (`?from=`). The grid (`AvailabilityWeekGrid`) is shared with the request and Find a time screens to come.
- **Painting.** Choose Free, Maybe, Busy or Clear, then drag down one day with a mouse, or tap (or press Enter on) where the stretch starts and then where it ends; arrow keys move between cells. A day's heading paints the whole day. Each stroke is one `POST /api/availability/paint` for one day.
- **Clear** takes back what was painted: it cuts the stroke out of the person's entries on that day, so the day falls back to the pattern or to unmarked time. An entry over several days keeps its other days, split into pieces that keep the entry's `created_at`, so which entry wins (A4) never changes.
- **Pattern days.** The first stroke on a day a pattern covers asks "Just these days" or "Change the pattern", and the answer holds for the visit; a select beside the palette changes it. "Change the pattern" cuts the stroke into that weekday's lines and joins neighbours of one level; with no pattern covering the day it is refused `availability.pattern.none`. Creating and dating patterns is S2, the next slice.
- **Read.** `GET /api/availability/me` takes dates (`from`, `to`, at most 62 days) and answers each day's pieces in minutes past midnight, worked out per A4 with the person's own reasons, so the grid needs no time-zone arithmetic. It also answers the grid settings and the requests asking the person, with "This is right" on the screen.
- **Settings** `grid.day-starts` (09:00) and `grid.day-ends` (23:00, at most 23:59, the kit's time kind) are added; the grid opens scrolled to the first.
- **Not yet:** commitments on the grid (after `core.person-busy`), calendar imports (S14), S2 patterns (now built, below), S4 and S5 (now built, below).

## Weekly patterns (S2), 24 November 2026

- **Screens.** `/members/availability/patterns` lists the person's patterns, newest first, with their dates and the days they mark; "Remove" asks first. "New pattern" takes a name, a first day, an optional last day and, in place of "Copy from last term", a choice of any earlier pattern whose times to copy (the newest by default). It opens the pattern's page.
- **Editing** happens on `/members/availability/patterns/<id>`: the name and dates above the same grid as My availability, showing a typical week (Monday to Sunday). Strokes change the page only; "Save pattern" sends the name, dates and every line in one `PUT`, and leaving with changes asks first. A clash with another pattern's dates is refused naming it, as before.
- **A mouse click** paints the one cell clicked; dragging paints a stretch. Tapping start then end is for touch screens and the keyboard.
- **Read.** `GET /api/availability/patterns` answers the person's own patterns with their lines and the grid settings.

## Requests (AV-09, AV-10, S4, S5, S11), 24 November 2026

- **Asked of me (S4)** at `/members/availability/requests`, linked from My availability: open requests first, each with its period, answer date and the person's state (Not answered, Answered, Past the answer date). Requests that withdrew the person are left out.
- **Answer a request (S5)** at `/members/availability/requests/<id>`: the organiser's message, the grid from the request's first day (Earlier, Later and "Start of the period" move it), the same palette and pattern question as S1, and "This is right" as the one button. Painting saves as it goes and never answers (R4). The palette, the pattern question and the key are components shared with S1 (`AvailabilityPaintTools`, `AvailabilityPatternAsk`, `AvailabilityLegend`).
- **Requests I have sent (S11)** at `/members/find-a-time/requests`, in the members' menu for holders of `availability.request.send` until Find a time arrives. "Ask for availability" takes a title, groups the organiser plans for, a period, an answer date (offered `requests.default-due` ahead) and a message. Naming single people is left to Find a time. The detail page counts people by state, lists them by name with when they answered, and offers "Remind now", "Stop asking" per person and "Close request" (not on a request a module opened).
- **Reminders (R3).** An hourly task, `availability.send-reminders`, reminds each person still asked once as each of `requests.first-reminder` and `requests.second-reminder` passes before the due date; `reminders_sent` holds how many settings have passed, so two passed at once send one reminder and answering in between stops the second. Nothing goes after the due date or once closed.
- **Remind now** goes to everyone still asked, at most once in 24 hours (`reminded_at`, refused `availability.request.reminded-today`), only while the request is open.
- **Joiners and leavers (R2, R6)** run first in the daily `availability.sweep-requests`. Members of the chosen groups not yet on the request are asked and told with the opening notice; people asked or lapsed who are in none of the groups and were not named (`account_refs`) are withdrawn. A request whose group kind has gone (its module switched off) is left alone. Someone withdrawn, by hand or as a leaver, is not asked again if they rejoin.
- **Withdrawing** a person by hand (R6) leaves their availability untouched; they stop seeing the request.
- **Reads.** `GET /api/availability/requests` (S4), `GET /api/availability/requests/sent` (S11, with the groups and the offered answer date) and `GET /api/availability/requests/<id>` (S5 and S11's detail: the request and the viewer's state to anyone asked; counts and each person by name only to the organiser or a holder of `find.anyone`; anyone else gets not found).
- **Settings** `requests.default-due` (7 days), `requests.first-reminder` (3 days) and `requests.second-reminder` (1 day), staff audience.

## On someone's behalf, who can see me, retention (AV-20, AV-21, AV-23), 24 November 2026

- **On someone's behalf (A8).** `availability.entry.on-behalf` lets a planner mark a member of a group they plan for busy or maybe, from "Mark as away" on a person in a request they sent (S11). Holding `find.anyone` is not enough: the person must be in a group the planner plans for. Free is refused (`availability.on-behalf.level`), and so is marking oneself. The reason is stored unshared, so other planners see only the level; the person and the planner who added it see the reason. The share box is not offered. The person gets the notice `availability.entry-added-for-you` and may change or remove the entry from My availability, where it is marked "added for you". The planner sees and may remove only what they added (`GET` and `DELETE /api/availability/on-behalf`).
- **Who can see my availability (S6)** at `/members/availability/privacy`, linked from My availability: the person's groups from every `availability.group` contribution, each with who plans for it (the contract's optional `plannedBy`, in words), then what planners see and what nobody else ever sees. Nothing about other people is read.
- **Retention.** The daily sweep deletes exceptions whose last date is more than `retention.past` (90 days) ago, and requests closed longer ago than `retention.history` (12 months) with their people; an entry that answered such a request keeps its days and loses the link. Both settings confirm on change, as shortening one deletes rows at the next sweep. Finished polls and past meetings join the same sweep when they are built.
- **Not yet:** erasure and merge handling beyond the kit's delete-row (AV-22, with the merge handler), Find a time, polls, meetings and calendar imports.

## Find a time (AV-11 to AV-13, S7), 24 November 2026

- **Find a time** at `/members/find-a-time`, in the members' menu for `availability.find.use` (new ability). The organiser chooses groups they plan for, dates (at most 62 days), hours on the grid step, a length and, optionally, rooms with "Room optional". `POST /api/availability/find` is a read with a body, since a find names groups, people and rooms; nothing is stored until polls arrive. `GET /api/availability/find/options` gives the groups, the rooms and the grid's step and hours.
- **People (F1)** are each group's members through `plannable`, so a group the organiser does not plan for is refused; named people (not yet offered on the screen) are counted in `hidden` when left out. At most 200 people and 30 must-have people.
- **The grid (F2)** sends each person's levels per day as pieces, the same shape as My availability, and the screen counts each cell ("2/3", greener the more are free). A cell someone who must come cannot do is grey and struck through; a cell where a chosen room is taken is striped. Choosing a cell or a best time lists everyone by level, each at their least available over the time, with reasons only where A7 allows.
- **Best times (F3)** are worked out on the server (`shared/find.ts`, also used by the screen): slots of the length on the step inside the hours, not before now, where every must-have person is free throughout; ranked by most free, then fewest maybe, then fewest not known, then earliest; ten at most. Ticking "Must come" beside a person finds again.
- **Rooms (F5)** come from the occupancy service, so Availability now requires `occupancy` (a support module, on while Availability is on). A slot overlapping a taken stretch is not a best time unless "Room optional" is ticked; the side panel says what holds the room where the reader may see it.
- **Commitments (F4).** Availability requires `person-busy` too. One `personsBusy` read covers everyone in the find over its dates; Availability's own stated busy rows are dropped (its meetings are kept), and each other row is laid over the person's day where it is worse than what they said (`overlay` in `shared/find.ts`): firm as busy, not firm as maybe. Such a stretch carries the row's translation key, and "Who" shows it in place of a reason.
- **Stated busy time (AV-07)** is contributed to `core.person-busy` (`server/contributions/core.person-busy/availability.ts`): busy stretches only, kind `unavailable`, label `availability.unavailable`, never firm, never a reason. Arranged meetings add theirs (below); imports will when built.
- **Not yet:** "Turn into…" (AV-17).
- The gallery fixture contributes a "Gallery company" group so the browser test can plan for someone.

## Polls (AV-15, AV-16, S8, S9), 24 November 2026

- **Tables.** `availability_finds`, `availability_find_people`, `availability_find_slots` and `availability_find_votes`, as part 2. The references carry no cascade (the kit's `col.ref` writes none), so the retention sweep deletes votes, slots, people and then the find. `context_ref`, `target_id` and `outcome_ref` stay empty until targets (AV-17).
- **Sending (F7).** On Find a time, "Add to poll" beside a best time or in a slot's side panel picks it (two to six); "Send a poll" asks for a title and an answer date (answers close at the end of that day) and stores the find, its slots and its people in one unit. The people are the find's, worked out again on the server through `plannable`; the organiser is not told, and a time already started is refused. `POST /api/availability/polls`, ability `availability.find.use`.
- **Answering (S9)** is at `/members/availability/polls/<id>` rather than part 5's `/members/polls/<id>`, so it sits under My availability in the members' menu; open polls also head Asked of me (S4). Each time shows the person's own level over it, and yes, if need be or no; one save writes all answers, changeable while the poll is open. The save is guarded by a conditional write on the poll's state and answer time. Answers never touch availability rows.
- **Privacy.** `GET /api/availability/polls/<id>` gives a person asked only their own answers and levels; the organiser (or a holder of `find.anyone`) gets tallies and each person's answers; anyone else is told there is no such poll (404).
- **Deciding and giving up (F8).** S8 at `/members/find-a-time/<id>` shows the tallies, each person's answers, "Choose this time" for any time at any point, and "Give up". Both are conditional on the poll still polling, announce `availability.poll.decided` for a decision, and tell everyone asked (`availability.poll-decided`, `availability.poll-abandoned`). Find a time lists the organiser's own polls before a search.
- **The daily task** gives up a poll still undecided `polls.abandon-after` (new setting, 14 days) after its answer time, telling everyone asked and the organiser (`availability.poll-expired`), and deletes finished polls older than `retention.history`.

## Arranged meetings (AV-18, S12), 24 November 2026

- **Tables.** `availability_meetings` and `availability_meeting_invitees`, as part 2, except that the agenda (`notes`) is internal, as a request's message is: the kit refuses free text on a table with no subject. Invitee rows are kept on erasure (`keep-row`). `space_booking_ref` and `context_ref` stay empty until Spaces' target (AV-28) and Productions' schedule (M6).
- **Arranging (M1)** needs the new ability `availability.meeting.arrange`. From a decided poll ("Arrange a meeting" on its results page) the people are the poll's and the poll's `outcome_ref` becomes `availability.meeting:<id>`; from Find a time (the slot's side panel) they are the chosen groups' members through `plannable`. A meeting starts and ends on one day here (part 3 allows up to 24 hours), on the grid step, and not in the past. Find a time's options say whether the viewer may arrange, so the screen tests no ability.
- **The page (S12)** is `/members/availability/meetings/<id>` rather than `/members/meetings/<id>`, under My availability in the members' menu; upcoming meetings head Asked of me, and Find a time lists the organiser's own. Invitees see the meeting, their answer and the counts; the organiser also sees each person's answer.
- **Answers (M2)** are going or not going, a conditional write while the meeting is arranged and not over. **Changing (M3)** a time sets every answer back to pending, announces `availability.meeting.moved` and tells everyone; other changes tell people only when "Tell people" is ticked. **Cancelling (M4)** takes an optional reason and tells everyone. Notices: `availability.meeting-invited`, `-moved`, `-changed`, `-cancelled`.
- **Busy (M5).** Each invitee not saying not going is busy, firm, kind `meeting`, label `availability.meeting`, with the meeting as the record, through the same `core.person-busy` contribution.
- **Retention.** The daily task deletes meetings that ended longer ago than `retention.history`, with their invitees, and clears a poll's link to them.
- **Not yet:** the calendar feed (M6, C-70: keyed by the meeting id alone so a move keeps the UID, public-safe titles, cancelled meetings sent as cancelled; waits for lane A's calendar-feed module), and the production schedule entry.

## Commitments on My availability (A5, S1), 24 November 2026

- **Read.** `GET /api/availability/me` returns `commitments`, by date, from one `personsBusy` read over the days shown (`server/commitments.ts`, shared with Find a time). Availability's own stated busy rows are left out; its arranged meetings and every other module's rows stay in. Each carries its translation key and whether it is firm, never a record title.
- **Shown.** The week grid draws each commitment over the levels as an outlined block, with a lock and its label where it starts, and each cell's accessible name adds "…, a commitment". The key gains "Held elsewhere" when the week has one. Painting still works underneath: it changes what the person states, never the commitment.
- **From a meeting.** A meeting's page links to "See it on my week", which opens My availability on the meeting's date.

## Under-18 warnings (AV-14, F6), 24 November 2026

- **Read.** `server/safeguarding.ts` asks core's `ageStatuses` only (A9, R3-25): who is under 18 on each date of a find, asking again only for those still under 18 as the dates go on, and whether the people are exactly one adult and one under-18. Unknown is never taken as adult.
- **Find a time.** The result carries `under18`: the latest finish in minutes from `core.under-18.latest-finish` (null while unset, so no late warning), the under-18 people on each date, and `oneToOne`. `lateFor` in `shared/find.ts` names who a slot keeps past the latest finish. Best times show a warning triangle; the slot panel names the people; a one-to-one find shows one line above the best times.
- **Meetings.** The organiser's view of a meeting carries `under18` for everyone not saying not going, on its day and end time; invitees never see it. Nothing blocks.
- **Setting.** `availability.safeguarding.one-to-one` (on by default, Matt's answer 9), under the settings heading "Under-18s".

## Calendar imports by secret address (AV-26, I1 to I6, S14), 24 November 2026

- **Tables.** `availability_calendar_connections` (`avcc`) and `availability_imported_blocks` (`avib`), as part 2. The address is `col.special` (sealed with the core key, never shown again, never in a list or log). `token` and `provider_subject` are there for Google and Microsoft (AV-31), unused until then. One column beyond part 2: `last_tried_at`, so the sync task knows which connection is due and "Refresh now" can hold to once a minute. A connection's blocks are deleted with it in the same unit (the kit's `col.ref` has no cascade).
- **Connecting (I1).** `/members/availability/calendars`, linked from My availability as "Your calendars". Only the person, while signed in. `webcal://` is read as `https://`; anything not https, or on a private or local network, is refused with core's `refusedAddress` (C-49, now exported from core's services). The address is read once before it saves; a file that is not a calendar is refused. At most `import.max-connections` (3).
- **Reading (I3).** `server/ics.ts` parses with `ical.js`: busy and out-of-office events are busy; tentative ones (status, Outlook's busy status, or the owner's own reply) become the connection's choice (maybe, busy or nothing); free (transparent), cancelled and declined events are dropped. Recurring events are expanded within the window with their moved instances. All-day and floating times are the theatre's local days and times. Only start, end and level are kept.
- **Not our own (I4).** Blocks matching one of the person's firm commitments from `core.person-busy` to within a minute are dropped. The feed-marker half waits for the calendar feed (C-70); `readCalendar` already takes an `ours(uid)` test for it.
- **Levels (A4, I6).** Imported blocks sit between exceptions and the pattern; busy beats maybe where they overlap. `resolveDay` now takes the person's rows and options (`own` shows the connection's label). Only the person sees imported time hatched, with "from your calendar …" in each cell's name; planners, Find a time and `core.person-busy` see plain levels.
- **Refreshing (I5).** Task `availability.calendar-sync` every 15 minutes queues job `availability.refresh-calendar` (bulk lane) for each connection not broken and not tried within `import.refresh` (1 hour). A refresh replaces the blocks in one unit and announces `availability.entry.updated` over the window. A failure counts; three in a row make the connection broken and tell the person once (`availability.calendar-broken`). A broken connection is no longer refreshed by the task; its blocks are dropped seven days after its last good read; "Refresh now" that reads again makes it working. Turning `import.ics` off deletes address blocks at the next sync.
- **Fetching.** `server/calls/calendar/fetch.ts`, 15 seconds, at most 5 MB. A read changes nothing at the other end, so it is not an `externalCall`; failures are counted on the connection.
- **Not yet:** Google and Microsoft (AV-31: Identity's consent service, token revoke on disconnect and erasure, proof P-AV-1); the feed-marker check (C-70); "Mark free instead" from a tapped imported block (painting free over it already does this).
