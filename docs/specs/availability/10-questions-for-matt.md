## 1. What unmarked time means

- **(a) Unknown, person may choose free.** Grey on the grid; never a best time for a must-have person. Honest, and nudges people to fill in.
- (b) Free. Fewer grey grids, but an empty profile looks like someone free all week, which is how rehearsals get double-booked.
- (c) Ask each person on first use, no default.

**Recommendation: (a).** A CallTime grid serves one production for a few weeks; ours is used everywhere and for months, so an empty week must not read as a free one.

## 2. Reasons

- **(a) Private, shareable per entry.** A person may tick "Share the reason with people who plan for me" on any entry. No ability overrides it.
- (b) Planners always see reasons. Simpler for directors, but people stop writing honest reasons (medical, family, work), and under-18s' reasons would be visible to adults by default.
- (c) Store no reasons at all.

**Recommendation: (a).** It settles Rehearsals' question 4 the same way for every module.

## 3. Rehearsals requires Availability

- **(a) Rehearsals requires** **`availability`** (as now agreed with the Rehearsals thread). One place for absences; switching Rehearsals on switches this on.
- (b) Rehearsals keeps a small conflicts table for when Availability is off. Two ways to say the same thing, and two privacy rules.

**Recommendation: (a).**

## 4. Planning beyond one's own groups

- **(a)** **`availability.find.anyone`****, privileged and audited, suggested for** **`committee-member`****.** The committee often needs "anyone free to help on Saturday".
- (b) Nobody: only groups. Tighter, but a committee member would have to build a group first.
- (c) Committee members and administrators.

**Recommendation: (a).** Administrators are left out on purpose: running the site is not a reason to see everyone's week.

## 5. Polls

- **(a) First release (AV-15, AV-16).** Most real choices end in "which of these three evenings".
- (b) Later, with Find a time alone first.

**Recommendation: (a).**

## 6. Personal calendars

- **(a) Later (AV-26): an ICS address read daily, busy-only, never titles, removable at once.**
- (b) Never.
- (c) First release. Calendar addresses are secrets in the URL and need careful storage; not worth delaying the first release for.

**Recommendation: (a).** The calendar feed out (C-70) already covers the other direction. Matt chose (c), first release, on 24 November; part 3's I rules carry it.

## 7. People with no account

- **(a) Later (AV-27): a signed link for one request, rows kept against the email address and erased with it.** Covers visiting creatives and festival acts.
- (b) Never: everyone needs an account. Matt's one-account ruling makes this nearly true already.

**Recommendation: (a).** It is how CallTime works and it is cheap once requests exist.

## 8. Seeing other answers in a poll

- **(a) No: invitees see only their own; the organiser sees tallies and names.**
- (b) Yes, as on Doodle.
- (c) The organiser chooses per poll.

**Recommendation: (a).** Groups now include under-18s, and "who said no" is not something a cast needs to see.

## 9. One-to-one warning

- **(a)** **`availability.safeguarding.one-to-one`** **on by default.** Warns, never blocks, organiser only.
- (b) Off by default.

**Recommendation: (a).** NNT has 17-year-old members (23 November); a warning costs nothing when it does not apply. The late-finish time is core's shared key and stays unset until the theatre chooses one (R3-25).
