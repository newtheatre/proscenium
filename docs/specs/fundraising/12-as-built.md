# 12. As built

Where the build departs from, or is narrower than, parts 1 to 11. The build is the reference from here on.

## Slice 1: funds and giving online, FR-01, FR-02, FR-05 to FR-08, FR-17 (24 November 2026)

- **Module.** `layers/fundraising`, switchable, requiring `payments` and `finance`. It declares only what is built: abilities `fundraising.donation.view` (audited read, implies `core.account.find`), `fund.manage` and `settings.manage`; records `fundraising.fund` and `fundraising.donation`; settings `giving.presets`, `giving.min`, `giving.max`, `giving.pay-within` and `retention.donations`, with the rules that min is below max and every preset lies between them. The treasurer's role template gains the three abilities.
- **Tables.** `fundraising_funds` and `fundraising_donations` as part 2, with every donation column so later slices need no rebuild. `campaign_id` and `declaration_id` are plain ids until their tables exist. Donations add `link_hash`, the keyed hash of the thanks page's link token (never the token). `show_amount` is nullable, as the kit requires of a column erasure clears.
- **No guard trigger.** The kit declares no triggers on ordinary tables, so the rule that a received gift's amount, currency, channel and date never change is kept by the services: every write is predicated on the state. `fund_id` is left out of that rule anyway, since D7's correction changes it.
- **FR-01.** The listener `default-fund` on `core.module.enabled` makes "General fund", default and offered, once. Each fund's account is `fundraising.donations` scoped by the fund's id, named "Donations: <fund>", made in the unit that makes the fund. The Gift Aid accounts come with Gift Aid.
- **Funds (FR-05).** Admin > Modules > Funds: new, edit, make default, archive and restore, archived below a divider. A name is unique among active funds, ignoring case. Restricted needs a purpose and cannot change once the fund has received a gift (checked again on the statement). The default fund is always offered and cannot be archived. Ordering by drag is not built; funds keep the order they were made in.
- **Giving (FR-07, D1, D4, D5).** `/support/give`: the preset buttons (none chosen), Other with the limits stated, the fund where more than one is offered, an email for a guest, an optional name, how they would like to be thanked in public and a note. The start is `POST /api/fundraising/give` (public, Turnstile, idempotent): a pending donation and a payment intent of its own through `collect`, expiring after `giving.pay-within`. The answer is the link token; `pay`, `check` and the thanks read take it in the path. A guest must give an email address; a signed-in donor's gift is on their account.
- **Settling (FR-02, M1).** The `payments.settlement` handler: `settle` receives the gift (reference `G-1001` upward, taken in the batch; received on the theatre's date) and credits its fund's account; `refunded` raises `refunded_minor` and marks the gift refunded when it reaches the amount; `release` marks a pending gift abandoned; `describe` names it by reference and donor. The events carry no currency: a payload string must be an id, an enum or a code, and the theatre has one currency.
- **Thanks (FR-08, D8).** `fundraising.donation-thanks`, sent once per gift (deduplicated by the donation) from Payments' batch, to the account or the guest's address. It states the amount, fund and date. The thanks page `/support/thanks/<token>` asks the provider while the gift is pending and shows the reference, amount and date once received. Neither carries the Add Gift Aid link yet: that comes with Gift Aid.
- **Donations (FR-17).** Console > Money > Donations: received and refunded gifts newest first (200 at a time), searched by reference or donor, filtered by fund or by a state (being paid, not completed, refunded). The record page shows the gift, the donor, the public acknowledgement and the note. Refund, Correct and Record a gift come with later slices.
- **Not yet built:** campaigns, checkout and till gifts, recorded gifts (FR-04's `receiveTransfer`), Gift Aid, sponsorships, small donations and collections, grants, the support page, Reports figures, erasure holds and the record tabs.
