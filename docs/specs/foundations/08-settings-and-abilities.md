# 6. Settings and abilities

## Foundations' own settings and abilities

Foundations defines the registries; it also uses them. These are the settings and abilities of the system area of `core`, written in the registry formats. Identity, the core domain and the interface add their own `core.*` entries in their documents.

## Settings

No operational number is fixed in code. Defaults are proposals a theatre can change within the limits shown.

| Key | Kind | Default | Limits | Audience | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `core.site.name` | text | unset | 1 to 80 characters | public, quotable | Set at setup |
| `core.locale.time-zone` | choice | `Europe/London` | Any IANA zone | internal | Change needs confirm: shows the tasks and deadlines that will move |
| `core.locale.currency` | choice | `GBP` | ISO 4217 | public | Cannot change once a ledger row exists |
| `core.locale.language` | choice | `en-GB` | Shipped catalogues | public |  |
| `core.locale.day-start` | time | `04:00` | 00:00 to 08:00 | internal | The hour a show night ends, so a late finish belongs to the right day |
| `core.updates.channel` | choice | `stable` | stable, beta | internal | Managed by deployment (`site.config.ts`), shown read-only |
| `core.updates.apply-migrations` | choice | `button` | button, automatic | internal | Question 3 |
| `core.audit.retention` | duration | 7 years | 1 to 25 years | internal | Change needs a reason |
| `core.jobs.retention` | duration | 30 days | 7 to 365 days | internal | Finished jobs |
| `core.jobs.max-attempts` | integer | 5 | 1 to 10 | internal | A job may declare its own |
| `core.health.notify` | boolean | true |  | internal | Tell the Owner when a check fails for more than a day |
| `core.health.notify-after` | duration | 24 hours | 1 hour to 7 days | internal |  |
| `core.security.fresh-sign-in` | duration | 15 minutes | 5 to 60 minutes | internal | How recent a sign-in must be for abilities marked fresh. Read by identity. |
| `core.maintenance.message` | text | a plain default | up to 280 characters | public | Shown while migrations are pending |

Kit limits that are not a theatre's business (statements per unit, bound parameters per statement, back-off base) are constants in the kit with a comment citing the platform limit, not settings.

## Abilities

| Ability | On | Risk | Owner role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `core.module.view` | site | low | grants | See the Modules screen |
| `core.module.switch` | `core.module` | high | grants | Fresh sign-in; audited |
| `core.setting.view` | site | low | grants | Sensitive values are never shown to anyone |
| `core.setting.change` | `core.setting` | medium | grants | A setting may name a narrower ability of its own module in `change` so that module's manager can change it |
| `core.setting.import` | site | high | grants | Fresh sign-in |
| `core.job.view` | site | low | grants |  |
| `core.job.manage` | `core.job` | medium | grants | Retry, cancel, run a task now |
| `core.credential.view` | site | medium | grants | See which are set, never the value |
| `core.credential.change` | `core.credential` | high | grants | Fresh sign-in; audited without the value |
| `core.health.view` | site | low | grants |  |
| `core.update.apply` | site | high | grants | Apply migrations |
| `core.audit.view` | site | medium | grants | Every search is audited (`audit: 'read'`) |
| `core.audit.export` | site | high | grants | Fresh sign-in |

All default to the Owner role only. `core.audit.view` is also offered to the data-protection lead role that identity defines. None can be reached through a fact: system authority is never derived from a shift or a training record.

## Record types

| Record type | Table | Parent | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `core.module` | `core_modules` | none | none |
| `core.setting` | `core_settings` | `core.module` (the declaring module) | none |
| `core.job` | `core_jobs` | `core.module` | none |
| `core.credential` | `core_credentials` | `core.module` | none |

The parent link is what lets a module's manager be granted `core.setting.change` on that module's settings alone.

## Secrets

| Secret | Needed | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CORE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` | always | Encrypts stored credentials and columns of class special. Generated by the theatre at setup, held as a deployment secret and in its password manager; rotating it is a documented procedure with a key version. |
| `CORE_SETUP_CODE` | until an Owner exists | Proves the person at `/setup` is the person who deployed |
| `CORE_TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` | when-used | The challenge on public forms and sign-in (declared by identity, verified by core) |

## Rate-limit classes

| Class | Counted per | Default allowance | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `default` | account or network address | generous | Reads |
| `write` | account | moderate | Any change |
| `sign-in` | address and network address | strict | Sign-in and sign-up |
| `public-form` | network address | strict | Forms open to people with no account |
| `export` | account | very strict | Downloads of many records |

A route names a class; the numbers are settings under `core.limits.*` with the same pattern as above.
