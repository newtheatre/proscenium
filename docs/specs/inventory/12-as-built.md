# 12. As built

Where the build departs from, or is narrower than, parts 1 to 11. The build is the reference from here on.

## Gate (INV-01 to INV-04), 24 November 2026

- **Tables.** All fourteen, as part 2, in one migration (`20261124185005_inventory-gate`). Item events and inspections are append-only by trigger. One column beyond part 2: `inventory_loans.due_back_at`, the close of `due_back_on`'s operating day (L8), written with it from core's `dayBounds`, so the promise check compares instants in plain SQL. Changing `core.locale.day-start` does not yet rewrite it; that arrives with the L9 listener.
- **Loans' subject** is `{ account: 'responsible_account_id', unlinked: 'staff' }`: the kit allows an account or an email as a subject, never both, so an outside borrower's loan, which has no account, is found by staff. Erasing an outside borrower by their verified addresses (part 2, "Erasure and merge") comes with an erasure handler in INV-27. Both loans and faults name their retention setting; the R5 sweep that erases rather than deletes comes with INV-27 and INV-37.
- **The promise check** is `layers/inventory/server/promise.ts`. `freeSql` is one predicate: C4 (active, lendable, in a category of the loan's department, no open disposal, no blocking kind overdue before the window's first operating day) and C1 to C3 together, as "the item's quantity is at least this line's plus what Promised and Out lines hold over the window". An individual item has quantity 1, so the same sum is C1. An Out line counts only what is not yet back, and one past its due time counts to the right end (C3). The statement that moves a line to Promised carries it and is guarded with `expect(1)`, refusing `inventory.loan.clash`.
- **Containers (C6).** Promising a container's line inserts a Promised line for each content item, linked by `with_line_id`, each carrying the check, and the container's own statement also expects its contents to be the number read. One failure refuses the whole unit.
- **Approval (L7)** reads first and refuses `inventory.loan.clash` with `detail` naming each failing line (`taken:<loan ids>` or `not-usable`); the statements then carry the check again, so a line taken between the read and the write still refuses the whole approval. Wanted lines stay wanted (L6).
- **Moves (I7).** Every move a record may make is the table in `shared/states.ts`; a loan's move is one update naming the states it may come from, refused `inventory.loan.moved` when someone else moved it first. Requested, approved, declined and cancelled announce `inventory.loan.<state>` with the loan and department ids.
- **Who may borrow (L3)** is on the loan's insert: the department must be active and lend to the kind of borrower, else `inventory.loan.not-lent`. A line names an item of the loan's own department that may be lent, else `inventory.line.wrong-item` (L2).
- **Settings**: all thirteen. `loans.default-length` and `loans.max-length` are not quotable: the kit lets only a public setting be quoted, and these are for signed-in members.
- **Abilities** are declared with the first route that needs each one; the gate has no routes, so only `settings.manage` so far.
- **The proof**: P-1 to P-6 pass against local D1 in workerd (`layers/inventory/tests/integration/gate.test.ts`), with C4 and L7 cases beside them. A run against a deployed D1 is still to do.

## Setting up (INV-05 to INV-09), 24 November 2026

- **Screens.** Three Admin tabs of the module, each a page of its own: Departments (departments, each with its categories), Stores (the locations tree) and Inspection kinds. Tags are the module's Lists tab, the `inventory.tag` vocabulary, starting empty. All need `inventory.setup.manage`, declared here with its routes. The cog modal on Items (S10) arrives with Items.
- **Setup steps.** `inventory.departments` and `inventory.locations`, both required, pointing at the two tabs.
- **Starting lists.** The four departments and four inspection kinds of part 6, each made by a button while there are none, once. Months in part 6 are stored as days: 365, 91 and 182.
- **Departments (I2, I3, L3).** A prefix is up to four capital letters, unique among all departments, and refused if another department's items already carry tags with it, so a tag can never be made twice. Changing a prefix keeps the counter. Archiving refuses while the department has active categories, naming the count; restoring refuses if an active department has taken its name.
- **Categories (N1, I8).** A category keeps its department for life, so grants on a department stay true. Putting an inspection kind on a category writes a due for every individual item in it, due on today's operating day; taking it off deletes the dues never inspected and keeps the rest as not applying. Taking a kind off and putting it back makes it apply to every item again. Archiving refuses while the category holds any item not disposed of, naming the count.
- **Inspection kinds (N7, N10).** Changing the interval rewrites, in one statement, the next due date of every applying due whose last inspection was a pass, as that pass's date plus the new interval; recorded inspections keep theirs. Archiving a kind keeps the categories' links to it, hidden while archived, so restoring brings it back where it was.
- **Locations (I5, I8).** Four levels at most, checked on the statement with a recursive query, as is never moving a location inside itself. A space is chosen within its venue. Archiving refuses while any item not disposed of is kept there (home or current) or an active location is inside it. Drag and drop for the tree is not built yet: a location is moved by choosing what it is inside.
- **Requirements** (departments' and categories' training needs, K1, and inspectors' requirements, N5) are stored and kept on save but not yet edited on screen; they wait for a picker over `core.requirement`.

## Items (INV-10 to INV-16), 24 November 2026

- **Abilities.** `inventory.item.view` and `inventory.item.edit` (which implies view), both on a department, declared with these routes. A grant on a department reaches its categories and items through the record resolvers. The console's Inventory group starts with Items, shown to anyone holding `item.view` anywhere; the cog on it opens the module's Admin tabs.
- **Adding (I1, I2, N1, N4).** One unit adds an item, or a run of individual items alike, with tags read from the department's counter inside the insert, so two people adding at once never share a tag. A counted item is one row with a quantity and has no inspections. A run is capped by `core.units.set-rows-limit`. Each item's dues follow `inventory.inspections.first-due`.
- **Changing (I4, I12).** An item moves only between categories of its own department. Tracking is fixed once the item has been on a loan or inspected. A new category brings its inspections; the old category's never inspected are deleted and inspected ones stay, not applying. Each change writes `edited`, and `recategorised` or `condition-changed` where they apply.
- **Moving (I5, I6).** A move names the destination; a container takes its contents, an item inside a container cannot move alone, and an item on loan cannot move here. Moving an item to its home clears where it is now.
- **Reading (I9).** The list holds every item of the departments the person may see, filtered on the screen by department, category, store (with everything inside it), state and "inspection due", which means due on or before today plus `inventory.inspections.remind-before`. Typing or scanning a whole tag opens the item. Replacement value, serial number and owner reach only those who may change the item; `inventory.item.export` arrives with reporting.
- **Screens.** S1 at `/console/inventory`, S2 at `/console/inventory/items/[id]` with Details, Inspections and History tabs, and S3 at `/console/inventory/items/new` and `.../[id]/edit`, one form validated with the schema the server uses. The category list names the inspections each brings; its training needs show on the item page. Right-click on a row offers Open and Move. Print label, Report a fault and Withdraw arrive with labels (S11), faults and disposals. A label's QR code is read as the tag, or an address ending in it.
- **Not yet:** photos, attachments and editing an item's own requirements, which wait for the picker over `core.requirement`.

## Labels (S11), 24 November 2026

- **The sheet** is `/console/inventory/labels?items=…`, in the plain live layout, printed black on white: one label per item with its QR code, tag, name and department, four to a row at `small` and two at `large` (`inventory.labels.size`). It is reached from the item page's menu, a row's right-click menu, and the list's menu ("Print labels for these"), which takes what the filters show, up to 200 to a sheet.
- **The QR code** holds the item's address, so it opens only for someone signed in who may see the item. Scanning on the items list reads that address, or a tag typed or read by a barcode scanner. Items a person may not see are left off the sheet.

## Lending and deciding (INV-19, INV-21), 24 November 2026

- **Abilities.** `inventory.loan.view` (privileged, read audited, implies `item.view`) and `inventory.loan.approve` (implies `loan.view`), on a loan, granted on a department. A loan resolves under its department; its title names the department and due date, never the borrower.
- **Lending straight away (INV-21, L3, L4, L7).** A holder of `loan.approve` on the department lends to a member, chosen with the person picker through `/api/inventory/loans/borrowers`, or to an outside borrower by name and email. One unit makes the loan, approves it and promises every item; any item not free over the window is named in the refusal's detail before anything is written, and the statements carry the check again. A loan may run no longer than `inventory.loans.max-length`. Each item is lent one at a time; lending part of a counted item's quantity comes with the kit desk.
- **Deciding a request (INV-19, L7, L17).** Approve promises every listed line or none; the loan page shows each line as "Free then" or why not, asked as approval will ask, and a line in the way can be taken off (with any contents promised with it) before approving the rest. Decline takes an optional note and releases every line. Cancelling is refused once a loan is out (L16). Telling the borrower of a decision arrives with reminders (INV-24).
- **Screens.** S4 at `/console/inventory/loans`: Requests, Approved, Out and Overdue in tabs, finished loans below the divider. S5 at `/console/inventory/loans/[id]`: the facts, the lines with their state and whether each is free, Approve as the primary action, Decline and Cancel in the menu, and a box to add what is wanted in words. "Lend kit" at `/console/inventory/loans/new`. Matching wanted lines, pull lists and the production's Kit tab are below.

## Pull lists and the Kit tab (INV-17, INV-18, S15), 24 November 2026

- **Ability.** `inventory.pull-list.edit` on `core.production`. Productions has no team fact yet, so stage managers hold it by a grant on the production until Productions adds one; `catalogue.browse` is not implied until Borrow kit declares it.
- **Starting (INV-17, L2, L5, L9).** "Start a pull list" on the production's Kit tab makes a draft production loan for one department, with the starter as the responsible person. A production has one open list per department; a second is refused (`inventory.pull-list.exists`) on the statement. It is due back on the operating day of the last performance not cancelled plus `inventory.loans.production-grace`, marked `from-production`; with no performances yet a date must be given, marked `by-person`. `inventory.loans.max-length` applies as for any loan. Following moved or cancelled performances (the L9 listeners) arrives with reminders and production dates (INV-24 to INV-26).
- **Gathering (L5, L6).** While the list is a draft, the production's people add lines in words or as items, take them off, match wanted lines and cancel the list. The department may do the same until the loan goes out, and sees drafts on the loans list under "Being gathered". "Send to the department" makes it Requested; the department approves all or none as for any request, or approves a draft straight away.
- **Matching (INV-18, L6).** "Match to an item" on a wanted line searches the department's lendable kit by tag or name (`/api/inventory/loans/[id]/candidates`, twenty at a time), each saying whether it is free over the window. Before approval a match lists the item; after approval it is a promise under C1 to C6 in the same unit, refused as `inventory.loan.clash` when the item is taken.
- **Who sees a loan.** The loan page answers anyone holding `loan.view` on it or `pull-list.edit` on its production, and audits each read either way. Its `can` says whether the reader may approve, change lines, cancel or submit.
- **Kit tab and fact (S15).** On a production's page, for holders of `pull-list.edit` (every list) or `loan.view` (their departments' lists): each list with its state, counts of wanted, found and out lines, and its due date. The summary fact reads "3 out, 1 overdue", or the number of lists. `loansForProduction` is not yet a service export: nothing outside Inventory asks for it.

## Kit desk (INV-22, INV-23, S6), 24 November 2026

- **Abilities.** `inventory.loan.issue` (on a department, implies `loan.view`) works the desk; `inventory.requirement.override` (privileged, implies `loan.issue`) lets kit go to someone who does not meet what it needs.
- **The desk** is `/console/inventory/desk`, in the live shell, reached from Inventory in the console. It is not under `/live`, so no practice scenario covers it yet. A scan of a loan number, an item's label address or its tag opens the loan the item is out on, else the approved loan it is promised to that starts soonest. With a loan open, scanning a tag on it ticks that line. Camera scanning and working offline (INV-49) are not built: a barcode scanner types into the box.
- **Check-out (L11, K2 to K5).** From the operating day the loan starts. "Who is collecting" picks a person (the responsible person if left empty), who becomes the loan's responsible person (K5). Each line's needs are its department's, category's and item's requirements together, checked for that person now with one call; an unavailable source shows "not checked" and lets the line go. An outside borrower with no account meets nothing. A line whose person misses a need goes out only when a holder of the override chooses why; the line records who overrode. **Departure:** the reason is one of four codes (`supervised`, `trained-elsewhere`, `contractor`, `other`) rather than words, because audit detail may not hold free text. Each line records its condition going out, which becomes the item's condition; contents go with their container. An item out of service, proposed for disposal or overdue a blocking inspection is refused at the moment it would go out.
- **Return (L13 to L15).** Ticked out lines come back with a condition; a counted line takes a number and stays out until all are back. "Damaged" asks what is wrong and opens a fault from the return, taking the item out of service, in the same unit (L14). "Lost" makes an individual item and its contents missing; a lost counted line is marked lost, and proposing its disposal arrives with disposals (INV-34). The unit that clears the last promised or out line makes the loan Returned.

## Borrow kit and My loans (INV-20, INV-27, S12 to S14), 24 November 2026

- **Abilities.** `inventory.catalogue.browse`, and `inventory.loan.request` which implies it; `pull-list.edit` now implies browsing too. Giving them to every member is a role for now: membership has no way yet for another module's abilities to follow its holder fact. `inventory.loan.view` now grants the borrower their own loan by ownership, and `inventory.loan.approve` refuses it, so nobody approves their own request (L4).
- **Borrow kit** at `/members/kit`, in the members' menu for holders of `loan.request`. It lists lendable items of departments that lend to members or anyone, grouped by department, with category and tag, never a value, serial or owner (I9). Choosing dates shows how many of each are free then, worked out for the whole list in one statement with the promise check's own predicates. "Add to request" builds a basket kept for the visit. While `inventory.catalogue.members-browse` is off the catalogue and requests answer `inventory.catalogue.closed`; the menu entry still shows to role holders. Photos arrive with the item photo slot.
- **Request kit** at `/members/kit/request`: dates, what it is for, and the basket, counted items with a number. A basket from several departments is sent as one requested loan per department, said before sending (L2). The member is responsible; the department decides as for any request.
- **My loans** at `/members/kit/loans` lists the person's loans, merged accounts included, and each opens read-only at `/members/kit/loans/[id]`. A member cancels their own request while it is Requested (L16). Asking for more time (INV-26) and erasing old loans' personal details (R5) come with reminders.
