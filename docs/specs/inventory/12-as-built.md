# 12. As built

Where the build departs from, or is narrower than, parts 1 to 11. The build is the reference from here on.

## Gate (INV-01 to INV-04), 24 November 2026

- **Tables.** All fourteen, as part 2, in one migration (`20261124192009_inventory-gate`). Item events and inspections are append-only by trigger. One column beyond part 2: `inventory_loans.due_back_at`, the close of `due_back_on`'s operating day (L8), written with it from core's `dayBounds`, so the promise check compares instants in plain SQL. Changing `core.locale.day-start` does not yet rewrite it; that arrives with the L9 listener.
- **Loans' subject** is `{ account: 'responsible_account_id', unlinked: 'staff' }`: the kit allows an account or an email as a subject, never both, so an outside borrower's loan, which has no account, is found by staff. Erasing an outside borrower by their verified addresses (part 2, "Erasure and merge") comes with an erasure handler in INV-27. Both loans and faults name their retention setting; the R5 sweep that erases rather than deletes comes with INV-27 and INV-37.
- **The promise check** is `layers/inventory/server/promise.ts`. `freeSql` is one predicate: C4 (active, lendable, in a category of the loan's department, no open disposal, no blocking kind overdue before the window's first operating day) and C1 to C3 together, as "the item's quantity is at least this line's plus what Promised and Out lines hold over the window". An individual item has quantity 1, so the same sum is C1. An Out line counts only what is not yet back, and one past its due time counts to the right end (C3). The statement that moves a line to Promised carries it and is guarded with `expect(1)`, refusing `inventory.loan.clash`.
- **Containers (C6).** Promising a container's line inserts a Promised line for each content item, linked by `with_line_id`, each carrying the check, and the container's own statement also expects its contents to be the number read. One failure refuses the whole unit.
- **Approval (L7)** reads first and refuses `inventory.loan.clash` with `detail` naming each failing line (`taken:<loan ids>` or `not-usable`); the statements then carry the check again, so a line taken between the read and the write still refuses the whole approval. Wanted lines stay wanted (L6).
- **Moves (I7).** Every move a record may make is the table in `shared/states.ts`; a loan's move is one update naming the states it may come from, refused `inventory.loan.moved` when someone else moved it first. Requested, approved, declined and cancelled announce `inventory.loan.<state>` with the loan and department ids.
- **Who may borrow (L3)** is on the loan's insert: the department must be active and lend to the kind of borrower, else `inventory.loan.not-lent`. A line names an item of the loan's own department that may be lent, else `inventory.line.wrong-item` (L2).
- **Settings**: all thirteen. `loans.default-length` and `loans.max-length` are not quotable: the kit lets only a public setting be quoted, and these are for signed-in members.
- **Abilities** are declared with the first route that needs each one; the gate has no routes, so only `settings.manage` so far.
- **The proof**: P-1 to P-6 pass against local D1 in workerd (`layers/inventory/tests/integration/gate.test.ts`), with C4 and L7 cases beside them. A run against a deployed D1 is still to do.

## Setting up (INV-05 to INV-09), 24 November 2026

- **Screens.** Three Admin tabs of the module, each a page of its own: Departments (departments, each with its categories), Stores (the locations tree) and Inspection kinds. Tags are the module's Lists tab, the `inventory.tag` vocabulary, starting empty. All need `inventory.setup.manage`, declared here with its routes. The cog modal on Items (S10) arrives with Items.
- **Setup steps.** `inventory.departments` and `inventory.locations`, both required, pointing at the two tabs.
- **Starting lists.** The four departments and four inspection kinds of part 6, each made by a button while there are none, once. Months in part 6 are stored as days: 365, 91 and 182.
- **Departments (I2, I3, L3).** A prefix is up to four capital letters, unique among all departments, and refused if another department's items already carry tags with it, so a tag can never be made twice. Changing a prefix keeps the counter. Archiving refuses while the department has active categories, naming the count; restoring refuses if an active department has taken its name.
- **Categories (N1, I8).** A category keeps its department for life, so grants on a department stay true. Putting an inspection kind on a category writes a due for every individual item in it, due on today's operating day; taking it off deletes the dues never inspected and keeps the rest as not applying. Taking a kind off and putting it back makes it apply to every item again. Archiving refuses while the category holds any item not disposed of, naming the count.
- **Inspection kinds (N7, N10).** Changing the interval rewrites, in one statement, the next due date of every applying due whose last inspection was a pass, as that pass's date plus the new interval; recorded inspections keep theirs. Archiving a kind keeps the categories' links to it, hidden while archived, so restoring brings it back where it was.
- **Locations (I5, I8).** Four levels at most, checked on the statement with a recursive query, as is never moving a location inside itself. A space is chosen within its venue. Archiving refuses while any item not disposed of is kept there (home or current) or an active location is inside it. Drag and drop for the tree is not built yet: a location is moved by choosing what it is inside.
- **Requirements** (departments' and categories' training needs, K1, and inspectors' requirements, N5) are stored and kept on save but not yet edited on screen; they wait for a picker over `core.requirement`.
