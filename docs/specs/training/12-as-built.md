# 12. As built

Where the build departs from, or is narrower than, this specification and the second-round base it amends. The build is the reference from here on.

## Base (TR-01 to TR-08, TR-17 to TR-20), 24 November 2026

- **Tables.** All seventeen tables, with their checks and indexes, are in the first migration (`training-base`), so later slices add code, not schema. The places trigger that keeps `training_sessions.places_taken` is added by hand to that migration, as Bar's sale-line triggers are.
- **Step kinds.** The course editor's input takes session, practical, reading and certificate steps. Lesson, quiz, activity and declaration steps are accepted once the slices that sign them off are built; the table already allows all eight.
- **Superseding.** A new training record is written in three statements: every awarded record for the person and course is first marked superseded by itself, then the new row is inserted, then those rows are pointed at it. This keeps both the one-awarded index and the reference to the new row true at every statement.
- **What counts after an award.** A renewal or a retraining counts only sign-offs made after the training record it follows (or, after a revocation, after the revocation), so the sign-offs that earned a record never renew it.
- **Reading steps.** Opening the material is noted in the cache for a day, keyed by the sign-in session and the step; confirming without that note is refused `training.reading.not-opened`.
- **Guards and the Owner.** The own-sign-off and own-award guards apply to everyone, the Owner included: they are four-eyes rules, which Matt's ruling of 23 November 2026 on own records leaves in place. Revoking one's own record is not guarded, since it is never to one's benefit.
- **Abilities** are declared with the first route that needs them, as the conformance check requires. This slice declares `course.view`, `course.manage`, `sign-off.record`, `record.view`, `record.award`, `record.revoke` and `settings.manage`. The fact `training.competent` comes with competencies.
- **The point `training.step-source`** is declared in this slice, for Front of house's FH-51; its contract file came with shadowed shifts (below).
- **Notifications.** `training.record-awarded` (topic `training.records`, can be switched off) and `training.record-revoked`; the expiry, lapse and cut-short notices came later (below).
- **Settings.** The seventeen of the second round plus `evidence.chase-after` and the two quiz settings. The video settings and Stream credentials come with uploaded video. Training's settings are on its page under Admin > Modules, following the platform's convention, not at `/console/training/settings`.

## Competencies (TR-22, TR-23, TR-35 to TR-37, TR-50), 24 November 2026

- **Verdicts** (`layers/training/server/verdicts.ts`) follow R2 to R8: one query for the asked competencies' active route items, one for the training records of the asked accounts over the merged account scope, then one kit `requirements.check` for the recognised items of pairs no route made of courses alone already holds. Nothing is stored.
- **The requirement source** answers `training:<competency id>`. A retired or unknown competency answers `unavailable`, so the host's `whenUnavailable` decides, as for Training switched off. It has no `checkAbility`: a rota asks about anyone. A person not signed in is not met, with a reason asking them to sign in.
- **Recognised items and other sources' guards.** A recognised item is checked as the actor of the request, so a source with a `checkAbility` (Membership, Awards and access schemes have one; roles do not) answers not met for someone else unless the actor may ask it.
- **Saving routes** sends every route in order. A route sent with its id keeps it; one sent without an id is new; a route left out is retired and kept after the others, never deleted (R9). A route's items are replaced whole. Course items must name existing courses.
- **Holders before and after** are both in the answer to the save, not shown before it: the screen shows the count beside the routes, and the save's answer says what it became. Counting holders reads people with a current record for a course of the competency, so a person holding it only through a recognised item is not counted.
- **References** of recognised items must look like `<source>:<id>`; a `training:` or `fact:training.` reference is refused `training.route.self-reference` (R7), as the table's check also does.
- **The learner's page** (`GET /api/training/me/competencies/<slug>`) lists active routes with progress per item. The next session and the next action per item come with sessions.
- **The fact `training.competent`** is declared `pin: true` with no default abilities, as part 4 says. It is judged by the same verdict as the source, now.

## Sessions (TR-09 to TR-14), 24 November 2026

- **The last place** is one conditional insert: booked only while `places_taken < capacity` on the statement, so of two people pressing at once one gets the place and the other is refused `training.session.full` with the waiting list offered (P2). The places triggers keep `places_taken`, and the table's check backs them.
- **Promotion** reads the first people waiting, then promotes them in the same unit, each only if still waiting when the statement runs, and tells them (P3, P4). A place promoted from the waiting list keeps its `waiting_since`, which is how it may be cancelled up to the start.
- **Rooms.** Publishing, or moving a published session, asks core's `isSpaceFree` (leaving out Training's own contribution) and Training's own published sessions, and refuses `training.session.space-taken` (P10). The refusal does not yet name what holds the space. Training contributes published sessions to `core.space-busy` as `training-session`, labelled "Training session", never giving way; so Training now requires the support module `occupancy`, as Spaces does.
- **Trainers of a session** are those named on it, or else the course's trainers. They are told of changes and cancellation, and named when it is too late to cancel.
- **Attendance** is marked from the start until `training.sessions.mark-within` after the end, never on one's own place (`training.own-attendance`). Marking attended writes one sign-off per session step of the version the session is held under, and evaluates the award, per person in the same unit; marking absent voids those sign-offs. These are statements per person rather than one set-wise statement, since each person's award is evaluated on its own; a session's capacity bounds them.
- **Walk-ins** without an account are found or made through `findOrCreateAccount` with origin `staff`.
- **Reminders** are sent by the task `training.send-reminders` every 30 minutes, once per place through the notification's dedupe key rather than a marker column. The reminder can be switched off under the topic `training.sessions`; every other session notice cannot.
- **Not yet:** the overdue-attendance card for trainers (P8), telling a holder whether attending would renew (P6), refusing to delete a space with future sessions, and the screens.
- **Abilities** added: `session.manage`, `session.run` (mapped by default to the course-trainer fact, beside `sign-off.record`), `place.book` and `place.manage`.

## Online lessons and quizzes (TR-39, TR-40, TR-42 to TR-45), 24 November 2026

- **Lesson sections** are saved on their own for a lesson step of the version in preparation (`PUT .../steps/<step>/sections`). Saving the course's steps keeps the sections of a step that stays a lesson. Starting a version copies every section, each carrying from the one it copies; ticking "changed" clears the carry (O5).
- **Reaching** is one row per person and section; reaching again inside the window changes nothing, and a reach from before the progress window or the last award is renewed. Progress on a section counts for the sections that carry from it. Reaching the last unreached section writes the `lesson` sign-off and evaluates the award in the same unit (O2). A practice section is reached the same way, by "I've tried it" (O3).
- **Video** in a section is an `https` address for now. The allowed hosts and uploaded video come with the video slice.
- **Quizzes.** A quiz step names a form attached to the course in the Forms slot `training.quiz`. Publishing the version pins each quiz step to its form's live version, and refuses `training.quiz.not-marked` unless that version has correct answers and a pass mark (Q1). For this, Forms gained one read in its services, `slotQuiz`.
- **Taking a quiz** sends the answers to Forms' `addResponse` with the pinned version in the same unit, inserts the attempt with the failed count as the statement's predicate (`training.quiz.limit`), and on a pass writes the `quiz` sign-off and evaluates the award (Q2, Q3). A refused attempt leaves no response in Forms.
- **What the person sees** is the score and pass or fail always, and which questions were right with their feedback as the step's `show_answers` allows. The correct answers themselves are not sent yet.
- **A passed quiz** is refused `training.quiz.passed` while it still counts towards completing the course; once a training record is awarded, taking it again counts towards renewal (A2).
- **Allowances** need `training.sign-off.record` at the course and are refused for oneself (`training.own-allowance`).
- **Sessions on a new version.** Publishing a version moves sessions not yet started onto it (V7).

## Shadowed shifts and step sources (TR-49), 24 November 2026

- **The contract** for `training.step-source` is `layers/training/server/points/step-source.ts`: part 5's table, with the picker as an optional component name and `since` a theatre-local day. A source's parameters are checked against its schema when the steps are saved, and a source that is not on is refused `training.step.source-unknown`. `GET /api/training/step-sources` lists the sources of modules switched on, for the course editor.
- **An activity step** takes `sourceId`, `sourceParams` and `timesRequired` (1 to 20). Starting a version copies the source and its parameters.
- **Reading a source** asks for the person's merged accounts at once, from the later of the start of the progress window and the day of their last award. An occurrence is skipped, never refused, when it is unconfirmed, confirmed by the person themself, outside that range, or already signed off for the course. Each new one writes an `activity` sign-off dated on its day, keeping the source, the occurrence and who confirmed it, and the award is evaluated once per course after them. The partial unique index settles two reads at once: the second insert does nothing.
- **Once per course.** An occurrence counts once towards a course, even when two of its steps read the same source; it counts again towards another course that reads it.
- **When Training reads** (H2): on `POST /api/training/courses/<id>/activity`, which the course page calls when the person opens it; from the listener `step-source-confirmed`, which reads the sources whose `event` is the one heard; and in the daily task `training.sweep-records` at 06:00, one unit per person and course, for everyone with a sign-off inside the progress window on a live course with an activity step.
- **Listening.** Training's `events.listens` cannot name an event no module announces yet, so the entry `'front-of-house.shadow.confirmed': 'step-source-confirmed'` is added in Front of house's FH-51 pull request. Each later source adds its own line naming the same listener.
- **Tests** use the fixture module `stint` in `tests/fixtures/modules`, whose source `stint.shift` keeps shifts in memory, so Training's tests do not need Front of house.

## Certificates and declarations (TR-21, TR-46 to TR-48, TR-52), 24 November 2026

- **Steps.** A certificate step takes its recognised qualifications (up to 20, each with issuers) and whether another may be named (default yes). A declaration step takes whether a file is required, optional or forbidden, and optionally a form attached in the Forms slot `training.declaration`, pinned to its live version on publishing like a quiz (V9; refused `training.declaration.form-unavailable` without one).
- **Files.** Training uploads evidence files through its own route (`POST .../steps/<step>/evidence-file`), under a new evidence id that the person then sends with the evidence; core checks the file in its job, and sending refuses a file still being checked. The slot `training.evidence-file` has `record` access, answered by Training's `core.file-access` contribution: the subject, or a holder of `training.evidence.verify` at the course, whose opening is audited as a read (E4). A step forbidding files refuses an upload and a reference (E2).
- **Who sends.** The person sends their own while `training.evidence.self-submit` is on; a holder of `training.evidence.verify` at the course may send for anyone. Only one piece of evidence per step waits at a time (`training.evidence.waiting`); withdrawing it allows another.
- **Seen only.** A certificate seen by a verifier (`POST .../evidence/seen`) is written verified at once with no file or reference, and signs off in the same unit. Only certificate steps take one.
- **Checking.** Verifying or approving writes the `evidence` sign-off, dated the day it was checked, and evaluates the award in the same unit (E1); evidence that expired while it waited is refused (E3). Rejecting needs a reason, which the person sees on their list (E6). Nobody decides their own (`training.own-evidence`), whatever their role.
- **Qualifications.** With a list on the step, a qualification must be chosen; one not on the list is refused unless the step allows another, and the queue marks it `notOnList` (E7).
- **Declarations** need a statement; the step's questions, if any, are sent to Forms' `addResponse` against the pinned version in the same unit (E5). A declaration's own end date, if given, brings the record forward as a certificate's does (E9).
- **The queue** (`GET /api/training/evidence`) lists waiting evidence oldest first on the courses where the person may verify, filterable by kind and course, and marks what has waited past `training.evidence.chase-after` as overdue (E8). The console home card and the weekly digest come with the screens and the digest job.
- **Notifications.** `training.evidence-submitted` goes to the holders of `training.evidence.verify` at the course (optional, topic `training.records`); `training.evidence-decided` tells the person the outcome and cannot be switched off.
- **Not yet:** deleting evidence files on erasure and in the retention sweep, and adding them to a person's download.

## Expiry warnings, lapses and cut short (L3, V6), 24 November 2026

- **The daily task** `training.sweep-records` (06:00) now also warns each holder once when `training.records.warn-before` remains (`training.record.expiring`, notice `training.record-expiring`) and announces each lapse once after the date (`training.record.lapsed`, notice `training.record-lapsed`). Once is kept by the columns `warned_at` and `lapse_announced_at`, set on the statement's predicate; each record is its own unit; a run takes at most 20 pages of 100 and the next run carries on. Lapsed is still read from the date: the row stays `awarded`.
- **Cut short.** Publishing a version that requires retraining enqueues the bulk-lane job `training.notify-holders`, which announces `training.record.cut-short` for each record it cut and tells the holders a page of 100 at a time (`training.record-cut-short`, which cannot be switched off), enqueuing itself for the next page.
- **A warning already sent** is not repeated when a version later cuts the record short; the cut-short notice says the new date.
- **Dates in messages** are written in words ("14 March 2027") by Training's `dayInWords`, since notice variables are text.
- **Not yet:** `training.sweep-retention` (anonymising ended records and their sign-offs, deleting evidence and its files past their dates) and the weekly digest `training.send-digest`.

## The members' area: learning (M1 to M6, M12), 24 November 2026

- **Navigation.** One entry, `training.home` ("Training", `/members/training`, shown with `training.course.view`). The teaching entry comes with M7.
- **M1 Training** (`/members/training`) reads `GET /api/training/me`: the latest record per course that a version has not replaced, each with its status word and, when it has lapsed, is expiring or ends early, the next published session still taking sign-ups; the person's places; courses part-way through (live courses with at least one step done and not held now); and their latest evidence on each step that was not approved. Its cards run lapsed, ending, not approved, places, then part-way through; then "What you hold".
- **M2 Courses** (`/members/training/courses`) reads `GET /api/training/catalogue`: live courses only, each with its ways to learn (rule W1) and the person's status with steps done of steps needed. It filters by a way to learn and by "Only ones I do not hold", both kept in the address. Course categories are not offered yet, since the category vocabulary is not declared.
- **M3 A course** (`/members/training/courses/<slug>`) reads `GET /api/training/me/courses/<slug>`, which refuses a course that is not published or has no live version (`training.course.not-found`, 404). Each step carries its action by kind: a session step points to the Sessions tab, a practical step says a trainer signs it off, reading opens the material and confirms it, a lesson or quiz says how far the person is (the lesson and quiz screens come with M10 and M11), a certificate or declaration opens its panel or shows it waiting or why it was not approved, and an activity step names what counts. A declaration whose step has its own questions is sent from M12 once the form can be filled there, with M11. The tabs are Steps, Sessions (published sessions to come) and What changed (every published version with its note).
- **M4 A session** (`/members/training/sessions/<id>`) reads the session route, which now also answers the course's address and `times`: when sign-up closes, until when the person may cancel (the start, for a place that came up from the waiting list), whether the waiting list is on, the person's place in line, and the course's next published session for a full one.
- **M5 My training** (`/members/training/mine`) lists every training record the person has held, with its standing and dates, and the evidence they sent with its state and any reason; waiting evidence can be withdrawn there. "Add a certificate" opens the courses learnt from outside, since a certificate is sent against a course's step.
- **M6 and M12** are one panel over M3. The file is uploaded first and the evidence sent with it; while core is still checking the file the panel asks again a few times before showing the refusal.
- **Not yet:** the Training section of My account, M1 as a members' home card, and the help pages under `docs/members/`.
